The Kaphtor Lexicon sets out the Linear A record as read in kaphtor: {lexicon} words drawn from {records} corpus records
at {sites} sites, each with its reading, the evidence for it, its tier and its rivals. The readings follow the Semitic
decipherment of Cyrus Gordon and Tom di Mino, in which Minoan is Tinitic, an archaic branch of Central Semitic written
in the syllabary whose values Linear B inherited. {semitic} words read as Semitic, {hurrian} as Hurrian, {egyptian} as
Egyptian and {aegean} as Aegean loans or names, and {with_root} derive their Proto-Semitic root from its associated
database, so a reading runs from the tablet to its cognates.

The tier carries the doubt. {confirmed} readings are CONFIRMED, where logogram, sound and arithmetic agree; {probable}
are PROBABLE, {candidate} CANDIDATE and {speculative} SPECULATIVE, and a reading rises one tier at a time, on new
evidence. The words are filed by field: personal names ({personal_names}), commodities ({commodities}), administrative
terms ({administrative}), personnel ({personnel}), ritual ({ritual}), toponyms ({toponyms}) and divine names
({divine}). Beside them stand one page for every inscription, {complete} of {translations} translations, the
{names} names of the onomasticon, the signs with their {sign_values} resolved and proposed values, and the {unread}
legible words that have no reading yet, each with its witnesses. {unread_intact} of them are undamaged in every
witness.
