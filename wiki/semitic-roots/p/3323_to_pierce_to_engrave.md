## Notes

PS \*ptḫ 'to pierce' (Huehnergard 1991: 694 apud SEDonline 1328). HALOT 988 compares Akk. patāḫu 'to bore into' with Hbr. II ptḥ; Hbr. and Ph. ḥ continue \*ḫ. Akk. derived patḫu 'holed', pitḫu 'perforation, wound' (CAD P 276, 435). Cf. 1014 \*pVtaḥ- 'open'.
