## Notes

Huehnergard 2019, 67 reconstructs \*pa/ʔap 'and then, and so'. DULAT 646 compares OAram. p, OSA f and Arab. fa. Not K0425 \*wV- 'and'; K0590 \*ʾVp- 'also' holds the ʾap forms.
