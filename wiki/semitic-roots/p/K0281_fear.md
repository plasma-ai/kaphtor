## Notes

Kogan 2025, 9 n. 23 rejects Fronzaroli's (2003, 21) identification of Ebl. ba-ḫa-du (ARET 13, 1 obv. xii 1–6) with Hebrew paḥad and relates it to Akk. palāḫu 'to fear', perhaps /paLḫat/ 'it is scary'.
