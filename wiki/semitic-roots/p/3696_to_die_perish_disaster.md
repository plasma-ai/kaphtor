## Notes

Lane 2456 gives fāda with aor. yafūdu and, as also, aor. yafīdu (art. fyd). Tomback 262 reads Phoenician ypd 'you shall come to ruin' in KAI 2; Krahmalkov 110 reads hny bʿlk 'I, your king' there. HALOT 925 compares Syriac pād 'sink, vanish, be mistaken'.
