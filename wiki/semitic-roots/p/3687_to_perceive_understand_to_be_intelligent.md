## Notes

Brockelmann compares Arabic faṭana 'intellexit' and cites the Syriac nouns from Isaac of Antioch and Jacob of Sarug (Brock. 564). Not SA018 \*pṭn 'assign land to s.o' (Sab fṭn; Biella 402 compares Arabic faṭana); not 3304 \*ptn 'to test, try'.
