## Notes

Krahmalkov 407 compares Heb. perek 'task' (Ex 1:13) with Phoenician prkm 'taskmasters' (KAI 37 A 6). HALOT 968 derives pārōket and perek from \*prk and sets Akk. parakku apart as a Sumerian loan. The parokhet separates the holy of holies from the holy place (Exod 26:33).
