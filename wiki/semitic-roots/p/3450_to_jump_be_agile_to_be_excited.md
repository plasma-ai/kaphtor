## Notes

PWS \*pzz 'to jump; to be agile' (SEDonline 1897); EDA I 408–409 (◊P0741) reports Zadok's tentative derivation of the name Pazūzu from pzz 'to be impetuous, agile' (Syr. pzz, JBA pazzīz, perhaps Arb. fzz). Cf. 2580 \*p(y)z 'to puncture, separate'.
