## Notes

HSED 906 prints Semitic \*gaw- 'man, people' with Hebrew gōy and South Arabian gw. Mari gāʾu 'group, gang (of workmen)' is a West Semitic loanword in Akkadian (CAD G 59; HALOT 182).
