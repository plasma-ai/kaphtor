## Notes

OLA80 §63.5 sets Libyco-Berber ižžar &lt; \*iggar 'field' and Agaw agār 'country' beside the Semitic forms, and doubts a loan from Sumerian a-gār 'water meadow' or from Indo-European \*agr-.
