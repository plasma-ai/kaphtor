## Notes

Brock. 121–122 sets Syriac gmūrtā (Prov 26:21) beside Arabic ǧamra. Lane 453 derives ǧamra from ǧammara 'he collected together' (Mgh). Not S62 \*gmr 'to be complete'; cf. 3530 \*gḥl 'burning charcoal'.
