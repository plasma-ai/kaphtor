## Notes

Verb of 2632 \*gVbVr- 'man, hero'; Payne Smith 59 derives the Syriac verb from gabrā 'man'. Ugaritic has the root only in the name gbrn (HALOT 175). Not 1898 \*ga/ibr- 'genital organ' or 3151 \*ʔbr 'to be strong'.
