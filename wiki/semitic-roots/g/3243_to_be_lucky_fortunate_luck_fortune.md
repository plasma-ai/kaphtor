## Notes

PWS \*gd 'to be good, lucky, excellent', a biconsonantal element (Kogan 2015: 33, 191; DRS 99‒100, 105‒106 apud SEDonline 40). SEDonline 40 also sets here Arb. ǯwd 'to be good, approvable, excellent' (Lane 481) and Sod. gud 'wonderful, marvelous, splendid' (EDG 261).
