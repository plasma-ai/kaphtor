## Notes

Kogan 2015, 196: PNWS \*gnb 'to steal', from \*ganb- 'side' (2353), ousted PS \*šrq (K0534). HALOT 198 adds Ph., Yaud., EgArm., Palm. and Mnd. gnb and Arab. ǧanaba 'to keep away'. Not 2353 \*ga(n)b- 'side' nor K0534 \*šarāq- 'steal'.
