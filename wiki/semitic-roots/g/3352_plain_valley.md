## Notes

Proto-MSA \*gāḥiy- 'plain, valley' (SEDonline 453). Cf. Tna. guḥo 'cave, crevice or hollow in a mountainside' (SEDonline 453). Distinct from 3310 \*gVy- 'valley, depression'.
