## Notes

Tomback 63 reads Phoenician bgw 'in the midst of' (KAI 17.1); Krahmalkov 138 reads GW there as a place name. Not 2355 \*gaw(w)iʾ- 'body; corps; chest, belly; interior', whose Notes set the non-anatomical \*gaw(w)- apart; not 3333 \*gaw- 'people'.
