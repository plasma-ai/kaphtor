## Notes

Emar gurnu 'threshing floor' (DULAT 305). HALOT 203 traces the sense from stone basin to threshing floor, with JArm. and Syr. gurnā 'basin, trough' and Akk. garānu; LGz. 203 adds Akk. garannu 'small vessel'. SED's 972 \*gūr- ~ \*gurVn- 'cavity; basin, tank' holds Arabic gurn-.
