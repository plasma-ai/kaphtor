## Notes

CAD S 300 marks sippu "Sum. lw.(?)"; Kaufman 1974, 92 takes it as cognate to Aramaic sippā, Hebrew sap and Phoenician sp, and rejects Salonen's Sumerian etymology. Hebrew hitpolel histôpēp 'to lie on the threshold' (HALOT 765). Not 311 \*sapp- 'bowl'.
