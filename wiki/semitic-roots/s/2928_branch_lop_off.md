## Notes

Hbr. sarʿappâ 'branch' (Ezk 31:5) is an Aramaic loan from this root (HALOT 770). HALOT 770 also cites Mehri śarġaif, Jibbali ṣeġlif 'flowering shoot of the palm', whose ġ does not answer Ar. ʕ.
