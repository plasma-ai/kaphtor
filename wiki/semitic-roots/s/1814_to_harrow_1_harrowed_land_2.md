## Notes

Sabaic s1kt occurs only as a proper name (ʔhl ḥrṯ s1ktn, Conti Rossini 1931, 195); reading s1k-t as 'plough' or 'harrow', and the context as 'folk ploughing with a plough or harrow', is highly hypothetical.
