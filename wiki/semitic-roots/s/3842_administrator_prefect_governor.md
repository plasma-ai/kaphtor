## Notes

A G participle of \*skn 'to be of use, take care of' (HALOT 755; DULAT 746). Akkadian sākinu corresponds to šakin māti 'controller of territory', of 2776 \*šakVn- 'to place, set' (HALOT 755); DULAT 746 sets OB šaknu 'governor' apart as a cf.
