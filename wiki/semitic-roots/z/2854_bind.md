## Notes

Hebrew zēq and Syriac zeqqā (also zanqā) assimilate the n that Arabic zināq keeps. HALOT 277 takes the Hebrew and Aramaic as loans from Akkadian sinqu; Kaufman 1974, 61 rejects that. Arabic ziqq and Geez zaqq 'skin, skin bottle' (HALOT 277) are another word: Arabic ziqq is from Aramaic, and Geez zəq perhaps from Arabic (LGz. 642).
