## Notes

HALOT 265 adds Tigre zāher 'clear, obvious'. HALOT 266 derives Hebrew zōhar 'glow' from this root and sets Aramaic zyhrʾ, zhrʾ 'moon' (2635) beside it. Arabic zahr 'flower' is 759, zahr- 'elite' 67; 2651 \*zhy 'be bright' is another root. HALOT 265 sets II zhr nif. 'to heed a warning', hif. 'to caution' apart after Schulthess, and cites Palache 25 for taking it with this root as 'to make clear'.
