## Notes

HALOT 265 adds Tigre zāher 'clear, obvious'. HALOT 266 derives Hebrew zōhar 'glow' from this root and sets Aramaic zyhrʾ, zhrʾ 'moon' (2635) beside it. Arabic zahr 'flower' is 759, zahr- 'elite' 67; 2651 \*zhy 'be bright' is another root.
