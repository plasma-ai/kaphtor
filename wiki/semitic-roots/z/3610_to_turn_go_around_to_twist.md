## Notes

LGz. 646 sets Akkadian zâru 'twist, roll, turn' beside the Ethiopic, and also compares Arabic zāra, Aramaic zār and Mandaic zur 'visit' and, as possible, Hebrew zār (zwr) 'turn aside from'. Leslau relates the root to Tigre dora 'go around' (d:z; 995 \*dVwVr-). Lane 1276–1277 files zayyara and ziyār under zyr; al-Ṣiḥāḥ enters them under zwr. Cf. SA175.
