## Notes

t-noun of 2109 \*wld 'to bear': CAD T 96 files tālittu (from OB on) with 'cf. alādu'; HALOT 1699 sets Akk. tālittu and Syr. tauledtā beside tôlēdôt, and LGz. 613 files Geez təwlədd (for təwləd-t) under wld. Sab tld (SD 147) is 'birth'. Al-Jallad (2026, 166) sets Safaitic tld beside Arabic tilād 'long-possessed animals' (Lane 312) and Sab tld '(servant) born in the house …; likewise, (animal) so born'.
