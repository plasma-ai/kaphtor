## Notes

LGz. 612 glosses the G stem wakkəḥa 'clamor', (T, Y, K) 'boast'. HALOT 410 compares Arabic wakaʿa 'reprimand' with ʿ for ḥ. Amharic (a)wäkka 'shout' and Tigre (ʾa)wkəʾe 'shout' (LGz. 612) are in the 'clamor' sense.
