## Notes

Noun of 430 \*wVbVl- 'lead'. Kaufman 1974, 44: BArm. bǝlô 'tribute' (HALOT 133) is a foreign word, perhaps Akk. biltu, perhaps LB bāru from Old Persian. HALOT 115 records Hbr. bûl (Jb 40:20) read '(pay) tribute' as a loan from Akk. biltu. Not SA004 \*blt.
