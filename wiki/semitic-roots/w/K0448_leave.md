## Notes

Biella (142): Eth. waḍʾa, waṣʾa, Heb. yāṣāʾ 'go out'; for use with the casting of lots (R3945/16?), cf. Josh 19:1 wayyēṣēʾ haggōrāl. Biella (143) compares Sab. mwḍʾ 'tribute (?)' with Eth. wāḍā 'gift'. See SA119.
