## Notes

PWS \*wṣ̂ḥ 'to feel pain' (SEDonline 1998), an Ethiopian–MSA isogloss (Kogan 2015:124 apud SEDonline 1998); the Jibbali form shows that Gz. ḫ is not etymological. Distinct from 2649 \*wṣ̂ḥ 'be clear, bright'.
