## Notes

Sabaic writes the verb with s1 (SD 162; Biella 133) and with s3 (SD 163; Biella 151); SD 163 refers WS3ʿ to WS1ʿ. HALOT 448 compares Hebrew yšʿ 'to help, save' (3332 \*yṯʿ) with Arabic wsʿ 'to be wide, roomy' and OSArb. wsʿ 'to take full care of'.
