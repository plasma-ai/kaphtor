## Notes

Arabic salaka, Geez salaka and Safaitic slk 'to travel, walk' are the š-stem of \*hlk. Krahmalkov 462 compares Arabic sallaka 'save, liberate' with Punic šlk; Lane 1411–1412 gives sallaka 'to make go along (a road), enter', in modern use 'to clear a passage'.
