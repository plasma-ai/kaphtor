## Notes

PS \*hadad- 'thunder' (Kogan 2011, 194: 'probably'), preserved as the name of the storm god in Akkadian, Ebla and Ugaritic; Soq. šed with hypercorrect š-; Arb. and Tna. more marginal. Not 554 \*hud- 'break' or 1107 \*ḥVdVd- 'forge'.
