## Notes

HALOT 251: Aramaic hn, later ʾn 'if', rarely 'behold'. Arabic ʾinna and Geez na- lack h-; LGz. 380 joins them. DULAT 337–338 gives Ug. hn both functions, and HALOT 251 refers I הֵנָּה to הֵן. CAD A/2 131 refers annānum to annû. Neo-Punic hnkt is hn + kt (Krahmalkov 161); T 83 compares Akkadian annakam. PS \*hanni- '(deictic element)' (Tropper 2001a: 17; Kogan 2015: 35 apud SEDonline 60). Ug. hnd is hn + d (DULAT 339).
