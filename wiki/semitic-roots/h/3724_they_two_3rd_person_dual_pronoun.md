## Notes

Biella 112 sets Arabic humā beside the Sabaic, and the suffix -hmy 'them both, their' (3 du.) beside Arabic -humā. Sabaweb lemma 2826 reads six hmy as the interrogative 'whether'. Cf. 3279 \*šumū̆ 'they (masc.)' and 3277 \*šū̆ 'he'.
