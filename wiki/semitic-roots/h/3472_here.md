## Notes

Adverb of the deictic base 3245 \*hanni- 'this'; CAD A/2 131 refers annānum to annû. Cf. 3482 \*hn 'behold'. Not 3033 \*halum(ma) 'hither'. Neo-Punic hnkt is hn + kt (Krahmalkov 161); T 83 compares Akkadian annakam.
