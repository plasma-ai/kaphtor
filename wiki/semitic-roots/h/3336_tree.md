## Notes

Proto-MSA \*hVrā̆m- 'tree' (SEDonline 104). The origin is uncertain; cf. perhaps Arb. harm- 'kind of saline plant', harmā 'old wood', Tgr. harämo 'straw' (Kogan 2015: 544 apud SEDonline 104).
