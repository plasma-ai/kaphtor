## Notes

Proto-MSA \*hā̆ 'I' (SEDonline 410). The origin of \*hā is obscure; deriving the MSA forms from PS \*ʾanā(ku) is difficult (Bittner 1913a:9–10, Zaborski 1994:256, Appleyard 1996:206–207 apud SEDonline 410). Distinct from 2496 \*ʾan-akV 'I'.
