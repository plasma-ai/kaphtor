## Notes

Noun of 1486 \*ṣVrVp- 'burn' (Akkadian ṣarāpu; Hebrew ṣārap 'to smelt, refine'): CAD Ṣ 113 enters ṣarpu as an adjective 'refined; silver', and Biella 430 compares Hebrew ṣārap. Sab ṣrf 'silver incense' (SD 144 '?'; Biella 430 '(?)') is another noun. Not 373 \*kasp- 'silver'.
