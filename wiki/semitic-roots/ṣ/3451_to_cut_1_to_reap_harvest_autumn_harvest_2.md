## Notes

PWS \*ṣrb 'to cut' (Kogan 2015: 575 apud SEDonline 1919). Cf. 3101 \*ṯ̣arb- 'piece of wood'. Biella 427 compares Modern Yemeni Arabic ṣorāb 'autumn harvest', ṣarab/yuṣrub 'reap, harvest'.
