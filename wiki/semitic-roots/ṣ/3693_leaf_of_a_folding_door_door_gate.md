## Notes

Lane 1680 files miṣrāʿ under ṣrʿ, the root of 496 \*ṣVraʕ- 'throw'. Sab dual mṣrʿy (SD 144; Biella 430), as Arabic miṣrāʿā bābin 'two doors set up, meeting together' (Lane 1680). Not K0815 \*ṣVrVʿ- 'leprous'.
