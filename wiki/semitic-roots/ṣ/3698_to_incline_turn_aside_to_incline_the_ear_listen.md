## Notes

LGz. 556 sets Aramaic-Syriac ṣəlā (ṣlw) 'incline' and Mandaic ṣla beside the Geez and calls doubtful the connection with ṣly 'pray' that Dillmann 1261 and Fronzaroli 1966 suggest; Syriac Pa. ṣallī 'to pray' (Payne Smith 478). Not 3493 \*ṣlw 'to roast' (Geez ṣalawa II, LGz. 556).
