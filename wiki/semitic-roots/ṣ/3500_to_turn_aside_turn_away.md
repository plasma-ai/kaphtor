## Notes

HALOT 1000 calls the verb denominative from ṣad 'side', and also finds likely Gesenius-Buhl's view that the verb is the root of the noun. Verb of the same root as 3501 \*ṣdd 'side'. Not 611 \*ṣad- 'shout'.
