## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṣtt 'to watch' (SEDonline 1523). The same meaning of ṣtt in the Arabic dialects of Dhofar and Oman may be due to MSA substratum or adstratum influence (Kogan 2015:570 apud SEDonline 1523).
