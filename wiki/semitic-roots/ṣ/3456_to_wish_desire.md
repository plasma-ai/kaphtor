## Notes

PS \*ṣbw 'to wish, to desire' (Kogan 2015: 95 apud SEDonline 2075). Arb. ṣabiyy- 'a youth' (Lane 1650 apud SEDonline 2075) mirrors the semantic evolution of PWS \*ptw 'to desire' (&gt; Arb. fataⁿ 'youth') (SEDonline 2075). Cf. 2727 \*ṣVbVġ- 'to dip, dye' (Akk. ṣabû 'to soak', another verb).
