## Notes

HALOT 1019 also gives Arabic saniḫ and zaniḫ 'to stink' and ṣanhat 'stink' (Gesenius-B.), with the radicals in another order and ḫ or h for ḥ, and Egyptian ḫnš 'to stink, decay' as borrowed from the West Semitic word.
