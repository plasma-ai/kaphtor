## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṣVlVl- 'ravine, wadi' (SEDonline 1518). Cf. perhaps Hbr. ṣll 'to sink' and especially məṣūlā 'depth, deep' (Kogan 2015:570 apud SEDonline 1518). Cf. 2792 \*ṣVl(l)- ~ \*ṣVwl- 'to float over deep waters; fountain'.
