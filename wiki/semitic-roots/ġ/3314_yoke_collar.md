## Notes

PS \*ɣull- 'collar, yoke' (Kogan 2001: 281‒282 apud SEDonline 2095). Akk. ullu is also written ḫullu and is neither two lexemes nor a Sumerian loan (Kogan 2001: 281 apud SEDonline 2095); EA ḫu-ul-lu glosses Akk. nīru (EA 296:38). Cf. 3247 \*ɣll 'to insert, enter; to plunge, dip; to dye'.
