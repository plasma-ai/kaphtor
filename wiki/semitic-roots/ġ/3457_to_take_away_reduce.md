## Notes

PWS \*ɣṣ̂ṣ̂ 'to take away, to deprive' (Kogan 2005c: 188 apud SEDonline 2091). SEDonline 2091 also sets here Gz. ˁaṣ̂ṣ̂ 'desolation, waste, bareness' (CDG 58, LLA 1027), Tgr. ˁəṣṣo 'distress, famine', ˁaṣo 'barren' (WTS 492). Cf. 983 \*ɣVyVṣ̂- 'become small'.
