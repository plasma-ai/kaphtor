## Notes

Lane 2277 opens the 'rancour' signification of ġll with its own break, apart from 'insert, enter' (3247 \*ɣll). Sabaweb 4202 gives Sab ġll H 'to damage'. 2588 \*ɣVlVl- 'mist, stagnant water', 3314 \*ɣull- 'yoke, collar' and 3062 \*rɣm 'anger, spite' are other roots.
