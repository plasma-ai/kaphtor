## Notes

Ug. ʕrb, Akk. erēbu and Sab. mʕrb continue \*ʕrb 'enter' (2675); Hebrew, Aramaic and Geʿez ʕ cannot tell \*ʕ from \*ɣ. Harari artu, ōrtu 'night' either &lt; Cu, or &lt;ʕrb 'with disappearance of b" LHar 32.
