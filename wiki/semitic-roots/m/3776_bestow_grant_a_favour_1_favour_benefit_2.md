## Notes

Arabic manna ʿalayhi also 'he reproached him for a favour he had bestowed' (Lane 3024); HALOT 602 joins that sense, Arb. mnn 'to reprove a good deed', to Geez and Tigre 'to despise, reject'. Not 2965 \*mann- 'manna' nor 850 \*mnḥ 'give, offer'.
