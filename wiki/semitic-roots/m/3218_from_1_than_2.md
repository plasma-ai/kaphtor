## Notes

PWS \*min 'from' (Kogan 2015, ch. 2 §4.2 no. 39). Ebla mi-nu 'from' is a PS archaism or early WS influence. Biella 45 sets Sab bn with Arabic min; Beeston (apud Biella 45) derives it from b- with the enclitic -n of prepositions (ʿbr/ʿbrn, ʿly/ʿlyn), beside Ugaritic b 'from'.
