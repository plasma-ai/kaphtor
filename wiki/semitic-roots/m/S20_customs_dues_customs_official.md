## Notes

The Punic attestation links customs collection to weighing (šql), connecting fiscal and metrological vocabulary. Kaufman 1974, 72: Akkadian miksu 'tax' &gt; Aramaic mks(ʾ) &gt; Arabic maks; the Arabic verb and noun forms appear secondary.
