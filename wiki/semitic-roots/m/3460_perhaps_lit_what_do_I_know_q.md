## Notes

Areal \*mīna ˀīdaˁ 'what I know?' (Kogan 2015: 338; AHw. 655; Tropper 2000: 146 apud SEDonline 2123). Formally or semantically similar are Hbr. maddūaˁ 'why?' (HALOT 548), OffArm. mdˁm 'something' (DNWSI 598), Gz. ˀəndāˁi 'perhaps' (CDG 28), Soq. ɛ́daˁ, ɛdáˁ 'perhaps' (LS 53) (Kogan 2015: 338 apud SEDonline 2123).
