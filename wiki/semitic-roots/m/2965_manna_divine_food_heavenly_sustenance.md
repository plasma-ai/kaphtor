## Notes

Exod 16:15 gives a folk-etymology, mān hûʔ 'what is it?', from the interrogative mān (HALOT 596, II מן, which lists Akkadian mannu 'who? what?'); the Akkadian is that interrogative, not a cognate of the substance. Arabic mann names the tamarisk exudation (Tamarix mannifera), a natural substance of Sinai.
