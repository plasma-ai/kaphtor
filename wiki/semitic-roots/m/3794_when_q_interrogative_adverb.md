## Notes

Huehnergard 2019, 67 reconstructs \*mataj. HALOT 654 adds EA matīma, Ph. mtm, OSArb. mt and Akk. immati &lt; in(a) mati. Not S73 \*ʾid 'when; then' nor 2504 \*mtt/y 'spread'. 2560 \*ma(h) 'what?' carries Soqotri mī 'when?'.
