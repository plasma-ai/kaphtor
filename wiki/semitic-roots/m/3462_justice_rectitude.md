## Notes

Areal reconstruction \*mayšar- 'justice, rectitude' (DUL 586 apud SEDonline 1854). Ebl. ma-sa-lu-um = AMA.GI₄. DULAT 586 derives Ugr. mšr from \*yšr and gives mšrt 'justice' (DNWSI 706) as Punic. Cf. 1446 \*yašar- 'straight', 2365 \*šrt 'to serve; service in the cult, worship'.
