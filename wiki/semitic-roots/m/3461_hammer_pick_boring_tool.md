## Notes

Areal reconstruction \*mnḳb 'hammer' (DUL 559 apud SEDonline 1839). Akk. maḳḳabu has a variant maḳḳibu. DULAT 559 gives the Ebla entry VE 763 as ma-qa-NI, with the rival readings ma-qa-li and ma-qa-buₓ. Cf. K0200 \*naqāb- 'pierce'.
