## Notes

Cf. 52 \*malik- 'king': Eblaitic malikum 'king, counsellor' there; HALOT 591 records a derivation of 'king' from \*malk 'counsel' (Eilers). Hebrew III melek 'advice' is conjectural (HALOT 592).
