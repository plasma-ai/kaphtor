## Notes

Brock. 377 compares Arabic and Sabaic mayr 'brought provisions' with Syriac mār, and adds Jewish Aramaic ʾmyr, Hebrew hēmîr and Akkadian māru 'to exchange'. HALOT 560 sets Syriac 'to import grain' and Arabic myr 'to supply, make provision for' under Hebrew I mwr hif. 'to exchange', with NB māru 'to buy' (WSem.). Biella 274 compares Sabaic myr with Arabic mīrat and māra. The Syriac root is mwr. Not SA056 \*mwr 'besiege'; cf. 3539 \*ɣyr 'provision of corn'.
