## Notes

Feminine of \*maniy- (see 2703). HALOT 602 s.v. מְנִי lists Arabic Manāt and Nabataean mn(w)tw with Hebrew Mǝnî; HALOT 603 s.v. מְנָת also lists Aramaic mintā and Akkadian manātu. Isa 65:11 pairs Meni with Gad in a fate-dyad.
