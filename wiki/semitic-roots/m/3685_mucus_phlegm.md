## Notes

Brock. 380–381 sets Syriac māḥūṭā beside Arabic muḫāṭ. Arabic maḫṭ 'the act of flowing; of going forth' heads the root (Lane 2695). Not 2087 \*npṭ 'to blow the nose' or 2369 \*naḫ- 'phlegm'.
