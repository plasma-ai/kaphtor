## Notes

SED II No. 52: not fully reliable, since independent onomatopoeic formations are likely. Cf., likely related with suffixed -m and perhaps interborrowed, Syr. būmā 'bubo', Mnd. bum, buma, NSyr. bûmâ 'owl', Arb. būm- 'hibou'; Amh. bum is an Arabism (SED II 72). Not 962 \*biʾ- 'hole' or 1827 \*(ʾV-)baʾ-at- 'cow'.
