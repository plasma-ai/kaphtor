## Notes

HALOT 151 asks whether the Hebrew is an Aramaic loanword (Wagner 45). Lane 233 gives first baqara 'he slit, ripped open', and HALOT 151 cites Arabic baqara 'to split'; Mandaic bqr 'to split, test' (HALOT 151). Geez baqala 'to examine, punish' (HALOT 151) has l. Akkadian b/paqāru 'to make a claim' is printed with ? (HALOT 151). Not 626 \*bVḳ- (bqy) 'examine' or 1832 \*baḳar- 'large cattle'.
