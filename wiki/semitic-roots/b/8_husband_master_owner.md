## Notes

Amorite ba'l reconstructed from Ha-ab-du-ʔHa-na-at /ʿAbdu-ʿAnat/ pattern; Ugaritic alphabetic confirms pharyngeal/velar fricatives fully distinct in Amorite (Lipiński OLA80 §19.7). Akk bēlī from \*ba'lī 'my lord' — ē from a + weak consonant (§21.10).
