## Notes

Agent noun of S44 \*bky- 'to weep, cry, lament'. Akkadian ša bikīti '(professional) mourner' (CAD B 225) is bikītu in a phrase. Arabic bakkāʾ 'one who weeps much' (Lane 242) is an intensive adjective.
