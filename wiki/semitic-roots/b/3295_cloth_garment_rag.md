## Notes

PWS \*bVt- 'cloth' (SEDonline 1764); EDA I 68–69 (◊P0097) finds a connection of Akk. bittu 'weft' with Ugr. bt, Hbr. bat, Arb. batt- and the EthS 'rag' words not unlikely. Cf. 1640 \*batt- 'bottle'.
