## Notes

Biconsonantal CīC paradigm (Lipiński OLA80 §44.5-8). OB: idīn (pret), idân (imperf), dīn/dīnā (imp), dīn/dīnū (stat). Heb: yādīn. CīC sub-class alongside šīm and šīr.
