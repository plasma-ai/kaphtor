## Notes

Biella 82 sets Arabic dalla 'show, indicate' and dalīl 'guide' beside the Sabaic. The agent nouns are two formations: Sab dlwl 'guides' (Biella 83), 'scout' (SD 35), 'scouts, spies, guides' (Sabaweb lemma 2413); Arabic dalīl 'a guide' (Lane 901). 3627 \*dll 'to hang down, dangle' is another root.
