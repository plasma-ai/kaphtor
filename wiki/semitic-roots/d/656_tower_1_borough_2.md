## Notes

Kogan 2025, 4, after Naumkin et al. 2016, 65, relates Akk. dīmtu to Yemeni Arabic daymah 'cottage, small house', Mehri dēmēt 'warehouse (for wood); byre, cow-house, pen' and Soqotri díme 'sheep pen'; the comparison implies a long vowel (dīmtu), as Sargonic construct dīmti (not dimat) confirms.
