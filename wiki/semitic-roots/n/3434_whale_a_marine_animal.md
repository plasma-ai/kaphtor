## Notes

PS \*nḫr 'to snore' (Kogan 2015: 332 apud SEDonline 314), related to PS \*naḫīr- 'nostril' (SEDonline 314). Akk. nāḫiru and Ugr. ˀanḫr are different formations of the root. Cf. 172 \*naḫīr- 'nostril, nose'.
