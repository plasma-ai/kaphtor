## Notes

Noun of K0231 \*nVṣ̂Vḫ- 'sprinkle'. Lane 2807 glosses Arabic naḍīḥ and naḍaḥ 'a watering-trough', as Safaitic nḍḥ (Al-Jallad–Jaworska 103). Sab mnḍḥ 'irrigation god' (SD 92; Biella 313) is another noun of the root.
