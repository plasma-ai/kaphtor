## Notes

Verb of 3655 \*nḳr 'hollow, cavity'. LGz. 400 sets Geez naqʷara beside Arabic naqara, Hebrew nāqar and Syriac nəqar; HALOT 722 adds Tigre 'to hack out, be one-eyed'. HALOT 722 compares Akkadian naqāru 'to scratch out', which CAD N/1 329 glosses 'to tear down, demolish'. Cf. 2255 \*nVḳVr- 'kind of bird', 3646 \*wḳr 'to hew' and K0318 \*quwr- 'bore'.
