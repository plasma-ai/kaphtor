## Notes

HALOT 678 traces nāweh from 'destination point (of semi-nomadic tribes)', Arabic nawan, niyyat 'destination', to 'pasture' and 'settlement'. Streck 2000, 108 leaves open whether Mari nawū is Akkadian or an Amorite loan; Sanmartín GlOS 2, 116 calls \|n-w-y\| a culture word.
