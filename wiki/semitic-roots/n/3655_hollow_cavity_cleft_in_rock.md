## Notes

Noun of the verb nqr 'to dig out, gouge out' (HALOT 722); Arabic naqara 'he pecked' (Lane 2837), 'he bored, perforated' (Lane 2838). Not 3645 \*nḳḳ 'cleft, crevice' (HALOT 721, under nqq). The verb 'to dig out, gouge out' is 3686.
