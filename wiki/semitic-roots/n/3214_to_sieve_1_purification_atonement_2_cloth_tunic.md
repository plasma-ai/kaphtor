## Notes

PS \*npy 'to sieve', one of two PS roots for sieving, beside \*nḫl (3215) (Kogan 2011, 242). DULAT 630 sets Latin mappa 'tablecloth', a Punic word (DNWSI 673), beside npyn 'tunic'.
