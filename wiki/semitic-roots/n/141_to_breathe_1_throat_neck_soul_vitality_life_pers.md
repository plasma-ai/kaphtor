## Notes

Reflexive pronoun base across Semitic: Heb nišba' bā-napšō 'he swore by himself'; Ethiopian nafs &gt; nass in reflexive constructions; base of Tigrinya/Amharic 3p independent pronouns (nəssu &lt; nāfsu 'his person') (Lipiński OLA80 §36.12, §36.28). PES \*nVfās- 'wind' (Gez. nafās, Tna. nəfas, Amh. nəfas, Sod. nəfas, Wol. nəfas) derives from the PES verb \*nfs 'to blow', from PS \*npš 'to breathe' (Kogan 2015, ch. 7 §4.1 no. 11).
