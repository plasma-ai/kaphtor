## Notes

HALOT 675 sets Arabic manhal under nhl 'lead, escort'. Hebrew nahălōlîm (Isa 7:19) is 'watering places' or 'thorn bushes' (HALOT 676). Al-Jallad (2026, 102) compares Arabic nahila 'to quench (thirst)' (Hava 796) and al-nahal 'the first drink' (Lisān 11:680).
