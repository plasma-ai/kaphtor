## Notes

Verb of 3655 \*nḳr 'hollow, cavity'. LGz. 400 sets Geez naqʷara beside Arabic naqara, Hebrew nāqar and Syriac nəqar; HALOT 722 adds Tigre 'to hack out, be one-eyed'. Cf. 2255 \*nVḳVr- 'kind of bird', 3646 \*wḳr 'to hew' and K0318 \*quwr- 'bore'. Al-Jallad (2026, 104) sets Safaitic nqr 'to write (on a stone)' beside Arabic naqara fī l-ḥaǧar 'to write upon a stone' (Lane 2837) and Aramaic nqr 'to dig up, hew out'.
