## Notes

Ugaritic nkr 'foreigner, stranger' (DULAT 622). HALOT 699 sets 'recognise' and 'treat as strange' under one nkr, with Akk. nakāru. OLA80 §15.8 relates Egyptian ntr 'god' to Semitic nkr 'stranger' (k &gt; t), or to Agaw nkara 'soul, spirit'.
