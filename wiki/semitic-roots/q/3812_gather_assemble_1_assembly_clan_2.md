## Notes

DULAT 682 compares MHb. qybwṣ 'gathering, reunion'; HALOT 1061 sets Ug. qbṣ 'assembly' beside qibbûṣ. HALOT 1062 adds Eth. qabṣa 'to draw together, collapse' and Arb. qabaḍa 'to seize, take, grab', whose senses differ. Not SA072 \*ḳbḍ 'seizers', K0307 \*qapaṣ- 'contract' (Geez qabṣa), 1025 \*ḳVhVl- 'gather' nor 2763 \*kanVs- 'to gather, assemble'.
