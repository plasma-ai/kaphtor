## Notes

HALOT 1122 takes Hebrew qᵉṣîʿāh as a primary noun, the source of Greek κασία. Sanmartín GlOS 1, 354 calls it a culture word and adds Akkadian kaṣīʾātu 'cassia, cinnamon'.
