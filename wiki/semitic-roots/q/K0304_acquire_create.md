## Notes

HALOT 1111 I qnh 'acquire, create'; qāneh 'reed' is a separate primary noun (HALOT 1113; 3035). Geez qanaya I, i.q. qānaya 'modulari, concentum facere' (LLA 447), is another root; the Geez cell is qanaya II 'acquirere, emere' (LLA 447–448).
