## Notes

Brock. 676 sets Arabic qanaṭa 'desperavit' beside Syriac qnaṭ 'timuit'. Al-Jallad–Jaworska 110 set Safaitic qnṭ beside Arabic qanaṭa 'he despaired most vehemently of a thing' (Lane 2568). 3235 \*yʾš 'to despair' is another root.
