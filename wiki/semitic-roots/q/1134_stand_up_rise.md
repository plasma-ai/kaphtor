## Notes

Akkadian qāmu is from Canaanite (LGz. 456). SED's Akkadian ḳamām- 'stand up straight (of hair), stand on end' is cited from AHw through LGz., which does not print it; CAD Q 76 has qamâmu (kamâmu) 'to dress hair', lexical texts only, cf. qimmatu 'hair of the head'.
