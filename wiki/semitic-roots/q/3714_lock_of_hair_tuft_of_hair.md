## Notes

LGz. 438 sets Arabic quzʿa, qunzuʿa, qunḏuʿa 'lock of hair' beside the Geez; Arabic quzʿat lacks the n, and BK 2 820 refers qunḏuʿat to qunzuʿat. Geez qʷanāz (Gt) 'plaited hairdo' (LGz. 438).
