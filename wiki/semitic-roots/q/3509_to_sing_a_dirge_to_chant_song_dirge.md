## Notes

HALOT 1097 and LGz. 437 also print Arb. qayna(t). Hbr. qyn is denominative of qînâ (HALOT 1096); Payne Smith 511 derives qanqen from qīntā. Gez. qānā 'melody' (LGz. 433) is qnw II (LGz. 437), another root.
