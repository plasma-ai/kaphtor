## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṯVfVr-at- 'dung pellet' (SEDonline 1548). Likely related to Tgr. šəfər 'manure, dung' (WTS 230); further cognates are uncertain (SED I No. 282) (Kogan 2015:575 apud SEDonline 1548). Cf. 2038 \*ṯapr- 'perineum; vagina; anus'.
