## Notes

OLA80 §35.7-8: Lipiński reconstructs an initial lateral, \*ślṯ, on South Semitic Geʿez śalās and Sab. s²- (cf. Libyco-Berber kraḍ/šarḍ); NWS, Arabic and Akkadian point to \*ṯalāṯ-.
