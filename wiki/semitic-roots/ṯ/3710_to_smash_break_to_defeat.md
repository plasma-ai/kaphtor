## Notes

Biella 544 sets Arabic ṯalaʿa 'break, smash (the head)' beside the Sabaic causative. BK 1 234 refers ṯalaġa to ṯalaʿa; Sabaweb lemma 1904 cites the ġ form ṯalaġa 'to smash (the head)' from ibn Fāris and the Lisān.
