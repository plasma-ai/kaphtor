## Notes

Biconsonantal root \*ṯ-m with long ū (possibly &lt; \*ṯawm- via monophthongization, yielding triconsonantal \*ṯ-w-m). Regular interdental reflexes: Arabic preserves \*ṯ; Hebrew/Akkadian \*ṯ &gt; š; Aramaic \*ṯ &gt; t. The Hebrew attestation (Num 11:5) lists garlic among the foods of Egypt remembered in the wilderness.
