## Notes

Verbal derivative \*tanāyu(m) 'to repeat, do for second time' (Lipiński OLA80 §35.34). Ordinal ṯānī 'second'. An early change n &gt; r explains the by-form ṭir- of ṭin- in Aramaic and Modern South Arabian (cf. Gurage, §17.6); Egyptian šn-w 'two', šn 'brother' (Lipiński OLA80 §35.4a). SD 151 enters ṮNY II apart from ṮNY I 'two'.
