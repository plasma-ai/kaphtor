## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṯḥr 'to wound' (SEDonline 1547). Krebernik (personal communication apud SEDonline 1547) compares Sargonic Akk. šaʾāru 'to conquer, to win, to destroy' (CAD Š/1 2).
