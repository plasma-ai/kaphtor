## Notes

Kaufman 1974, 65 takes kūru 'furnace' as common Semitic and keeps it apart from kīru 'oven' (Hebrew and Arabic kīr), which has a Sumerian counterpart GIR4 and may be an old culture word. Not K0411 \*tVnVr- 'furnace' (tannūr).
