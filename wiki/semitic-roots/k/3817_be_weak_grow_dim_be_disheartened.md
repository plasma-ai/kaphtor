## Notes

LGz. 217 derives Geez hakaya and Tigre hakka from the root by metathesis and adds Syr. qᵉhā (qhy) 'be blunt, be dull'. HALOT 461 keeps II khh 'to rebuke' apart. Not 943 \*maha/un-, 948 \*wVba/uṭ- or K0728 \*ḥalaš-, the other 'be weak' rows.
