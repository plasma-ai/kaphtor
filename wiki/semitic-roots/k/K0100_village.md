## Notes

Arb. kafr 'village' is from Syriac (HALOT 494). Pitch: Heb. kōper (Gn 6:14), Syr. kūprā 'pitch, bitumen' (Payne Smith 210) and Arb. kufr, qafr go back to Akk. kupru (HALOT 495). 'Wipe, cover': 2997.
