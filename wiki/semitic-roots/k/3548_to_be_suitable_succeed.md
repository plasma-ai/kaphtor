## Notes

Verb of the root of 2773 \*kuṯar- 'Kothar', whose Hebrew cell holds kōšēr 'prosperity, skill'. Aramaic kšr (Syr. kšar 'to prosper, succeed', Payne Smith 229) has š for \*ṯ and is from Canaanite or Akkadian (HALOT 503).
