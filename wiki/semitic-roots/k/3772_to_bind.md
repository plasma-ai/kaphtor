## Notes

LGz. 297 compares Arabic katafa 'tie the hands behind the back' and takes the k-p-t forms of K0101 \*kVpVt- 'bind' as a metathesis. BK 2 861 gives katafa 'to strike at the shoulder blade' as its first sense, beside katif 'shoulder blade' (1948).
