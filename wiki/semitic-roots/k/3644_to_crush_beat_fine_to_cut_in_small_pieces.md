## Notes

Leslau marks Geez katta K\* (LGz. 298). DULAT 465 joins Ugaritic ktt with Hebrew and Aramaic ktt. LGz. 298 adds Arabic (Daṯina) katt 'demolish, cut down' and Akkadian katātu 'be short'; HALOT 507 gives katātu 'to vibrate' (AHw.).
