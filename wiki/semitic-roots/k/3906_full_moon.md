## Notes

HALOT 487 takes Hebrew keseʾ as a loan from Akkadian kusēʾu. Al-Jallad (2026, 83) adds Jewish Babylonian ksʾʾ (kessā). The one Safaitic attestation reads b-ksʾ mlḥ 'at the setting of mlḥ' (C 523).
