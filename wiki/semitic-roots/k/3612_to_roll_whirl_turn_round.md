## Notes

Reduplicated in every branch; HALOT 500 heads it II krr. The noun is 1097 \*kakkar-/\*kikkar- 'round loaf, disk' (LGz. 292; HALOT 473: orig. form \*karkar). DULAT 450 compares Arabic karra, karkara 'to return' (Lane 2600–2601), which are 3565 \*krr 'to return; to repeat'. Distinct from 2642 \*garr- ~ \*grgr, with g, from 2503 \*krr 'put, throw', and from Geez karkara 'grind coarsely' (LGz. 291).
