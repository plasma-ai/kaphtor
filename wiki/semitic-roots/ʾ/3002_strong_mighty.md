## Notes

HALOT 6 compares Akk. ab/pru 'strong' and takes ʾābîr as artificially differentiated from ʾabbîr. Cf. 1847 \*ʾabbīr- 'stallion; bull', where SED finds no trace of 'strength'. Root \*ʔbr 'to be strong', with Akk. abāru 'strength': 3151 (SEDonline 773).
