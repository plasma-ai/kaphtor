## Notes

Huehnergard 2019, 53 reconstructs \*ʔantum(±uː). DULAT 118 compares Hb. ʔtm, Akk. attunu, Arab. ʔantum and Eth. ʔantǝmu. Not 2550 \*ʾanta 'thou', 3233 \*ʾantinna 'you (fem. pl.)' nor 3285 \*-kVmV, the 2 pl. suffix.
