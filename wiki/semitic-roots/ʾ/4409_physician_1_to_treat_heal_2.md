## Notes

A culture word from Sumerian azu 'physician' (Sanmartín GlOS 1, 47; CAD A/2 344). HALOT 73 reads the Hebrew PN ʾĀsāʾ, Asa, as 'God is (my) physician' with Stamm, beside Noth's short form of ʾĀsāp and Löw's Akkadian asu 'myrtle'.
