## Notes

Also numeral 'thousand' in Ugaritic, West Semitic, South Arabian ('alp-). Etymology either 'clan' or 'ox'; South Ethiopic bāqər 'hundred' may support 'ox' origin. Distinguished from East Semitic \*l'm (li-im) 'thousand' (Lipiński OLA80 §35.21). The verb 'to be accustomed to; to learn' is 3628.
