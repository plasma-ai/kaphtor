## Notes

Ethiopic 'old' &lt; 'sturdy, strong' (SEDonline 774). Akk. abru 'strong' (CAD A/1 63: meaning uncertain) and Amh. əbrit 'conceit, vanity, arrogance; period of wealth; right, rule' (AED 1195) apud SEDonline 773. Adjective \*ʾabīr- 'strong, mighty': 3002.
