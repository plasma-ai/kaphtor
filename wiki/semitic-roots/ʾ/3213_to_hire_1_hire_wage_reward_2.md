## Notes

PS \*ʾgr 'to hire'; in continental MSA it yields the terms for male and female slave (Kogan 2011, 237). Kaufman 1974, 33 takes Common Aramaic and Arabic ʾgr as cognate with Akkadian agāru. Not 658 \*ʾigār- 'wall' or K0481 \*śVkVr- 'hire'.
