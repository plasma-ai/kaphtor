## Notes

OLA80 §35.9: Semitic rbaʿ- 'four' is independent of the Afro-Asiatic words for four (Libyco-Berber kkuẓ, Egyptian fdw, Bedja fāḍig, Hausa fuʾdu). OLA80 §35.34 derives the Arabic Stem II and V verbs from the numeral. HALOT 83, LGz. 460 and SEDonline 854 file 'forty' under the numeral.
