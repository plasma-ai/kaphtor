## Notes

Kogan 2011, 196 sets 'light' (Akk. urru, Ug. ảr, ỉr, Heb. ʔōr) and 'heat, flame' (Ug. ủr, Arab. ʔirrat-, ʾuwār-, Tgr. ʾarwa, Soq. ʾérir) on one PS biconsonantal element; HALOT 25 derives ʔūr 'fire' from the root of ʔōr.
