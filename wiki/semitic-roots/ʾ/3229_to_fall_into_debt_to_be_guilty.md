## Notes

PCS \*ʾṯm 'to fall into debt, to be guilty' (Kogan 2015, ch. 3 §5.3 no. 1). Jib. ʾéṯəm 'sin' and, likely, Mhr. šəwṯūm, Jib. s̃ēṯəm are Arabic loans; Gez. ḥaŝama 'to be bad' is unrelated.
