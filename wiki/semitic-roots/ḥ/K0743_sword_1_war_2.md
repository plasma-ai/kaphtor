## Notes

Borrowed as Greek ἅρπη 'sickle'; Mishnaic ḥereb 'ploughshare' (M. Kelim 21:2), and Ugaritic ḥrb beside grain in KTU 1.6 (Rendsburg 2026, 209–210). Egyptian ḥa-r-pa \*ḥarba 'dagger, short sword', a loan (Hoch 1994, 233–234 no. 324).
