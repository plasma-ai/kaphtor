## Notes

ḥlk is ḥkl with its last two radicals transposed. HALOT 313 adds a Tigre verb 'hide oneself' (WTS 88a) without printing its form, and derives Hebrew חַכְלִילִי 'dull, dark' (Gen 49:12) from ḥkl with a query.
