## Notes

PCS \*ḥp 'to walk barefoot' (Kogan 2015, ch. 3 §5.3 no. 11), a biconsonantal element with various extensions. Mhr. ḥáyfi 'to be without sandals and unable to walk' is likely an Arabic loan.
