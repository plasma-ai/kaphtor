## Notes

PCS \*ḥlm 'to be mature, fat, vigorous' (Kogan 2015, ch. 3 §5.2 no. 25), usually derived from \*ḥlm 'to dream' (2067). Ugr. ḥlm 'mature animal' is doubtfully attested.
