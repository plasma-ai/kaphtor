## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ḥašy- 'earth, soil' (SEDonline 471). Related to Arb. ḥisan 'accumulated sand beneath which is hard rock', Tna. ḥašäwa 'sand, strand, sandbank', Amh. ašäwa 'sand' (Kogan 2015: 558 apud SEDonline 471).
