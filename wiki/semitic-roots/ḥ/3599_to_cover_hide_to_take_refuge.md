## Notes

HALOT 337 and LGz. 246 compare Akkadian ḫesû 'to cover (up), hide' (CAD Ḫ 176), with ḫ against Hebrew and Geez ḥ. The Ethiopic sense 'to lie' comes from 'cover, hide' (LGz. 246).
