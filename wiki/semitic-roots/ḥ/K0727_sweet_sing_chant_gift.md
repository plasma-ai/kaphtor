## Notes

Based on biconsonantal \*ḥal-. Biella 176 compares Sab ḥly 'gift' with Arabic ḥalā. Geez ḥəlyān 'bribe, gratuity' is from Arabic ḥulwān (LGz. 231). 3727 \*ḥlʾ 'to pay (money); to pay a fine' is another root.
