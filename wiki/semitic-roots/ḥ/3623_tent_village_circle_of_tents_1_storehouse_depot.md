## Notes

HALOT 296 refers Hebrew ḥawwōt to ḥwh 'to gather' (Arabic ḥawā); Krahmalkov 179 compares Neo-Punic ḥwt with Hebrew ḥawwâ. Hebrew has the place name Ḥawwōt Yāʾîr (HALOT 296). Not 181 \*ḥawiy- 'intestines', 252 \*ḥay(aw)- 'animal' or 2760 \*ḥayy- 'to live'.
