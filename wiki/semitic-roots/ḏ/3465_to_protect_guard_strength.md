## Notes

PS \*ḏmr 'to protect, guard' (HALOT 274, DUL 287 apud SEDonline 2137). HALOT 1581 notes that Amor. zmr/dmr and Ugr. ḏmr 'to protect, guard' answer Hbr. šmr with z and d. Sab. ḏmr 'to protect, protection' (HALOT 274, 1581) is read 'to initiate legal process against s.o.' (SD 39) and 'to bring a charge, accuse' (Sabaweb lemma 2117). Cf. K0524 \*šVmVr- 'guard', K0703 \*ḏVmVr- 'sing'.
