## Notes

Phoenician skr &lt; zkr &lt; \*ḏkr 'to remember', with z from \*ḏ devoiced to s; the same name is written zkrbʿl on three 11th-century BCE arrowheads and t-k-r-b-ʿ-r (skrbʿl) in Egyptian transcription (OLA80 §14.2).
