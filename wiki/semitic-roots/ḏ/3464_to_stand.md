## Notes

Areal reconstruction \*ḏwḏ 'to stand' (Kogan 2015, 338–339 apud SEDonline 2124). Akk. izuzzu has a variant uzuzzu. Kogan 2015 (ch. 5 §4.3.2) counts Ugr. n-dd 'to stand' among the basic lexemes unlikely to be borrowed. DULAT 611–612 reads /n-d-d/ 'to go, move; to disappear; to hurry, arise', with Hb. ndd 'to flee'.
