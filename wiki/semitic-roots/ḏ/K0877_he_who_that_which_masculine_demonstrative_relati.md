## Notes

SA grammatical morpheme; masculine counterpart of \*ḏāt- (K0876). Paradigm: ḏy (m.sg) / ḏt (f.sg). HALOT 246 analyses Hebrew hallāzeh as \*ha-l-ḏi and sets it beside Old South Arabian ʾl ḏī and Arabic allaḏī (relative).
