## Notes

HALOT 356 sets Akk. ḫarāṣu 'to cut in, cut off, designate' beside Hebrew I ḥrṣ and gives Ugaritic ḫrṣ without gloss. Cf. 3058 \*ḫrṣ 'estimate, assess (crops)'; not 2687 \*ḫurāṣ- 'gold' or 2896 'be yellow'.
