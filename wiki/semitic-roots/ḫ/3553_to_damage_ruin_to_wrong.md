## Notes

HALOT 285 also files Arabic ḫabala 'to confound', OSA ḫbl 'to be wild' and Ethiopic 'to act corruptly' here; K0768 \*ḫVbVl- 'bold' holds Arabic ḫabala 'maddened' and Geez ḫabala 'dare'. Kaufman 1974, 56 n. 122 sets Akk. ḫabālu A 'to ravage (a person)' beside Arabic ḫbl 'to despoil, damage' as cognates.
