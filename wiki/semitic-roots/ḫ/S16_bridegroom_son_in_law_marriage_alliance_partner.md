## Notes

Hebrew and Aramaic ḥ answers \*ḫ, which Akkadian, Ugaritic, Taanach ḫa-at-nu-tam, Arabic and ESA keep. In the Amarna letters one king calls another aḫija ḫa-ta-ni-ia 'my brother, my son-in-law' (EA 21:3; CAD Ḫ, 148). Hebrew ḥatan dāmīm 'bridegroom of blood' (Exod 4:24–26).
