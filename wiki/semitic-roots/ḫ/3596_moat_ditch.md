## Notes

Noun of 3555 \*ḫrṣ 'to cut in, incise'. Middle Hebrew ḥārîṣ 'channel' (HALOT 352). Kaufman 1974, 53–54 takes the Aramaic and Hebrew as a native North West Semitic formation, not a loan from Akkadian ḫarīṣu. Not 2687 \*ḫurāṣ- 'gold'.
