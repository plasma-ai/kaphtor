## Notes

Fronzaroli reconstructs PS \*ḫāḫ- 'plum-tree' (Kogan 2011, 204); terms for '(thorn-)bush' (Hbr. ḥōaḥ, Syr. ḥōḥā, Tgr. ḥaḥot, Tna. ḥeḥot) are possibly related (3662). Not 1755 \*ḫawḫ- 'hole, aperture'.
