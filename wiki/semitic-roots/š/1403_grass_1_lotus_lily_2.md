## Notes

Sense 2 is a culture word from Egyptian sšn (Erman-G. Wb. 3:485 apud HALOT 1454), from which HALOT derives the Hebrew. Sense 1: Akk. šišnu (šišanu, šešanu) '(a rush)'; CAD Š/3 126 compares Syr. šišnā 'rush'.
