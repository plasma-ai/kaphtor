## Notes

OLA80 §35.34. Denominative verbal formations from the numeral šabʿ- 'seven' — due to use in spells and conjurations. Palaeosyrian D-stem = adjuring; Hebrew Hiphil = adjure; Hebrew Niphal = to swear. OA confirms initial š: ᵈSi-bī = ši/še (deified Seven); OA ša-be. Later AB sebe/seba from š&gt;s. MSA: Mehri hōba, Soqotri yhobəʿ (š&gt;h/yh). Denominative verb: Palaeosyrian D-stem šibba 'adjure!'; Heb Hiphil hišbīʿa 'to adjure'; Heb Niphal nišbaʿ 'to swear' (Lipiński OLA80 §35.12, §35.34).
