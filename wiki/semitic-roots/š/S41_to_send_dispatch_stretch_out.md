## Notes

Arabic sāraḥa 'to dispatch' shows l/r interchange with Heb/Aram šlḥ (Lipiński OLA80 §17.5). Heb šallāḥīnī lāmā ʾāmitēk 'send me away before I kill you' — lāmā as preventive conjunction (§58.3).
