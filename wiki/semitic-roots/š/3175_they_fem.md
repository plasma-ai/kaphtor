## Notes

PS \*šina 'they (fem.)' (HALOT 252, Kogan 2009:68, Huehnergard 2019:51–52 apud SEDonline 1535); initial š- and h- are distributed as in the 3rd person singular pronoun (Kogan 2009:66, 68 apud SEDonline 1535). Gz. ˀəmmāntu continues PWS \*himā extended by -n-t-u, with irregular \*h- &gt; ˀ (Kogan 2009:68 apud SEDonline 1535).
