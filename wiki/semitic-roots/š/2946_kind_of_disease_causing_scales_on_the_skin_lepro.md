## Notes

SED I No. 243. Akk. š against Hbr. and Jud. s leaves the sibilant open, and Arb. ʔaslaɣ- 'lépreux' allows \*ɣ as the third radical. Cf. Akk. šīlu (šēlu) 'a perforation', šalattinnu 'an illness', salāʔu 'become ill', silītu (salaʔtu) 'illness'; Arb. sāliḫ- 'gale'; Amh. su(l)lo (SED I 214–215). Not 1777 \*šVlaʕ- 'pierce, split'.
