## Notes

Originally a determinative pronoun, weakened to a relative in Phoenician and Hebrew (HALOT 1365). Ugaritic š only in the personal name šbʿl 'the one of Baal'; the Ugaritic relative is d/dt (HALOT 1365; K0877).
