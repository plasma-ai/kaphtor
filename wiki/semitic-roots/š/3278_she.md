## Notes

PS \*šī̆/šī̆ˀ/šī̆ya 'she' (Kogan 2009: 66 apud SEDonline 1512). The initial s- of the MSA forms is irregular; Tgr. həta is formed secondarily from hətu, and Tna. ʾəta (TED 1467) survives only as the remote demonstrative (SEDonline 1512). Cf. 3175 \*šina 'they (fem.)'.
