## Notes

HALOT 1612 takes Hebrew I šʿʿ as cognate with, or a by-form of, Middle Hebrew šwʿ 'to smooth', the Judaic Aramaic verb. Payne Smith files the Syriac under šwʿ, šʿʿ and šʿʾ; Lane 1485 files the Arabic under syʿ. Not 2873 \*ṭVyVḫ- 'plaster' or 3534 \*ŝyd 'lime, plaster'.
