## Notes

HALOT 1641: JArm. šqṭ 'to trip up', Akk. šaqātu(m) 'to trip up'. Huffmon APNM 253: cf. Ar. sqṭ 'fall down', or skt 'be calm' with dissimilation. Cf. 3250 \*škt 'to be silent, quiet; to rest'.
