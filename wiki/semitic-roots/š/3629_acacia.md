## Notes

A loanword from Egyptian šnd.t, Coptic šonte (HALOT 1473). Hebrew place names: Beth-Shittah (Judg 7:22), Abel-Shittim (Num 33:49). Not 731 \*ṭal(V)ḥ- 'acacia'.
