## Notes

HALOT 802 separates II עור 'wake up' from I עור 'be blind' (2054). Syriac ʿīrā 'a watcher, guardian angel' is the participle as a noun (Payne Smith 407); Geez ʿer 'watcher, guardian', an epithet of angels, is from Hebrew ʿer through Greek eir (LGz. 68).
