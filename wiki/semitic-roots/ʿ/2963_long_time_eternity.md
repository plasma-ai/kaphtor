## Notes

Northwest Semitic. HALOT 798: etymology uncertain; essentially equivalent to Akk. dāru 'duration, eternity'. Arb. ʿālam is of Aramaic-Syriac origin (Jeffery 208, cited CDG, 61); Nöldeke 1910:37-8 holds G. ʿālam an Aramaic-Syriac loan (CDG, 61). Not 2624 \*ʕlm 'hide; know'.
