## Notes

Kogan 2015, ch. 3 §5.3 no. 3 sets Arabic ʿǧl 'to hasten' (Lane 1963) under \*ʿgl 'to be circular, to roll'; Payne Smith 399 files the adverb ʿgal 'quickly' under the same root.
