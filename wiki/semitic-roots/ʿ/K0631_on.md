## Notes

Proto-MSA \*lV- 'against, on' (SEDonline 1462). The MSA prepositions are semantically equivalent to PS \*ʕalay rather than to PWS \*li; the etymological \*ʕ persists in Soqotri forms with pronominal suffixes such as ʕəy 'on him' (Kogan 2015:562 apud SEDonline 1462). Cf. K0116 \*lV- 'to'; 1538 \*ʕall- 'height, the above'.
