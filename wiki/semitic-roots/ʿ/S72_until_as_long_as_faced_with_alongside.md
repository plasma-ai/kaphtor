## Notes

OLA80 §58.11. Two prepositions used as temporal conjunctions: ʿadi 'until/as long as', qadu(m) 'faced with/alongside'. Ugaritic ʿd tšbʿ 'until she was sated'. Geez ʾi-hal-lona ʾəm-qədma 'we did not exist till'.
