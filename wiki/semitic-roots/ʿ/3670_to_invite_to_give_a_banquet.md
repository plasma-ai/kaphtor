## Notes

Verb beside 43 \*ʕaŝVr- 'clan, friend, family', whose Notes take it from \*ʕaŝVr- 'group, society, common meal'. DULAT 185 calls the etymology uncertain and compares Geez ʿaśśara and Arabic ʿāšara 'he mixed with him, consorted with him, held social or familiar intercourse' (Lane 2051).
