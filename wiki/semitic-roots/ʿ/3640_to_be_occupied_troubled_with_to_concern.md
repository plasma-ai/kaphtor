## Notes

HALOT 854 sets III ʿnh 'to be busy' apart from I ʿnh 'to answer' (606), II 'to be humble' (K0642) and IV 'to sing' (620); Payne Smith 419–420 and Lane 2180 file the forms in one root article with 'answer' and 'mean'. HALOT 857 takes Hebrew ʿinyān as an Aramaic loanword (Wagner 222).
