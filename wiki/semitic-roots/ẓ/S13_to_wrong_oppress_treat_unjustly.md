## Notes

Arabic ẓ and Syriac ṭ point to \*ṯ̣. Al-Jallad (2026, 177) compares Aramaic ṭlm 'to oppress' and Sab ẓlm 'to be unjust, be bad', ẓlm 'injustice, wickedness'.
