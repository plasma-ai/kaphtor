## Notes

Geez tamr 'date, date palm' and Tigre tamar (HALOT 1756) are probably from Arabic tamr 'dates' (CDG 576). Kogan lists Geez tamr, tamart (CDG 576) among the reflexes (Kogan 2015, ch. 2 §4.2 no. 61).
