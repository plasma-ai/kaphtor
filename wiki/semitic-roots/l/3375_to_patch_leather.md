## Notes

Proto-MSA \*lkd 'to patch leather' (SEDonline 1467). Leslau probably rightly connects Hbr. lkd 'to catch animals by trapping' (HALOT 530) and Arb. lkd 'to cleave, to stick' (Lane 2671), all from 'to tie, to bind, to attach'; Sab. mlkd 'cistern' (SD 82) likely belongs to the same root (Kogan 2015:563 apud SEDonline 1467). Cf. SA052 \*lkd 'cistern'.
