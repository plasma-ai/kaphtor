## Notes

Proto-MSA \*lawḳ-at- 'jar, bottle' (SEDonline 1468). Cannot be separated from Tgr. loḳota 'skin for honey' (WTS 37), Tna. läḳwäta, loḳota 'pod, skin bag used as a container for grain, flour' (TED 94) (Kogan 2015:563 apud SEDonline 1468).
