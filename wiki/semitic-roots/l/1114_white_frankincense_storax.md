## Notes

Not 3239 \*la/ibin(-t)- 'brick'; II lbn 'to make bricks' is denominative from it (HALOT 517). HALOT 518 names the storax-tree libneh for its white racemes and takes Ethiopic leben from it; SEDonline 1796 \*lVbn- 'incense tree' covers storax and frankincense.
