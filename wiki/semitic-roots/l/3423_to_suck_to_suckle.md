## Notes

Proto-MSA \*lʕb 'to suck' (SEDonline 1463). Hardly connected with Hbr. lʕb 'to make sport of' or Arb. lʕb 'to play, to joke (of children)' (Kogan 2015:562–563 apud SEDonline 1463).
