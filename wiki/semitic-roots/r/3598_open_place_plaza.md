## Notes

Noun of 880 \*ra/uḥa/ub 'be wide'. HALOT 1212 compares Akkadian rebītu 'rectangle, plaza, underbelly' (AHw. 964b); CAD R 317 has ribītu A 'street, main street, thoroughfare', with no sense 'square'; cf. 2001. Biella 485 compares Sab rḥbt with Arabic raḥba 'wide place, open country, plain'.
