## Notes

HALOT 1279 sets Syriac rap, raprep and Arabic raffa 'to quake, convulse, flap, wave' beside Hebrew rpp po. 'to sway, totter' (Jb 26:11); Brock. 740 sets Arabic raffa 'tremuit' beside the Syriac. Not K0381 \*rVḫVp- 'hover' or K0365 \*razam- 'wink'. Arabic raff 'shelf' is 675, rff 'eat much' 829.
