## Notes

LGz. 465 sets Arabic riǧriǧa 'muddy remainder of a pool' beside the Geez (so also Armbruster 327, apud LGz. 465). Lane 1033 files riǧriǧat under rǧǧ 'to put in motion, agitate', and gives it also 'water mixed with slaver' and 'flowing saliva'.
