## Notes

HALOT 1281 takes Akkadian raṣûm Š 'to give in payment' from Canaanite (AHw 960a), and adds OSArb. rḍym 'negotiable currency', which Beeston 1982, 115 enters as Sabaic rḍy-m 'good coin' under rḍw/rḍy 'to please, satisfy'.
