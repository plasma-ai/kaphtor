## Notes

HSED 2111 prints Semitic \*rVŝ- 'sprinkle' with Arabic ršš and Harsusi reś. Arabic š, Harsusi ś and Syriac s are the regular reflexes of \*ŝ; Hebrew rss 'drip, sprinkle' (HALOT 1249) has s for the expected ś.
