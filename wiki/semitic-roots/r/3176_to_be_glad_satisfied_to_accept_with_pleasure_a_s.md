## Notes

PWS \*rṣ̂w 'to be glad, satisfied' (Kogan 2015: 126 apud SEDonline 1308); Common Semitic except Akkadian (Huffmon APNM 265). Ug. rṣy 'to treat graciously' (HALOT 1280) rests on an uncertain text.
