## Notes

PES \*rkb 'to meet, to find' (Gez. rakaba, Tgr. räkbä, Tna. räkäbä, Amh. räkkäbä, Msq. räkkäbä, Sel. räkäbä) cannot be separated from PS \*rkb 'to ride; to be joined' (Kogan 2015, ch. 7 §4.1 no. 13). Cf. 2665 \*markab- 'vehicle, boat, chariot'.
