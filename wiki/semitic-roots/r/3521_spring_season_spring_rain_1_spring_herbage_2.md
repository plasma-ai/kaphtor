## Notes

Geez rabiʿ 'spring (season)' is from Arabic (LGz. 460). Al-Jallad (2026, 119) compares Arabic rabīʿ 'herbage produced after the rains' (Lane 1018c–1019a) and tarabbaʿa 'to pasture upon the herbs of the rabīʿ' (Lane 1016c).
