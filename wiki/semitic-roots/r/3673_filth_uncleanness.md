## Notes

Geez rakʷsa 'be unclean, be impure, be defiled' is the verb (LGz. 470), with Tigrinya räḵʷäsä 'be dirty', Tigre räksa and Amharic räkkʷäsä. Lane 1146 gives riks as i.q. rijs; Leslau calls riks related to riǧs (Ambros apud LGz. 470). Not K0348 \*rakās- 'bind'.
