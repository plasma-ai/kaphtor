## Notes

Biella 433 takes Sabaic ṣbʾ as a by-form of ḍbʾ. Cf. Arabic ḍabaʾa 'come forth unexpectedly' (Lane 1763). Tomback (T 275) compares Akk. ṣabū 'group of people, troop of soldiers' and Ug. ṣbʾ. SED reconstructs the noun as \*ṣ/ṣ̂V̄bVʾ- ~ \*ṣ/ṣ̂ābaʾ-.
