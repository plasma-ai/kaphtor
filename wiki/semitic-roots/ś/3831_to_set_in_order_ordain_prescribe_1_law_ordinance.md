## Notes

LGz. 533 compares Ar. šaraʿa 'prescribe', šarīʿa 'law' and SAr. s₂rʿ 'erect, construct'. HALOT 1358 leaves open whether these belong with Hb. śrʿ 'to stretch out, extend'. Not 450 \*ŝāriʕ- 'road' nor K0396 \*šVnVn- 'to prescribe, institute'.
