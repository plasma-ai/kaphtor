## Notes

SED I, verbal roots, No. 69. The \*ŝₓ is very tentative, resting on Arb. š against the MSA sibilants. Tgr. šərum is formed as a passive participle of šärmä 'break through'. Probably connected with \*ŝrm {} \*ĉrm 'cut, split, break' (Akk. šarāmu, Syr. šrm; SED I 324), for which see 1118 \*ŝxVrim- 'pierce'.
