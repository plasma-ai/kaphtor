## Notes

Arabic šyṭ against Syriac swṭ. The noun is 2605 \*ŝiwāṭ- 'fire', whose Notes derive the MSA nouns from \*ŝwṭ 'to burn' (Kogan 2011, 195). Not K0497 \*śVwVṭ- 'wander' or 3668 \*swṭ 'to mix'.
