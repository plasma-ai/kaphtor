## Notes

PCS \*ŝaml-at- 'outer garment, cloak' (Kogan 2015, ch. 3 §5.2 no. 59), perhaps from \*ŝml (Arb. šml 'to cover, to envelop'). The Aramaic form is from the Uruk incantation, its only Aramaic attestation. Mhr. šámləh 'woolen garment' is an Arabic loan.
