## Notes

Ebl. sí-piš and Ugr. špš &lt; \*šampšu, with p inserted after m (OLA80 §11.9); Kogan 2011, 193 calls the Ugr. p unexpected. Soqotri šam: ʸhom, šɔm 'sun; day' derives from \*yawm- (786), not from this root (SLOnline 7297).
