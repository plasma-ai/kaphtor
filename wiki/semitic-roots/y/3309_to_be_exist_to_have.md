## Notes

PS \*yṯw 'to be' (SEDonline 2032). Ugr. ʔiṯ is lexicalised as a verbal functor (DULAT 120–121). The negatives join lā to it: Akkadian laššu 'there is not' (CAD L 108), Amorite lāšū (Gelb AS 21, 24), Syriac layt (Payne Smith 241), Arabic laisa (HALOT 443).
