## Notes

HALOT 424 compares Akk. (w)apû 'to be visible', šūpû 'to make visible' with Heb. ypʿ (hif.) 'to shine forth'. Akk. wapû: K0432. Huffmon APNM 212 separates \*wpʿ 'be brilliant' (Heb. hōpīaʿ, Akk. wapû) from \*ypʿ 'raise up; avail' (OSA, Arab., Ugar.), "due to the disagreement between \*w and \*y".
