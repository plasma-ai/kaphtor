## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṭapḥ- 'side' (SEDonline 1542). A connection with PWS \*ṭapḥ- 'span, palm of the hand', supposed in SED I No. 279, is unlikely (Kogan 2015:574 apud SEDonline 1542). Distinct from 2330 \*ṭap(a)ḥ-.
