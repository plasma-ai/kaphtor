## Notes

Phoenician ṭbʿt 'stamped seal' (Krahmalkov 201; T 119) is the ring noun ṭabbaʿat, an Egyptian loan (HALOT 369, &lt; ḏbʿt); HALOT 369–370 takes Aramaic and Arabic 'to seal, coin' as denominative from it and keeps ṭbʿ 'to sink' (Akk. ṭebû) apart.
