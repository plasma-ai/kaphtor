## Notes

Proto-MSA \*ṭlf 'to skim off' (SEDonline 818). The origin is uncertain, although an ultimate connection with PES \*ṭlf 'to snatch, to abduct' is not unlikely (Kogan 2015:574 apud SEDonline 818).
