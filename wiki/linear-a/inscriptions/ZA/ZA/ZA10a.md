### Notes

A roster of single words with numerals; PA 1 on line 1 stands in the logogram column, partly preserved (GORILA III 169). A-KU-MI-NA 1 and A-DU-KU-MI-NA 1 stand among names (A-MI-DA-U 1, DA-I-PI-TA 1) as cult titles on kummi 'cella' + -ān, the second tied to Addu, not cumin allocations, since cumin is always measured (MY Ge 605 ku-mi-no Z 2).
