## Translation

**SPECULATIVE**, complete.

> The prefect: wine-beer (SA-VIN). Gift for the Heroes 57; ʾItniša 10. Mixed wine: for the corvée gang 3; wine-stewards 6; libation portions 2½; Yaśmaʿū 5; groats 4; the one of Shechem 5.
