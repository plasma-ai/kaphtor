### Notes

Round serpentine libation table from Petsophas, broken at both ends: \]SI · I-PI-NA-MI-NA · SI-\[ (GORILA IV 30–31). Formula positions 4–6, restored as on KO Za 1 (Soldani 2012, 219 n. 115).

## Corpus note

Round serpentine libation table (HM 1586), Petsophas; broken at both ends (GORILA IV 30–31). A short curved stroke after the last SI fits RU but is too small to identify; Soldani 2012, 219 n. 115 reads si-rºuº-\[ and restores u-na-ka-na\]-si i-pi-na-mi-na si-ru-\[te, and Younger gives SI-RU\[ as possible.
