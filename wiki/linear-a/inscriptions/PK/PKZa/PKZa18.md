### Notes

Context: broken stone-vessel inscription from Palaikastro. Diplomatic text: \]-TE · I-DA · JA-JA-\[…. Both outer groups run into breaks; I-DA alone is complete between dividers. 'Ida, \[the Living One …\]' is a contextual restoration, not a word-for-word decipherment of the damaged groups.
