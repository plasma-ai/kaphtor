### Notes

The Neopalatial Petsophas fragment preserves complete JA-SA-SA-RA-ME after a divider, followed by Ụ-NA-KA-\[ or possibly Ụ-NA-KA-ṆẠ\[ before the break (Knappett, Del Freo, and Zurbach 2017, 73–80). It belongs to the same abbreviated formulaic pair as IO Za 9 but is not an independently complete duplicate. No preserved element means 'holy.'
