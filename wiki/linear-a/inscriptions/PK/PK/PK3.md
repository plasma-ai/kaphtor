## Corpus note

No edition in GORILA or RILA-S1. A four-sided bar from Palaikastro, published by Bosanquet and Dawkins (1923, 146, fig. 128, pl. XXIIIb) and Brice (1961, pl. XXVII, IV 6); it cannot be dated with certainty but is either MM III or LM I (Schoep 2026, 145). Its script is uncertain, Linear A for Brice (1961, 21) and a dubitandum for Petrakis (2017b, 80), as Tour (2026, 155) reports. The encoding opens with a lacuna before QE.
