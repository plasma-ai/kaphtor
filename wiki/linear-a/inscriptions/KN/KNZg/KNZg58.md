## Corpus note

Ivory handle KN Zg 58, four faces. α: two vessels, each in ligature with a syllabogram, then 10; a probable new sign; Hieroglyphic \*180 and \*181. β: four signs, the first damaged; a probable hide sign. γ: boar and probably pig, no numerals. δ: two vessels with a ligatured sign above, units, and six different fraction signs in sequence. No transcription published.
