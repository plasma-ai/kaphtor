### Notes

Context: ritual text painted inside a mended short conical cup; no numerals.  GORILA IV, 125 begins directly with complete A-KA-NU-ZA-TI and marks no opening lacuna, so there is no positive evidence for a lost word before A-KA-NU. Gordon segments that continuous group as A-KA-NU \| ZA-TI. ZA-TI may be /zāt/ 'this' (historically perhaps /zāʾt/), with TI spelling final /t/ and a nonphonemic support vowel. Hebrew zō(ʾ)t supplies the lexical parallel. Phoenician ʾGN is feminine, and FK D 35 hʾgn hz 'this bowl' supplies the closest structural parallel (Krahmalkov 2000, 30). Its postposed Semitic order is rendered naturally in English as 'this bowl.' The former /ḏāti/ 'of' analysis is rejected because its final /i/ is a live genitive ending and the required agreement frame is absent. The segmentation remains CANDIDATE: ZA-TI is a hapax, SigLA reads ZE rather than ZA, and final TI = /t/ is not independently established. DU-RA-RE is glossed conservatively as 'freedom/release': compare Akkadian darāru and (an)durāru (CAD D, 109; CAD A/2, 115–17) and Hebrew dĕrōr (HALOT, 230). The supplied relation '\[for\] release' is interpretive because the syntax after 'this bowl' remains uncertain. A Nabataean inscription from Petra begins \[d\]nh ʾgnʾ dy qrb, 'This is the basin which ... offered,' and proceeds to the deity and bšlm šlm, 'in peace, peace' (CIS II 423b; Milik 1972, 109; Alpass 2013, 102 no. 16); it supports the vessel–deity–blessing frame but contains an explicit dedication verb. Safaitic f h lt s¹lm w h bʿls¹mn rwḥ, translated 'O Allāt \[grant\] security and O Baalshamin \[grant\] relief' (OCIANA AAEK 123 = ASFF 246), independently supports a verbless divine petition with editorial '\[grant\].' Athirat, not the bowl, is therefore the most defensible implied subject. The final group is WI-PI-\[·\] (GORILA IV, 125), one sign lost after PI. Not readable.

## Corpus note

GORILA IV, 122–125 begins the diplomatic transcription with complete A-KA-NU-ZA-TI and marks no opening lacuna; there is no positive evidence for a lost word before A-KA-NU. Gordon segments A-KA-NU \| ZA-TI, provisionally /ʾagānu zāt/, 'this bowl.' The closest inscriptional vessel formula is Nabataean \[d\]nh ʾgnʾ dy qrb, 'This is the basin which ... offered,' followed by a deity and bšlm šlm, 'in peace, peace' (CIS II 423b; Milik 1972, 109; Alpass 2013, 102 no. 16). Safaitic f h lt s¹lm w h bʿls¹mn rwḥ, 'O Allāt \[grant\] security and O Baalshamin \[grant\] relief,' supplies a structural parallel for an omitted petition verb after the divine invocation (OCIANA AAEK 123 = ASFF 246). DU-RA-RE is therefore retained conservatively as 'freedom/release' (CAD D, 109, s.v. darāru A; CAD A/2, 115–17, s.v. andurāru; HALOT, 230, s.v. III דְּרוֹר); '\[for\]' remains supplied because its relation to the bowl phrase is uncertain. The mended cup is damaged at the end of the final group: WI and PI are readable, followed by an unidentifiable terminal trace or lost continuation. Record the group as WI-PI-\[…\]; whether the missing element was another sign or a divider cannot be determined.
