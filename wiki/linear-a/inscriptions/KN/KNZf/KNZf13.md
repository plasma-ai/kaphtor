### Notes

Gold ring HM 530, from compartment E of Chamber Tomb IX at Mavro Spilio, the compartment of a larnax; the silver pin KN Zf 31 comes from the same tomb (Olivier 1982). 19 signs in a spiral from the outer band inward, no dividers; RI (12, 15), MU (16) and KU (19) dotted (GORILA IV 152–153). The editors read sign 16 first as ME (Olivier 1982, 22), then as 23, MU (GORILA V 179), which Younger and SigLA follow; a short stroke crosses its stem, as in ME. The corpus sets the boundaries (SI-DI-, -DI-NA, PA-JA-, TE-RI, A-JA-KU), Olivier's frequency division with two groups split. The ring was worn, its inner diameter 1.30 cm, and its signs are cut to be read on it; neither it nor the pin lay in place (Olivier 1982, 15, 18–19). Olivier notes slightly wider gaps between some signs, and signs straightened to stand upright on the spiral (1982, 24); the bezel is convex and one centimetre across, and read as breaks the gaps leave A-RE alone and join MU to A-JA-KU. The owner and his city, then a lament: the weeping in the house, and the Psalm idiom 'you lift up my head' (Ps 3:4) addressed to ʾAyaku. Kirta's death-lament gathers the same words: bt 'house', bky 'weep', rỉš 'head' and ỉk 'how?' (KTU 1.16 I 2–28). Rival reading: 'Ark of Sidon. There is weeping in the tomb. You lift up my head, O ʾAyaku.' ʾarān is Hebrew ʾărôn, Joseph's coffin and the Ark (HALOT 85); Ahiram's inscription opens with the word, ʾrn z pʿl \[ʾ\]tbʿl (KAI 1:1), and Tabnit of Sidon pairs ʾrn and ṣdnm on his coffin (KAI 13); bayta 'house' is the tomb, as bêt ʿôlām 'eternal house', the grave (Eccl 12:5; Ps 49:12), also Punic and Palmyrene (HALOT 124f.).

## Corpus note

GORILA IV 152–153 (gold ring HM 530): a spiral read from the outer band inward, with no dividers. The normalized line underdots RI (signs 12, 15), sign 16 and KU (19). Sign 16 is ME there and in Olivier 1982, 22, and 23, MU, in GORILA V 179 (-04-53-23-), which Younger and SigLA follow. SigLA reads ku without comment.
