### Notes

Gold ring HM 530, from compartment E of Chamber Tomb IX at Mavro Spilio, the compartment of a larnax; the silver pin KN Zf 31 comes from the same tomb (Olivier 1982). 19 signs in a spiral from the outer band inward, no dividers; RI (12, 15), MU (16) and KU (19) dotted (GORILA IV 152–153). The corpus sets the boundaries (SI-DI-, -DI-NA, PA-JA-, TE-RI, A-JA-KU), Olivier's frequency division with two groups split. A lament: the coffin, the weeping in the house, and the Psalm idiom 'you lift up my head' (Ps 3:4) addressed to ʾAyaku. Kirta's death-lament gathers the same words: bt 'house', bky 'weep', rỉš 'head' and ỉk 'how?' (KTU 1.16 I 2–28). Tabnit of Sidon pairs ʾrn and ṣdnm on his coffin (KAI 13). Rival for signs 12–16: SE-TE-RI-MU sitrum 'protection'; Ps 27:4–6 has the words of both readings, bêt YHWH, sēter and yārûm rōʾšî, in three verses.
