### Notes

Longest multi-word metal inscription in the Linear A corpus. 8 of 9 words are hapax legomena. Four words are read, all SPECULATIVE; five are unread. Possibly a donor list, dedicatory prayer, or roster. DA-DU-MI-NE is read as 'Beloved of Minos', a theophoric PN.

### Source

- di Mino: DA-DU-MI-NE, A-WA-PI, A-DA-RA
