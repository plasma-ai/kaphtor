## Corpus note

A tablet fragment dated MM IIA, a dubitandum for Salgarella (2020, 43 n. 6, 48). Schoep (2007) classes it only as possibly Linear A; Olivier (2024, 60–61) calls its first publisher over-cautious, holds that the writing is certainly Linear A, and takes the fragment for the script's most ancient evidence, and RILA-S1 (34–35) formally includes it in the corpus (Petrakis and Steele 2026, 168).
