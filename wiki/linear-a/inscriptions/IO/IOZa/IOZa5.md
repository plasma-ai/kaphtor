## Corpus note

Broken at both ends (GORILA V 22–23): \]I-JA-RE-DI-JA · I-JA-PA\[. The divider after the fifth sign is clear. The fourth sign is damaged; GORILA reads DI and the drawing also allows A. PA runs into the break.
