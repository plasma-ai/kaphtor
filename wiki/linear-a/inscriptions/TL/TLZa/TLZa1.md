## Translation

**PROBABLE**, complete.

> I make myself a dwelling, O Sukar, in righteousness. So behold my gathering, \[in the presence of sacred song\].

### Notes

Context: Troullos peak-sanctuary libation formula on stone ladle HM 1545. The first four groups through U-NA-KA-NA-SI are preserved. Only NA-MA and SI-RU survive after the damaged interval; I-PI and final TE are formulaic restorations. O-SU-QA-RE is provisionally parsed as vocative ʾō + Sukar, with final E as the dead spelling vowel after /r/. Geʿez ʾo- supplies a later structural parallel for the vocative (Leslau 1987, 1), not proof of the Bronze Age form; the Egyptian Sokar identification remains CANDIDATE.
