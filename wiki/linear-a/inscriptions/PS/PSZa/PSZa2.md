### Notes

The Dictaean table carries the sentence that IO Za 6 writes on Iouktas, in the same three groups: TA-NA-I-\*301-U-TI-NU · I-NA-TA-I-ZU-DI-SI-KA · JA-SA-SA-RA-ME, 'May you answer us, my Anat, Breast-Who-Gives-Drink, in righteousness.' Here the middle group is broken at its start and ends -JA-(TI/E), where IO Za 6 has the goddess's name and title.

With \*301 = NA, TA-NA-I-\*301-(TI/E) is the N-stem of √ʿ-n-y 'answer', the bare form of IO Za 6's verb, without the indicative -u, the 2fs clitic -tī and the object -n(u) 'us'. Read with E, it is tanʿanē, 'may she answer' or 'may you answer': final -e comes from the III-y stem, and TA- leaves the person open. GORILA's TI gives tanaʿʿīnā-tī, di Mino's marked 2fs 'may you (f.) answer'.

JA-SA-SA-RA-ME, yaššaram 'in righteousness', closes the sentence, as at Iouktas, Troullos and Palaikastro. \]-RE-I-KE is broken at its start and stays unread.

## Corpus note

Steatite offering table from the Psykhro (Dictaean) Cave, MM III–LM I, in two fragments: the left corner, Ashmolean Museum AN1923.661, and a larger fragment with the rest. PS Za 2.1 \]-RE-I-KE runs along the opposite edge, broken at its start. PS Za 2.2 begins at the table's corner, so TA-NA-I-\*301-(TI/E) is whole and opens the line.

On the corner fragment, NA's stem is three drilled dots and I has five prongs. The sign after \*301 is an arch whose legs are joined by a V-shaped bar, with no stroke from the apex. E (LA 38) has a bar between the legs and TI (LA 37) has none, so the stone supports E (Ferrara, Montecchi, and Valério 2022, 87) over GORILA's uncertain TI.

After the divider, the break cuts the first sign of the middle group: a long upright with two short bars to its right, low down. Its identity is open. The drawing on the museum's record restores a whole sign there and closes the V of the arch, but the stone shows neither.
