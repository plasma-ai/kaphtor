### Notes

The roundel is whole: face a \*320-\*320-MA, face b RO (GORILA II 155), as PH Wc 39 has JA-DI on face a and RO on face b. \*320 is read as TI. Rival: a personal name in the slot of JA-DI.

## Corpus note

GORILA draws A 320 twice and then MA on face a, and on face b a cross, RO (II 155); Salgarella reads the two faces as one sign-group, 320-320-MA-RO (2020, 324).
