## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Šima: ʾiyyate (island)

| Word | Reading | Gloss | Confidence | Lexicon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[wiki/linear-a/words/S/SI_MA\|SI-MA]] | Šima | personal name ("Name/Renown/Progeny", = Shem; cf. Amorite Sumu-abum, Sumu-la-El) |  | CANDIDATE (lexicon) |
| [[wiki/linear-a/words/I/I_JA_TE\|I-JA-TE]] | ʾiyyate | island (feminine construct, √ʾ-y) |  | CANDIDATE (lexicon) |

### Notes

Vessel label at Phaistos. SI-MA as PN Šima (= Shem) parallels Amorite šumu- onomastic element (CAD Š/III 281). I-JA-TE may be provenance ("of the island") or a second name/toponym.
