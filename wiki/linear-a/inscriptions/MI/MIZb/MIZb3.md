## Corpus note

A jug handle with a post-firing inscription across its face, from an unknown findspot on Melos; the jug is likely local, 'probably' of the Second or Early Third City (Sherratt 2000, 350–51, apud Donnelly 2026, 71), roughly LM IA. Outside GORILA, a doubtful inscription in RILA-S1 (D 9, 152). Donnelly reads the two signs as the Cypro-Minoan sequence CM 023-082, which a Canaanite jar handle from Enkomi, ##115 ENKO Avas 008, also bears (Donnelly 2026, 71–72).
