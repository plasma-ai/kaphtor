## Corpus note

GORILA prints the two faces as one group, A-KI-DA-RO (III 104). Salgarella notes that it nonetheless classes them as two signs, A 640 (316 with a smaller KI) on face α and A 501 (DA with RO) on face γ, and takes A 640 as more likely the opening a-ki- of the word (2020, 63 n. 50, 93, 131). Davis sets A-KI-DA-RO beside KI-DA-RO as evidence for a prefix a- (2026, 46–47).
