## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> \[Terebinth\] carried by ship \[roundel receipt\].

### Notes

Context: KHWc roundel—sealed receipt; the seal impressions on the rim count the units (Hallager convention).

A fragment: the hull survives and the terebinth sign above it is broken away; Hallager calls the ideogram 'incomplete; it may be the same 'ship-ideogram'' (1995, 97). Three impressions survive (Papapostolou 1977, 162).
