### Notes

Context: KHWc roundel—sealed receipt; the seal impressions on the rim count the units (Hallager convention).

Twelve impressions of seal KH 5 (Papapostolou 1977, 162; Hallager 1995, 93 n. 36): twelve units. The terebinth sign stands above a pointed figure with a cross-stroke, a long stroke down and another rising to the right.
