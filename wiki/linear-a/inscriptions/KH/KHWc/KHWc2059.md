### Notes

Context: KHWc roundel—sealed receipt; the seal impressions on the rim count the units (Hallager convention).

Three impressions survive and part of the roundel is missing (Papapostolou 1977, 162), so three is the least count on this fragment. RILA-S1 enters the roundel as KH Wc 2059 \[+\] 2091 \[+\] 2092: the fragments 2091 and 2092 carry two impressions each of the same seal (1977, 164), seven in all.
