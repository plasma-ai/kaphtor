## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Men. Terebinth carried by ship \[roundel receipt\].

### Notes

Context: KHWc roundel—sealed receipt; the seal impressions on the rim count the units (Hallager convention).

The man sign stands on one face and the ship ligature on the other, where a break cuts the upper right of the terebinth sign: the men who brought or received the cargo.
