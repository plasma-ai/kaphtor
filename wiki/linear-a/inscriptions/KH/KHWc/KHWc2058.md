## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

### Notes

Context: KHWc roundel—sealed receipt; the seal impressions on the rim count the units (Hallager convention).

Nine impressions of one seal (Papapostolou 1977, 162): nine units. The drawing sets the gridded terebinth sign on the right part of a hull.
