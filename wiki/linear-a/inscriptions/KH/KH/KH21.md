## Corpus note

Line 3 \*306-QE-DU follows SigLA. GORILA III 55 reads \*306 · 100\[: the circle is the numeral 100, its inner marks undetermined, with traces to its right ('peut-être' a further sign). QE is SigLA's reading of the circle and DU of the traces, so both are uncertain.
