## Corpus note

Published as KH 99 (Andreadaki-Vlasaki and Hallager 2007, 11–13, Fig. 4). Found out of context in an LM IIIA layer, Hole B4; the editors date it LM IB, since fragments of LM IB Linear A documents had several times been found out of context in the Greek-Swedish Excavations, and quite a few LM I sherds were noted in the deposit (11 and n. 24). Davis (2026b, 134 n. 501), citing it as KH 99, counts it among the LM IIIA documents. The editors' reading is tentative: sup. mut., .1 A 352 A 306\[, .2 RO VIN\[, .3 KE A 704, inf. mut.; the first sign of .1 could be a large DE, the second is incomplete, RO is written over an erased ZA, and A 306 is 'probably the same ideogram as OVISf in Linear B' (12).
