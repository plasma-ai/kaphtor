## Corpus note

Found in a disturbed LM IIIC deposit, mostly LM IIIB (Hallager et al. 1991, 36–37, apud Davis 2026b, 134 n. 501); Davis counts the tablet itself among the LM IIIB documents. The excavators group KH 94–96 with the LM IB tablets (Andreadaki-Vlasaki and Hallager 2007, 9 and n. 14). Salgarella lists it among the LM IB tablets (2020, 48) but under LM IIIB in her Table 5 (44).
