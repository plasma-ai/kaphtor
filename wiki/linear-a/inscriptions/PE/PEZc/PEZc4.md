### Notes

A-JA is complete at PE Zc 4 and SY Za 2; MA 4b reads A-\[?\]-\[?\]-\[?\]-JA and is not an independent attestation. Mari ha-ia-/a-ia- for West Semitic ḥaya ‘lives’ makes /ḥaya/ or /ḥayyā/ orthographically possible (Weeden 2009, 96–100), but Haya, Aya, and Davis's structural reading remain rivals. The whole reading remains CANDIDATE.
