### Notes

Grain rations at ½ unit a head: 26½ is half of 53, restored from the broken 5̣0\[, and 36 half of 72 (Younger, Linear A Texts, after Tsipopoulou & Hallager 1996, 25–30). KU-PA-RI and E-KA\[ are two groups of men; KA-PA kapa(r) 'village' heads VIR+\[?\] 62 on HT 94a in the same frame. U-KA-RE A-SE-SI-NA ʾugār ḥašīšin 'the hay meadow' heads the list; men of the ugāru draw provisions in Akkadian: kurummat 27 ṣābī ša ú-ga-re-e īpušū 'provisions for 27 men who worked the meadowlands' (BE 14 56a:26; CAD U/W 30). The broken E-KA\[ is unread. Line .6 is blank.
