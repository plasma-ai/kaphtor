### Notes

Sherd of a handmade pithos from the cult building of area K at Tel Haror, MB III, in clay of the Myrtos-Pyrgos region, an ex-voto (Pierini 2022, 44). Olivier reads three logograms: animal head, cloth, figs. The script, Cretan Hieroglyphic or Linear A, is open (RILA-S1 D 13).

### Source

## Corpus note

Three logograms: animal head (BOS), cloth (TELA+TE), figs (FIC), as Olivier reads them in the editio princeps (RILA-S1 D 13; Younger). In the facsimile the TE hatching is drawn inside the cloth sign, a ligature, not WA followed by TE. Whether the script is Cretan Hieroglyphic or Linear A, RILA-S1 leaves open. Its date is uncertain; a possible Middle Bronze Age date, IIC or III, has been suggested (Karnava 2005, 838, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 122). Pierini has it a sherd of 'a handmade pithos', already broken in antiquity, from the cult building of area K with its offering altar, incense burners and figurines, one that 'can be dated to the late 17th or early 16th century BCE and interpreted as an ex-voto', its clay from the south coast of Crete (2022, 44). Rival: VIN+TE, wine, by its likeness to A 588 on the pithos KN Zb 34 (Pierini 2022, 46–47); Pierini has the ligature 'directly comparable to Linear B tela+te' (44), and Ugarit offers cloth, trmt lbš w ktn (KTU 1.43:3; DULAT 866).
