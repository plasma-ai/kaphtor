### Notes

One word appears on each face: face a (palm/volute relief) I-DA-DA, face b (lozenge-net relief) A-RO-TE. A-RO-TE remains untranslated. Exact Linear B a-ro-te occurs once on the Pylos textile tablet PY La 622 and may be related to a-ro-ta 'unsewn'; Killen nevertheless calls a-ro-te obscure. This is the strongest formal parallel, but its meaning cannot be transferred securely to the Cretan seal. Unattributed Cretan provenance.
