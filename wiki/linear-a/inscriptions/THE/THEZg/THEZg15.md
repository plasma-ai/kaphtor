## Corpus note

A discoid loom weight pierced with two holes, of non-local, likely Cretan fabric, inscribed before firing and found in an MC Phase C (early MM III) context, which for Donnelly (2026, 68) makes it the earliest Linear A inscription from Thera. The text runs upside down to the holes, its incisions slight, narrow and 'almost superficial' (Karnava 2019, 501, apud Donnelly 2026, 68).
