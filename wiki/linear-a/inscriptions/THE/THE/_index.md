[[wiki/linear-a/inscriptions/THE/THE/THE11|THE11]]: Tablet, Thera, SigLA codes the one sign A 614, GORILA's 302+53, OLE+RI (GORILA V xxvi), and an erased or unknown mark after it; Younger reads ]OLE[. On SigLA's tracing a slanting stem stands against the break at the right, with a long bar at its top, a shorter one at mid-height and a detached dash below, all to the left: the oil sign's upper part. The RI is not confirmed on the tracing, and the fragment is no witness of the lexicon's OLE+RI.. Translation: none.
