### Notes

One word, three signs whole with no divider (GORILA II 74). WA is drawn small and square, as inside a word; the cloth sign of HT Wc 3019 stands tall with four uprights. The twin of HT Wc 3008. Rival: WA · DI-NI, cloth and Dīnī 'My judgment', after the point GORILA's transcription prints on HT Wc 3008.
