### Notes

Face a WA-DI-NI, one word: GORILA's transcription prints a point after WA, its drawing at most a tick against the sign's right stroke, and WA is drawn square, as inside a word; face b RU-JA-TA-DI, no divider (GORILA II 74). The same name as HT Wc 3007. Rival: WA · DI-NI, cloth and Dīnī 'My judgment'.
