## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, incomplete (status: incomplete).

> The sanctuary. The queen: pressed oil (\*308) ½; libation oil, a fraction; qab of PU 1¹⁄₁₆; qab of RE 1; purple dye 1; SU-RE 108; honey 65; libation oil \[…\] RE 104; honey 53.

### Notes

A third tablet under the queen, by the hand of HT 33 (Montecchi 2010, 22). SU-RE has its second sign dotted. Each honey figure follows a larger one as a sub-entry.
