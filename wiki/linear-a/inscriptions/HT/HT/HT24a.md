### Notes

Context: LM IB two-section wool ledger. Wool allocations under the headings KU-PA₃-RI-JA and PA-SA-RI-JA.  GORILA I 42–43: .1 KU-PA₃-RI-JA · KỊ-MA+RU\[ .2 6 MA+RU-ME 10\[ .3 9 ½ MA+RU-MẸ 6 .4 PA-SA-RI-JA · MA+RU-MẸ\[ .5 RỤ-Ị-KO · MA+RU\[. The wool ligature MA+RU (A559) opens the group on every line, and marūm 'the wool' carries the quantities. Complete when RU-I-KO is resolved.

## Corpus note

GORILA I 42–43: .1 KU-PA₃-RI-JA · KỊ-MA+RU\[ .2 6 MA+RU-ME 10\[ .3 9 ½ MA+RU-MẸ 6 .4 PA-SA-RI-JA · MA+RU-MẸ\[ .5 RỤ-Ị-KO · MA+RU\[. MA+RU is the wool ligature (A559), RU's arms drawn across MA, and it opens the group on every line. On .1 KI is dotted and was written in after the ligature (note .1), which sits on the broken right edge. The second transcription opens .5 with a bracket.
