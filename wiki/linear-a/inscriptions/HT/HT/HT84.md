## Corpus note

GORILA's copie tabulaire sets \] before NU on .2 (GORILA I 128–29); SigLA reads NU-TI whole, both signs sure, a word of its own after one unread sign at the end of .1. That sign follows DU of \*21F-JA-DU: one upright with shorter marks, neither a numeral nor a fraction, so the word runs on into it.
