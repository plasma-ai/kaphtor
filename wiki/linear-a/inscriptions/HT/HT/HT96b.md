### Notes

Heading A-PA-RA-NE · QA-\*118-RA-RE · \*516 over grain 40¾, U-oil 4 and figs 2 K (K = 1/10). A-PA-RA-NE also heads HT96a, where A-\*123-TE stands in the slot of QA-\*118-RA-RE. QA-\*118-RA-RE = qaṭara (√q-ṭ-r 'to burn incense'), contingent on \*118 = ṭa; the commodities include no aromatic. .4–6 are blank over an erasure, with an erased group and traces on .5 (GORILA I 157).
