### Notes

Personnel roster, mostly 1 per entry. Visible entries sum to 22 against KU-RO 31: a lost pair sums to 9. Cross-face: KU-RO 31 + KU-DA 1 + KU-RO(b) 65 = PO-TO-KU-RO 97. Shares PA-DE, \*306-TU, \*324-DI-RA with the HT9a/b wine roster.
