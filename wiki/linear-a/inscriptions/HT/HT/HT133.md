### Notes

Context: Storm god grain allocation — LM IB  Short allocation tablet dedicating 55 units of grain as a sacred contribution (terûmā) under the Storm God heading. Compare HT92 (A-DU + GRA 680) for a parallel Addu grain allocation at much larger scale.
