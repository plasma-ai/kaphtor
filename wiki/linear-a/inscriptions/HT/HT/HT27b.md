### Notes

Two sections, headed by MI-DA and PA-SE. Each line names a commodity and then SA n RE m, the RE figure always the larger. SigLA classes SA and RE as transaction signs and reads VIN · GRA as one composite (A597), VIN · SI as A590. RE 70 rests on six clear tens and a seventh at the broken edge.

### Source

- GORILA I 50–51: transcription, damage
- Younger, Linear A Texts: MI-DA, \*591
- SigLA: A597, A590, SA, RE
