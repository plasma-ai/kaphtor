## Corpus note

LM IB multiple-commodity transfer list, six entries under U-TA-RO · TE. The left edge is chipped. .3: OLE+MI 6, the sixth stroke dotted (GORILA: perhaps 5). .5: OLE+\[ \], the adjunct lost on the chipped edge; GORILA prefers KI to DI, and SigLA codes OLE+KI. \*304 is a commodity of the oil register, not OLE.
