## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete (status: complete).

> Royal allocation—contribution—wine: Padī 5¾; ʿAttu 10; Dīnāyu 4; the official 2; Sōdir 2½; TA-I-AROM 2½; Ḫaru 4¼. Total 31¾.

Recto of HT9a/b. Entries sum to 31 against KU-RO 31¾: residual −¾ (J+E), probably a scribal omission. HT9b presents a closely corresponding roster under the untranslated heading KA-\*305.
