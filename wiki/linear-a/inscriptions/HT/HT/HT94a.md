## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, incomplete (status: incomplete).

> Village (KA-PA): men 62; the ship's crew (\*86) 20; TI+A 7; men of the class VIR+\*313b 18; wardens 4. Total: 110. Royal establishment (SA-RA₂): cyperus 5, figs 3⅙, 2; servants of the table (\*318-\*306) 11: cyperus (double mina), figs (double mina) 14½, figs ⅕.

### Notes

Recto of HT 94b. The five personnel lines sum to 111 against the inscribed KU-RO 110 (Schoep 2002, 160 n. 242). A numeral 2 follows the fig entry on its own line. TI+A is unread.
