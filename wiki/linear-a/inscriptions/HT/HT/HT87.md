### Notes

Context: personnel register with no commodity logograms. Six entries of 1 unit follow the \*21F-TU-NE and MA-KA-RI-TE rubrics. MA-KA-RI-TE is 'merchants', as on HT 117a (Ugaritic mkrm, DULAT 537); rival makritu 'red' (CAD M/1 138). Possible Hurrian names include Pidi-kešḫi and Dikise; for the latter compare Laroche's directly attested tagiše/tegiše 'beautiful' or 'beauty' and the Amarna personal name Tagi. One entry, A-RA-\[?\]-A-TU, is damaged.
