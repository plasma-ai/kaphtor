## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Fine-flour (account): Yāh—grain+QE 5, U-oil 2, KI-oil ½, anointing oil 1, TU-oil ¹⁄₂₀. The queen/royal establishment—honey-oil 1, figs 2, wine 3, VIR+KA-VIN 6. Arum-adal—grain 5, \*304 2, honey-oil 3. ʾIttaya—honey-oil 10.

### Notes

Recto of HT28a/b; A-SI-JA-KA heads both faces, and JA-\*21F and SA-RA₂ recur on 28b (GORILA I 53). The fraction after OLE+TU is L2, ¹⁄₂₀.
