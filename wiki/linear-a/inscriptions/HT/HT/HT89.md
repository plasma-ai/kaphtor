## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Captives of the sanctuary. Personnel classes: \*305 23; A542 22; water(-men) 24; VIR+\*313a 13; gatekeepers 5—total 87. Cyperus 2 + fraction; figs 2 + fraction; wine 6(?).

### Notes

HT89 is a personnel list followed by a separate commodity section. A-SA-RA₂ is one group on the tablet: /ʾasīr-/ 'captives' (Ug. åsr, DULAT 111; Akk. asīru, CAD A/2 331–32), SPECULATIVE; rival A + SA-RA₂ 'concerning the queen'. Uchitel includes \*305, A542, MA-I-MI, VIR+\*313a, and TA-RA among qualifications of personnel. Their counts sum exactly: 23+22+24+13+5 = KU-RO 87. Only after that total do A303/CYP, AB30/FIC, and AB131a/VIN appear. \*307 is dubur 'sanctuary' (PK Za 8 = PK Za 15); MA-I-MI 'water(-men)' and TA-RA 'gatekeepers' are SPECULATIVE. The expansions 'sacrificers,' 'offerings,' 'perfumers,' and 'turtledoves' are not established. Fraction values ¾ and ¼ are model-dependent, and the final wine numeral is damaged.
