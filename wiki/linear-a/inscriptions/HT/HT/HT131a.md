### Notes

Recto of HT131a/b summary account tablet. Opening damaged (𐝫). Large quantities indicate aggregation, not single allocation. KU marks subtotal line (353). I-QA-\*118 (SPECULATIVE) introduces a second commodity section, as it does on HT44a; this repeated position favors an administrative heading, although a personal or institutional name remains possible. If \*118 = ṬA, compare Arabic iqṭaʿ ‘cut, divide’ &lt; √q-ṭ-ʿ, without treating it as an attested Bronze Age formula. Verso (HT131b) continues with NI 30, OLIV 2, VIN+SA ?, and grand total PO-TO-KU-RO 451½. Implied VIN+SA quantity ≈ 66½ (451½ − 353 − 30 − 2).
