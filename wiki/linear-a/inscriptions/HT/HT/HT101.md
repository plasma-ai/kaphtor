### Notes

Context: mixed-commodity administrative account, Haghia Triada Casa Room 7, LM IB, HT Scribe 2. GORILA I 166–167 and Younger preserve six lines. A horizontal rule after line 4 separates the lower account. The tablet is substantially abraded and fractured; the lacunae after KU-PA₃-, before 20, and across OLE+KI 2\[ / \]3 are grammatically and administratively significant. Younger treats the three upper headings as parallel entities or toponyms; Salgarella and Petrakis (2026, 80–81) classify HT101 among mixed-commodity transfer lists. No KU-RO total is written.

## Corpus note

Mixed-commodity administrative tablet, GORILA I 166–167; Younger classifies it as Schoep type Ia. The horizontal rule after line 4 begins a separate account. Damage must be retained after KU-PA₃-, before the quantity 20, and across the line-5/6 sequence OLE+KI 2\[ / \]3. The upper portion contains three parallel entity headings: ZU-\*22F-DI, SA-RA₂, and KU-PA₃-\[…\]. SA-RA₂ is therefore an entity, recipient, institution, or title rather than a demonstrated surplus; ‘queen/princess’ or ‘royal establishment’ remains a candidate. KU-PA₃-\[NU\] is the strongest corpus-internal restoration, but the ending is lost and is not counted as an attestation. ZU-\*22F-DI remains opaque because the probable syllabographic use of \*22F is uncertain (Judson 2017, 88 nn. 237–239). DI+QE is an unresolved commodity compound. \*304 is securely a commodity in the oil/fat register, while its proposed identification with Semitic \*pidr- ‘fat, tallow’ remains tentative. OLIV+TU is a distinct olive subtype; its precise sense is unknown.
