### Notes

A mixed inventory below two lost lines: a vessel, animals, a weighed item and two kinds of cloth, each with a small count. Peruzzi saw the animals standing among contributions of another kind (1958, 10), and Sundwall the cloth sign twice in ligature (1956, 44). \*554-\*118 is unread; Peruzzi writes \*554 as a+ka, granting Meriggi's ka+ja for the order of its parts (1958, 10), and GORILA I 73 draws a sign over a circled cross.
