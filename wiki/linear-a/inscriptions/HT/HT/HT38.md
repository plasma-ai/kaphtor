## Corpus note

GORILA I 73: .2 \*403VAS+DA+RO+PA 1 · AB85 1 · AB21 3. DA-RO-PA is ligatured into the vessel sign (cf. HT31 \*415-VS-SU-PU, \*416-VS-KA-RO-PA₃); the third sign is AB21 (sheep), not AB22 (goat), as SigLA.
