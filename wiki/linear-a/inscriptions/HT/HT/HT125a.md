### Notes

GORILA I, 214–215 describes the upper part as damaged (sup. mutil.). The inscription is an oil-and-grain account, not continuous prose. Fraction D/A703 is ⅙ under Corazza et al. 2021; J is ½. The commodity subtypes are secure, but their proposed Semitic expansions remain CANDIDATE.
