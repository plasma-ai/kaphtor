### Notes

Totals of HT 116a. GRA 100\[ is broken at the right, and the recto's grain sums to 109. \*304 15 matches the recto. OLE 17 sums the oil subtypes: 18 on the recto, or 17 if OLE+MI on a.3 is 5 (its sixth stroke is dotted). \*304 is a commodity of the oil register; compare pidru/peder 'fat, suet'.
