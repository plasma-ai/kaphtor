## Translation

**SPECULATIVE**, complete.

> The village: Daṯar, offering, figs 15; Bittaya 24; JA+RU ⅓; the infants 10; ʾŌl-dīn 2; the village's commissioner 5¾. The town: baskets 22¾; figs 15½.

### Notes

A fig account in two sections, the village (KA-PA) and the town (DA-QE-RA) (GORILA I 13). KA-PA-QE runs over the line break, KA ending .4 and PA-QE heading .5.
