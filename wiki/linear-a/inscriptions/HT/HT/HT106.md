### Notes

Context: Casa Room 7 commodity allocation — LM IB. Scribe: HT Scribe 19.  MI-NU-TE heads a quantified allocation and is read against Akkadian minūtu 'amount, number; reckoning' (CAD M/2 98), rather than as the goddess Minut. The three SI entries (5½ + 1 + 3 = 9½) may be sub-distributions. The separator followed by DA-\[·\] 8 + SI 3 begins a second party or category. Compare MI-NU-TE in the Addu section of HT86a.
