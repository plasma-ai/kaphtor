### Notes

Two halves under the same heading, the first closed by a divider standing alone (GORILA I 237). In the second, KA and pressed oil alternate down the tablet.

\*86-SI-NI and \*86-SI-\*118-KA are unread. GORILA allows NI on .1 and KA on .4 to be logograms, and Uchitel divides both groups: the ship sign and SI, then figs 1\[; the ship sign with SI and \*118 as its complements, then KA 3. He takes KA for a container of liquids, its ratio to the oil running from 4:3 to 1:2 (2016b, 12–13). He prints the oil sign of the second half as \*302+RO; it is the sign of HT 2 .1 and .3, OLE+U.
