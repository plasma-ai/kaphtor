### Notes

Grain allocation roster. Header KI-RE-TA-NA (Cretan gentilic) + VIR 1, then GRA+H 70 (GRA+H = fraction rider, grain in 1/16 measure), divider, three recipients: DI-NA-RO (2), DU-SU-NI (4\[, a trace after the numeral), \[?\]-RA₂-TI (10). JU-\[ on .3 opens a word broken at the edge (GORILA I 175). DU-SU-NI: Dišon, as Hebrew Dîšôn 'antelope'; rival a Hurrian name in -nni. Remains incomplete: VIR+\[?\] damaged, \[?\]-RA₂-TI and JU-\[ broken.
