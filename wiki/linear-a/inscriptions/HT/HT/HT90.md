## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> At the horn: for the queen/royal establishment—grain 20, figs 10, honey-oil 3. Ṣill-marʾita, 'Shade of the Lady' (personal name)—grain 1, figs 1, anointing oil 1, unidentified commodity 1

### Notes

Two-recipient commodity allocation under a toponym heading. SA-RA₂ receives the bulk allocation and is treated as a royal recipient or establishment, not 'remainder.' SI-RU-MA-RI-TA₂ is one word (GORILA I 143), the second recipient, with token quantities. OLE+MI occurs only in its line, versus OLE+DI in the main line. \*304 is an unidentified commodity ideogram in the oil/fat register; pidru(?) 'fat, tallow' is tentative.
