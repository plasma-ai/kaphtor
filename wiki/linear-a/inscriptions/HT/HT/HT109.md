### Notes

Top and left edge broken (GORILA I 176–177). .1 \]RE 4\[ and .2 \]TA 4 keep only their last sign; .3 \]KU-RO 129 totals the lost entries, and .4 \]A-RA-JU 123 follows the total, starting at the same margin. With JU = yu, A-RA-JU spells ᵘʳᵘA-ra-yu, Ugaritic ary (RGTC 12/2, 30); rival Aralu under JU = lu, nearest Alalaḫ (RGTC 12/2, 8). Remains incomplete: the entries above the total are lost.
