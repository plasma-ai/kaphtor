### Notes

GORILA I 217 and the photograph show that the right side and lower center are lost; this is a damaged account, not continuous prose. Only SI-DI-JA can presently be identified beyond its administrative role. The initial break before \]U-\*49 prevents HT126a from supporting either a complete U-\*49 word or the translation 'and palm/tree produce.'

## Corpus note

GORILA I 217: .1 DA-NA-SI · \*318 ·\[, broken after the second divider; .2 A-NA-\[·\] 1, the 1 dotted, a lost sign after NA, not a divider (its note: a pebble makes the sign hard to identify); .3–9 U\[ \]J SI-DI-JA 1\[ \]U-\*49 1 PI\[ \]62\[ \]2\[. GORILA I 217 and the photograph show extensive loss along the right and lower edges. DA-NA-SI is intact but remains untranslated; \*318 is an unresolved administrative or transaction sign. A-NA-\[?\], U\[, PI\[, and the final quantities are incomplete. SI-DI-JA is probably the personal name Sidiya/Šadiya: compare Ugaritic alphabetic śdy (BAH 214, 7, o 14; ORACC P521356.17.3) and syllabic {m}ša-di-ia (RS 15.0173, r. 2′; ORACC P503354.14.2). Its earlier derivation from Shaddai is not established. Because the break precedes \]U-\*49, HT126a preserves the sequence but does not establish a complete U-\*49 word or an initial conjunction.
