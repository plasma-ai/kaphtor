### Notes

A partnership account: three partners' shares and their total. The arithmetic is exact: 45½ + 20½ + 29 = 95. TI follows each name, and each name stands without it elsewhere (DA-KU-SE-NE on HT 103, PA-RO-SU on HT 20, i-du at Ebla); GORILA I 171 allows TI to be an ideogram, and what it counts is not named.

## Corpus note

The corrected reading of .3–4 is PA-RO-SU-TI: GORILA prints PA-DA-SU-TI, every sign dotted, and does not exclude PA-RO (I 171); Schoep reads PA-RO-SU-TI (2002, 47). GORILA I 170–171; LM IB clay tablet from Casa Room 7, written by HT Scribe 5. HT104 is best classified as an inter-entity transfer list: TA-PA is the unidentified header entity, TE+RO, GORILA's A 505, is a hapax transaction sign, and the commodity and direction of transfer are unexpressed. The upper-right edge is chipped, but the current sign-level transcription records no lost sign after A505. DA-KU-SE-NE-TI probably contains the Hurrian-style name Takušenni; bare DA-KU-SE-NE occurs twice on HT103. Repeated final TI may be a suffix or classifier. GORILA allowed an ideographic interpretation; HT104 does not establish an ablative. I-DU-TI and PA-RO-SU-TI are the other two partners, Yidu and Parʿōš, each with TI. I-DU-TI is not securely Ida, which is written I-DA-A at KOZa1; a Semitic onomastic base /Yidu/ ‘strength’ is possible but unproved. The quantities total exactly: 45½ + 20½ + 29 = 95.
