### Notes

A list of persons with grain and cattle under \*47-NU-RA-JA Šū-nūraya, on a palimpsest; HT 115b repeats the frame with TI-NU-JA · NU-WI. \*301-U-RA is erased on .2; the copie tabulaire joins RỊ-TẠ-MẠ-NU-WI, and GORILA's note on .2 allows the divider; the tablet breaks after SU 2 ⅙ (GORILA I, 188–91).
