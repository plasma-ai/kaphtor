Context: Accounting tablet with GRA+BOSm (grain + cattle)  A list of persons with grain, opened by \*47-NU-RA-JA Šū-nūraya; HT 115b repeats the frame with TI-NU-JA. \*301-U-RA is in erasure on HT 115a.2 (GORILA I, 188–91).
