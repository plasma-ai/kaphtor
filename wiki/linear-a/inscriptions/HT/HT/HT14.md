## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> PU-VIN—contribution: grain 30, anointing oil 3, oil (DI) 3, fat(?) 9, olives 13. A-PU₂-NA-DU—grain 47, anointing oil 5, oil (DI) 4, fat(?) 6, olives 14.

### Notes

Context: Two-section commodity allocation—identical commodity sets distributed to two recipients in varying quantities.  Formulaic parallel structure—same five commodities (grain, anointing oil, oil DI, \*304, olives) allocated to two different recipients. A-PU₂-NA-DU receives 47 grain against 30, with comparable oil and olive amounts (GORILA I 29). TE between heading and commodities marks the entry as a contribution.
