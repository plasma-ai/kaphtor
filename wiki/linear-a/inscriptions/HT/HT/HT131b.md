### Notes

Verso of HT131a/b summary account tablet. Continues commodity list from recto. VIN+SA is a hapax compound logogram—VIN (wine) with SA qualifier, possibly a wine subtype or wine-remainder. Numeral damaged. PO-TO-KU-RO (bat kol 'grand total') closes the account at 452½.
