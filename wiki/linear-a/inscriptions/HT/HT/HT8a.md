## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> JE-DI: KI-oil 10. Baqarāti 1½; PA 3½; Tēnā ('Fig') 2; Qaynī ¾; SI-KI-RA ¼; Kretana ½.

### Notes

KI-oil (OLE+KI) list headed JE-DI; each of the six entries takes a quantity as a recipient, and QA-\*310-I receives again on HT8b. TE-\*301 is the name Tēnā 'Fig', also a recipient on HT98a.
