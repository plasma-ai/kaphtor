## Corpus note

GORILA reads VIR+\[?\] 68; on the photo and facsimile the patch after VIR is damage, with no legible added sign. The numerals read \*327 34 (three tens, four units), VIR 68 (six tens, eight units) and KU-RO 160 (one hundred, six tens). The nine entries sum to 159 against KU-RO 160, rounded (Younger 2023). The total includes \*327 34 and VIR 68, so every entry counts persons: KU-PA₃-NA-TU 7, Kubānatu 'devotees of Kubaba', is a personnel group and TI 7 a personnel category. \*327 33 stands in the same slot on HT97a. RI-MI-SI matches the Amorite name element Rimši (Gelb AS 21, nos. 831, 3912, 5285–5286). \*327 and \*345 are unread.
