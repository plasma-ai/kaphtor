### Notes

Allocation tablet, companion to HT11b (same scribe, same findspot). Section 1: A-RU-RA-\[?\] \]3, KA-RO-NA (Qarna) 2, \*322-RI 1, KU-RO 10. GORILA I 23 prints the first numeral broken before 3 (\]3), so the entries can reach KU-RO 10. Section 2: A-SU-JA 1, VIR+\[?\]-I 3, TA₂ 15. \*322-RI has no reading: the SPECULATIVE logogram value PIRRI 'fruit' takes its final -ri from this very word.

## Corpus note

GORILA I 23 normalizes .1-2 A-RU-RA-\[ \]3: a fourth sign lost at the end of .1 (two dotted candidates in the note, RU written over an erasure), no divider, and the numeral broken before 3 at the start of .2. A remnant of the fourth sign survives at the broken corner, too little for SU or TA. The A this string carries at the end of .2 is read by neither edition nor seen on the photograph or facsimile.
