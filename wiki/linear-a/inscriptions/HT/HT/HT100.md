## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Personnel or accounting categories: VIR+KA 58, \*304+PA \[4 inferred\], TI+A 12, KI 2, \*305 5, VIR+\*313a 16. Total: 97. For the queen/royal establishment: cyperus 5¼, figs 2⅓, wine 2½, oil (U) 2 + A706, anointing oil(?) 3, oil (NE) ¾.

### Notes

Context: personnel register followed by a separate commodity allocation. The quantity after \*304+PA is lost; including every preserved category requires 4, since 58+4+12+2+5+16=97. Montecchi's alternative restores 6 and excludes KI 2 by expanding KI to KI-RO, but RO is not written. \*305 is securely a personnel category, but its exact class is unknown. SA-RA₂ is a recipient or institutional heading; 'queen/princess' or 'royal establishment' remains CANDIDATE. OLE+MI is tentatively 'anointing oil': Hebrew šemen ham-mišḥā supplies the exact commodity expression, and Ugaritic mšḥ supports the root. CYP denotes cyperus. \*304 is not read syllabically.
