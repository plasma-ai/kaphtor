## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Kret(a)—for the queen/royal establishment: grain 10, oil 7, figs 1, wine 1, young bull 3.

### Notes

KI-RI-TA₂ is the /i/-vowel variant of KI-RE-TA₂ (HT129). SA-RA₂ is treated as a recipient or institution, provisionally the queen or royal establishment, rather than as a balance marker. The \*23M = mūru livestock reading remains CANDIDATE.
