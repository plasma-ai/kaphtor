### Notes

GORILA I 25: .4–5 MA+RU · \*118 5, the wool ligature (A559) before \*118. CYP+E is a fraction rider (CYP ¼ measure), not a syllabic qualifier. \*304 is read pidru(?) 'fat' (CANDIDATE). Unresolved: QA-TI-DA-TE, the two QA₂ compounds and the \*118 word. DA-I 50 totals the allocations.
