### Notes

Context: Administrative allocation from Casa Room 7, Haghia Triada, LM IB; HT Scribe 2. The tablet is substantially intact and no written group is cut by an edge. SigLA records an erased GRA sign, which is not part of the surviving transliteration. TE marks the transaction or category; A-DU may name Addu or an administrative work-stint. GRA is secure, whereas \*304 remains an unidentified oil/fat-register commodity. No particular measurement units can be inferred from the 680:12 ratio.
