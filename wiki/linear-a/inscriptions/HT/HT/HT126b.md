## Corpus note

GORILA I 217: reverse of the broken HT 126 tablet. Preserved entries are \]\*305 3; \]6 TWE 4 · OVISf\[; \]vest.\[; \]TWE\[; \]TU 6; the lower field is lost. GORILA I 217 and the photograph show loss along the left and lower edges. This is an administrative list, not continuous prose. \*305 is a counted personnel category whose exact class remains unknown. AB87/TWE functions as a quantified logogram here, but its referent is unresolved. The animal sign on b.2 is OVISf, 'ewe,' not syllabographic \*21F=/ha/; its quantity is lost. The second TWE likewise lacks its quantity. Final \]TU 6 occurs in the same animal-register context and is compatible with the candidate TU logogram 'bull/cattle' (&lt; Semitic \*ṯawru), but the initial break requires the reading to remain tentative.
