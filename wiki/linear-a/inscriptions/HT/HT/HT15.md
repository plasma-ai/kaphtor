### Notes

A grain account in two entries, 684 and 570, closed by \*188 · KI-RO 400. U-\*34-SI heads it as it heads both halves of HT 140 (Uchitel 2016b, 12–13).

The last line is read as terebinth owed, the sign counted as on HT 103 and owed as at Knossos. Rival: the 400 is grain owed, \*188 standing as a sub-heading before the KI-RO (Uchitel 2016b, 12–13); Gaya Nuño reckons it as 684 less half of 570, which is 399, and takes the doubled sign of the second entry for halves (1951, 94–95).
