## Translation

**CANDIDATE**, complete.

> Fine-quality offering for Šapšu: genuine oil 8, U-oil 7. Royal allocation: genuine oil 8, U-oil 2; 2. All oil \[subtotal\]. \[break\]. Anointing oil 2. Šaddû: \[allocation lost\]. Rēʿtu: genuine oil ³⁄₄, U-oil \[quantity lost\].

### Notes

Context: Oil allocation ledger: three oil grades (genuine/kīnu, U-grade, anointing/mišḥā) distributed under headings including Šapšu offering and two personal-name recipients. Joined tablet (HT42 + HT59), Villa Magazine, LM IB.  Joined tablet (HT42 + HT59), 20 signs, 11 unique words. Oil-only allocation ledger—no grain, wine, or figs—suggesting a specialized oil store or ritual oil supply. Three oil grades appear: OLE+KI (genuine/standard), OLE+U (U-grade), OLE+MI (anointing). Structural pattern: compound heading (TA · SI+SE · TE) → allocations → SA-RO royal-allocation block (coordinate with the Šapšu block: same two oil grades) → allocations → KU subtotal → break → OLE+MI section → PN recipients. The two PNs (Šaddû, Rēʿtu) receive small amounts in the tail, possibly individual rations or personal allocations from the temple store. SU-DU and RE-TU are proposed as PNs at SPECULATIVE confidence.
