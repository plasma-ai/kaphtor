### Notes

Grain list under a heading of three groups (GORILA I 157: only the divider after I-TI-TI-KU-NI is certain, and TI-TI-KU, though attested alone, is not to be isolated here), then a ruled section headed \]KU-MA-RO with TE and figs; .5 and .6 have a trace to the left. A-PA-RA-NE also heads HT96b, where QA-\*118-RA-RE stands in the slot of A-\*123-TE.
