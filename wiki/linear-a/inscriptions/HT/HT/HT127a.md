### Notes

GORILA I 218–219 and the photograph show extensive loss above and at the word edges. This is a fragmentary administrative list, not continuous prose. The source marks both quantities 1 typographically as uncertain.

## Corpus note

GORILA I 218–219 marks the tablet broken above and both lexical groups broken at the left; the second group also ends at a break. GORILA I 218–219 reads a.2 \]DU-NE-MI 1\[ and a.3 \]\*304+PA-DA-\*47-KU\[ 1, with {\*629-DA-\*47-KU\[} as the alternative sign identification. The photograph confirms extensive edge loss. There is no divider between \*304+PA and DA-\*47-KU, so the latter is not an independent word. Both groups remain untranslated; DA-\*47-KU may resemble Akkadian damqu 'good, fine' only if \*47=/mo/, but this segmentation and sign value are unproved.
