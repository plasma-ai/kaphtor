### Notes

Context: HT Zb series—vessel labels. A clay vessel from Casa VAP (La Rosa and Pugliese Carratelli 1987).

The edition reads the numeral 3, and the drawing has three unit strokes, two beside DI and one lower down. The label names what the jar held.
