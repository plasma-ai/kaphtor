# *21F

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: As a class (h ḥ ḫ), 7/11 word types, and no rival class above 8/11, so root shape does not decide. In context H gives \*21F-\*118 ḥiṭṭa 'wheat' (KH 88, ZA 5a), \*21F-TU-NE hatune 'son-in-law' (HT 7b, HT 87, HT 117b) and TU-\*21F-RI-NA ṭuhārīna. HT Scribe 11 writes \*21F-TU-NE (HT 7b, a roster of men) and QE-TU-NE (HT 12, among goods), keeping \*21F apart from Q.

### Forms cited for the value

- TU-\*21F-RI-NA = ṭuhārīna "the pure ones" (√ṭ-h-r): of the gutturals only h gives a recipient
- \*21F-TU-NE = ḫatun "bridegroom, son-in-law" (HT 7b, 87, 117b), √ḫ-t-n
- JA-\*21F = Yāh, beside PU-RA₂ on HT 28b
- \*21F-\*118 = ḥiṭṭa "wheat" (KH 88 before figs; ZA 5a with wine), if \*118 = ṭa
- A-SE-TU-\*21F = Aštuḫḫa "Female" (PH 2), Hurrian ḫ: Nuzi ᶠAš-du-uḫ-ḫa-a-a (NPN 37)

### Note

An optional H-sign covering h, ḥ and ḫ, as cuneiform's Ḫ-series writes West Semitic ḥ and ḫ alike. Its vowel follows the word: ḥi in \*21F-\*118 ḥiṭṭa, as Egyptian group writing spells that word with an a-sign (Hoch 1994, 85, no. 104), and none in NA-\*21F-NE-MI-NA naḫnemina. H is ordinarily unwritten, as in Linear B, where a₂ (ha) is optional: pa-we-a 33 times, pa-we-a₂ 5. Linear B keeps \*21 as the syllable qi and as the sheep ideograms. Salgarella suggests that the qi of Minoan-looking men's names, drawn with three strokes in a-qi-ru (KN Ce 50.1a) and qi-ta-ro (KN Dk(1) 936), and with two in a-qi-ro (KN Da 1123), the same name adapted to Greek, may be a remnant of the female sign's syllabic use in Linear A (Salgarella 2020, 214, 244, 265–66, 279). Standing alone with a numeral, \*21F is the logogram OVISf (ewe): HT 132, ZA 9, ZA 26a. No word is yet attested both with and without \*21F. Scribes appear to have begun writing it inside words in Late Minoan I. Every dated document that does so belongs to that period, at Haghia Triada, Khania, Zakros and Arkhanes. PH 2, from Phaistos, is of unknown context and carries no date. Rival: qi, the Linear B value, as Davis transcribes the sign inside words: qi-\*118 (KH 88, ZA 5, ZA 14), here \*21F-\*118, and qi-ja-du\[ (HT 84), which he sets beside the Linear B man's name qi-ja-to (KN Db 1140.B) and Βίαθθος, a man at Polyrrhenia, an example he calls rather weak (Davis 2026b, 77 n. 282, 117 and n. 445, 155, the Greek after Hitchman 2006, 83).
