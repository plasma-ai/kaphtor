# *350

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: the Linear B sign's shape, on one token. A 350 is 'similar in shape to LB wo' and 'might be an alternative good candidate to represent a possible ancestor (or source) of LB 42/wo' (Salgarella 2020, 171), and with wo A-TA-\*350 reads ʾatāwu 'what has come in' on KH 11. Against: the sign stands once, and Salgarella's reserve is 'if not actually a variant of A 306'

### Forms cited for the value

- A-TA-\*350 = A-TA-WO ʾatāwu 'what has come in' (KH 11; Ugaritic ảtwt, DULAT 119–120; Ebla a-du-wu-mu, GlOS 1, 49)

### Note

A350, KH 11 .5 only. 'Similar in shape to LB wo', and 'if not actually a variant of A 306' it 'might be an alternative good candidate to represent a possible ancestor (or source) of LB 42/wo' (Salgarella 2020, 171). One word, so nothing checks the value a second time.
