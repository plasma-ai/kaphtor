### Forms cited for the value

- JI-NU-MA-RE = yīnu-mārē 'wine-lords/wine-stewards' (√y-y-n + mārē)—3× on Zakros distribution tablets (ZA4a, ZA5b, ZA15a); CANDIDATE, and Negri (SMEA 43/1, 2001, 82) reads i-nu-ma-re as a personal name
- JI-TI = yittī 'he gave / given portion' (√y-t-n)—HT62+73; SPECULATIVE, yittī matches no G-stem form
- J-series gap-fill: JA, JE, JU exist—no JI in standard grid (weakened: the house reads JU = /lu/)
- Wine-tablet association: 4 of 5 tablets carry VIN (ZA4a via verso ZA4b, ZA15a, HT62+73, ZA6b)
- Competitors open: WE, ʾu, ay/ē, and the published /no/ and /aj/ links (see alternates)
- Co-occurrence proof: JA appears on ZA4a alongside \*28B—proves distinct signs
- √y-t-n attested independently in JE-TA-NA = Yatana (HTWa1020); JI-TI = yittī is a second form of the root

### Note

I (AB28) with an added thumb at top right; working value JI (/yi/), CANDIDATE. GORILA V (p. XXXIII) and Salgarella 2020 (160, 162) file it as a variant of I, but the scribes contrast the two in one word (ZA6b) and on one tablet (ZA5b, ZA15a), across two Zakros hands. The value rests on JI-NU-MA-RE = yīnu-mārē 'wine-stewards' (√y-y-n + mārē), which WE reads equally well; JI-TI = yittī is SPECULATIVE. WI is excluded because AB40 WI is attested; WE and NO are unclaimed in Linear A and stay open, with ʾu and ay/ē (Soldani's aj, see alternates). The ZA6b token may stand apart before GRA+PA, as Younger reads it (see I-KU-JU-TI).
