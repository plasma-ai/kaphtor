### Forms cited for the value

- Linear B \*29 = pu₂, /pʰu/ or /bu/, 'the one certain representative' of the 'voiced' labial series, inherited from Linear A (Melena 2014, 71)
- DU-PU₂-RE dubur (PK Za 15, HT Zb 160), \*307-PU₂-RE (PK Za 8), beside Linear B da-pu₂-ri-to-jo (KN Gg 702.2)

### Note

Linear B pu₂ writes /pʰu/ or /bu/, from \*/bʰu/ (Melena 2014, 71–72); Salgarella gives /pʰu/, /b(ʰ)u/ and counts AB 29 among the additional signs with a graphic antecedent in Linear A (2023, 410), and Del Freo's phonographic signary includes it (Petrakis and Salgarella 2026, 31, Fig. 0.1, after Del Freo 2023, 81). Rival: a bilabial fricative /φ/, said \[φ\] or \[β\], for the whole secondary p-series PA₃, PI₂, PU₂ (Davis 2026b, 124–30, Hypothesis 8).
