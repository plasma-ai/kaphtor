# *34

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: Linear B \*34 = ai₂ points to a vowel; ∅ 4/5, q 3/5; QI is already written by AB21. Without the H, ʿ, K and MI-NA doublets: qi reads \*34-TI-RI, U-\*34-SI and SI-DU-\*34-KU-MI; ai possible in PI-\*34-TE

### Forms cited for the value

- SI-DU-QI-KU-MI = ṣidqī-kummī "Seat of Righteousness" (√ṣ-d-q + Akkadian kummu)—HT110a
- PI-\*34-TE may be piqitti "delivery/inspection" (√p-q-d) if \*34 = QI—supporting but not independent evidence from HT116a
- QI-TI-RI = qiṭrī "my incense" (√q-ṭ-r)—KNZc6 stone libation vessel

### Note

Completes the Q-series (QA, QE, QI). 8 occurrences across 7 tablets. Co-occurrence elimination: \*34 appears with SI, KI, SE, QA, ZU on same tablets—all eliminated as candidates. No complementary distribution conflict (QI is a new phonemic value). \|\| Linear B writes the same sign as a₅ /hai/ (Melena 16, 21), so ai is another possible value: PI-\*34-TE bayti 'house' (HT116a; DULAT 243), \*34-TI-RI ʿaiṭirī 'my fragrance' (HALOT 905).
