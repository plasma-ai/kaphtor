# *306

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: ʿ leads by one word (7/7 against 6/7 for p, r, l, n, m, w); the anchor \*306-QE-DU (KH21) is read \*306 · 100\[ in GORILA. The alternation with A (\*306-TI-KA-A-RE, HT4, beside A-TI-KA-A, ZAWc2) makes \*306 a laryngeal with a, which removes p, r, l, n, m and w; it rests on one long pair

### Forms cited for the value

- \*306-TI-KA-A-RE = ʿatīq+are "the ancient/noble" (√ʿ-t-q)—unique to ʿayin
- \*306+MI = ʿAmmī "my kinsman", a personal name (√ʿ-m-m), unique to ʿayin
- \*306-TA-JE = ʿaṭāye "gift, offering" (√ʿ-ṭ-w), a votive label (ARKHZc8); \*306-TU = ʿAttu, a personal name (HT9a, HT9b, HT119, HT122a)
- Co-occurrence: \*306 and SI share 7 tablets but never same word—different phonemes
- Alternation with A: \*306-TI-KA-A-RE (HT4) and A-TI-KA-A (ZAWc2) share TI-KA-A after the first sign, and every other sign put in \*306's place gives only two-sign coincidences; \*306+MI beside A-MI (KH102, PH14b) agrees, weakly. A writes a laryngeal with a (ʾa, ha, ḥa), so \*306 is a laryngeal with a: p, r, l, n, m and w each have their own a-sign, and ʿ is the laryngeal the grid lacks

### Note

Voiced pharyngeal fricative. Fills the highest-priority gap in the phonemic inventory (ayin). 23 occurrences across Haghia Triada, Khania, Arkhanes, Fourni. 8 standalone occurrences at Khania suggest acrophonic/abbreviatory administrative use. Salgarella takes the dotted Khania form of A 306 (KH 11.3, 11.6, 19.1, 84.2) for a possible graphic source of Linear B \*42 wo, which H 124-A in the Room of the Chariot Tablets draws without its bottom line; the lateral curl aside, the shapes match, and the hapax A 350 (KH 11.5) is her alternative candidate (Salgarella 2020, 157, 171, 246, 296). The Linear A antecedents of the o-series remain controversial (Salgarella 2023, 411 n. 9). Davis lists \*306 among the apparently syllabic Linear A signs absent from Linear B, 'strong evidence that LB did not borrow all LA syllabic signs' (2026b, 96 and n. 387), and expects some of the still-untransliterated signs to 'represent additional Minoan fricatives, most likely /x/ and/or /h/' (2026b, 169, Implication 16). ʿ is a fricative Greek lacked.
