### Note

AB 74 stands alone 47 times, on the tablet HT 16 (SA-PO ZE ¹⁄₁₆), on 40 Haghia Triada nodules (HT Wa 1282–1321) and on six from Khania (KH Wa 1005–1010), and joins WI in the ligature WI+ZE, A 527, on KH 43; it occurs inside no word. GORILA classes it on the nodules as a contremarque; Salgarella reads it there as logographic, though "a phonetic value, perhaps by virtue of the acrophonic principle, cannot be excluded a priori" (2020, 170 n. 95). Del Freo's phonographic signary of Linear A leaves AB 74 out (Petrakis and Salgarella 2026, 31, Fig. 0.1, after Del Freo 2023, 81); Davis's Linear A grid after Duhoux 1989 has no ZE, and his fifty Linear A–Linear B main-series homomorphs omit it (2026b, 18, Table 1.3; 236, Table 7.1). In Linear B ZE is the acrophonic logogram for 'pair', the first syllable of unattested \*ze-u-ko, set with EQU, ROTA and BOS; Melena calls that pair-and-unit counting "a plain Greek innovation, not borrowed from the Linear A parent script" (2014, 153), as Salgarella notes (2020, 215).
