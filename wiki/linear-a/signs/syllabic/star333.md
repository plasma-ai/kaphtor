### Forms cited for the value

- \*333-DI-SU-KA (HT Wc 3009) and \*333-DI-NA-SU-KA (HT Wc 3010), two roundels: bīdi 'in the charge of' before a name, Ug. bd 'into the hands of, (intended) for' (DULAT 211)
- \*333-SA-MU (MO Zf 1, a lead weight): biśāmu 'balsam', Heb. bōśem (HALOT 163)

### Note

Speculative. Three attestations, each the first sign of its word: two roundels from Haghia Triada and a lead weight from Mokhlos. The value rests on those three words. Olivier sets the Mokhlos sign beside Linear B \*18, in its simplest form (1989, 143–145).
