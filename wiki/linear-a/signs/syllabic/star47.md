### Note

SPECULATIVE. Ten tokens; two stand alone on MA 1 (a, b) without a numeral. \*47-KU-NA, heading the ZA 15a wine issue, is sōkuna 'prefect, steward': the ā of sākinu on its way to ō, a vowel between the two, as the Canaanite gloss su₂-ki-na (EA 362), and the -kun of Phoenician Sakun (Krahmalkov 2000, 343). Linear A writes no other so; \*324 claims it only at SPECULATIVE 0.30. \*47-NU-RA-JA (HT 115a) is a person, Šū-nūraya 'He is light': so writes šū, as Ebla su-nu-ri₂ šū-nūrī (Pagan 1998, row 4282), with a vowel mismatch only. mo, from the AB15 gap, reads \*47-KU-NA as mōgun- 'regalement'; do reads it as Dagan. so also reads PA₃-SI-\*47 (MIL Zb 1) ba-si-so, basīsa 'meal worked with oil' (Lane 201). Linear B keeps \*47 apart from \*15 mo. HT 127a \*304+PA-DA-\*47-KU is unsegmented. Co-occurrence with MA, MI, ME and MU excludes those four values only.
