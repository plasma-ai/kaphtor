# *320

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: shape and distribution, on five tokens at one site and period. A 320 draws TI's protuberance as a ring, and stands where TI is missing: Phaistos MM II has no secure TI among 94 sign tokens inside words, against two to five at TI's later rates, and three A 320s. Against: no A 320 word recurs spelled with TI, and GORILA and Salgarella keep the two signs apart

### Forms cited for the value

- Shape: TI depicts 'a convex shape with a protuberance on top', and 'in many variants of the LA sign ... the protuberance is marked by a dot, or by a short line or curve' (Davis 2026b, 318 n. 802); GORILA draws A 320 as a ring on a Λ (I 307; II 155)
- Distribution: all five tokens of A 320 are at Phaistos in MM II (PH 16a, PH 16b, PH Wc 37, PH Wc 40 twice), and there TI has no secure token. The one candidate, PH 30 .1, is an arch at a break that GORILA dots as doubtful (I 317). Phaistos MM II has 94 sign tokens inside words; at TI's later rates, 2.4% in LM I and 4.9% in MM III, two to five of them would be TI, and three are A 320

### Note

CANDIDATE (0.50). The Phaistos MM II form of TI (AB37), as \*364 is the Zakros form of \*324. Salgarella classes A 320 a syllabogram and lists it only at Phaistos (2020, 324). Read as TI, PH Wc 40 gives TI-TI-MA, PH 16a \]TI-NA\[, and PH Wc 37 a TI after KA-\*314-SI. Against: GORILA and Salgarella keep A 320 apart from TI, and no A 320 word recurs spelled with TI. Transliterated \*320, GORILA's sign.
