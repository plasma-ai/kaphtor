# *362

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: the forerunner of the Linear B sign, on one token. Melena takes A 362, a hand, for the forerunner of Linear B no (2014a, 84–88, apud Salgarella 2020, 162, 337), and with no WA-\*362 reads waynu 'wine' before the must on ZA 10b. Against: the sign stands once, Linear B no has a thumb and no strokes across the stem, 'unlike its LA counterpart', and A 28b is another possible source (Salgarella 2020, 294)

### Note

A362, a hand, ZA 10b .1 only. Melena takes it for the forerunner of Linear B no (2014a, 84–88, apud Salgarella 2020, 162, 337). Against: Linear B no has a thumb on the right and no strokes across the stem, 'unlike its LA counterpart', and A 28b is another possible source (Salgarella 2020, 294). One word, so nothing checks the value a second time.
