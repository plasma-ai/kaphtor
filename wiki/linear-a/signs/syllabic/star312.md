# *312

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/syllabic/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: its own readings, with no support from another script. Alone and inside the cloth sign it is bitt 'a length of cloth' (Ug. bt, DULAT 247–248), which takes the 1¾ of HT 123+124b; \*312-TA and \*312-TA₂ read bīta 'the house', drawing grain beside the estate and the storehouse, and \*312-TE-TE bittīt 'of the cloth lengths'. Against: the rival ZO has the Linear B ligature TELA+ZO and the sign's shape (Montecchi 2010, 31 n. 38; Melena 2014, 46 n. 50); the lexicon writes bīt with PI, and \*333 is also proposed as BI

### Forms cited for the value

- TELA+\*312 (HT38) and \*312 alone (KH8, ARKH3a, HT123+124b) = bitt 'a length of cloth': Ug. bt (III) '"length, piece" (?)' of cloth (KTU 3.13:16; DULAT 247–248), Heb. bāttîm 'woven dress', a conjectured reading (2 Kgs 23:7; HALOT 166)
- HT49a \*312 1 in a list of names = bitt 'the daughter': Ug. bt, written bi-it-ta at Ras Shamra (DULAT 241–243), the word of PI-TA-JA Bittaya
- HT123+124b \*312 1¾: a length takes the fraction
- \*312-TA = bīta 'the house' (HT10b; ZA6b \*312-TA₂; Ug. bt, DULAT 243–247), drawing emmer beside DA-RE 'the estate' and U-\*325-ZA 'the storehouse' (HT10a)
- \*312-TE-TE = bittīt 'of the cloth lengths' (HT26a), over MI-KI-SE-NA 'coverings'
- PI-SA beside \*312 on HT123+124b: the sign is no variant of PI

### Note

CANDIDATE (0.40). BI, the voiced partner of PI as PA₃ is of PA. The sign alone and inside the cloth sign is bitt, the cut length of cloth, written by its first sign; a length takes the 1¾ of HT 123+124b. In words it reads \*312-TA and \*312-TA₂ as bīta 'the house', an institution drawing grain, and \*312-TE-TE as bittīt 'of the cloth lengths'; On HT 49a, in a list of names, the lone sign is bitt 'the daughter'; Ugaritic writes house, daughter and cloth length alike as bt. Weak point: the value rests on these readings alone, with no support from another script; the lexicon writes bīt with PI in PI-TA-KA-SE, and \*333 is also proposed as BI.

Rival: ZO. The cloth ligature compares with Linear B TELA+ZO, the sign is closest to Linear B zo, and at Khania it stands where ZO does, as one unit in a list with cyperus at ¼ (KH 8, KH 57). On THE Zg 5, ZO 9 is taken for an abbreviated textile name (Michailidou 1992–1993; Boulotis 1998; Burke 2010, 49, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131). GORILA keeps A 312 apart from AB 20, and the drawings differ: KH 57's ZO has two open arms meeting at an apex and no foot, \*312 a closed head and a footed stem; Melena takes KH 57's sign for a form of A 304. AB 20 stands twice in the corpus (KH 57, THE Zg 5), \*312 nine times at four sites. \]MU-\*312\[ (HT 78) is broken.
