### Forms cited for the value

- RA-\*164-TI = rāšōt 'the choicest, first-fruits' (HT17, HT19; Ug. rảšt, DULAT 713–14; Akk. rēšātu, CAD R 271–72), heading a wine contribution

### Note

SPECULATIVE (0.30). SO, the one syllable of the S-series with no sign: RA-\*164-TI heads both wine tablets of HT Scribe 18 as rāšōt 'the choicest', and the scribe writes SA and SI beside it (SA-RO, SI-DA-RE). A separate š-sign is excluded: the S-series writes š throughout, in this root too (RI-SU-MA, RI-SE). \*164-RI-DA on the loom weight THE Zg 15 is unread, its reading doubted (Del Freo, Rapport 2016–2021, 106). Weak point: one heading by one scribe; the sign, a frame with two facing curves, is like no Linear B syllable, and Linear B \*164 has no syllabic counterpart (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 126). Alone, the sign is the cloth logogram of the Khania roundels, Linear B \*164.
