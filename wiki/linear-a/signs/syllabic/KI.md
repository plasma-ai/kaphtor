| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Linear B | B 67, value ki |

| Value | Confidence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

| /qi/ | 0.20 | K-class neutralization |

### Forms cited for the value

- KI-RE-TA-NA = Kretana (6x HT)
- KI-RO = kiro 'owed/outstanding'

### Note

No CH ancestor—Linear A innovation (Ferrara et al. 2022).
