### Note

Never standalone—always appears with phonetic complement (+RU, +RO) or compound modifier (+\*304). +RU = ruqqû "perfumed/refined oil" (initial ru-); +RO = rōqēaḥ "compounded ointment" (initial rō-). Both subtypes on KH91 prove distinct products. +\*304(pidru) = fat-based aromatic. Co-occurs with CYP, OLE, \*308(šamnu), \*304(pidru) in aromatic/oil register. Fractional quantities (¹⁄₅, ¹⁄₁₆). 13/15 at Khania. Mutation testing: INERT/CONTEXTUAL_MARK = non-syllabic confirmed.
