### Note

A319 is the standard designation; \*904 is a legacy alias. Never initial or medial in a word: it closes MA-TI-TI (HT Zd 155, 157) or stands before a number (HT 132.1 A319 5; HT Zd 156 A319 2¼, A319 3⅜). A logogram with no sound value, on HT 132 'a probable transaction sign' (Steele 2024, 103). Not \*171 (curved top, stem crossbar, forked foot) and not Linear B T (no fractions). Cretan Hieroglyphic comparandum CH 065 (Meissner & Salgarella in Civitillo, Ferrara & Meissner 2024, Table 7.2, p. 152).
