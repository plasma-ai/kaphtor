## Logogram

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gloss | ṣiṣe, the salt-works, an establishment drawing grain, oil and figs; rival Šipšu, the Sun-deity |
| Commodity | salt-works (ṣṣ) |
| Semitic term | Ugaritic ṣṣ 'salt-works' (DULAT 781); WSem. ṣēṣūma 'salt marsh' at Ras Shamra, ṣi-e-ṣi-ma (CAD Ṣ 150) |
| Root | ṣ-ṣ |

### Note

Standalone on HT116a (GRA, OLE, OLIV), HT42+59 (TA · SI+SE · TE, oil) and HT99b (SI+SE FIC 6, AB30 read as the fig logogram by SigLA). Read in sign order, ṣi-ṣe, as Ras Shamra ṣi-e-ṣi-ma 'salt marsh'. Rival: Šipšu, Ebla sí-piš, with the coda p unwritten. SI+SE-RA+KA (HTWa1021) is separate morphological evidence.
