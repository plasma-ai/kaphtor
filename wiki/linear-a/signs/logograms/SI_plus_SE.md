### Note

The exact standalone ligature occurs on HT116a and HT42+59 as a quantified administrative entity or category. The expanded forms SI+SE-NI and SI+SE-RA+KA are separate morphological evidence and do not prove a standalone logographic value. Ugaritic Šapšu remains a speculative comparison, because the proposed outer-consonant abbreviation š…š omits medial /p/ and is not independently established for SI+SE. Oil allocations do not by themselves identify a solar deity or cultic context.
