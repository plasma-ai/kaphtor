## Logogram

| Commodity | PALM/TREE |
| Semitic term | Hebrew tāmār, Akkadian gišimmaru |
| Root | t-m-r |

### Note

Semantic reading: PALM/TREE, based on the pictorial form and counted administrative contexts. TAMIR is the proposed Semitic lexical name, and /mi/ its proposed syllabic use. Both are speculative: TO-\*49-RE yields t-m-r only after /mi/ is assumed, while Linear B tu-\*49-mi places \*49 beside ordinary MI. Dawmi for HT120 is a contingent name reading.
