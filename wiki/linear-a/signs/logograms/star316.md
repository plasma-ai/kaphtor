### Note

Commodity ideogram \*316 (6 att., Khania + Phaistos). KI complement on nodules KHWa1001/1002 gives initial syllable /ki/. On KH91, positioned between VIN (wine) and NI (figs) in a rations list alongside GRA and \*401+RU/RO—food commodity context. On PH1a, allocated to named individuals with CYP. Akkadian kiššanu 'bean' (CAD K 454); no Hebrew or Ugaritic reflexes confirmed.
