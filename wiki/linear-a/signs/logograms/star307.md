## Logogram

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gloss | sanctuary, inner sanctum |
| Commodity | DUBUR |
| Semitic term | \*dubur- (PS √d-b-r), Akkadian duburu, Hebrew dəbîr |
| Root | d-b-r |

### Note

Value from a spelling variant: JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-\*307-PU₂-RE (PK Za 8) and JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-DU-PU₂-RE (PK Za 15), \*307 in the place of DU. 8/0/0 inscription test (0 LESS READABLE). Forms: VIR+\*307 (sanctuary personnel; KH 9, 14, 19), OLE \*307 1½ (PE Wy 5, two signs), KU-RO \*307 156 (HT 127b), and \*307 doubled (A 638) heading the personnel lists HT 85a and HT 97a and HT 36. Montecchi 2008, 315–16 reads A 307 as a transaction sign for distributing rations or payments, A 638 its doubled variant; Uchitel 2006, 75–76 as a general qualification of personnel groups.
