# *312

[[wiki/linear-a/signs/logograms/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

**Status: proposed.** A proposed value.

Evidence: rests on \*312 = BI. Alone before 1, 2 and 1¾ (KH 8, ARKH 3a, HT 123+124b) and inside the cloth sign (HT 38); Ug. bt '"length, piece" (?)' of cloth (DULAT 247–248). On HT 49a, in a list of names, the same sign is bitt 'the daughter'

## Logogram

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Gloss | a length of cloth (bitt), written by its first sign; inside a word BI |
| Commodity | length of cloth |

### Note

Nine contexts. Alone before a numeral: HT 49a 1, HT 123+124b 1¾, KH 8 1 among CYP and NI, ARKH 3a 2. In ligature: TELA+\*312 (HT 38). In words, a syllabogram: \*312-TA 6½ (HT 10b), \*312-TA₂ GRA+PA 40 (ZA 6b), \*312-TE-TE after \*406VAS+KE (HT 26a), \]MU-\*312\[ (HT 78). One shape in every use, a pointed loop over a bar on a footed stem. As BI it abbreviates bitt 'a length of cloth' (Ug. bt, DULAT 247–248): the length alone, cloth in cut lengths inside the cloth sign, and a length takes the 1¾ of HT 123+124b. Rival: ZO, as Montecchi takes it (2010, 31 n. 38), with Linear B TELA+ZO (KN L 433; Del Freo 2020, 58; Killen 2024, 724); see the sign record.
