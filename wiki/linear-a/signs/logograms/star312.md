### Note

9 corpus contexts: 4 standalone (HT49a, HT123+124b, KH8, ARKH3a), 4 in words (\*312-TA, \*312-TE-TE, MU-\*312, \*312-TA₂), 1 compound (TELA+\*312, HT38), which ties the sign to cloth. KI-TA-NA 'linen' is spelled out on PEZb3. In \*312-TE-TE (HT26a) the logogram writes KI, the first syllable of kitnu, as \*307 writes DU in \*307-PU₂-RE: KI-TE-TE = Akk. kitītu (CAD K 466). Standard KI beside standalone \*312 on HT49a does not block this, since \*312 stands for its syllable only inside a word. ZA6b \*312-TA₂ GRA+PA 40 is an entry receiving grain, set out like I-KU-JU-TI-\*28B GRA+PA 50¼ above it, not a grain heading. MU-\*312 (HT78) stands alone on a fragment. Alternative: MANÛ 'measure/portion' (√m-n-y).
