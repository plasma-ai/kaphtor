### Note

3× standalone on Khania roundels (KHWc2026, KHWc2027, KHWc2098) = commodity logogram behavior. PS \*ṣrr 'bag, purse' (Hebrew ṣərôr, HALOT 1054); Schoep compares Linear B \*158 (Schoep 2002, 91), possibly a textile and listed among the textile logograms of Knossos (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 126, 130). The sign of HT 11a \*322-RI is a tall loop with a small hook on its right, against the roundels' flat oval with an upright box (GORILA I 23; III 124, 141): another sign.
