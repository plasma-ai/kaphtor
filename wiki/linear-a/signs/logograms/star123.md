### Note

SPECULATIVE (0.30). AB123, seven texts, never alone: A-\*123-TE (HT 96a), TA-I-\*123 (HT 9a, 9b, 39), TE-\*123 (ZA 20), A-ME-\*123 (KH 53), DU-\*123-A (HT 15). Cretan Hieroglyphic 157, 'Spice jar? For 'spices'' (Meissner and Salgarella 2024, 153), and Linear B AROM. No sound: each open consonant (b, ʿ, ḥ, ṭ, ṣ) set in its place reads at most two of the five words. The word rests on A-\*123-TE ha-quṭōrt 'the incense', in the slot of QA-\*118-RA-RE on HT 96b. On HT 9b TA-I-\*123 2 counts inside the wine total, where the sign tags the recipient. For Schoep the sign 'occurs in Linear A only as a syllabogram' (2002, 91); Steele answers that it stands 'just before numerals in four out of seven examples' (2024, 64).
