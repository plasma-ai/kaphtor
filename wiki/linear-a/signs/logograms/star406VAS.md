### Note

Pictographic vessel ideogram. Always appears with +KE phonetic complement on HT26 (both sides, as tablet header). Bare form \*406-VS on HT93a (alongside DA-RI-DA, qty 2). KE complement indicates initial syllable of vessel word: PS \*kaʾs- "vessel for beer, bowl" → NWS \*kēs (vowel fronting from medial aleph) → KE. KA-SI on HTWa1027 nodule may be the syllabic spelling of the same word (skeleton k-s, distance 0.000).
