### Note

HT 21 PI-TA-KA-SE · TE · GRA 161, then OLE+U 11, OLE+E 3¾, \*304 7¾, OLIV 1¼, the heading of a grain and oil account (GORILA I 39). bīt 'house of' + kissē 'throne': Akk. bīt kussî 'royal property' (CAD K 593), and grain booked to persons ša kussî 'belonging to the crown' (CAD K 592). PI writes bi as in DA-I-PI-TA. Its man is PI-TA-KE-SI (HT 87), bīt-kissi with the genitive. Rival: the Hurrian name Pidi-kešḫi.
