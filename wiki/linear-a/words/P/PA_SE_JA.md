## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role administrative roster label.

PA-SE + -JA (nisbe). Clean on the matched roundel pair HTWc3001–3002 (GORILA II 72), as WA-DI-NI and TA-NI-KA are on theirs. HT93a .8 \]PA-SE-JA 20, broken before and PA dotted (GORILA I 147); the photographs allow \]PI-SE-JA as well. PA-SE heads HT18 and opens the second entry of HT27b after MI-DA. Cf. LB pa-sa-ja, a spice recipient at Knossos after pa-si-te-o-i (KN Ga 953) and listed under the theonym si-ja-ma-to (KN X 451), with a different vowel. Cf. Nuzi Paššija, Pa-aš-ši-ia, the son of Pulahali who also bears Pašši-tilla, from Hurrian pašš- 'send' (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, s.v. PAŠŠIJA; 243); it predicts PA-SI-JA. No reading.
