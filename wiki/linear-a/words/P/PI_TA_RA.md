### Note

HT 96a .4 PI-TA-RA · 1, whole, between \*323-\*317 1 and \*323-\*344 1 in a grain list (GORILA I 157). bitra 'a portion', sign for sign, the vowel of TA empty: Emar bi-it-ru 'section, half', pl. bi-ta-ri (Pentiuc 2001, 39; GlOS 1, 130), Heb. beter 'piece' (HALOT 167), on b-t-r 'to cut into pieces'. An accounting term among transaction signs. Rivals: bidāla 'trade agents', Ugarit bi-da-lu-ma glossing LÚ.MEŠ DAM.GÀR and Ebla ba-da-lum 'merchant' (GlOS 1, 110), RA writing la; the names Paṭir 'released' (Gelb 1980, no. 1151; Pagan 1998, row 1090) and Batūl 'bachelor' (Gelb 1980, no. 1153); Ug. bṭr, 'tailor' in Watson 2018b, 379, 'emancipated, free' in DULAT 249. One witness.
