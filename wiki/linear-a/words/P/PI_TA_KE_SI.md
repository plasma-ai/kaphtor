### Note

Hurrian-type compound. Second element kešḫi 'throne' (ki-iš-hi = Akk. kussû; Laroche 1980, 143–44), with the cluster šḫ written by SI. The first element matches the pidi/u- forms Laroche collects without a gloss (1980, 200; Richter 2012, 319); TA writes the dental under stop neutralization. Part of a Hurrian-heavy personnel register on HT87 alongside DI-KI-SE (Dikise; cf. Hurrian tagiše/tegiše) and related KU-PA₃-NU, TU-SU-PU₂ on HT49a.
