### Note

London Medical Papyrus (BM 10059), Spell 33, between the divine names r-p-y and ʾ-m-ʾa: pꜣ wr, the Egyptian article pꜣ and wr 'great one' (AED lemmas 851446, 47280), written as Egyptian words among foreign words in group-writing. Steiner prints 'the Great One' (Steiner 1992, 198) and Haider 'the great god', the epithet of the god named before it, who is male: a goddess would take tꜣ wr.t (Haider 2001, 480). Rival: Kyriakidis takes wr for its sound, p(a,i)-'ur (Kyriakidis 2002, 213 n. 19). See RP-33-Y.
