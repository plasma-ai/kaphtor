### Note

KN Zc 6, an inked cup: PA-KU is written as one unit after DA-KI-TI, a segment of continuous writing on a dedicatory object, where an oil-vial word fits: Hebrew pak 'small jug' (HALOT 926), the flask of royal anointing (1 Sam 10:1; 2 Kgs 9:1–3). Ugaritic bk 'beaker, large cup' (DULAT 216) stands in the same passage as krpn, the counterpart of HT 31's KA-RO-PA₃. Rival: bk is commonly compared with Greek βῖκος, and Smith and Pitard (2009, 108) call it 'apparently an Indo-European Kulturwort'. One attestation. See PU-KO.
