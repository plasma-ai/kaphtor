### Note

HT115a: PA-RA-NE receives quantity 1 in GRA+BOSm list alongside \*301-U-RA (naura 'light'), SE-KU-TU, KA-PO-RU, RI-SU-MA. HT115b: receives ½. Both by HT Scribe 8. Short form of A-PA-RA-NE (HT96a/b, same scribe): the A- prefix = initial ʿayin (√ʿ-b-r) or prosthetic aleph (√b-r-ʾ). Same dual reading as A-PA-RA-NE but without the guttural initial. \|\| Amorite Ḥa-pí-ra-an (Huffmon APNM p. 36; element ḤPR ḥapir-, \*ʿapir- = the ʿApiru/Ḥabiru word, p. 202) and ʾAPR-AN AP-RA-AN (Gelb AS 21 no. 863) match the short form with -ān; cuneiform ḫ writes ʿ. Root disputed: Borger (via Huffmon p. 202) derives ʿapīru from ʿ-p-r 'dust', not ʿ-b-r 'cross'; PA writes p and b alike.
