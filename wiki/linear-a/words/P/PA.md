### Note

Single-sign commodity logogram, possibly denoting flour or ground grain. PS \*parūs- 'crushed, broken' (Heb. pāras 'to break, divide'). Dual role: standalone commodity (16×, 5 sites) and compound modifier (GRA+PA 19× = ground grain/flour, \*304+PA 3× = rendered fat). Clusters with TI (figs) in commodity community. ZA12a/b quantities (64, 40) indicate bulk agricultural product. No phonetic complement confirmation—single-consonant skeleton makes reading tentative. On HT8a PA takes KI-oil 3½ in a list of recipients, an abbreviation there and not the commodity.
