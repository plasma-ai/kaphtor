### Note

HT 104 .3–4 PA-DA-SU-TI 29, the third share under TA-PA · TE+RO, after DA-KU-SE-NE-TI 45½ and I-DU-TI 20½; KU-RO 95 is their sum. All four signs are dotted, PA-DA at the end of .3 and SU-TI at the head of .4, and the editors do not exclude PA-RO for PA-DA (GORILA I 171); Schoep reads PA-RO-SU-TI (2002, 47).

With PA-RO the word is PA-RO-SU, Parʿōš, the name that heads HT 20, and TI. SU is the š of the name, as at Taanach Pu-ra-gu-uš. TI stands after each of the three partners, and each base stands without it elsewhere: DA-KU-SE-NE twice on HT 103, PA-RO-SU on HT 20, i-du as a name at Ebla. The editors' note: TI on .1–2, .2–3 and .3–4 'pourrait être idéogramme' (GORILA I 171). As a sign apart it is the good the partners put in, counted by the half: the lexicon's TI 'figs'. Weak point: Haghia Triada writes figs with NI.

Gordon took -TI for a feminine ending, with a masculine PA-DA-SU on HT 20 (1966, §§135, 153); that word is PA-RO-SU. See PA-RO-SU, DA-KU-SE-NE-TI, I-DU-TI, TA-PA and TI.
