TY 2 .2–3: PA ends line 2 and DA opens line 3, then \*309 with RU written above it, JU, KI and 100; the tabular copy sets the six signs as one entry with no divider (GORILA I 325). Every other syllabic entry on the tablet opens with \*309, so PA-DA stands before a \*309 entry and is not set off as a word. Linear B pa-da\[ (DMic II) is broken after. Not a word.
