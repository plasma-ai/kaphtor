## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

On the Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5, PU 5 is one of the single signs before quantities taken for abbreviations of Minoan textile names (Michailidou 1992–1993; Boulotis 1998; Burke 2010, 49, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131). Linear B writes PU on its cloth sign, 'quite likely the abbreviation of pu-ka-ta-ri-ja, puktaliā', a cloth of double thickness, and the word 'may be linked with πτυκτός' (Killen 2024, 724, 729). If that word is Greek, the ostracon's PU abbreviates another. HT 34 has PU ⅛ after E+KA ¹⁄₁₆, and the graffito KN Zd 46 carries the sign alone.
