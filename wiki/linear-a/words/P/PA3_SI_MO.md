### Note

Anchor reading for \*47=MO. Hebrew bōśem (בֹּשֶׂם) 'spice, balsam, perfume'. Single-word vessel inscription from Miletos—a Minoan trade colony on the Anatolian coast. Balsam was among the highest-value aromatics in Bronze Age Mediterranean trade. PA₃=/ba/, SI=/si/, \*47=/mo/.

### Cognates

Hebrew bōśem (בֹּשֶׂם) 'spice/perfume'; beśāmīm (בְּשָׂמִים) 'spices' (pl.); Ugaritic bšm 'spice' (offering lists); Akkadian basmu 'fragrant plant'; Aramaic busmā (בּוּסְמָא) 'aromatic'; Arabic balsam (بلسم) with l-infix → Greek βάλσαμον → English 'balsam'; Ethiopic baśam 'to be fragrant'. Pan-Semitic root. Minoan form ba-si-mo preserves the archaic triradical without Arabic l-infix.
