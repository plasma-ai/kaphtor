### Note

KN Zf 13, signs 7–8. PI writes b, as A-PA-KI ʾabkī 'I weep' (KN Zb 40) writes the root with PA. With PA-JA-TA: bikē bayta 'weeping in the house'; at Ugarit the women mourners come into the dead man's palace (KTU 1.19 IV 9–10). Rivals: pīka 'your mouth'; pak 'flask'. One attestation.
