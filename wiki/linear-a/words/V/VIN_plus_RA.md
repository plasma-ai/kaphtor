### Note

VIN ideogram + RA adjunct in four current corpus records: KE Zb 5, ZA 15b, ZA 6b, and the doubtful seal CR Zg 3. Its occurrence beside plain VIN on ZA 15b establishes a distinct wine category; ZA 6b likewise contrasts it with VIN+KA. The exact variety is unknown. Of four ra- candidates, raḥṣu 'washed/filtered' (√r-ḥ-ṣ; Ug rḥṣ, Heb rāḥaṣ) supplies a possible acrophonic expansion, but the single adjunct cannot select that word over its rivals. The uncertain etymology caps the entry at CANDIDATE.
