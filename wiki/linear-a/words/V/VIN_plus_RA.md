### Note

VIN ideogram + RA adjunct in four current corpus records: KE Zb 5, ZA 15b, ZA 6b, and the doubtful seal CR Zg 3. Its occurrence beside plain VIN on ZA 15b establishes a distinct wine category; ZA 6b likewise contrasts it with VIN+KA. RA writes the ra- of raqḥ 'spiced', Hebrew yayin hāreqaḥ 'spiced wine' (Song 8:2; HALOT 409): the wine of the r-q-ḥ series with OLE+RI riqqu 'perfumed oil', \*401 ruqqû and RO-QA rōqaḥ. Rival: raḥṣu 'washed, filtered' (√r-ḥ-ṣ; Ug. rḥṣ, Heb. rāḥaṣ). One adjunct sign caps the entry at CANDIDATE.
