### Note

HT 89, HT 94a, HT 100, HT 127b and KH 7a: VIR marks a human referent, and the counts make VIR+\*313 a class of men: on HT 89 VIR+\*313a 13 stands in the total 23+22+24+13+5, KU-RO 87, and on HT 100 VIR+\*313a 16 is counted in KU-RO 97. Three palaeographic variants stand in comparable lists: VIR+\*313a (A570) on HT 89 and HT 100, VIR+\*313b (A571) on HT 94a and KH 7a, VIR+\*313c (A572) on HT 127b. No phonetic complement fixes \*313. Rival: \*rāqiḥū 'perfumers', √r-q-ḥ: Ug. rqḥ 'perfumer' (DULAT 734), Akk. raqqu 'oil presser, perfume maker' (CAD R 173), Heb. rōqēaḥ 'ointment-mixer' (HALOT 1290), which rests on co-occurrence with aromatics. CYP and the other aromatics are not confined to these tablets, and on HT 89 CYP follows the personnel total, in a separate section.
