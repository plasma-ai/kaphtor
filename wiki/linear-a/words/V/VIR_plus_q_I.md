## Unread sign

A compound. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads VIR, [[wiki/linear-a/words/I/I|I]].

HT 11a .4–5, counted 3, and HT 93a .5–6, counted 6: the man sign and I side by side in the word column, with no divider, GORILA's 100/102-28 (V 272). On HT 93a the two entries above it end on a divider and I before GRA+PA, A-SE · I GRA+PA 26 ¾ and QA-QA-RU · I GRA+PA 5 (I 147), so the man sign stands where the recipient stands and I is the lexicon's I 'ration'. On HT 11a two short strokes project from the man sign's right side (I 23).
