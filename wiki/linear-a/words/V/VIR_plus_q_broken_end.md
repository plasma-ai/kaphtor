### Note

The person sign with a joined element the transcriptions leave unnamed: men, counted. 29 times at five sites (Haghia Triada, Khania, Petras, Phaistos, Tylissos), after a name or heading and before a numeral: HT 94a KA-PA VIR+\[?\] 62; PE 1 KU-PA-RI VIR+\[?\] 50 and E-KA VIR+\[?\] 72; HT 105 234 and 235; KH 25 140. On HT 25b VIR+\[?\] 28 and VIR+\[?\] 24 make KU-RO 52. Alone on the roundel KH Wc 2004. The joined element is not one sign throughout. See VIR+KA, VIR+\*313, VIR+\*307.
