### Note

ZA 6b: VIN+RA 40, VIN+KA 10, two kinds of wine in one entry after grain 50¼ (GORILA III 159). KA names the kind by its first sign, as RA does beside it: ḫamr 'fermenting wine', ḫ written with KA. Ugarit has ḫmr beside yn as a kind of wine: ảlp kd yqḥ b ḫmr 'a thousand jars he took of ḫmr-wine' (KTU 1.3 I 16), šty b ḫmr yn 'drink of the ḫmr-wine' (KTU 1.23:6; DULAT 390); ḫamr is 'wine' at Ebla and Emar (GlOS 1, 273), and Hebrew ḥemer is the wine still fermenting (HALOT 330). The same KA rides the dye MI+JA and E on HT 33 and HT 34: one word on three liquids. VIR+KA, on men, is another KA. Weak point: one tablet, and no text spells the word. Rival: KA as the measure kapp 'bowl', wine by the bowl. See VIN+RA, MI+JA+KA, KA.
