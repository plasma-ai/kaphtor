### Note

IOZa2 line 2, in the frame TA-NA-\[X\]-U-TI-NU shared with IO Za 6 (X = I-\*301) and IO Za 11 (\]U-TI-NU). TA- = 2fs prefix; -NA- = N-stem n-, unassimilated before /r/ (later Hebrew תֵּרָצֶה tērāṣeh; Lipiński §41.16); RA-TE = √r-ṣ-y 'be acceptable' (Hebrew Niphal, Lev 1:4; TE = ṣ unproved); U = indicative -u of the yaqtulu conjugation, preceding the subject pronoun (Ugaritic ʾargmk /ʾargumu-kī/, KTU 1.3 III 21; modal 'may' sense, cf. Huehnergard 2012, 55–56; -u "remains attached directly to the verbal base, and pronominal object suffixes follow it," Bjøru and Pat-El 2021, 56), the least secure element; TI = archaic 2fs nominative subject clitic (subject clitics from the nominative pronouns, usable on other bases: Pat-El 2025, 330, 332), extended analogically to a finite host; NU = 1cp object 'us' (PS \*-na, Ugaritic -n/-nā), energic n-extension the alternative. Controls: Enūma eliš I 119–20 ul taremmināsi 'you (f.) do not love us'; Josh 2:18 hôradtēnû; Ugaritic tšqy-n (KTU 1.19 IV 53). Principal objection: ordinary 2fs \*taqtulīna, with -na after gender-number suffixes (Bjøru and Pat-El 2021, 48), a Central Semitic innovation beside shared -u (Bjøru and Pat-El 2021, 66).
