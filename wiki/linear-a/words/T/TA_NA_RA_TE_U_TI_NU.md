### Note

The petition that closes the libation formula, whole on IO Za 2 .2 between SI-RU-TE and I-DA-\[ (Karetsou, Godart and Olivier 1985, 129, fig. 12); IO Za 11 keeps \]U-TI-NU (1985, 142–143). The editors open the 'formule secondaire' here (1985, 134, 137), and Monti a new clause (2022, 112). One attestation.

TA- is the 2fs prefix, -NA- the N-stem n-, unassimilated before r (Huehnergard 2019, 52), RA-TE √r-ṣ́-y 'be acceptable', U the indicative -u, which stands before the object suffix (Bjøru and Pat-El 2021, 56), TI the 2fs subject clitic -tī (Pat-El 2025, 330, 332), and NU the 1cp object 'us', Amarna yâtinu (EA 74:26). TE writes \*ṣ́ (Huehnergard 2019, 50), as in A-RA-TU ʾarṣ́-. Byblos marks person twice the same way: ta-šap-pár-ta 'why were you sent?' (EA 102:10; Baranowski 2014, 120).

The root is the verb of acceptance at the altar: Hebrew wə-nirṣāh lô 'and it shall be accepted for him' (Lev 1:4) and û-pōʿal yādāyw tirṣeh 'and accept the work of his hands' (Deut 33:11), Samʾalian wʾl yrqy bh 'and may he not look favourably upon it' (KAI 214:22; DNWSI 1083), Hadramitic rḍwt ʿlh-n-s¹ 'She was pleased by him' (Raybūn-Kafas/Naʿmān 148), Amorite Ra-ṣa-Dagan 'Dagan is pleased' (Huffmon 1965, 265). Rivals: Davis's 'gives' (2013, 46), Thomas's -TI as third-person plural agreement (2020, 13), Finkelberg's Luwian -wa-ti-(a)n (1990, 51).
