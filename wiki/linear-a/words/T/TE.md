### Note

TE occurs in 56 documents at six identified sites plus AN Zb 1, whose site is unrecorded in the corpus. Commodity-number entries and 25 one-sign HT nodules securely establish an administrative category or transaction function. Hebrew terûmā 'contribution' and Ugaritic trmt 'offering' support the proposed sense, but Linear A TE does not spell the full word and no TE-RU-MA form is attested. Terûmā therefore remains a proposed acrophonic or logographic value, not a lexical identification. VIN+TE may be a compound commodity marker but does not independently prove 'wine-offering.'
