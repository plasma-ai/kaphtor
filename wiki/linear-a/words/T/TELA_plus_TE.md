### Note

TEL Zb 1, between the ox and the figs: the cloth sign with TE written inside it, on a pithos sherd left as an ex-voto in the cult building of area K at Tel Haror (Pierini 2022, 44). TE marks a contribution on the Haghia Triada tablets and rides the wine sign on two pithoi as VIN+TE; Ugarit offers cloth by the same word, trmt lbš w ktn (KTU 1.43:3; DULAT 866). At Pylos TELA+TE itself is 'a contribution to or from female religious personnel, the priestess(es) and the key-bearer(s)' (PY Un 6; Nosch 2012, 326). Weak point: the sign has no fringes, which for Petrakis 'make it non-canonical' as an ancestor of Linear B \*159 (in Nosch 2012, 306 n. 11). Rivals: te-pa, the heavy woollen cloth of Linear B TELA+TE, seven units of wool to the piece (Luján 2010, 380), TE 'the abbreviation of a Pre-Hellenic word' for Duhoux (in Killen 2024, 724), though Crete has Linear B TELA+TE in its latest archive alone (Nosch 2024, 341); VIN+TE, wine, by the sign's likeness to A 588 on the pithos KN Zb 34 (Pierini 2022, 46–47). One sherd. See TE, WA.
