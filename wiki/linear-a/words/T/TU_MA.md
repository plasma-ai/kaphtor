### Note

Pan-Semitic √t-ʾ-m 'twin': Akkadian tuʾāmu, Hebrew tōʾam (תֹּאֲם, Gen 25:24, 38:27), Aramaic tōmā (→ Greek Θωμᾶς 'Thomas'), Arabic tawʾam. CV mapping: tuʾma → TU-MA (medial ʾ drops by guttural transparency). On HT94b (LMIB, 1500–1450 BCE), first entry in a 5-person personnel list (qty 1 each, KU-RO 5). Birth-circumstance name 'Twin' is one of the most widespread NWS naming patterns. Cf. TU-ME = tamm- (√t-m-m)—different root despite surface similarity.
