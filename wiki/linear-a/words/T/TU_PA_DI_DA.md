### Note

HT 123+124b .3–4 TU-PA-DI-DA J+L2, whole and undotted, on a face written over an erasure, after \*188-DU 10 and before KA-NẠ\[ (GORILA I 213). ṭūb 'the best of' in construct, PA for b, before dida, the beer extract of KAŠ.Ú.SA: fine billatu, KAŠ.Ú.SA SIG₅, as in Akkadian (CAD B 226, 228). A product among the \*188 and KA-NA entries, measured in fractions. One attestation. Cf. KA-NA.
