## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone twice on HT 126b. Linear B \*87 twe: Davis takes it for a sign Linear B borrowed from Linear A with the same or a very similar value, and leaves /tʷ/ out of his inventory for want of a syllabic use (2026b, 95–96 n. 385, 154 n. 579). Linear B writes twe only at Knossos, in o-da-twe-ta, and for Salgarella 'it cannot be excluded' that Linear A had an ancestor of it in the sign of HT 126b .2–3, which GORILA V therefore sets among the AB signs, p. xlii (2020, 39 and n. 105); her later chapter counts twe among the complex signs Linear B introduced (2023, 411). Del Freo's current phonographic signary of Linear A leaves AB 87 out (Petrakis and Salgarella 2026, 31, Fig. 0.1, after Del Freo 2023, 81). No reading.
