### Note

Credible Semitic name candidate within the bronze-bowl scriptio continua A-RA-KO-KU-QA-WA-SA-TO-MA-RO-AU-TA-DE-PO-NI-ZA. The onomastic comparison is Bronze Age: Gelb's Amorite index gives the one-element name TAMAR-U /ta-ma-ru/ (no. 6027) and TAMAR in two compound names (nos. 5715–16). Compare also Hebrew Tāmār as a woman's name (Gen 38; 2 Sam 13), Hebrew tōmer 'palm' (Judg 4:5), and proposed syllabic Ugaritic /tamaru/ 'date palm' (Huehnergard 2008, nos. 175, TMR). TO-MA-RO can therefore represent a local Tōmar/Tamar-type name if final O is a support vowel writing coda /r/; initial /tō-/ could reflect local vocalism or \*ā &gt; ō. The limitation is epigraphic: GORILA prints no divider before or after TO-MA-RO, so its word boundary and exact vocalization remain inferred. The personal-name reading is retained as CANDIDATE; no grammatical case can be assigned from the context.
