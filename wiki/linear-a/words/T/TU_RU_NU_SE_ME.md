### Note

HT 128a .1–2 TU-RU-NU-SE-ME GRA 10, TU dotted, a grain recipient before WA-TU-MA-RE and MI-TA (GORILA I 221). Two nouns in construct, Ṯ̣ūr nušme 'Rock of the people': TU-RU ṯ̣ūr 'rock', Hebrew ṣûr (HALOT 1016–1017), the emphatic interdental written as a dental; NU-SE-ME the genitive of Ugaritic nšm 'people, men' (DULAT 640), Hebrew nᵉšāmâ 'breath, living being' (HALOT 730). 'Rock' builds names with a kin or people word: Amorite Ṣūra-ʿammu 'The paternal ancestor is a rock' at Tuttul (Buck 2018, 350), Amarna ṣú-ra-šar (EA 319:4; Hess 1993, 138), Hebrew Ṣûrîšadday (Num 1:6). One attestation.
