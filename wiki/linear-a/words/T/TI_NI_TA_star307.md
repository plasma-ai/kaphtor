### Note

HT 27a opens TI-NI-TA · \*307, each set off by a divider, over a roster of men from VIR 90 to VIR 42, KU-RO 335. The goddess and her sanctuary head the men who serve it, as VIR+\*307 'sanctuary personnel' heads entries at Khania. \*307 is drawn like a pole tent: two sides narrow to a peak over an upright, a cross-stroke tops it with a short upright above, and a bar crosses the body (HT 27a, 89; HT 127b squarer, under a roof line; doubled as A 638 on HT 85a and 97a).

Tinit Dubur 'Tinit of the Sanctuary'. TI-NI-TA is Punic Tinnit, Θινιθ on the El-Hofra stele (Krahmalkov 2000, 126). \*307 reads dubur from a spelling variant: JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-\*307-PU₂-RE (PK Za 8) writes \*307 where JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-DU-PU₂-RE (PK Za 15) writes DU, Hebrew dəbîr 'inner sanctuary' (1 Kgs 6:5). Its picture answers to the Hebrew miškān 'tabernacle' (Exod 25:9) and ʾōhel môʿēd 'Tent of Meeting' (Exod 33:7), and at Ugarit "the gods went to their tents", ảhl // mšknt (KTU 1.15 iii 18; DULAT 31). A goddess takes her house as a title in Ugaritic bʿlt bhtm 'Lady of the Palace' (KTU 1.105:16) and Akkadian Bēlet-ekallim at Mari, Qatna, Alalakh and Emar (DULAT 208). Linear B Potnia da-pu₂-ri-to-jo (KN Gg 702.2), our reconstruction \*Baʿalat ha-Dəbîr, carries the same title.

Gordon read ti-ni-ta as Tinit, "the Phoenician-Punic goddess called 'our great Lady'" (Gordon 1966b, 36), and counted her among the Northwest Semitic names that show a cult "predominantly Canaanite/Phoenician" (EML §129). The Hagia Triada tablets were to Gordon lists of offerings "usually opening with the name of the god to whom the offerings are made" (Gordon 1965, 302), and HT 27a opens on hers. Montecchi finds A 307 set off at the head of the list on HT 27a.1, 89.1 and 127b.4, records of personnel and of personnel with foodstuffs, and takes it for a transaction sign of rations or payments (Montecchi 2008, 315–316). The two agree on the place: goddess and sanctuary stand as the heading, and the men and rations beneath belong to her house. One attestation. See TI-NI-TA, VIR+\*307.
