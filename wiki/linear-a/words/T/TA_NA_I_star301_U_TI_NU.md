### Note

Paradigm entry for the √ʿ-n-y verbal family (\*301=NA writes the proposed N-stem marker + root); conjugations are recorded here, not as separate entries. Members: TA-NA-I-\*301-(TI/E) (PSZa2, CANDIDATE; the last sign is an uncertain TI in GORILA, E in Ferrara, Montecchi, and Valério 2022, 87) = if TI, 'may you (f.) answer'; if E, 'may she/you answer'; A-TA-I-\*301-DE-KA (ZAZb3, PROBABLE) = 'who-answered-for-you'. Template parallel: TA-NA-RA-TE-U-TI-NU (IOZa2, √r-ṣ-y)—same TA+NA+ROOT+U-TI-NU frame with the root slot swapped. Ugaritic tʿnyn /taʿniyūna/ (KTU 1.23:12) supports the root and a t-…-n profile, not the segmentation. On IOZa6 the proposed 2fs verb addresses and puns on the following theonym I-NA-TA-I (ʿInatī, 'my Anat'). Position 1 of the IOZa6 libation formula.
