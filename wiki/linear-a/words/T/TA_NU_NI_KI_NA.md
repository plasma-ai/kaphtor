### Note

PL Zf 1, a silver pin from beside Platanos Tholos A: … JA-SA-SA-RA-ME · TA-NU-NI-KI-NA · NI-NU-NI …. tanūḥūn qīna 'they wail a dirge': TA-NU-NI is the prefix form of √n-w-ḥ 'to moan, lament' with the -n ending, Ugaritic tnḥn in k tnḥn ủdmm, of Udum moaning for her (KTU 1.15 I 7; DULAT 618). NU writes the root vowel and the ḥ is unwritten, as the ʿ of TA-NU-MU-TI ta-nʿum-u-tī before JA-SA-SA-RA-MA-NA (KN Za 10). KI-NA is the object, qīna 'funeral song, dirge' (Heb. qînâ, HALOT 1097), as Hebrew nāhâ nəhî 'wail a wailing' (Mic 2:4; HALOT 675). The subject follows, NI-NU-NI 'the offspring', as ủdmm follows tnḥn. The votive texts join words without a divider, as U-NA-KA-NA-SI wannā kanāsī. Weak point: NI for the -n ending, its vowel unexplained; rival -nī 'for me', the dead speaking. Rival: tannî qînâ 'recount a dirge!', the Piʿel of Hebrew tnh (Judg 11:40; HALOT 1759), with TA for ṯ and NU unexplained. One attestation.
