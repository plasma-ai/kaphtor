### Note

HT 94a counts four groups, KA-PA VIR+\[?\] 62, \*86 20, TI+A 7 and VIR+\*313b 18, then TA 4, one to a group, before KU-RO 110, where the entries give 111. HT 109 has RE 4, TA 4, KU-RO 129, broken on the left. TA-RA 5 holds the same place on HT 89, after four groups and inside the total of 87: TA is its first sign. ZA 10b closes a list of rations to named persons with TA 2 and DU 1. Linear B ends its personnel tablets alike: DA 1 TA 1 closes the Pylos Aa records and DA TA the Ab ration records, the Knossos Ak tablets carry both, and Killen takes the two signs as 'probably denoting workgroup supervisors' (2024, 428, 437, 440). Ug. ṯġr 'gatekeeper' and 'guardian', a class to which labourers are assigned, ṯlṯ b ṯġrm (KTU 4.141 III 2; DULAT 888–89).

Weak points: HT 89 counts five for four groups, and one tablet alone spells the word. Rival: Akkadian tārû 'male nursemaid' (CAD T 250), which predicts TA-RU. TA has no reading among commodities: TA 6 after KU 6 on HT 154a, TA 2 on KH 8, TA 4 on the stone THE Zg 5, and TA alone on 12 Haghia Triada nodules (HT Wa 1267–1278), KH 16, KH 64 and ZA 24b. See TA-RA.
