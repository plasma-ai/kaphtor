## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/T/TE|TE]], [[wiki/linear-a/words/R/RO|RO]].

GORILA A 505, 04+02: HT 104 .1 TA-PA · TE+RO ·, between two dividers in the heading. The editors' note: TE and RO 'with the two horizontal strokes running together' (GORILA I 171); the sign index lists it under 02 and under 04 (V 145, 151).

The contribution of capital, TE 'contribution' with RO 'principal', under TA-PA 'partners', heading three shares that sum to 95. Akk. kasap tappūti is 'partnership capital' (CAD T 192), and qaqqadu 'head' is the 'original amount, principal' of a loan (CAD Q 109), as RO is rōš 'head'.
