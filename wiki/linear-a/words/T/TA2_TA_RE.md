### Note

PK 1 .2 TA₂-TA-RE 1, a single-unit entry in a column of names, TA₂ distinct from TA (GORILA I 281). Linear B ta-ta-ro is a man on the Knossos roster KN As(1) 607.2 (VIR 1), also KN Pp 498, Xd 8605 and PY Eb 874, Ep 301.6, Eo 224.7. Final -RE answers Linear B -ro as in A-RA-NA-RE / a-ra-na-ro (KN As 1516), PA-JA-RE / pa-ja-ro (KN As 1519) and NA-DA-RE / no-da-ro (KN As 609), all on the same roster series. The Greek gloss Tantalos is the Mycenologists'; no Semitic etymology; possibly a name from another language of Crete. One attestation.
