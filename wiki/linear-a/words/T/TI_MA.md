## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

HT Zf (HM 767), a gold disc 3.4 mm across on the forehead of a bronze bull from the Piazzale dei Sacelli. Perna reads TI and MA, calls the readings uncertain, considers the vertical stroke between them accidental rather than a divider, reports possible traces of one or two more signs beside an abrasion over the right half, and leaves the script open between Linear A and Linear B (Kanta and Perna 2011, 291–293). RILA-S1 lists the object among its doubtful inscriptions (D 21). LB ti-ma is a man's name at Knossos (KN 317, per Perna). Not readable.
