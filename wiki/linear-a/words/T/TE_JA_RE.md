### Note

HT117a (Haghia Triada, Casa Room 9, LMIB): list of 10 items at count 1 each, KU-RO 10 total. Alongside KU-RA-MU (√k-r-m "vineyard"), KU-PA₃-NU (Kubānu, PROBABLE), NA-DA-RE (nadare "vow," CANDIDATE), under the MA-KA-RI-TE heading ("merchants," CANDIDATE). The NWS w→y shift turns \*ṯawr- into tayr-: JA renders the /y/. The initial /ṯ/ is written with TE because no /ṯe/ or /ṯa/ sign exists—only ZU=/ṯu/. This ṯ→T neutralization before non-/u/ vowels independently confirms ZU's vowel-specific function. Bull-horn dyad with KA-RO-NA (qarna "horn") on HT11a—same archive, same period. Ugaritic El is ṯr ʾil "Bull El"—the bull as divine epithet. PY Ta 711 (Linear B) qo-u-ka-ra = gʷou-kara ("bull-headed") ritual vessels belonging to the wanassa.
