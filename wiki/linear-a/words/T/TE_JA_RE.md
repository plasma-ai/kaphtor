### Note

HT117a (Haghia Triada, Casa Room 9, LMIB): one of ten entries counted 1 each, KU-RO 10, under the MA-KA-RI-TE heading ("merchants," CANDIDATE); a name on the noun 'bull', as Tēnā 'Fig' (TE-\*301). Alongside KU-RA-MU (Kurmu 'Vineyard'), KU-PA₃-NU (Kubānu, PROBABLE), NA-DA-RE (Nadr 'Vow', PROBABLE). The NWS w→y shift turns \*ṯawr- into tayr-: JA renders the /y/. The initial /ṯ/ is written with TE because no /ṯe/ or /ṯa/ sign exists—only ZU=/ṯu/. This ṯ→T neutralization before non-/u/ vowels independently confirms ZU's vowel-specific function. Bull-horn dyad with KA-RO-NA (qarna "horn") on HT11a—same archive, same period. Ugaritic El is ṯr ʾil "Bull El"—the bull as divine epithet. PY Ta 711 (Linear B) qo-u-ka-ra = gʷou-kara ("bull-headed") ritual vessels belonging to the wanassa.
