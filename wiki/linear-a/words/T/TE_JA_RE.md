### Note

HT117a (Haghia Triada, Casa Room 9, LMIB): one of ten entries counted 1 each, KU-RO 10, under the MA-KA-RI-TE heading ("merchants," CANDIDATE), alongside KU-RA-MU (Kurmu 'Vineyard'), KU-PA₃-NU (Kubānu, PROBABLE), NA-DA-RE (Nadr 'Vow', PROBABLE). Ugarit has the man's name Tiyaru, ti-ya-ru₃ (RS 14.0016 r 3), the same consonants t-y-r; Linear B has the man's name te-ja-ro (KN V 479). Rival: tayyār 'trader', √t-w-r 'travel about' (Heb. hattārîm 'traders', 1 Kgs 10:15; Ug. t-r 'scour, travel through'), on the qattāl pattern of Heb. ṣayyād 'hunter' (√ṣ-w-d); tayyār itself is unattested. Not 'bull': PS \*ṯawr- keeps its w in every reflex (Ug. ṯr, Heb. šôr, Akk. šūru, Ebla šūra), and no \*tayr- is attested.
