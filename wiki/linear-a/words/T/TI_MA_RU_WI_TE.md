### Note

PY R 1 .1–2 TI-MA-RU-WI-TE, a ligature and VIN 90, the only entry (GORILA I 322). TI-MA = Tīmar 'palm', final r unwritten before the l of lēwīt, both in the R-series; the i as Heb. timmōrāh. RU-WI-TE = lēwīt, fem. of lēwī as in DU-RU-WI and U-RE-WI (HT 25a). Name + title, as Minaean lwʾtn S¹lmy 'the priestess Salmay', a priestess's daughter (M 333). Rival: TI-MA-RU Tamar, Amorite TA-MA-RU (Gelb no. 6027), + WI-TE, perhaps √w-d-d as in WI-TE-JA-MU.
