### Note

HT115b TI-NU-JA · NU-WI · GRA ½. TI-NU + hypocoristic -ya (cf. \*306-TU-JA, same tablet). Ranked: ṯ-n-y 'second, to double'—Sabaic PN tny, Safaitic tnyt (Harding 1971: 148–49), TI for ṯ unestablished; Dīnuya √d-y-n 'judge'—the judge words here are written DI (DI-NA, DI-NA-U); Amorite template ti + nu-ya 0.35.
