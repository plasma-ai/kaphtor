### Note

HT 115b .2 TI-NU-JA · NU-WI · GRA ½, the frame of side a's \*47-NU-RA-JA · I · GRA+BOSm / NU-WI, so a person (GORILA I 191). ṯan-iya, ṯ-n-y 'second' + hypocoristic -ya as in \*306-TU-JA on the same tablet. Weak point: TI for ṯ is unestablished, and no Semitic root reads the word on TI's primary value. Rival: Dīnuya, √d-y-n 'judge', which needs TI = d, while the judge words here are written DI (DI-NA). One witness.
