### Note

The Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5 counts five single signs: MA 40, PU 5, TE 7, ZO 9, TA 4. MA is wool, and PU, TE and ZO are three of the adjuncts Linear B writes on its cloth sign, CLOTH+PA, +TE, +ZO, +PU and +KU (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 51, fig. 10); Linear A has TELA+TE on TEL Zb 1 and TELA+KU on HT 38. The single signs of the ostracon are taken for abbreviations of textile names (Michailidou 1992–1993; Boulotis 1998; Burke 2010, 49, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131). TE is then the cloth Linear B spells te-pa, made of seven units of wool, a word Killen takes for a loan 'since Linear A has an ideogram corresponding to TELA+TE', with the link to τάπης 'tempting' (2024, 729).

Cf. Heb. miṭpaḥat 'shawl', from ṭpḥ 'to spread out'; the comparison is of the root alone.

Rival: TE 'contribution', the transaction sign of the tablets, which takes no numeral of its own. Weak point: the ostracon has no cloth sign, 'the interpretation remains uncertain yet plausible' (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131), and no Linear A word begins TE-PA. PU, ZO and TA of the ostracon are unread. See MA and TE.
