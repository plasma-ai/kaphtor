### Note

Compare Ugaritic tdt /ṯādīt-/ 'sixth' (Tropper, §§63.113, 63.12). The consonantal sequence is close if Linear A T writes the interdental, but the written vowels do not reproduce Tropper's vocalization. On HT123+124b TI-DA-TA is followed by a divider, not an explicit 1/6; the tablet therefore supplies no mathematical confirmation. Single attestation. The relation to TI-TI-MA remains a project hypothesis rather than an independent control.
