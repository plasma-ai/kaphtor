## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

PYR Zb 5 \]TẸ-JA\[, a sherd broken on all sides, TE uncertain. Younger allows the inscription to be complete and JA whole if the sign is Linear A (Younger, Linear A Texts, other texts). Ferrara, Weingarten and Cadogan read the two signs as Cretan Hieroglyphic 025-056 with the top of the second sign broken off (Ferrara, Weingarten and Cadogan, SMEA NS 2, 2016, 87–88, 95). Sole attestation. Not readable.
