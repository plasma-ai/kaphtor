### Note

HT Zb 162, a clay vessel from Casa VAP (La Rosa and Pugliese Carratelli 1987): DI 3 TI-DU, the two signs complete at the right. Ebla's lexical list glosses the malt sign ŠE.ŠILIG.TITAB with ti-du, /ṭiṭṭ/ 'a sort of malt' (GlOS 2, 313), sign for sign the Linear A word; the sign is the Sumerian for beer mash, Akk. titāpū (CAD T 434). With DI dibšu 'honey' the label names two things a brewer puts in one jar. TI writes ṭ as TU does in ṭuhārīna and TA in ṭābu.

Rival: ṭīdu 'mud, clay' (CAD Ṭ 106), the same word's first sense, naming the vessel's material. Weak point: one attestation, and Ebla's form is a lexical-list gloss.
