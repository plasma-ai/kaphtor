## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

PH 13c \] E E ZU TE\[ as printed left to right, two fraction signs, ZU, then TE at the right break, without hyphens (GORILA I 303). Younger reads the face retrograde and sets TE and ZU apart in the logogram column (Younger, Linear A Texts, other texts). GORILA V indexes the signs one by one and leaves grouping and direction to the edition (GORILA V 138). No edition groups TE-ZU. Not a word.
