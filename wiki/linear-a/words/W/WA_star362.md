### Note

ZA 10b .1 WA-\*362 2 opens face b, before DU-RE-ZA-SE \*131B 5, 'for the must' ahead of its own logogram (GORILA III 171): wine by name, then must by name and sign. \*362, a hand, stands only here; Melena takes it for the forerunner of Linear B no (2014a, 84–88, apud Salgarella 2020, 162, 337), which gives WA-NO. WA alone writes the word by its first sign: VIN+WA on HT 27b, WA 60 on the Zakros stone vessel ZA Zg 34.

waynu 'wine', a culture word that keeps its w in Arabic, Old South Arabian and Ethiopic, beside Hittite wiyan= and Mycenaean wo-no; Ebla's wa-na is entered under wayn 'wine' with a query (GlOS 2, 366). The y goes unwritten, as the i of Linear B wo-no does, and the o carries the diphthong's colour, as in Greek οἶνος. \*28B-NU-MA-RE 'wine-stewards', on three other Zakros tablets, reads wēnu-mārē as well. Weak point: the value no rests on one occurrence, and Linear B no has a thumb and no strokes across the stem, 'unlike its LA counterpart' (Salgarella 2020, 294). Rival: a personal name at the head of the roster, Ebla wa-na or the Hurrian Wanni of Šemšara. One attestation. See WA, DU-RE-ZA-SE, JI-NU-MA-RE.
