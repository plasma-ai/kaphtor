### Note

Egyptian group-writing, rubric element 4 (Akulov 2023, 3): wbqy with the disease determinative. Kyriakidis reads the final sign as l, w-b-ql (Kyriakidis 2002, 213), and finds that 'the combination wbq may suggest a Semitic language' (214); Steiner transcribes w-b-ḳi and leaves it unread (Steiner 1992, 198). wa- 'and' + b(u)haq-ī, SED #135 \*bahaḳ- '(kind of) skin disease': Heb. bōhaq 'tetter' (Lev 13:39), Arab. bahaq, and Mandaic baqiata 'decay, gangrene' without the h. A skin disease named beside samāwnā (Akk. sāmānu 'the red one') in a skin-disease papyrus. Rivals: Arabic √w-b-q 'perish' (Q 18:52; 42:34); Akulov's Eg. wbnw 'wound', with q for n unexplained. Weak points: the unwritten medial h; the disputed final sign. See U.
