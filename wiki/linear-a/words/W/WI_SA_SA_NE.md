### Note

KH 5 .2 WI-SA-SA-NE CYP+E 2, intact, a recipient of cyperus in the section headed A-DA-KI-SI-KA · A-RA-U-DA · (GORILA III 25). wiššān, a qittāl of √w-š-n 'to sleep', as SI-DA-RE Siddāri; SA-SA writes šš as in JA-SA-SA-RA-ME (di Mino, Ya Diktu). Initial w- as in Arabic and Safaitic, where Ugaritic and Hebrew have y-; cf. WI-DI-NA. The Ugarit and Hebrew names take the pattern qatil. Rival: √y-š-š 'aged', the name Yəšîšay (HALOT 447), with no w- in any cognate. One attestation.
