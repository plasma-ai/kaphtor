### Note

AB 54 alone before a fraction: HT 16 · WA ⅓, HT 20 WA ¼, the roundel HT Wc 3019 WA ¼. Schoep has AB 54 'tentatively identified as a logogram for textile', with fractions on HT 16.2, HT 20.4 'and perhaps HT Wc 3019' (2002, 35); Nosch and Weilhartner take it as the cloth logogram on the same three (2026). The sign continues as Linear B TELA; in ligature it is TELA+KU and TELA+\*312 on HT 38, TELA+SE on THE 8 and TELA+TE on the Tel Haror sherd (Pierini 2022, 48 n. 23). Pierini draws the logogram 'pictographic and round', the syllable ''linear' and square' (2022, 43, fig. 3.2). Inside a word the sign is WA, as in WA-DI-NI on the roundels HT Wc 3007 and 3008, where it is drawn square. Rival: wayn 'wine' written by its first sign, as in VIN+WA: ARG Zg 1 sets WA beside VIN-MI and the stone vessel ZA Zg 34 has WA 60; the roundel KH Wc 2124 bears WA alone; ZA 10b spells the word out, WA-\*362 waynu, before the must. Weak point: every quantity is a fraction, as with the he-goat on HT 20; Linear B counts cloth in whole pieces and sets its fractions on the weighed wool (Luján 2010, 385–387; Duhoux in Killen 2024, 722). No witness spells a cloth word. See WA-\*362.
