### Note

HT 113 .2 WI-SA ·, whole, then a gap and O 2½, a recipient entry (GORILA I 184–185). Linear B writes the same spelling in wi-sa\[, a man at Pylos, broken after SA (PY Cn 328.12), and wi-so, a woman at Knossos (KN Ap 639.4; DMic II 436). Cf. Safaitic WS 'to reward' (Harding 1971, 641). One attestation.
