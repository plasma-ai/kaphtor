### Note

Heads both wine tablets of HT Scribe 18, whole on each: HT 17 RA-\*164-TI · TE · VIN 38, then SA-RO 10 and SI-DA-RE 5 (GORILA I 35); HT 19 RA-\*164-TI · TE · VIN 30, then SA-RO 5½ and DU-ME-DI 43½ (GORILA I 37). With \*164 = SO, rāšōt, the plural of \*raʾš- 'head' with ā &gt; ō: 'the choicest, first-fruits', as Ug. rảšt and Akk. rēšātu, and the heading reads 'the choicest · contribution · wine'. SO is the one syllable of the S-series with no sign, and the scribe writes SA in SA-RO and SI in SI-DA-RE beside it. The root is spelled with the S-series elsewhere: RI-SU-MA rīšum, RI-SE rēšī. Weak point: one heading by one scribe carries the value, and the sign is like no Linear B syllable. The Wor(l)ds of Linear A sets out HT 19 as a transfer of wine between the administrative centre and three named entities, RA-\*164-TI, SA-RO and DU-ME-DI (2026, 82, Table 4.5).

Rival: Rāmōt 'the Heights', a place (Heb. Rāʾmôt, HALOT 1164), with \*164 as mo. See TE, SA-RO.
