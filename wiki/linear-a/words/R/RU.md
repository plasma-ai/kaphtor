### Note

ZA 12, both faces, RU whole each time: a \]PA 64, TI 13, RU 28; b PA 40\[, TI 13, RU 26 (GORILA III 177). PA is wheat and TI figs, each by its first sign, and RU is the third good of the list: pomegranates, with wheat and figs among the seven species of Deut 8:8. RU writes the first syllable of the word, u in every vocalised form but the Hebrew: Akkadian lurmû, counted beside dates and figs (CAD L 255–256), Syriac rummānā, Arabic rummān (HALOT 1241–1242), Ugaritic lrmn (DULAT 499). Rival: three parties, as TA 2 and DU 1 close the names of ZA 10b, though single signs among names at Zakros take 1 to 5 and ZA 12 counts 13 to 64. Weak point: two tokens on one tablet, its heading lost. See PA, TI, RI-RU-MA.
