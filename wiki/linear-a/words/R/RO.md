### Note

ZA 1b \]RO GRA 47 and KH 100 RO VIN\[, before the commodity: 'principal grain', 'principal wine'; HT 110b RO 20 under GRA; PH Wc 39 JA-DI RO, on a roundel. RO stands alone on 90 HT Wa nodules, HT Wb 2001 and 2002, and ZA Wa 36, marking the sealed goods as of the first grade. rōʾš 'head; first, best': Heb. rōʾš, bəśāmîm rōʾš 'balsam of top quality' (Exod 30:23; HALOT 1166), Ug. riš 'head; top; first-fruits' (DULAT 713), Akk. rēšu 'head; top; beginning' (CAD R 277), Arb. raʾs, Aram. rēš. See RI-SU-MA.
