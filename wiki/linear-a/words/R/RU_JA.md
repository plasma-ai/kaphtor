### Note

KN Wc 26, a roundel: RU-JA on face a and NI-PI nīb 'fruit' on face b, one disc impression (GORILA II 85; CMS X 120); the Knossos findspot is queried. Ruya, the Ugaritic PN rwy (DULAT 738), \|r-w-y\| 'to be well watered', with Amorite ia-ar-wi-um and ru₂-ya \|rôya / rūya\| in an Egyptian place name (GlOS 2, 178). The name opens RU-JA-TA-DI Ruya-dādī (HT Wc 3008). See RU-JA-TA-DI.
