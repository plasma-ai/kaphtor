### Note

PK 1 .6 RA-NA-TU-SU 1, one word, written with its 1 and the next NI over an erasure, in a roster of names each counted 1 (GORILA I 281). rannat-u-šu: the verbal noun rannat- of √r-n-n 'cry out' (semitic_roots K0351), nominative -u, and -šu 'his', as in JA-DA-SU yadaʿšu; the geminate is unwritten. Arabic rannah keeps the a of RA; Hebrew rinnāh takes the same suffixes (rinnātî 'my cry', Ps 17:1). Akkadian DINGIR-tušu and Bānītušu join a -t- noun to -šu the same way (ORACC). Weak point: no West Semitic name ends in -šu. Rivals: two names, RA-NA and TU-SU (TU-SU stands alone on .6–7); Egyptian rnn 'jubeln' (DULAT 731), which leaves -TU-SU unread. One witness.
