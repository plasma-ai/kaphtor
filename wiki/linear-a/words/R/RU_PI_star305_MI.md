### Note

PE 2: RU-PI-\*305-MI 1⅛, after a broken line, followed by A-\*325-ZA, A-RI-PA, QA-QA-DA, TO-ME, TO-\*49-RE. RU-PI is the Amorite RIPʾ- 'heal' name element (Gelb 1980, nos. 5272, 5287–5293). \*305-MI read ḏa-mi(r), √ḏ-m-r 'protect', final r unwritten. Amorite has DIMR- only as a first element. Weak point: \*305 = ḏa is speculative; with da, \*305-MI is DA-MI. One attestation.
