## Unread

Status **unread** (nothing known).

LACH Za 1, a doubtful right-to-left inscription on a limestone vessel fragment from Tel Lachish, is broken at both ends. RI-DA-U is internally complete between dividers, but the missing context prevents a secure function or meaning. Finkelberg, Uchitel and Ussishkin (1996, 202–203) compare the related ri-da-(wa)-\*65 groups around vessel ideogram \*309 on TY 2 but leave the term unidentified. No reading.
