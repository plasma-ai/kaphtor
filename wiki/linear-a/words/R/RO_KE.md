### Note

HT 98a .4 RO-KE ½, whole and undotted, between TE-\*301 ¾ and KA-RI-\*310-I ¼, in a list of fractional issues with TA-NA-TI; wine on face b (GORILA I 161). The qōṭēl participle with Canaanite ō, as RO-E rōʿē; KE at its primary value k. The final l goes unwritten, as Linear B drops a word-final liquid: ma-te mātēr, pa-te patēr (PY An 607), and Linear A KA-PA kapa(r). Rival: rōqēaḥ 'perfumer', which needs KE for q.
