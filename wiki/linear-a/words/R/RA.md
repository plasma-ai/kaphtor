### Note

KH 47 .3 RA · \*306\[, whole at the preserved left edge (GORILA III 72–73): the Lady, then the servants, the two words of the Punic name ʿbdrbt 'Servant of (our) Lady' (Krahmalkov 441). Carthage invokes rbt ḥwt ʾlt, 'Lady Ḥawwot, goddess' (KAI 89.1), and Ugarit books gt rbt, 'Farmstead of the Lady' (KTU 4.125:16; DULAT 720). KH 19 gives \*306 to the sanctuary, \*306-VIR+\*307. KH 14 .1 has \]RA · A-MA-JA · CYP 6, broken before RA (III 45).

Weak points: one whole token, its number lost; no witness spells the word. Rival: rabbu 'chief' of a class, Ug. rb khnm, rb ḥršm, with rb qrt on the line after the gatekeepers (KTU 4.141 III 2–3; DULAT 717–18); one sign writes both. Rival: a name by its first sign. RA has no reading on HT 147, HT 53a, ZA 11b or the ring KN Zg 57b; on KH 29 \]-RA ends a lost word (III 61). See \*306, TA.
