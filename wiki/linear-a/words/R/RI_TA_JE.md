### Note

KH 6 .4 \]CYP+\[ \] J · RI-TA-JE CYP+D\[, intact and undotted, a recipient in a list of CYP+D J allotments (GORILA III 26–27). RI li, the ʾ of liʾt- unwritten, TA t; JE the hypocoristic -āy, as in \*306-TA-JE ʿaṭāye and the Ugarit names la-i-ia-ya (GlOS 2, 6). lītu 'might' names the god's victory in ᵈŠamaš-lītum and ᵈŠulgi-līti (CAD L 223). Rival: Amorite Rašiyya, RA-ŠI-A (Gelb 1980, no. 5264), √r-ṯ-y 'acquire' with TA for ṯ, against the vowels of RI-TA. RI the i of Amorite liʔy 'power', li-i-um, li-i-ᵈda-gan (GlOS 2, 6), beside the verbal laʾijum (Gelb 1980, 23). One attestation.
