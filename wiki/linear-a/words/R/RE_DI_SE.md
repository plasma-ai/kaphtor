## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role administrative roster label.

HT85b .4 RE-DI-SE 1, whole and undotted (GORILA I 133). Eighth of eleven single-unit entries under KI-KI-RA-JA ·, between ME-ZA 1 and WA-DU-NI-MI 1. GORILA's note on .3 reads 1 KA · DI · over an erased 1 RẸ-DI-SE ·, so the scribe wrote the name once, erased it and wrote it again on .4. WA-DU-NI-MI occurs only here and on HT6b, where RA-TI-SE stands next to it. Probably the same name as RA-TI-SE. No reading.
