### Note

HT 6b .2 RA-TI-SE 1, after WA-DU-NI-MI 3 and a fraction, on a face written over an erasure (GORILA I 13). The name RE-DI-SE (HT 85b), which also stands beside WA-DU-NI-MI, with TI for Egyptian d: Semitic scripts write Egyptian d with ṭ (Hoch 1994, 427), and Linear A has no ṭ series. Rival: Safaitic RDS, Ar. radasa 'to stone, break' (Harding 1971, 275).
