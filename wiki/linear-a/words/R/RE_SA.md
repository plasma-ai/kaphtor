THE Zb 14 (formerly THE Zb 15), a graffito on a lamp base from Akrotiri, published by Tzachili and Karnava (Minos 39, 2016). The report gives the reading AB 09-A 332 2, that is SE-\*332 2, and states that doubts still weigh on it (Del Freo, Rapport 2016–2021, 105–106). RILA-S1 lists it among its doubtful inscriptions (D 15). Not readable.
