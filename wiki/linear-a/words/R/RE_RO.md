### Note

HT Wc 3012, a roundel: ZU-DI-RA on face a, RE-RO on face b, both whole and undotted (GORILA II 75). The thing received, one suckling, against the receiver ṯudira. lalʾu 'suckling': Ug. llu 'suckling (lamb or kid)' (DULAT 494), Akk. lalû 'kid' (CAD L 51). RE writes la as in RE-ZA ʾaraz, RO the -lu as in KU-RO kull, the ʾ unwritten. One attestation. Cf. ZU-DI-RA.
