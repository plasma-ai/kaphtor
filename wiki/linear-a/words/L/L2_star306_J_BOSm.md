## Unread sign

A compound. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/star/star306|\*306]], BOSm.

Four signs of two lines and no sign of its own. The record KH97 is GORILA's KH 87 under another number, the same down to the dimensions: a fraction closes a lost entry, a damaged \*306 ends .2, and the fraction J opens .3 before the ox, counted 1 at the break (GORILA III 100–101). The published KH 97 is another tablet (Andreadaki-Vlasaki and Hallager 2007, 8–9).
