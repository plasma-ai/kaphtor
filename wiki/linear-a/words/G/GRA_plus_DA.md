## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/G/GRA|GRA]].

HT 133: A-DU · TE GRA+DA 55, the grain sign carrying DA after TE. Under the same heading A-DU, HT 86a and HT 95b spell DA-ME before grain (DA-ME GRA+B 20; DA-ME 10), and HT 120 has DA-ME GRA+K+L 74: DA is the first sign of DA-ME, as Linear B TELA+PA carries the first sign of pa-wo (Melena 2014, 143). One occurrence; HT 133 is the work of another scribe than HT 86a and HT 120. See DA-ME.
