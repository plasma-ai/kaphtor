### Note

A pointed form over a hanging loop, alone on the Khania roundel KH Wc 2028, a sealed receipt (Salgarella 2020, 316). Among the logograms for military gear it is the helmet, AB 191, the one 'which belongs to this category for sure, attested only once in the whole Linear A corpus, on KH Wc 2028' (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 118, fig. 6.3). Linear B GAL; the Greek word there is ko-ru-to 'of the helmet' (Salgarella 2020, 215 n. 108). Cf. Heb. kôbaʿ, qôbaʿ 'helmet', a 'foreign word' that wavers between k and q, '? introduced by Philistines' from Hittite kupaḫ(ḫ)i 'headgear' (HALOT 463); Ethiopic qobeʿ and Syriac qubbʿā 'cap, hood' have the same word. Weak point: one witness, with no numeral and no word beside it, and no Linear A text spells the word; KU-PA and QA-PA₃ are read otherwise.
