### Note

GORILA's A 551, the MI+JA core of MI+JA+RU with I, three upright prongs, in place of RU (Schoep 2002, 130). Whole numbers only, and large: HT 34 MI+JA+I 245, before raisins 100; KH 81 MI+JA+I 1000\[. On HT 37 it heads a list of persons, with KA-KI 11, A 15 and \]-QE 17 under KI-RO: amounts owed. It shares no tablet with MI+JA+RU, which the oil recipes take in small parts (Schoep 2002, 130–131). Liquids are booked whole at this scale, E 240 at Knossos and VIN 104 at Zakros, and the dye was made by the vat (Stieglitz 1994, 46–47).

I names the kind by its first syllable: ʾiqnaʾu 'violet purple', Ug. ỉqnủ, the word for the purple itself in the letters and lists of Ugarit, 'from it the purple is still lacking' (KTU 2.87), with šmt for the reddish purple beside it (KTU 4.168:1; DULAT 90–91). The dye was made in many shades, red, blue and dark purple (Stieglitz 1994, 48), and the blue one took the name of lapis lazuli: the Akkadian of Ugarit writes it SÍK.ZA.GÌN, takiltu (DULAT 91), and Hebrew tĕkēlet is the bluish purple beside the reddish ʾargāmān, both from the snail (HALOT 1733).

Ugarit counts it as MI+JA+I is counted, by the hundred and the thousand with no unit written: mỉt ỉqnỉ, mỉtm ỉqnủ, ảlp ỉqnỉ (KTU 3.28:14, 2.99:12; DULAT 91), and a palace account books 4,500 of it under the same sum of pḥm, the ruby purple (KTU 4.203:3–6; DULAT 91, 657). It is owed there as on HT 37: ten of silver and a hundred of purple ʿl stry bn ktš, 'against Stry son of Ktš', in a list of debts of silver and oil (KTU 3.28:13–15).

Linear B's cloth sign \*161 is built of mi and i, and Salgarella offers A 551 as its "possible comparandum" (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 124). Linear B names a cloth's kind the same way, by one sign set at the logogram: mi for mi-ja-ro, pe for pe-ko-to, PU for pu-ka-ta-ri-ja (Killen 2024, s.vv. MI, PE, PU).

Weak point: one sign, and no tablet spells the word. Rival: I as ḥipru 'ration', its sense alone between a recipient and a commodity, the dye as an issue. See MI+JA+RU.
