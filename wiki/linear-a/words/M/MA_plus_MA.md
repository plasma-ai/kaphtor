## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/M/MA|MA]].

GORILA A 563, \]80+80, the 80 dotted (GORILA V xxv): PH 3a .2, after \]MA+A 1 and before a fraction; the tabular copy sets \] before it and the line transcription none (GORILA I 291), and on the drawing the surface between the numeral and the sign is whole (I 290). The tablet lists four ligatures of MA in a row: \]MA+\[·\] 1 (A 556), \]MA+A 1 (A 557), MA+MA and \]MA+RU (A 558), the last the lexicon's wool.
