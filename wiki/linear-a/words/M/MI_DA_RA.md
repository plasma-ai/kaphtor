Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal or place name.

PK Zb 25 MI-DA-RA, the only word on a coarse red handle, probably of an amphora (l. 8.5 cm), a chance find of 2000 at the gate to the Roussolakkos excavations: the first Linear A inscription on a vessel handle. MI and DA are clear; the third sign is tentatively RA, lacking its lower bar and curved right side. The editors leave open whether it names a person or a place (Schoep and Driessen 2003, 77–80). Sole attestation. Not readable.
