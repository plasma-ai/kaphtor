### Note

Hapax on PK1 personnel list (13 names, each allocated 1). Compound PN: MA-TI- as the first element of Nuzi Matip-tešup and Mat-teja, unglossed (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, 234) + Semitic \*zayt "olive" + -e ending. Hybrid Hurrian-Semitic compound parallels Palaikastro contact-layer evidence. Alternative: Semitic matāʿ-zayte "olive goods/property" (PS \*matāʕ-) less likely given personnel-list context.
