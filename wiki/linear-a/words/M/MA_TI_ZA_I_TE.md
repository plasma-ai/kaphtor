### Note

PK 1 .7 MA-TI-ZA-I-TE 1, whole and undotted, in a roster of names each counted 1 (GORILA I 281). Amorite MATI+ (Gelb nos. 4568, 4570, 4571, 4573) with zayt "olive", which no held onomasticon uses in a name; or Hurrian mat- (NPN 233–234: Matip-tešup, Mat-teja) with -te, which leaves ZA-I unread. No reading.
