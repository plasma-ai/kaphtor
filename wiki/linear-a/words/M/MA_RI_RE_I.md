### Note

HT 6b: MA-RI-RE-I 3, after RA-TI-SE 1 and before DU-DA-MA 66, on an aromatic allocation tablet whose face also counts SA-MA 'herb' 35. marīrēy 'the bitter stuff', myrrh: a qaṭīl adjective of √m-r-r with the nisbe -ēy, as Heb. mōr 'myrrh' and mar 'bitter', with Mandaic and Ethiopic marīr (HALOT 630). Song 4:14 lists mōr beside qāneh and karkōm 'saffron'. One attestation. See MI+JA+RU.
