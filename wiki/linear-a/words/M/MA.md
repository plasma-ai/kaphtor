### Note

MA alone before a quantity among commodities: HT 110b MA 5½ and MA 2, between NI and \*301; KH 61 MA with ⅓, before OLE+TA, CYP and GRA; ZA 29 MA 100; the stone THE Zg 5 MA 40, with PU 5, TE 7, ZO 9 and TA 4. The wool word is written MA-RU (HT 117a), MA+RU (HT 12) and MA+RU-ME (HT 24a), and MA is its first sign, as KI is of KI-RO. Rival: any commodity in MA-; Linear B ma. is fennel, ma-ra-tu-wo, in the Mycenae spice tablets (Bartoněk 2003, 123). Weak point: the first sign alone ties MA to wool. MA has no reading on HT 146, MA 30 among names counted 30 each, at the broken head of HT 3, or on KH 62. See MA+RU, MA-RU-ME.
