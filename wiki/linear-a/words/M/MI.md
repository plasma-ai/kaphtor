## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone on the nodule KN Wa 51. The tablets hold no whole token: KH 9 .2 MI\[ is broken after MI (GORILA III 35), ZA 20 .2 \]MI\[ has traces of three signs after it (GORILA III 193), and HT 113ter sets MI over 4 on a fragment whose surface is lost beside the sign.
