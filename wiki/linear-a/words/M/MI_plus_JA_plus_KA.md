### Note

GORILA's A 552, on HT 33 (⅕) and HT 34 (quantity lost), both headed SA-RA₂ and, on GORILA's reading, both without MI+JA+RU. HT 32, a third SA-RA₂ tablet by the hand of HT 33, has MI+JA+RU 1 (Montecchi 2010, 22). KA rides two more liquids in the same way, a small sign over a larger (Salgarella 2022): E+KA on these two tablets and VIN+KA 10 beside VIN+RA 40 on ZA 6b, one kind of wine against another, as Linear B sets mi against pe on one cloth (Killen 2024, s.vv. MI, PE). ḫamr- 'fermenting, foaming', ḫ written with KA: Ug. ḫmr, ḫamr 'wine' at Ebla and Emar (GlOS 1, 273), Heb. II ḥmr 'to ferment, foam' (HALOT 330). The dye mass stood in vats and simmered for days (Stieglitz 1994, 46–47). On wine the word is at home: Ugarit drinks ḫmr beside yn (KTU 1.3 I 16, 1.23:6; DULAT 390), so the three riders are one KA, and VIR+KA, on men, is another. Weak point: no tablet spells the word. Rival: ḫašmānu, a purple of wool in the Akkadian of Ugarit (DULAT 657–658), "a blue-green color" in CAD Ḫ 142. KA is put forward as a purple word on cloth as well: TELA+KA on the Knossos ivory as κάλχη 'murex, purple' (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 129). See MI+JA+RU, E, VIN.
