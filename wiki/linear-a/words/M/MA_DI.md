## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

A personal name: recipient with a numeral on six tablets at two sites (HT 118 pigs, PH(?) 31a with \*21M, HT 3 and HT 97a beside other names, HT 85b in a one-unit roster after WA-DU-NI-MI). Linear B ma-di is a man on the Knossos roster KN As(1) 603.2 (VIR 1) and on the sheep tablet KN Db 1168. Cf. Hurrian mad=i 'wisdom' (Wilhelm, "Hurrian," 102), at Nuzi chiefly a final name element, Enna-mati, Tešup-mati (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, 234, 265), beside Matija and a broken Ma-ti-… (ibid., s.vv. MATIJA, MATI . . .). No reading.
