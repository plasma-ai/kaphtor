### Note

Bare form of the mimation doublet MA-RU/MA+RU-ME (HT24a 'the wool'). On HT117a one of ten entries counted 1 each under MA-KA-RI-TE 'merchants' (KU-RO 10), between KU-RA-MU (Kurmu) and KU-PA₃-NU (Kubānu): a name on the noun, as Tēnā 'Fig' (TE-\*301). Linear B ma-ro-ne, a man's name in the dative, receives oil at KN Fh 347 (DMic s.v.). The wool word is anchored by logogram A559 = MA+RU composite (Salgarella 2022; Nosch & Weilhartner 2026), source of Greek μαλλός.
