### Note

Bare form of the mimation doublet MA-RU/MA-RU-ME (HT24a 'the wool'). On HT117a a roster item assigned '1', between KU-RA-MU 'vineyard' and KU-PA₃-NU (Kubānu)—a list mixing persons with property items (bull, votive, earthenware), where 'one (unit of) wool' fills an otherwise unglossed slot. The word's reality is anchored by logogram A559 = MA+RU composite (Salgarella 2022; Nosch & Weilhartner 2026), source of Greek μαλλός.
