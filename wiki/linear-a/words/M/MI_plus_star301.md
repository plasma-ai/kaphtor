### Note

KH Wc 2064 and KH Wc 2099, two Khania roundels, each with the ligature MI+\*301 alone. A roundel's sign names the thing received and its seal impressions count the units, so each impression is one portion handed over.

minā 'portion, ration': Ug. mnt 'portion, ration', of the one 'who consumes his portion in the sanctuary' of the god (KTU 1.17 I 32; DULAT 558); Heb. mānâ 'share, portion (of meat or food offered as a sacrifice)' (Exod 29:26, Lev 7:33, 1 Sam 1:4; HALOT 599); Akk. minītu 'amount, number' (CAD M/2, apud DULAT 558), which gives the i vowel. With \*301 as nawā, the shrine, the ligature reads two ways that agree: spelled out, mi-na 'portion', or \*301 qualified by MI, 'the shrine's portion'. See MI-NU-TE.

Rival: the weight, Heb. māneh 'mina' (HALOT 599), Akk. manû 'mina' (CAD M/1 219), from manû 'to count' (CAD M/1 221), Ug. mn 'mina' (GlOS 2, 45); a unit names no thing for the impressions to count.
