### Note

HT 104 .2–3 I-DU-TI 20 ½, I at the end of .2, DU-TI at the head of .3, all three signs whole and undotted, the ½ dotted (GORILA I 171). Eblaite names built on yidu 'strength', as yidu-na 'our strength' (Pagan 1998, Ebla personal names, row 2466). Ebla also has i-du as a name by itself (ARET 15 8, 31 and 34; EbDA), and Ugarit Yadu-Baʿal and Yaduwa (Kinlaw 1967, 128). TI stands after each of the three partners under TA-PA and is a sign apart, which the editors allow as an ideogram (GORILA I 171): the name is I-DU. One document. See PA-RO-SU-TI, DA-KU-SE-NE-TI, I-DU-WI, I-DU-NE-SI and JA-DI.
