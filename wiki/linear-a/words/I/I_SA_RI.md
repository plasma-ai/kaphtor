### Note

PH 6: within I-DA-PA₃-I-SA-RI, after the b= proclitic. The medial I writes the ʾ of ʾišar-, and the final -ī is the genitive a preposition governs (cf. la-piṭrī, PK Za 11). Ebla writes i-sa-rí 'just, favourable', sign for sign (GlOS 2, 377). PH 54 .1 I-SA-RI-KE\[ opens a line at the left margin and breaks after KE (Militello 2014, 159): the sequence begins a word there, in the same palace a period later. √y-š-r, the root of the formula's yaššaram (JA-SA-SA-RA-ME); the nominal shape is Akk. išaru 'straight, just, fair' (CAD I/J 224). Rival: Yišar 'the Upright', a divine name, as Ebla i-sar (Pagan 1998, row 2604) and Amorite Yaśar (Gelb 1980, Glossary). See I-DA-PA₃-I-SA-RI.
