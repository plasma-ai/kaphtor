### Note

PH 6 (MM IIB) .4, the last line, after I-NA-WA · A-RI / I-ZU-RI-NI-TA / A-RI: harrī ʾIdā 'mountain of Ida', the mountain over Phaistos, then bi-ʾišarī 'in righteousness'. PA₃, a proposed ba, writes the proclitic b-, with the a of the Megiddo gloss ba-di-ú 'in his hand' (EA 245) and of Geʿez ba (Tropper 2000, §82.11); medial I carries the ʾ of ʾišar-, and -ī is the genitive after the preposition, as la-piṭrī (PK Za 11). I-SA-RI is Ebla i-sa-rí 'just, favourable', sign for sign (GlOS 2, 377), and PH 54, of the same palace, opens a line with I-SA-RI-KE\[ (Militello 2014, 159). The formula sets the same two roots together, the verb of √n-w-y with yaššaram (JA-SA-SA-RA-ME): PH 6 writes b- and the genitive where the LM I vessels have the accusative with mimation.

Rival: ʾi-Ṭāba-Yišari, a divine name in the slot of I-ZU-RI-NI-TA, with its prosthetic I-: DA-PA₃ ṭāba 'is good', as Alalakh ì-lí-da-ba ʾIli-ṭāba (Buck 2018, 337), and Yišar 'the Upright', Ebla i-sar, a name and a divine element (Pagan 1998, row 2604); Hebrew sets the two words together, ṭôb wəyāšār YHWH (Ps 25:8). Against it: a sentence name after 'mountain of'; ṭāba in -a beside Yišari in -i; and ṭāb- first, the Akkadian order (Ṭāb-ṣilli-Aššur; Huffmon 1965, 303). Weak points: the phrase is one group with no divider, and the proclitic has no second secure witness. One attestation. See I-DA, PA₃ and I-SA-RI.
