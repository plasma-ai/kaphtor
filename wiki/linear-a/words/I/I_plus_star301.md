### Note

KN 2 lists it third with a whole number, RU-NA 2, \*118 3, I+\*301 5; HT Wa 1022 carries it alone. I 'ration' joins the sign of what is rationed, GRA+PA on HT 43, HT 93a and HT 120, and the man sign on HT 93a and HT 11a; here it joins \*301, the camp. MI+\*301 'portion' is the same build at Khania. Fractions stand with both members: I ½ on HT 115b, \*301 ¾ on HT 110b, and ½ on another face of HT Wa 1025. See I and \*301.

Rival: I as a syllable, the pair a word I-\*301.
