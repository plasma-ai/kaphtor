### Note

Complete, undivided IOZa6 group, tentatively segmented I-NA-TA-I + ZU-DI + SI-KA. Ugaritic btlt ʿnt 'the young woman Anat' (KTU 1.3 III 11; Huehnergard 2012, 86) and Middle Babylonian Ištar-ṣēri provide deity-plus-epithet controls. ZU-DI = ṯudī 'breast'; SI-KA = šiqā 'she who gives drink.' KTU 1.23 has b-ṯd aṯrt, 'at the breast of Athirat,' supplying the same divine nursing context. The segmentation and exact vocalization remain project hypotheses.
