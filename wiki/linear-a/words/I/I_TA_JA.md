### Note

HT 28a: I-TA-JA OLE+DI 10, the last recipient of the A-SI-JA-KA list, after A-RU-DA-RA GRA 5. Gemination unwritten; -aya written JA. Hebrew ʾIttay, Ittai the Gittite, who marches with the Cherethites and Pelethites (2 Sam 15:18–19). Linear B i-ta-ja is a woman's name at Knossos (KN Ap 769.2): the name outlives Linear A on Crete, which fixes the name but not its language. Two consonants, one attestation.
