### Note

MA 1a, a three-sided bar, whole: X, the mark that opens the face, then I-DU-WI · \*47 · QE-DE-MI-NU · (GORILA I 268–269). Yaduwi, he of Qēdmīnu: a man named by the one he belongs to, as Akk. PN šu PN₂ (CAD Š/3 152); face b sets A-MA 'people' in the same place.

Ugarit writes the name ia-du-PI in cuneiform (Syria 15, 1934, 137:6) and ydy in the alphabet (DULAT 945). The sign PI is wa, wi and ya alike: Kinlaw reads ya-du-wa (1967, 128), Watson ia-du-ia (1996, 99), and I-DU-WI spells it with wi. I writes ya, as in I-DU-TI and I-DU-NE-SI.

Rival: yidu 'strength', the Eblaite name element of I-DU-TI and I-DU-NE-SI, with WI an ending. Weak point: one witness, and the name's etymology is uncertain. See \*47, QE-DE-MI-NU, I-DU-TI and I-DU-NE-SI.
