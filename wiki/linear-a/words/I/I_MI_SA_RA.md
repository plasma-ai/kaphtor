### Note

HT27a .3: I-MI-SA-RA 43\[ on personnel allocation tablet headed by TI-NI-TA \*307 (Tinit of the Sanctuary). The word is line-initial at an intact edge, though a carry-over from line .2 is not ruled out; the numeral meets a break, so 43 or more. CAD M/2, 118 also lists ᵈIl-mīšaru (KAV 42 i 34), which I-MI-SA-RA spells with coda l unwritten. Phonological mapping: I = /ʾī/ — the West Semitic ʾî/ỉy 'where is—?' element of liturgy and onomastics: Ugaritic ỉy 'where?' (Huehnergard 2012, in contact lexicon; DULAT 130); cultic cry ỉy ảlỉyn bʿl 'Where is Mighty Baal?' KTU 1.6 IV (= CTA/UT 6\[49\].4.28–29; Pope, Song of Songs, AB 7C, p. 589); Hebrew ʾî-kāḇôd (1 Sam 4:21), ʾIyyôḇ (HALOT 38, s.v. III אִי, 2. 'where? (?)', listing אִיכָבוֹד; Noth, Die israelitischen Personennamen, on ʾî-names). Amorite i-initial name elements (Huffmon 1965, APNM: ila p.165, išar p.216, itur p.270) are congruent but include lexical elements and the Akkadian 3ms preterite i- (itur), not a distinct theophoric-prefix convention. MI = /mī/, SA = /ša/ (š→S neutralization), RA = /ru/. Clean 4-sign → 4-syllable mapping. Alternative reading: \*maṣṣariya "guard/watchman" (√m-ṣ-r)—but requires three accommodations (unexplained prefix, lost geminate, lost -iya suffix) vs one for Mīšaru (theophoric prefix). Context favors divine reading: sanctuary allocation, not garrison roster. If correct, HT27a contains both members of the Akkadian justice dyad: Tinit (subsuming Išartu/Yashara as heading deity) and Mīšaru (as personnel category). Cross-reference: SA-SA-RA = Yašara(t)/Išartu, the feminine counterpart. Huehnergard: /yušru/ "uprightness" (yuCCu nominal pattern); confirms adverbial accusative with -m.
