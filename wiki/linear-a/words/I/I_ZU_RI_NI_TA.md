### Note

Feminine counterpart of ZU-RI-NI-MA (KN Zb 52, masculine with mimation). I- = prosthetic vowel (cf. I-DA-MA-TE vs DA-MA-TE); ZU-RI-NI = base \*ṯawrīn-\* (bull + relational -īn); -TA = feminine -t (replacing masculine -MA). Context on PH6: governed by A-RI = harrī "mountain of" (the "lion" flanking reading is circular support). Final -TA where the construct chain expects genitive -TI remains an open caveat: the gentilic family shows both -TA (HT25a, ZA8) and -TI (KHWc2005 kanaʿanītī), and divine names may stand diptotic/frozen. ZU = /ṯu/ (a proposed value). The masculine parallel, the A-RI context and the regular morphological pattern place it above SPECULATIVE.

### Cognates

PS \*ṯawr- "bull, ox" (#269); Ug. ṯr/ṯrn "bull" (KTU deity epithet, PN element); Heb. שׁוֹר šōr "ox" (&lt; \*ṯawr with interdental→sibilant); Ar. ṯawr "bull"
