### Note

Hapax at Syme, position-2 slot in the libation formula (after A-TA-I-\*301-WA-JA). I-DA = Mount Ida (the sacred mountain, cf. I-DA-A on KOZa1); -MI = √m-y-y 'water' (Hebrew מַיִם mayim, Ugaritic my). 'Ida-waters'—a reference to the sacred springs and caves of the Idaean sanctuary. The Dictaean and Idaean caves are famously associated with water sources (the Dictaean cave has an underground lake; the Idaean cave complex is spring-fed). Alternative reading: -mī as 1cs possessive suffix 'My Ida'—the worshipper claiming personal relationship with the mountain-deity. Parallels each peak sanctuary naming its own sacred mountain in position 2: JA-DI-KI-TU (Dikte) at Iouktas, I-DA-A (Ida) at Kophinas, I-DA-MI (Waters of Ida / My Ida) at Syme.
