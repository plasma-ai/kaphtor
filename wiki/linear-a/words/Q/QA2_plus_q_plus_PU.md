### Note

GORILA A 510, analysed 16+\[?\]+50 (GORILA V 173), nine times, in the aromatics lists of Haghia Triada and on KH 8: HT 23a VIN 10, QA2+\[?\]+PU 10, QA2+\[?\]+RE 10, under the heading KA-NA; HT 32 QA2+\[?\]+PU 1¹⁄₁₆ beside QA2+\[?\]+RE 1; HT 12 and KH 8, 1; HT 30 ⅕; HT 34 ≈⅙; HT 35 ¹⁄₁₆; broken on HT 23b and HT 60. A 511, 16+\[?\]+50+27, sets RE beside PU: HT 12 ¹⁄₁₆ (GORILA V 187). Its neighbours are cyperus, MI+JA+RU, \*308, the OLE riders, wine and E.

The sign is QA, the lens on a stem with a foot, written inside the \*402 cup of HT 31: a rim bar over tapering walls that close on QA's stem, QA's foot standing for the cup's foot; on HT 33 QA's side loops show through the walls. GORILA dots the 16 and leaves the cup unidentified, \[?\]. HT 31 writes QA-PA₃ on that cup (\*402-VS-QA-PA₃ 10), so the ligature is the cup named by its first sign: QA-PA₃ qab, the measuring cup and its measure, Heb. qab, divided as rōbaʿ haq-qab 'a quarter of a qab' (2 Kgs 6:25). Akkadian qû is likewise 'a measuring vessel of standard capacity' and 'a measure of capacity', with 1 qûm of bronze in an Old Akkadian list (CAD Q 288–89). Linear A writes syllables on vessel signs elsewhere: \*402+A (MA 10b), \*406VAS+KE (HT 26a), \*412+E, \*413+SU. The fractions fit a measure of the commodity, not a count of cups. PU hangs from the right end of the rim.

PU names what was measured and has no reading. It stands alone as an entry on HT 34, PU ⅛, on the same tablet as QA2+\[?\]+PU ≈⅙, and it joins RE in QA2+\[?\]+PU+RE (HT 12) and QA2+\[?\]+RE+PU (HT 33, HT 45b), so PU and RE are not alternative grades of one product; HT 23a books the two in equal amounts, 10 and 10. Rivals: an unidentified commodity logogram, among Schoep's candidates for cyperus or coriander (Schoep 2002, 93); the cup as qubbaʿ 'goblet', the rival reading of QA-PA₃. See QA2+\[?\]+RE, QA-PA₃.
