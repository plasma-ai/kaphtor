### Note

SKO Zc 1, painted on a chalice rim in two non-joining fragments, order uncertain: NA-TU-\*301-NE (natūnān, di Mino, Ya Diktu) and QA-KI-TI. Akkadian qaqqadu 'head; top (of a field, a city)' (CAD Q 100, 108); Hebrew qodqōd, Ugaritic qdqd. Weak points: KI for q beside QA, T for d, i for a; CAD gives no mountain-top sense. QA-QA-DA (PE 2) writes qaqqadu with QA and DA. One attestation.
