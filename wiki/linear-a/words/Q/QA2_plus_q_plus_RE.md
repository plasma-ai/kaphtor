### Note

GORILA A 508, analysed 16+\[?\]+27 (GORILA V 187), six times at Haghia Triada: HT 23a 10, after QA2+\[?\]+PU 10; HT 23b 1; HT 32 1; HT 33, a fraction; HT 34 ¼; HT 60, broken. A 509, 16+\[?\]+27+50, sets PU beside RE: HT 33 1¼ and HT 45b twice, each with a fraction. Its neighbours are cyperus, MI+JA+RU, \*308, the OLE riders, wine and E.

The sign is QA, the lens on a stem with a foot, written inside the \*402 cup of HT 31: a rim bar over tapering walls that close on QA's stem, QA's foot standing for the cup's foot; on HT 33 QA's side loops show through the walls. GORILA dots the 16 and leaves the cup unidentified, \[?\]. HT 31 writes QA-PA₃ on that cup (\*402-VS-QA-PA₃ 10), so the ligature is the cup named by its first sign: QA-PA₃ qab, the measuring cup and its measure, Heb. qab, divided as rōbaʿ haq-qab 'a quarter of a qab' (2 Kgs 6:25). Akkadian qû is likewise 'a measuring vessel of standard capacity' and 'a measure of capacity', with 1 qûm of bronze in an Old Akkadian list (CAD Q 288–89). Linear A writes syllables on vessel signs elsewhere: \*404+A (MA 10b), \*406VAS+KE (HT 26a), \*412+E, \*413+SU. The fractions fit a measure of the commodity, not a count of cups. RE, the Ψ, stands above the rim; on HT 12 (A 511) it stands free to the right of PU, a rider of its own.

RE names what was measured and has no reading; it is not a grade of PU's product, since the two combine (QA2+\[?\]+RE+PU, QA2+\[?\]+PU+RE). Rivals: an unidentified commodity logogram, among Schoep's candidates for cyperus or coriander (Schoep 2002, 93); the cup as qubbaʿ 'goblet', the rival reading of QA-PA₃. See QA2+\[?\]+PU, QA-PA₃.
