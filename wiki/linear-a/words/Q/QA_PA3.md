### Note

HT 31: written on the \*402-VS cup, quantity 10, one of Gordon's four vessel words on the tablet (Gordon 1966 §115). QA-PA₃ spells qa-ba, the final vowel empty: Heb. qab 'kab, measure of capacity' (HALOT 1060), divided as rōbaʿ haq-qab 'a quarter of a qab' (2 Kgs 6:25). Akkadian qû names the same vessel and measure, 'a measuring vessel of standard capacity' and 'a measure of capacity', written qa-a in the lexical lists, with 1 qûm of bronze in an Old Akkadian list (CAD Q 288–89). The tablets measure by this cup: QA written inside the \*402 cup, with PU or RE attached, is booked in fractions among the aromatics (QA2+\[?\]+PU, QA2+\[?\]+RE). The sign is a handleless conical vase with a curved upper wall, taller than the cups of SU-PA₃-RA and PA-TA-QE and twice as wide at the base (Duhoux 2002, 39–40). Rivals: qubbaʿ 'cup, goblet', Heb. qubbaʿat (Isa 51:17, 22), Emar qú-pa-ḫu, qu-bá-ḫu ZABAR (GlOS 1, 338), Ug. qbʿt (DULAT 681), which needs QA to write qu; Gordon's qa-pa kappu (√k-p-p 'palm, vessel'; §115), whose geminate pp resists the voicing PA₃ would need. One attestation as a word. See QA2+\[?\]+PU, QA2+\[?\]+RE.
