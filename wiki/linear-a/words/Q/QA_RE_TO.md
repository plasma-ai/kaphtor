### Note

HT 132: QA-RE-TO · \*21F 27, ewes, between A-SE · A319 5 and PA₃ · \*305 1. qarēt- 'banquet', √q-r-y 'invite', Akk. qarētu beside qerītu (CAD Q 240): 27 ewes for the feast, as Akkadian counts sheep of the banquet, immeri qerēti (Hh. XIII 115). QA-RE writes qarē-; the final -TO is unexplained. Rival: qart 'town', Ug. qrt /qarītu/ (Huehnergard 2012, 44), polyglot qa-ri-t\[u₄\] (GlOS 1, 354), Phoenician qrt as in Melqart, and the Ugaritic town qrt, \*Qarātu (DULAT 703; Astour 1995, 68), a place sending or receiving the ewes; RE is then a vowel the word lacks. One attestation.
