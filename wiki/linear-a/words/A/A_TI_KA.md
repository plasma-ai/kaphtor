### Note

ZA Wc 2, a Zakros roundel: .1 A-TI-KA-A, .2 DỤ-KO-⟦MI⟧, the erasure brackets dotted; GORILA divides A-TI-KA · A-DU-KO-⟦MI⟧, though the four signs of .1 are evenly spaced (GORILA II 98). Linear B a-ti-ka is a man in a catalogue of men at Knossos (KN V 831.4; DMic I 119). Anastasiadou (2026, 40–41) makes the comparison and reports the rival divisions: Hallager reads one word A-TI-KA-A-DU-KO with the last sign the numeral 2, and Petrakis sees no epigraphic break after KA. GORILA draws the last sign as MI. No etymology is adopted. One attestation.

### Source

DMic I 119; Anastasiadou 2026, 40–41, in Salgarella & Petrakis (eds.) 2026; di Mino
