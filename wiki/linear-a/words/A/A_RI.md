### Note

Initial h- carried by plain vowel sign per guttural-carrier convention; genitive -ī as the construct chain requires (nawā harrī DN 'dwelling of the mountain of DN'). harrī is preferred over ʿalē 'upon, for' and ʾarī 'lion': the A-RA/A-RI/A-RU full u/i/a triple argues nominal inflection against a frozen preposition, and the house dative is la- (RE/RA). A higher tier needs an undamaged A-RI + established toponym, identification of RU-DI (HT141, damaged context \[...\] 1 A-RI RU-DI \[...\]), or governed -TA proper-name parallels. PH28a (same site and period) has A-RI-JA-\[, broken after JA.
