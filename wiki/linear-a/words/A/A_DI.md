### Note

Opens register .2 of KNZc6 after QI-TI-RI and governs DA-KI-TI: ʿadi daqqati '(ground) until fine', the Hebrew ʿad … dāq of grinding to powder. Evans (PoM I 615 n. 1) reports a dividing mark before DA, which GORILA IV 121 does not print. Initial A- writes ʿa- (ʿ drops).
