### Note

HT96a+b (Scribe 8, Casa Room 7, LMIB). HT96b heads major institutional allocation: QA-\*118-RA-RE (qaṭara 'incense') + \*516 (SIPRU) + GRA 40¾ + OLE+U 4 + NI 2. Short form PA-RA-NE attested on HT115a/b (same scribe) \|\| TWO COMPETING ETYMOLOGIES: (1) √ʿ-b-r + -āne agentive = 'the crosser/one from beyond' (= ʿibrī 'Hebrew'). Dual reading requires PA→BA neutralization. (2) √b-r-ʾ + -āne = 'creator' (aleph drops) \|\| REJECTED: Aaron (ʾAhărōn)—PA ≠ /ha/, fatal mismatch \|\| Amorite Ḥa-pí-ra-an (Huffmon APNM p. 36; element ḤPR ḥapir-, \*ʿapir- = the ʿApiru/Ḥabiru word, p. 202) matches A-PA-RA-NE (initial A = ʿa) with -ān; cuneiform ḫ writes ʿ. AP-RA-AN (Gelb AS 21 no. 863) is a separate name, normalized ʾApran; cuneiform AP- writes ʾ and ʿ alike. Root disputed: Borger (via Huffmon p. 202) derives ʿapīru from ʿ-p-r 'dust', not ʿ-b-r 'cross'; PA writes p and b alike.
