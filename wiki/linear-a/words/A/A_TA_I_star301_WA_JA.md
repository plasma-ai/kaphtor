### Note

The verb that opens the libation formula, 'toujours en début d'inscription, et seulement là' (Karetsou, Godart and Olivier 1985, 128). Eleven stone vessels at five peak sanctuaries carry it, Iouktas (IO Za 2, 3, 7), Kophinas (KO Za 1), Troullos (TL Za 1), Palaikastro (PK Za 12) and Syme (SY Za 1, 2, 3, 4, 8), whole on eight, with signs dotted on SY Za 1 and SY Za 8 and the JA of SY Za 3 lost (Thomas 2020, 18–20). In 236 of its 272 attestations \*301 stands alone, on the Hagia Triada nodules and at Khania, Knossos and Zakros, abbreviating nawā 'dwelling, temple' (di Mino, Ya Diktu).

A- is the 1cs prefix, -TA- the tG prefix, I an epenthetic i, as Geʿez inserts a after the tG prefix (Hutton and Marzouk 2012, 22–23), and \*301-WA-JA (\*301 = na) the root √n-w-y: /ʾatainawaya/ 'I make myself a dwelling', denominative from nāweh 'abode', as wə-hitnabbîtā 'you will make yourself a prophet' (1 Sam 10:6) is from nābîʾ (di Mino, Ya Diktu). Arabic keeps the t-stem of the root in this sense, intawā l-qawmu manzilan 'the people betook themselves to a place of abode' (Lane 3040), its -t- infixed as in the Akkadian Gt. A-TA-I- prefixes it, as the Hebrew Hitpaʿel and Geʿez tä-qätlä do, and Hebrew keeps the prefixed tG in relics such as tirgaltî (Hos 11:3; Hutton and Marzouk 2012). The worshipper acts on herself before the deity, as in wā-ʾetnappal lipnê YHWH 'and I prostrated myself before YHWH' (Deut 9:18), and Exod 15:2 wə-ʾanwēhû is 1cs √n-w-y with the deity as object.

Nāweh joins divine habitation to pasture: nəwēh qodšekā 'your holy abode' (Exod 15:13), nəwēh-ṣedeq 'abode of righteousness' (Jer 31:23), Amorite nawûm 'steppe; encampment (man and beast)' (Huffmon 1965, 237). Greek has it as ναός 'temple', Mycenaean na-wi-jo 'of the temple' (PY Jn 829), and Rendsburg accepts naos = nāweh, the noun of Exod 15:13 and the verb of Exod 15:2 (1989, 77–78).

Same paradigm: A-TA-I-\*301-WA-E (PK Za 11a), which weakens JA to E as A-SA-SA-RA-ME and A-DI-KI-TE-TE do there, and JA-TA-I-\*301-U-JA (AP Za 1, 3ms). TA-NA-I-\*301-(U-)TI(-NU) shares the frame with √ʿ-n-y 'answer' and A-TA-I-\*301-DE-KA (ZA Zb 3) with √ʿ-n-w 'be humbled', and \*301-WA nawā 'dwelling' stands on IO Za 4. Rivals: Duhoux's 'gives', which Davis adopts (Davis 2026, 53), and Finkelberg's two units, atai- and a particle chain -301-wa-ja (1990, 49–51). Thomas takes the invariant I-\*301 for the root of one family (2020, 2–3), and the editors set TA-NA-I-\*301-U-TI-NU in it (Karetsou, Godart and Olivier 1985, 134), where Ya Diktu finds two roots, √n-w-y and √ʿ-n-y. HALOT files Exod 15:2 under II נוה 'glorify', with the Peshitta and the Palestinian Targumim. See TA-NA-RA-TE-U-TI-NU, the closing petition.
