### Note

SK Zb 1, a clay vessel from Skhinia, the word alone. A-MA-RA-NE writes ʾAmarānu, the ʾAmar- names of Amorite (ʾAMAR-ʾILI, ʾAMAR-ADDU, ʾAMAR-U; Gelb AS 21 nos. 408, 720, 724) with -ānu. Weak point: the final E. Rival: marʾānu 'lordly one' (PS \*marʾ-), with A- unexplained. One attestation.
