### Note

PKZa12 preserves A-DI-KI-TE-\[ without a following divider, so it may open a longer Diktaian name or title; neither an independent word boundary nor a vocative use is established there. Palaikastro lies beneath the Dictaean mountains. Compare complete A-DI-KI-TE-TE on PKZa11 and JA-DI-KI-TU elsewhere. \|\| COGNATES: those of JA-DI-KI-TU, the same name (Diktu/Dikte).
