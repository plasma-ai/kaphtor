## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role title.

Both attestations are damaged. KH102, a 2.5 × 1.6 cm LM I fragment, preserves two signs in part, A and 'presumably MI' (Hallager et al. 2014, PoDIA 7: 216, Fig. 11). PH14b (MM IIB lame) is retrograde: A-MỊ-\[, MI dotted and broken after it (GORILA I 305 prints \]-MỊ-A in left-to-right order, Younger A-MI-\[), so A-MI may begin a longer word (cf. A-MI-DA-O, A-MI-TA). Not readable.
