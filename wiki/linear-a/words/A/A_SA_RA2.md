### Note

HT89 A-SA-RA₂ · \*307 (dubur 'sanctuary') heads five personnel classes, KU-RO 87, then rations of cyperus, figs and wine. Written as one group, with no divider or space between A and SA (GORILA I 141). A-SA-RA₂ writes ʾasāra with the vowels of Ebla ʔa₅-za-ru₁₂ ʔas(ā)r 'locked house' (GlOS 1, 46): the sanctuary's work-house, named over the classes it holds, as VIR+\*307 names sanctuary personnel. Its staff are of the kind of the asirū of Alalakh VII, bonded household staff in grain-delivery lists (Niedorf 2008, 80 n. 305); cf. the Gibeonites made hewers of wood and drawers of water for the house of God (Josh 9:23, 27) and the captives given to the Levites (Num 31:47); compare MA-I-MI, TA-RA. Rivals: ʾasīr- 'captives' (Ug. åsr, Akk. asīru), which wants SI for /sī/; A + SA-RA₂ 'concerning the queen', A as abbreviated Akkadian aššum (EA 287:34; Huehnergard 2008: 285, 399), a cut the tablet does not mark. One witness.
