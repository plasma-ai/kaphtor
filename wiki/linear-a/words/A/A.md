## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone on 3 Haghia Triada nodules (HT Wa 1147–1149) and the Petras vessel PE Zb 8. One tablet keeps it whole as an entry: HT 37 .5 A 15 under KI-RO, the 15 dotted and the half-line written over an erasure (GORILA I 71). No reading. Every other tablet breaks at the sign: A\[ on HT 57a .2 (I 111), KH 45 .1 (III 71), ARKH 1b .1 (III 5) and ZA 21a .7, there dotted (III 195); \]A\[ on PH 27 .2 (I 313); A-\[ on ARKH 5 .3, where the editors allow A-DA\[ (III 17). KH 17 .4 and KH 33 .2 break straight after a dotted A (III 49, 63), and on KH 73 .1 breaks after a dotted A and .3 has A, a dash and the break (III 91). The fraction once read after A on HT 57a stands on the next line, broken at both sides. The A before SA-SA-RA-ME on PK Za 11 is the first sign of A-SA-SA-RA-ME and is counted with that word.
