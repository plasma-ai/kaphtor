## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone on 3 Haghia Triada nodules (HT Wa 1147–1149) and the Petras vessel PE Zb 8. On tablets at Khania, Haghia Triada, Arkhanes, Phaistos and Zakros it stands as an entry, with a quantity on HT 57a (A ⅛). No reading.
