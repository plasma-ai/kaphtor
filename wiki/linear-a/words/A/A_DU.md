### Note

Five Haghia Triada tablets, all from the Casa del Lebete: A-DU opens the tablet or a section and never takes a number of its own. It heads men on HT 85a (A-DU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+\[?\], seven entries, KU-RO 66) and HT 88 (VIR+KA 20, then KI-RO, six men owed, KU-RO 6), barley on HT 86a and HT 95b (Montecchi 2008, 319), and mixed foodstuffs on HT 99a under SA-RA₂ (CYP 4, FIC 4, VIN 1⅙, GRA+QE).

adû 'work quota per man-day, work assignment, assigned task of menial work', a Sumerian loan in Akkadian (CAD A/1 135): at Mari 'a work assignment for a large group of men' (ARM 3 3:7), and oil as 'the balance of the work quota (of the oil processors)' (ARM 7 103:1; CAD A/1 135). At Alalakh a-du, a-du-un, a-ad-du is 'assigned portion, ration', 'a common type of ration of grains for animals and men': (barley and emmer) a-na a-du (AT 242:13), (vetches) 30 a-du LÚ APIN (AT 246:31); Nuzi a-du-ú is said of oil rations (Draffkorn 1959, 152–54). Men, grain and mixed foodstuffs are what A-DU heads. Linear B heads work allotments with ta-ra-si-ja talasiā, 'amount weighed out and issued for processing' (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 408).

Alalakh writes the god ᵈIŠKUR, i-na tu-ku-ul-ti ᵈIŠKUR (RIME 4.34.1.1:2), and the ration a-du; Linear A has no determinative, and the homograph A-DU (HT92, HT133) is the god where grain is booked to it with TE. Rivals: a place or person from which the administration receives, or to which it sends, men and grain, or a sanctuary or festival for which it collects them (Montecchi 2008, 319–20); 'assessment'?, a transaction word (Younger, Linear A Texts, 'Transaction Words'; Hogan 2026, 93), which Schoep counts, tentatively, among the transactional terms, a place name not excluded (Schoep 2002, 160, 166). Weak point: HT 95a heads the list of HT 95b with DA-DU-MA-TA, a district, and Montecchi asks one interpretation for both headings (2008, 319). KH 23 A-DU\[ breaks after DU (GORILA III 57). See A-DU \*307+\*307, A-KA-RU and DA-DU-MA-TA.

### Note

The god Addu as recipient of the grain booked with TE on HT 92 and HT 133 (Gordon 1966 §129): see lexicon A-DU (HT92, HT133). At Alalakh IV sixteen personal names carry Addu/a against three with Teššup (Niedorf 2008, 44). On HT 85a, 86a, 88, 95b and 99a the same spelling heads personnel and ration lists as the word adû 'work assignment, ration' (lexicon A-DU); KH 23 A-DU\[ breaks after DU and stays unread.
