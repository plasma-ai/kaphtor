### Note

Arkhanes tablet. Compound theophoric: A-DU (Hadad/Addu storm god, the dominant theophoric in the corpus) + NI-TA-NA (nittāna, N-stem perfect of √n-t-n 'to give', &lt; \*nintan 'was given'). Parallels: Amarna EA Addu-dāni, OB Addu-nārī. Both root elements independently attested in corpus: A-DU heads HT88 register; √n-t-n underlies NA-TU-\*301-NE (SKOZc1). Single attestation. Both constituent elements independently attested: A-DU (CANDIDATE, 8 documents at two sites) and √n-t-n (via NA-TU-\*301-NE at SKOZc1, PROBABLE). ZA10a demonstrates A-DU as detachable theophoric prefix (A-DU-KU-MI-NA ~ A-KU-MI-NA). Active parallels: Akkadian Adad-iddinam, Iddin-Dagan; West Semitic Adda-natan (SAA 14 014), whose G perfect natan the NI- spelling does not fit. One attestation. Member of the Arkhanes ration-formula family (named recipient + GRA/\*131B-must + quantity; ARKH2, ARKH5 ↔ ARKH3a/b)—the recipient slot on ARKH5. NB: the recurring element across ARKH2+ARKH5 is \*131B, not this name; attestation remains ARKH5 only.
