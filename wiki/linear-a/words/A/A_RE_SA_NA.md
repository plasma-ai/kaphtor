### Note

THE Zb 2, an Akrotiri clay vessel, the sole word: the owner's or dedicant's slot. ʾAriš-ān 'The Requested One', a votive birth-name from √ʾ-r-š 'to request, desire' with the hypocoristic -ān: the Amorite name ʾARIŚ-AN, ḪA-RI-ŠA-AN (Gelb 1980, no. 2190), -NA writing its closing n; Ug. /ʾ-r-š/ 'to request, wish, demand' (DULAT 104), Heb. ʾărešet 'desire' (Ps 21:3; HALOT 92), pre-Islamic Arabian ʾršn (Harding 1971, 37). RE writes ri, as KI-RE-TA₂ beside KI-RI-TA₂. One attestation.
