### Note

Sole word on THEZb2, an Akrotiri clay vessel—the owner/dedicant slot. Weak points: hapax; final -a where ARIŠ-AN-U freezes -u (the Yarīmān -ē caveat); RE = /ri/ by the routine e/i wobble. A votive Semitic name on a Theran vessel.

### Note

THE Zb 2, the sole word on an Akrotiri clay vessel, in the owner's or dedicant's slot. ʾAriš-āna 'the requested one', √ʾ-r-š 'to request' with hypocoristic -ān; cf. Amorite ARIŠ-AN (Gelb, AS 21, no. 2190) and ARIŠ-AN-U (no. 2191). Weak points: final -a; RE for /ri/. One attestation.
