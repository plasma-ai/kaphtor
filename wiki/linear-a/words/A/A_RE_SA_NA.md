### Note

THE Zb 2, a ewer from Akrotiri (Delta 4, LM IA), its one word cut before firing (GORILA IV 102; Younger, Linear A Texts): the owner's or dedicant's slot. ʾAriš-ān 'The Requested One', from √ʾ-r-š 'to request, desire' with -ān, -NA writing its closing n: Phoenician and Punic ʾRŠ, \*ʾariš 'request, desire', with ʾRŠTY 'My Desire' (Benz 1972, 276); the Ugarit names ỉršn (KTU 4.77:17) and ỉršy 'my wish' (Watson 1995, 220; DULAT 106), Ug. /ʾ-r-š/ 'to request, wish, demand' (DULAT 104); Heb. ʾărešet 'desire' (Ps 21:3; HALOT 92); pre-Islamic Arabian ʾršn (Harding 1971, 37). The Mari name ḪA-RI-ŠA-AN is ʾARIŚ-AN for Gelb (1980, no. 2190). RE writes ri, as KI-RE-TA₂ beside KI-RI-TA₂.

Rival: Ḥarišān 'craftsman', Huffmon's reading of the Mari name (1965, 205), Ug. ḥrš 'craftsman' (DULAT 366), A writing ḥa as in A-NA-NU. THE Zb 4 \]A-RE\[, a lid fragment cut after firing, may hold the same word (Younger). One attestation.
