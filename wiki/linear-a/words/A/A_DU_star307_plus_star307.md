### Note

HT 85a .1 A-DU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+\[?\] ·, heading a personnel register of 66 workers. The middle sign is \*307 written twice, joined by a top bar (A 638; GORILA I 131), as Montecchi also reads A 638 (2008, 316 n. 30). KA-RU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+KA heads HT 97a: the slot takes A-DU adû here and KA-RU there. The entry keeps the attested phrase whole: A-DU adû with \*307+\*307 'the sanctuaries'. Rival: Addu's sanctuary, with A-DU (HT92, HT133). See A-DU, \*307+\*307 and DU-PU₂-RE.
