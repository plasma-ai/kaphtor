### Note

KN Zf 31 · A-WA-PI ·, undotted, between dividers (GORILA IV 155). Causative with ʾa-, inflected as I-w (aw), final ʿ unwritten. Heb. hôpîaʿ, the theophany verb (Deut 33:2; Ps 50:2; 80:2), whose ô is old aw (HALOT 424); Ug. /y-p-ʿ/ 'go up, rise' (DULAT 958). JA-WA-? on PE Zb 7 may be the prefix form if its third sign is PI. Rival: ʿawp 'bird' (Heb. ʿôp), which would need \*306 for ʿa.
