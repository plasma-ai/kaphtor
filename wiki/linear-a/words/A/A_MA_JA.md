### Note

KH 14 .1 \]RA · A-MA-JA · CYP 6\[, whole and undotted between dividers, a cyperus recipient (GORILA III 45). A-MA ʿamma 'kinsman' + hypocoristic -ya, as A-SU-JA, PA-SE-JA: the Ugaritic name ʿmy ʿAmmaya (KTU 3.10:9), syllabic am-ma-ia (DULAT 73). Rival: ʾAmaya, short for ʾamat- 'maidservant' (Ug. amt, DULAT 71), with t lost.
