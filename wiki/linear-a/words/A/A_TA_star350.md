### Note

KH 11 .5–6 A-TA-\*350, then \*301 1 and \*306 1, the last section before SI CYP (GORILA III 39). \*350 stands only here; Salgarella finds it 'similar in shape to LB wo' and, 'if not actually a variant of A 306', a candidate for the ancestor of Linear B wo (2020, 171), which gives A-TA-WO. In SigLA's tracings the \*306 of .3 and .6, by the same hand, has a closed loop at the left and two short strokes at the right, and the sign of .5 has neither.

ʾatāwu 'what has come in', the noun of ʾ-t-w 'come, arrive' with its third radical written: Ugaritic ảtwt (DULAT 119–120), Ebla a-du-wu-mu for TÙR.DU₈ (GlOS 1, 49). Sabaic gives the account sense, hʔtw 'bring in; present (an offering)' and a noun ʔtw that Beeston queries as 'crop-yield' (1982, 9). The section books \*301 1, pasture produce, and \*306 1 as received. Weak point: the value wo rests on one occurrence. Rival: a personal name, as A-TO-\*349-TO-I stands before goods on the same tablet; the Amorite names on this root are built on ʔatā and yaʔti, without w (GlOS 1, 49). Second rival: ʕ-t-w 'give (a present)', known from Arabic. One attestation. See \*301.
