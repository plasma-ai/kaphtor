### Note

KN Zf 31, a silver pin, first word of the second line (GORILA IV 155), before TI-DI-TE-QA-TI 'fix it, you have fastened it'. ʾadra 'the fibula', Ug. adr (IV) 'fibula' (?), pl. adrm, pldm b adrm 'p.-garments (provided) with (?) fibulae (?)' (KTU 4.4:5; DULAT 22), and a hundred ảdrm for ten shekels, 'pin' (?) in Bordreuil and Pardee's reading (KTU 4.158:8; DULAT 22): the pin names itself at the head of the line that speaks of it. Watson glosses ảdr 'tie, strap' in the same lines, KTU 4.4:2, 5, from Akk. idru (or itru) '(a band, belt)' and Eg. idr, Gürtel, Gürtelschnalle, and reads the compound pldm dảdrm 'garment with fibulae' (Watson 2018b, 373, 378). Rivals: ʾAdara 'the Strong One', an epithet after DA-DU-MI-NE, QA-MI-\*47-NA-RA and A-WA-PI on the first line, sign for sign the Ebla name a-da-ra (Pagan 1998, row 558), ʾadar 'strong' (GlOS 1, 10), cf. Heb. ʾaddīr 'mighty' (Ps 8:2, 93:4); ʾaddart- 'mantle', Heb. ʾadderet (Gen 25:25; Josh 7:21, beside silver and gold; 1 Kgs 19:13), final -t unwritten. Spelling: ʾ dropped, single DA. See DI-DE-RU, A-DA-RO and DI-RE-DI-NA.
