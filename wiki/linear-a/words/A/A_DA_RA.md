### Note

(A) ʾaddart- 'mantle': Heb. ʾadderet (Gen 25:25; Josh 7:21, beside silver and gold; 1 Kgs 19:13 bə-ʾaddartô; Zech 13:4); Ug. adrt 'of good quality' (DULAT 22). A garment word on a silver dress pin; A-DA-RA's a-a vowels fit ʾaddar(t), final -t unwritten. (B) ʾaddīr 'mighty' (Ps 8:2, 93:4; Ug. adr), which needs ī where the spelling has a. Spelling: ʾ dropped, single DA.
