### Note

Palaikastro variant of JA-DI-KI-TU (Iouktas). A-DI-KI-TE + TE: the name Diqqite + determinative de "she of", before \[dubu\]re (A-DI-KI-TE-TE-\[DU-PU₂\]-RE). The second TE stands only before dubur (PKZa8, PKZa15, PKZa11); the name alone has one dental (JA-DI-KI-TU, A-DI-KI-TE\[, LB di-ka-ta-). TE = determinative de, cf. DU du; Ug. il d pid "El, the one of the heart" (KTU 1.4 IV 58–59). de CANDIDATE. PKZa12 A-DI-KI-TE\[ is broken after TE. Davis 2026 parses A- as prepositional prefix "to/for the Diktaian" (in Salgarella & Petrakis eds., The Wor(l)ds of Linear A, pp. 43-60). \|\| COGNATES: those of JA-DI-KI-TU, the same name (Diktu/Dikte).
