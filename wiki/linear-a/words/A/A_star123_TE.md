### Note

HT 96a .1–2 I-TI-TI-KU-NI · A-PA-RA-NE · A-\*123-TE, the heading of a grain list (GORILA I 156–157). HT 96b, the other face, heads its list A-PA-RA-NE · QA-\*118-RA-RE, qaṭar- 'incense': the word that holds the aromatics sign stands where the other face spells the aromatic. \*123 is Cretan Hieroglyphic 157, 'Spice jar? For 'spices'' (Meissner and Salgarella 2024, 153), and Linear B AROM, a logogram before Linear A and after it. ha-quṭōrt 'the incense': A the article, h unwritten, as in A-SI-KI-RA; the sign for the word; TE its feminine -t.

The feminine noun is of the Bronze Age: Egyptian writes qu₄-da-ru₂-ta, quṭōrt 'incense' (Hoch, SWET 305, no. 440, apud GlOS 1, 359); Hebrew qəṭōret (HALOT 1095–96) and Punic qṭrt 'perfume' (DNWSI 1007), beside Ugaritic qṭr 'smoke' (DULAT 709). Linear B names the goods it counts with AROM tu-we-a (PY Un 267), thuea, 'orig. 'fragrant smoke'' (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 410), the sense of q-ṭ-r.

Linear B sets syllabic signs on the same logogram, AROM+KO and ko AROM for coriander (MY Ge 606, MY Ge 603, PY Un 592). In Linear A phonetic complementation 'seems to be a widespread and highly varied practice', and Steele reports Salgarella's view that a complement is added to a logographic sign 'in order to specify a morphological form, such as a word ending' (2024, 68). PU-VIN ends in the wine sign in the same way. TE is no transaction sign here: on the tablets that sign stands apart, after a divider or at the head of a line, as on this tablet's own .5, \]KU-MA-RO · TE NI 20, and in the heading no divider parts \*123 from TE (GORILA I 157).

Weak point: no other word sets a logogram between two syllabograms, and A-ME-\*123 (KH 53, broken) and DU-\*123-A (HT 15) stand unread. Rival: an unread word with \*123 a syllabogram, since for Schoep AROM 'occurs in Linear A only as a syllabogram' (2002, 91); Steele answers that the sign stands 'just before numerals in four out of seven examples' (2024, 64). Rival: A a mark of the kind of aromatic, as it follows the sign on HT 15, Steele's 'AROM+a' (2024, 65). One attestation. See QA-\*118-RA-RE, TA-I, TE.
