### Note

SAM Wa 1, a Samothrace nodule of MM II: the seal impression holds two signs in a frame, the double axe and the forked sign, Cretan Hieroglyphic 042-019 (CHIC #137), and another face has TE 10 ¹⁄₁₆ incised. 042-019 is the first element of the Archanes formula, 042-019 with 019-095-052, A-SA / SA-RA-NE, whose Linear A counterpart is A-SA-SA-RA-ME; Younger prints the seal as JA-SA &lt;-SA-RA&gt;, the second element supplied. The double axe is A, so the seal has the ʾa- spelling of PR Za 1 and IO Zb 10. A sealed lot marked TE under the formula's opening. Weak point: whether the signs of a seal spell a word or stand as emblems is disputed, and SA-RA is not written on the nodule. See JA-SA, the clipped form with ya-.
