### Note

KN Zf 13, signs 1–3, opening the spiral (GORILA IV 152–153); construct before SI-DI-\*301 Ṣidna, 'the coffin of Sidon'. The ring lay in compartment E of Chamber Tomb IX at Mavro Spilio, the compartment of a larnax (Olivier 1982). Tabnit of Sidon pairs the two words: mlk ṣdnm … škb bʾrn z (KAI 13). Weak points: E for the ā of ʾarān, which Neo-Punic arun keeps (Krahmalkov 73); 'coffin' is first-millennium, and Ugaritic ảrn is only 'chest'. Rival: Ḥōrān (Horon). One attestation.
