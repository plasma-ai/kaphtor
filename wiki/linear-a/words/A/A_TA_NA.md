### Note

Root √ʾ-t-n (lexicalized divine name from √y-t-n after y &gt; ʾ weakening). Feminine \*yātanat &gt; ʾAtanat. Phoenician preserves the y-initial verbal form: BʿLYTN = Baʿal-yatōn 'Baʿal has given' (KAI). Greek receives the post-weakened form: Mycenaean a-ta-na-po-ti-ni-ja (KN V 52), Classical Ἀθάνα (Doric) / Ἀθήνη (Ionic). Beekes (EDG 2010): no IE etymology, classified pre-Greek. Founding myth preserves the etymology: Athena wins Athens by GIVING the olive tree. Six √y-t-n / √n-t-n forms across the corpus (JA-TA-NO and A-TA-NO are Gordon's misreadings of the formula verb and do not count). ZA9 reads /ʾAtanat/ 'She-who-gives' (di Mino, Ya Diktu p. 20). The A-/∅ alternation (A-TA-NA-TE vs TA-NA-TE on ZA10a) follows the same scribal convention as 4 other corpus pairs (A-PA-RA-NE/PA-RA-NE, A-MI-TA/MI-TA, A-SA-SA-RA-ME/SA-SA-RA-ME, A-DU/DU). ZA9, the only witness, reads A-TA-NA-\[·\] (GORILA III 166–67: "A-TA-NA-TE possible"): the last sign is lost and no divider follows NA, so ZA9 most likely writes A-TA-NA-TE as on ZA10a, and the bare stem stands only inside A-TA-NA-TE and A-TA-NA-JE. \|\| CONTEXT: A-TA-NA-TE on ZA10a and A-TA-NA-JE on THEZb6 have their own entries. ZA9 lists names with QI+SI and \*21F amounts under NU-TI—RO-SI-RA QI+SI 4, A-TA-NA-\[·\] QI+SI 2, \[·\]-MA-JU \*21F 2, WI-RA-RE-MI-TE \*21F 1—where the name reads most naturally as a person named for the goddess, not the goddess herself.

### Note

ZA 9, names with QI+SI and \*21F amounts: A-TA-NA-\[·\] QI+SI 2, the last sign lost (GORILA III 166–67: "A-TA-NA-TE possible"), probably A-TA-NA-TE as on ZA 10a. ʾAtanat 'She-who-gives' (di Mino, Ya Diktu p. 20), √y-t-n with y &gt; ʾ, the goddess of Mycenaean a-ta-na-po-ti-ni-ja (KN V 52); on ZA 9 most likely a person named for her. Rival: a pre-Greek name, Hurrian Nuzi atana lacking Hurrian morphology.
