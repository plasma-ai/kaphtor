### Note

Hebrew dāhar 'to dash, gallop' of a horse (Nah 3:2), dahărâ 'galloping' (Judg 5:22; HALOT 214): a name 'Charger'. Egypt borrowed the word for the chariot warrior, twhr (AED lemma 173060). A- is unaccounted for. Arabic dahr "time, fate, epoch" is semantically attractive but Arabic-specific—no Hebrew/Ugaritic/Akkadian cognate with "time/fate" meaning. Ugaritic ḏhrt "vision" is interdental /ḏ/, a different root. PN position on KH92 allocation list.
