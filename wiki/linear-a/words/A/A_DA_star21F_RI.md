### Note

PS \*dVhVr- "hunt" (exact skeleton match). Hebrew dāhar "to gallop" (Nah 3:2). Arabic dahr "time, fate, epoch" is semantically attractive but Arabic-specific—no Hebrew/Ugaritic/Akkadian cognate with "time/fate" meaning. Ugaritic ḏhrt "vision" is interdental /ḏ/, a different root. Not √ṭ-h-r: DA sign = /d/ (not /ṭ/), and the syllabary fully distinguishes D/T. PN position on KH92 allocation list.
