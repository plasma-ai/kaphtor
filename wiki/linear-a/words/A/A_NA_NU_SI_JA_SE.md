### Note

HT Zb 159, painted on a pithos and broken after SE: the owner's name with the vessel-label ending -SI-JA-SE. Ugarit Ḫananu (Kinlaw 1967, 55); Amorite Ḥanan- (Gelb 1980, nos. 475–478). Rival: ʿAnānu 'cloud'.
