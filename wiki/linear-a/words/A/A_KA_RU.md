### Note

Linear A writes A-KA-RU, hence Agaru; Ugaritic supplies Ugaru (ugr II), 'Field,' paired with Gapnu ('Vine') as one of Baal's two messenger-deities (DULAT 27; KTU 1.3; 1.5). A-KA-RU heads accounts at HT2 and HT86a/b as a field or estate heading; on HT86a its section stands beside one headed A-DU (Addu), so a divine or cult-institutional sense remains possible. Initial a/u remains unexplained. Akkadian ugāru is usually derived from Sumerian A.GÀR, so the term may be a Wanderwort rather than an inherited ʾ-g-r root.
