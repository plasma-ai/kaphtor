## Unread

Status **edge overlap** (a lexicon word sits at one edge; often a chance 2-sign overlap); role personal name.

IO Za &lt;1&gt;, a limestone votive ladle from the Juktas summit sanctuary, the inscription painted inside it, now lost and never seen by GORILA. Evans found it damaged by fire, with two legible signs and traces of a third (PM I 623–624, fig. 461). Younger prints A-JE-SA with SA marked doubtful (Younger, Linear A Texts). The third sign rests on traces. Not readable.
