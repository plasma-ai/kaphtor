### Note

HT 28a: A-SI-JA-KA heads a list of grain and oil, GRA+QE 5, OLE+U 2, OLE+KI ½, OLE+MI 1, OLE+TU, VIN 3, where a flour category fits a commodity allocation; HT 28b writes it again.
