### Note

Skeleton syk against šwq: š is written s and ḳ is written k. HT28a context is decisive: A-SI-JA-KA heads a list with GRA+QE 5, OLE+U 2, OLE+KI ½, OLE+MI 1, OLE+TU, VIN 3—mixed grain and oil commodities. A flour category in a commodity allocation tablet is semantically coherent. Arabic sawīq (parched barley meal) preserves the ancient trade commodity.
