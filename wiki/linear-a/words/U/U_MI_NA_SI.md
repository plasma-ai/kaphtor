### Note

Sentence name: ʾummī 'my mother' (geminate mm spelled with a single sign) + naśiʾ 'chief, exalted one' (√n-ś-ʾ 'lift'; the title independently PROBABLE at \*301-SI). Both attestations stand in the header, outside the count: on HT117a (MA-KA-RI-TE KI-RO U-MI-NA-SI) the roster's ten qty-1 entries sum exactly to KU-RO 10 with U-MI-NA-SI excluded—the responsible party, not a counted worker; on HT28b it stands in the header pair with A-SI-JA-KA before the commodity block. U- is radical, not the proclitic 'and': bare MI-NA-SI and DI-MI are unattested anywhere in the corpus, and the tablet's other U- words resolve as radical—U-SU (ʾUšu, mainland Tyre) and U-DI-MI (ʾUdumī, man of Udum), both Amarna-anchored origin labels—leaving no conjunctive u- on HT117a. The mother-element is attested in Amorite feminine names (Ummum-ka-abī 'the mother is like my father'); a mother-exalting name suits a goddess-primary administration. ALTERNATIVE (superseded reading): u-Minasī 'and-Minasī'—MI-NA-SI sign-for-sign = Neo-Assyrian Mi-na-si-i (Manasseh), √m-n-n with mnn→mnš dissimilation, beside MI-NA-NE (CONFIRMED); requires the conjunction, the dissimilation, and an eight-century reach to the Neo-Assyrian match—retained as the recorded rival. \|\| The Ugaritic mnn → mnš pair is not attested in the Copenhagen Ugaritic Corpus or DULAT; the Ugaritic tribute word is mnḥ (KTU 3.8:21, 3.10:1; mnḥy 'offering' KTU 1.2 I 38). The dissimilation support is withdrawn.

### Cognates

Ugaritic ʾum "mother", našaʾa "to lift, raise"; Hebrew nāśīʾ (Gen 23:6 nəśîʾ ʾĕlōhīm); Amorite mother-element in Ummum-ka-abī (Gelb AS 21 nos. 6246–7, FN) and Našiʾ name element (NIQM-I·LA·NAŠIʾ, no. 5051); = \*301-SI nāśī (PROBABLE)
