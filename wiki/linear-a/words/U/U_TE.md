## Unread

Status **unread** (nothing known).

VRY Zb 3, a kyathos fragment from the Vrysinas sanctuary (Rethymnon Museum Π 24605) with two incised signs. The report gives the editors' readings \]AB 37-04\[ or \]AB 10-04\[, that is \]TI-TE\[ or \]U-TE\[, the first sign fragmentary, and records that they do not exclude Cretan Hieroglyphic (Del Freo, Rapport 2016–2021, 107–108). RILA-S1 lists it among its doubtful inscriptions (D 18). Not readable.
