### Note

PL Zf 1, a silver pin from beside Platanos Tholos A: \]TA · WI-TE-JA-MU · U-QE-TI · JA-SA-SA-RA-ME (GORILA IV 161). Feminine participle of √w-q-d 'to burn': U writes w-, as in U-NA-KA-NA-SI wannā; QE the qe of Heb. yōqedet; the d before t unwritten; TI the feminine -t. Initial w- where Hebrew and Aramaic have y-. Rival: U- + QE-TI, the element of HT 7a, HT 58 and PO Zc 1. Weak point: one attestation.
