### Note

HT 103 .1 U-TA₂ · NI 40, the opening group, before the divider and the fig entry; DA-KU-SE-NE, \*188, DA-KU-NA and KI-RA follow. The heading slot fits an entity, institution, recipient or place named over the figs. Uta is a Neo-Assyrian personal name, ᵐu₂-ta-a (SAA 6 277). Rival: Uda, an Anatolian town near Firaktin, named at Ugarit (dIŠKUR ᵘʳᵘU-da, RS 17.338+; RGTC 12/2, 304), Hittite Uta. One attestation.
