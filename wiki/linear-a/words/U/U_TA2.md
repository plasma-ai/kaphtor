### Note

HT 103 .1 U-TA₂ · NI 40, the opening group, before the divider and the fig entry; DA-KU-SE-NE, \*188, DA-KU-NA and KI-RA follow. The heading slot fits an entity, institution, recipient or place named over the figs.

ʿŪta, a short name: Heb. ʿÛtay (עוּתַי), 'man's name, short form', of two men (Ezra 8:14; 1 Chr 9:4), from the root ʿwt, the Aramaic form of Heb. ʿwš 'to help', with the Nabataean name ʿwtw; HALOT calls the derivation uncertain and gives Arabic ʿtw 'to be proud, surpass' beside it (804). U writes ʿū, the ʿayin unwritten, and TA₂ gives final -ta as in KI-RE-TA₂. Neo-Assyrian ᵐu₂-ta-a (SAA 6 277) has the same shape.

Rivals: Hurrian Utte, ut-te, at Ašnakkum (Talon 1997, 137, apud Monti 2017, 376); Uda, an Anatolian town near Firaktin, named at Ugarit (dIŠKUR ᵘʳᵘU-da, RS 17.338+; RGTC 12/2, 304), Hittite Uta. One attestation.
