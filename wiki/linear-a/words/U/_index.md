[[wiki/linear-a/words/U/U_KA_RE|U_KA_RE]]: ʾugār: the field (of), meadowland (heading of a grain-ration list); rivals ʾikkār 'ploughman', the personal name Ugār. Lexicon, SPECULATIVE (tier 1).
