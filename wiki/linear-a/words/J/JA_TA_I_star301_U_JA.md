### Note

AP Za 1, a stone vessel from Apodoulou: the only word on the fragment, whole, then a divider and the break. The formula verb A-TA-I-\*301-WA-JA in the third person, whose reading and cognates it shares: JA-TA- for A-TA- is the 3ms prefix ya- where the formula has the 1cs ʾa-, and -U-JA stands for the formula's -WA-JA. See A-TA-I-\*301-WA-JA.
