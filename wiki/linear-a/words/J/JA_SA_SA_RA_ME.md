### Note

√y-š-r 'to be straight, upright, righteous.' SA writes /š/ (cf. SA-SA-ME HT23a = sesame: LB sa-sa-ma, Ug. ššmn); SA-SA writes the geminate /šš/ (di Mino, Ya Diktu), not a shin marker; the same root takes one SA, with no gemination written, in JA-SA-RA (KNZc7) and I-MI-SA-RA (HT27a). Vowels: Amorite JAŠAR-AM, IA-SA-RA-AM (Gelb 1980, no. 3448), base \*yašar-. Spelling encodes accusative -am: RA = /ra/ (not RU = /ru/ for nominative -um), ME = mimation. Primary reading: accusative \*yaššaram\* 'uprightly'; the long vowel of earlier \*yašāram\* is dropped for want of independent support. Amorite IA-SA-RA-AM is classed accusative (Gelb 1980, no. 3448). Davis and Thomas's 'offering' is a syntactic rival (object slot), not a lexical value. Davis 2026 functional gloss: "an offering/dedication" (in Salgarella & Petrakis eds., The Wor(l)ds of Linear A, pp. 43-60). Huehnergard: /yušru/ "uprightness" (yuCCu nominal pattern); confirms adverbial accusative with -m. PL Zf 1 preserves JA-SA-SA-RA followed only by a damaged vertical trace; \[ME?\] is a probable formulaic restoration, not an independent full attestation, and NE cannot be excluded. IO Za 2 preserves JA-SA-SA-RA-\[, ME restored by GORILA (V 19), and IO Za 9 only \]JA-SA-SA-, RA-ME restored (V 33). PK Za 14 ends JA-SA-SA-\[ at the broken edge (IV 40), probably the same word. Intact on IO Za 6 (V 25), PS Za 2 (IV 54) and TL Za 1 (IV 59). IO Za 12 has it whole on one line, second SA and ME dotted (Karetsou, Godart & Olivier, Kadmos 24, 1985, 143).
