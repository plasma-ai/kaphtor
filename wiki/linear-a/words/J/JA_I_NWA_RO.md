### Note

Hapax, SYZa4 word 2 (deity or recipient slot after the formula verb). RILA-S1 p. 84 reads JA-I-NWA-RO with RO underdotted, and rates GORILA's JA-I-NWA-ZA 'moins probable' (cf. SY Za 11; for ZA cf. KN Zf 31). Sign 3 is NWA (AB48) in both RILA-S1 lines, its only occurrence in the corpus; the value nwa is inherited from Linear B B48 (pe-ru-si-nwa ~ pe-ru-si-nu-wa). JA = vocative ya, as in JA-DI-KI-TU. I- writes the aleph, here ʾa (cf. I- for ʾu in I-KU-JU-TI); NWA writes the cluster /nw/; -RO writes final /-ru/. ʾanwaru is the ʾaqtal elative of √n-w-r: Arb. 'hāḏā ʾanwaru min ḏāka' (al-Zamakhsharī, Asās al-Balāgha, s.v. nwr). The epithet stands before PA₃-NI-WI 'the Builder', as Ugaritic pairs El's titles ṯr il dpid // bny bnwt 'Builder of Built (Ones)' (KTU 1.4 III 30–32; Smith & Pitard 2009, 39, 447). Same root as NU-RO nūr; Akkadian nawir 'is bright' of a god in OB names (Ilšu-nawir, Nawir-bēlī). Rival: ʾaṣwaru 'Fashioner' (√ṣ-w-r; Q 59:24 al-muṣawwir beside al-bāriʾ), which needs AB48 = a sibilant + w sign. The ya-inwā-ṣa reading stays withdrawn: it needed ZA. GORILA form JA-I-NWA-ZA.
