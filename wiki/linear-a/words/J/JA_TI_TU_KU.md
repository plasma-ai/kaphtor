## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

LA Zb 1 (bis), a pithos fragment from Larani (Metaxas collection), the group written twice, each time across a damaged area (GORILA IV 86–87). The copie tabulaire dots TI in both groups and the copie normalisée does not. Younger marks TI doubtful in both and notes that the first group sets TI-KU above TU, so that it reads JA-TI-KU-TU or JA-TI-TU-KU (Younger, Linear A Texts). Not readable.
