### Note

Component morpheme, broken out on its second compound attestation: within SI-JA-SE = šī-yāṣē "which goes out" (HTZb159, PEZb3—disbursement marker on jar labels) and now bare within JA-PA-RA-JA-SE (SYZa9—the libation as "that which goes out" of the vessel). Same two signs, same reading, two genres: what administration marks as outgoing goods, liturgy pours as the outgoing libation. Cf. Hebrew אֲשֶׁר־יֵצֵא (2 Kgs 12:13, Temple expenditure) for the administrative use; the cultic use has no direct biblical parallel—the poured offering as grammatical subject is Minoan.

### Cognates

Hebrew יָצָא (yāṣāʾ) "go out", participle יֹצֵא (yōṣē); Ugaritic √yṣʾ "go out, appear"; Akkadian (w)aṣû "go out", ṣītu "expenditure, outflow"
