### Note

PH Wc 39, an MM II roundel from Vano LI, JA-DI on face a, RO rōš on face b, whole and undotted (GORILA II 94). ya-di sign for sign as the Amorite name IA-DI-IM (Gelb 1980, no. 3205), mimation unwritten, \*yad- 'hand'. Rivals: Yadīn 'he judges' (Gelb no. 3206 IA-DI-IN-), Yadiʿ 'he knows', each with a final consonant unwritten. Not JA-DI-KI-TU, which divides JA \| DI-KI-TU. One witness.
