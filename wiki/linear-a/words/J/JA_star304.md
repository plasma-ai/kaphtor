## Unread

Status **unread** (nothing known).

PH 14a \]NỊ \*304 JA⟦JA⟧ as printed left to right, NI dotted at the left break, JA and \*304 whole and undotted, an erased JA beside JA (GORILA I 305). Younger reads the face retrograde as \]JA-\*304 followed by FIC, JA written over an erased JA (Younger, Linear A Texts, other texts). GORILA V indexes \*304 on PH 14a as an isolated sign and leaves grouping and direction open (GORILA V 138, 287). Sole attestation. No reading.
