GORILA IV 28–29: \] JẠ-Ụ-P̣A-ṂA-Ị-DA \[·\] DI\[, one string with no divider, five of six signs dotted, broken before JA, sign 4 drawn as MA and as a triangle, one lost sign after DA, line 1 vestiges only. If the signs hold, PA-MA-I-DA = bamat ʾIdā 'high place of Ida' (PS \*bVmVt-, Ug. bmt, Heb. bāmâ), Ida word-final only if the lost sign after DA opens a new word (cf. PK Za 17, PK Za 18). JA-U is either JA ending a word lost at the break with U = u- 'and', or vocative yā + u-, which is unparalleled. Not readable.
