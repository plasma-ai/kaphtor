### Note

SY Za 9, Syme, HM 5585, found 1987 (Muhly and Olivier 2008, 212–13, photo and drawing). Olivier prints one group, 57-03-60-57-09; the division JA-PA-RA + JA-SE is di Mino's. JA-PA-RA yapʾar(a), 3 m. sg. ya- prefix of √p-ʾ-r 'glorify, adorn', the ʾ unwritten as in JA-PA-QA yapqaḥ (PK Za 11, PK Za 12); final -A the volitive yaqtula or the echo vowel. JA-SE yāṣē, as in SI-JA-SE (HT Zb 159, PE Zb 3): 'may that which goes out glorify (her)', the outpoured libation as subject.

The verb belongs at the altar. 11Q5 XVIII 7–10 (Ps 154): ואדם מפאר עליון ירצה כמגיש מנחה … כמדשן מזבח 'a man who glorifies ʿElyon is accepted as one who brings a minḥa … as one who fattens the altar'. Ben Sira 50:20 (MS B), ובשם ייי התפאר 'and in the name of YYY he gloried', closes the high priest's rite after the wine poured at the foot of the altar (50:15). The deity as subject: Ps 149:4 יְפָאֵר, Sir 45:8 ויפארהו.

Weak points: sign 3 RA, with DA allowed (SY Za 9\*); sign 5 SE only by the form in SY Za 6; √p-ʾ-r is Hebrew alone, in piʿel and hitpaʿel; one attestation.
