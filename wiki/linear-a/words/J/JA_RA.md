### Note

KN Wc 30, an MM IIIB roundel, JA-RA its only word. Yaraḫ with ḫ unwritten: the moon god's name, borne by men as Amorite JARAḪ-T-UM (Gelb 1980, no. 3419). Rival: yarē 'he instructs', √y-r-h. One witness.
