## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

On the Akrotiri jug THE Zb 5 JE stands by itself, incised before firing, with nothing to read it by.

KH 1 is no second token: the edition prints \]JE · GRA+F 70\[, the tablet broken at the left before JE (GORILA III 23), so the sign ends a word whose beginning is lost. Khania has two more words ending in JE, RI-TA-JE before CYP+D on KH 6 and \]-JE 1 on KH 50.
