### Note

ARKHZf9 (Arkhanes, bronze votive object). Word 1 of 2-word inscription JA-KI-SI-KI-NU &lt;&gt; MI-DA-RU-RA. Morphological parse: ya- (3ms imperfect prefix) + √k-s-y 'to cover' (III-weak, G-stem imperfect \*yaksī) + -kin (2fp object suffix, addressing a group of women). Full form: \*yaksīkin 'May he cover you (women)!' Not 2fs -kī + energic -nū: an energic precedes an object suffix (Ar. la-ʾuḥarriqanna-kum, Fischer 2002, §198; Heb. -ennû). Read as -kī + 1cp 'us', the 2nd person would precede the 1st, against Fischer 2002, §271; kasā's second object is the covering, MI-DA-RU-RA. Biblical parallel: Jonah 3:6 וַיְכַ֣ס שַׂ֔ק וַיֵּ֖שֶׁב עַל־הָאֵֽפֶר—√k-s-y + ashes in penitential supplication before God. Lam 3:16 הִכְפִּישַׁנִי בָּאֵפֶר—'He covered me with ashes' (√k-p-š, different root, exact semantic match). Jonah 3:8 וְיִתְכַּסּ֣וּ שַׂקִּ֗ים—hitpaʿel (tG-stem) \*yitkassū "let them cover themselves," same root in reflexive ritual covering. Ugaritic ksy classified under 'cult' domain confirms ritual connotation in NWS.
