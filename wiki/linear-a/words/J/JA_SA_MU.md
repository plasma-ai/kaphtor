### Note

Zakros wine allocation list (ZA15a, stone vessel). JA-SA-MU receives 5 units alongside SI-PI-KI and SA-MI-DA-E (both read as Semitic). Proto-NWS sibilant merger (SA writes both /s/ and /š/) leaves the reading ambiguous: \*yasamu\* (with sāmek) or \*yašmuʿ\* (with šīn + ʿayin dropping). √š-m-ʿ "to hear" is the strongest parse—most productive NWS theophoric root in this shape, and the guttural-dropping rule accounts for the missing final radical. The Ishmael parallel is direct: Yišmāʿēl = yišmaʿ + ʾēl "El hears"; JA-SA-MU is the bare verbal element without the deity complement—a standalone sentence name "he (the god) hears." This is the first Linear A attestation of √š-m-ʿ, one of the most productive NWS theophoric roots. Secondary parse: Ya-ʾAšmu, Eshmun hypocoristic (ʾŠMN → ʾŠM- short form)—less likely because ya- prefix is not standard for Punic ʾŠMN short forms (which use ʾŠM-, not \*YŠM-). Huehnergard: infinitive replacing imperative /šamāʔu/ "hear!"; alternative parsing as jussive or infinitive-as-imperative. \|\| The form fits the yaqtal theme-vowel class with frozen nominative \*-ū, the citation form of an independent name (APNM pp. 104–15). Guttural-final root: cuneiform must write it (Ya-ás-ma-aḫ-{d}IM), Linear A must drop it—JA-SA-MU is the predicted Linear A shape of the Yašmaʿ hypocoristicon, APNM p. 132 Ya-ás-ma-ḫu-u\[m\]. Refines the earlier yaqtul parse (yašmuʿ); both are G imperfects with identical Linear A surface. Fourth data point for the Yašmaʿ type at Zakros, independent of WI-JA-SU-MA-TI-TI/yašmaṭu (HTZd157+156).
