### Note

KN Wc 26, a roundel in a private collection, its Knossos findspot queried: NI-PI on face b, opposite RU-JA. NI may be the fig logogram. No reading.

## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

KN Wc 26, a roundel (CMS X 120), RU-JA on face a and NI-PI on face b, one disc impression (GORILA II 85). Intact and undotted. The roundel is in the Søyen Collection, formerly Erlenmeyer, and Younger enters face b in the logogram column (Younger, Linear A Texts); its Knossos findspot is queried, and its NI and PI are early forms (Ferrara, Montecchi and Valério 2022, 103–4). NI alone is the fig logogram, but the function of NI-PI is unestablished. No reading.
