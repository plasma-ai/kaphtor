### Note

HT 97a .4 NA-TI 4, both signs whole and undotted, between DI 4 and MA-DI 5 (GORILA I 159), under KA-RU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+KA 82, men counted by place: KA-NU-TI 25, PA-I-TO 6. naṭʿ 'plantation', the I a dead vowel and the ʿayin unwritten: Hebrew neṭaʿ 'plantation' (Isa 5:7), and the town Nəṭāʿîm 'Plantations' in the Shephelah (1 Chr 4:23; HALOT 694), a place as Phaistos beside it is. Ugaritic mṭʿt 'plantation', beside the threshing floors (KTU 1.20 II 7; DULAT 594). With a full vowel, naṭīʿ: Hebrew \*nāṭîaʿ 'growing plant' (Ps 144:12; HALOT 694). One witness. See KA-RU.
