### Note

Standalone NA occurs in ritual, commodity-adjacent, and administrative settings, so its functions must be distinguished contextually. KN Za 10 is not a standalone witness: GORILA IV, 8–9 reads JA-SA-SA-RA-MA-NA as one word across the side break, with the divider after NA. IO Za 11 has NA only at the head of \]NA-\[·\]-DA-DA\[ (Karetsou, Godart & Olivier, Kadmos 24, 1985, 142). The secure ritual controls are PHWc40 (TI-TI-MA · NA) and KH97a (RE-A-JA · NA).

### Cognates

| Language | Comparison |
|---|---|
| Hebrew | נָא nā—precative/entreaty particle, ~400× in Hebrew Bible. Used with imperatives (Gen 18:3 nā ʾal taʿăbōr 'please do not pass by'), jussives (Num 12:13 ʾēl nā rəpā nā lāh 'O God, please heal her'), and cohortatives (Gen 18:21 ʾērdā-nnā 'let me go down') |
| Ugaritic | Possible cognate in Ugaritic emphatic particle n/nn, function debated (de Moor, Pardee) |
| Arabic | Possible reflex in Arabic ʾinnā 'indeed, verily' (weakened assertive &lt; precative) |
