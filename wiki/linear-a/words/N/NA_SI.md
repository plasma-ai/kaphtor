### Note

APZa2 line 1 begins at a broken edge with \]NA-SI and preserves a divider after SI. The sequence could be complete, but its beginning and syntactic role are unknown. Comparison with Semitic nasīʾ 'chief, prince' remains possible; it cannot be translated as the subject of a reconstructed offering clause. KH7a attests the distinct form E-NA-SI, not an independent NA-SI witness.
