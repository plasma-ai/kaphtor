### Note

Commodity identity CONFIRMED on the footing of GRA/OLE/VIN—four independent lines: (A) LB ideogram continuity with the su-za/σῦκον anchor on KN F 841—LA→LB commodity-logogram continuity (OLE, VIN, GRA, VIR, OLIV) is foundational, and NI shifting meaning between the scripts would be a singular exception; (B) acrophony—/ni/ abbreviates \*nikuleon exactly, while the honey candidates fail outright (\*nūb(at)- predicts NU, \*nōpet predicts NO); (C) metrology—NI carries the full small-fraction food-measure series, 20 NI+fraction co-occurrences glyph-verified (½ ⅓ ¼ ⅕ ⅙ ⅒ 1/16 1/24; HT30 NI 7+⅙, HT94a NI 3+1/16, KH8 NI ½ (erased)), matching the LB Fs practice of fraction-measured figs; (D) botanical pictograph (fig leaf/plant, CH 024 ancestor, AB30). 66 corpus records carry the NI token (HT×35, KH×22, KN×3, PH×2, ZA×2, CR×1, Tel Haror×1); 75 standalone occurrences, 66% numeric, neighbors VIN×12, CYP×8, GRA×4 \|\| Not nūb(at) 'honey' (√n-w-b bee→honey metonymy): the HT114a quartet is non-discriminating (the LB comparandum has NI = figs); the HT51a NI ≠ TI-NI argument is circular (TI-NI is itself an unconfirmed sibling hapax); 'figs counted whole' is factually wrong for the Aegean (LB figs take fractional T/V/Z units throughout Fs); 'no honey logogram otherwise' is refuted by DI = dibšu and by LB, which spells honey syllabically (me-ri, KN Gg 702); the companion fig signs (TI, TI-NI, TE-\*301) cannot corroborate it. No nūb 'bee/honey' attestation in Krahmalkov or Biella—the metonymy lacks NWS epigraphic support. \|\| Re-open only on a publication-grade re-analysis of the LB NI/Fs series, or an LA text spelling the NI-commodity syllabically as a honey word.
