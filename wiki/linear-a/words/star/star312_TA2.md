### Note

ZA 6b \*312-TA₂ GRA+PA 40, intact, after VIN+RA 40 and VIN+KA 10 (GORILA III 159): \*312-TA of HT 10b with TA₂ for TA, a pair Davis sets side by side (2026b, 77 and n. 284). The house draws grain 40, as I-KU-JU-TI-\*28B draws grain and wine above it. Zakros Scribe 1 writes TA₂ and ZA on this tablet (Davis 2026b, 158, Table 3.58). One attestation.
