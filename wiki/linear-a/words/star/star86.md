### Note

Uchitel takes the sign for a schematic ship, heading shipments (2016b, 9). The drawings show a long curved hull with raised ends and a structure on it (HT 94a, HT 94b, HT 26b), reduced to nested arcs on the Khania nodules; the sign of HT 11b, with a high hump and a detached arch, is the least like the rest. Counted with men: HT 27a VIR+\[?\] 90, 50, \*86 51 under KU-RO 335; HT 94a KA-PA VIR+\[?\] 62, \*86 20, with TA 4, under KU-RO 110. A heading: HT 94b \*86 · over two names, 1 each; HT 45a \*86 · \*305 2 beside \*318 · \*305 2; HT 11b \*86 over KA 40, 30, 50, 30, 30, KU-RO 180. Joined to the terebinth sign, \*86+\*188, on ten Khania roundels and HT 8b. The ship covers all three uses: its men, its account, its cargo. Ugarit counts men by ship, ṣbủ ảnyt 'crew' (KTU 4.40), written ÉRIN.MEŠ GIŠ.MÁ in the Akkadian of Ugarit (DULAT 82–83). Rival: naʿaru 'lads, pages' by its first syllable, with \*86 at na; it fits the head-counts and leaves the terebinth ligature unexplained. Inside a word the sign is a syllable (\*86-SI-NI, HT 140). See TA.
