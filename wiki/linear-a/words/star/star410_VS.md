## Unread sign

A vessel. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

HT 31 .1, the first vessel of the list: a bowl on three legs with two loops standing on the rim (GORILA V 313). The ivory ring KN Zg 57 has a tripod among the vessels of face A, one of the two vase signs there with no syllabogram above it; the editors point to HT 31 for the shape (Kanta, Nakassis, Palaima and Perna 2024, 38). Linear B \*201, ti-ri-po 'tripod', has the same bowl, three legs and two loops on the rim (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 324–325); \*409-VS has the legs with no handles and \*411-VS two bars at the sides (GORILA V 313).
