## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads \*417.

GORILA A 663, 417VAS 'L2' (GORILA V 315, 322): PH 8a .1, counted 7, the hatched vessel of KH Wc 2006 and 2007 with the fraction sign L2 beneath it, as \*418+L2 has on .3. The Phaistos drawing is broader than the Khania one, with a flat bottom and parallel strokes for the lattice (GORILA I 296; III 116).
