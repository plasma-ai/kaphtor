### Note

The he-goat, counted in whole numbers and fractions: HT 64 .4 \*22M 6, KH 6 .5 \*22M 1, HT 20 .3 \*22M ⅛; the roundel KN Wc 29 bears it alone. PH(?) 31b has \]-DU-RI \*22M 1 and \]NE \*22M 1, each after a name, on a tablet that counts \*22F, \*21F and \*21M the same way. GORILA lists sign 22m on these five and, erased, on HT 6b (V 178); Younger reads CAPm on the four. Linear B CAPᵐ. Cf. Heb. tayiš 'billy goat' (HALOT 1729). Five tablets, three sites.
