## Unread sign

A sign. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

KH 100 .1 \*352 \*306\[, under a lost upper edge. Its editors, who publish the tablet as KH 99, read the sign tentatively: the curved crossing strokes could be a variant of DE, unusually large, or A 352, 'found once on the roundel KH Wc 2100'; the second sign is incomplete too (Andreadaki-Vlasaki and Hallager 2007, 12). They take \*352 for a syllable in both places (2007, 17). On KH Wc 2100 it stands inside VIR+\[?\]-\*352-JA.
