## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

GORILA A 657, 407VAS+08 (GORILA V 312): HT 39 .5, after KU-RO and counted 100, a broad shallow vessel with a raised loop at each end of the rim and A above its middle. Nearest among the Linear B vessels is \*200, pi-je-ra₃ 'boiling-pan', 'a large shallow vessel … provided with high-swung handles' (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 324–325).
