### Note

An animal's head in profile, two projections above and a muzzle, with L2 beneath: counted 1 on PH 8a, set after \]-PU₂-RE-\[ on the stone vessel PK Za 28, and listed after four aromatic oils on KN Zg 57a. Salgarella asks 'CH 012 = A 418VAS = LB 227VAS?', the Linear B sign being 'the 'bull rhyton vessel', although the similarity is not entirely straightforward or unproblematic' (2020, 334–35). Knossos counts bull's head rhytons on K 872 (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 330). With a fraction beneath, a vase sign stands for 'a commodity contained or measured in it' (Schoep 2002, 127): a rhyton's measure. Cf. Akk. qarnu 'horn as container, rhyton' (CAD Q 134). Weak point: the species is not fixed, and the sign of PK Za 28 is not securely that of PH 8a.
