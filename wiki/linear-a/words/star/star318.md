### Note

A heading before counts of people at Haghia Triada: HT 45a \*318 · \*305 2, under \*86 · \*305 2; HT 45b \*318 · \*305 7; HT 94a \*318-\*306 11, with \*306 in the place of \*305; HT 126a DA-NA-SI · \*318 · over names counted 1 each. The sign is a flat top on crossed legs. Uchitel takes it for a table and the people for cooks (Uchitel 2016b, 10); for Schoep it is a transaction sign used with people (Schoep 2002, 141–142). The table as the household's board: Ugarit lists men 'of the table' (KTU 4.13:36), which DULAT glosses 'the king's table' (DULAT 892–93), and the prophets of 1 Kgs 18:19 eat at Jezebel's table. Weak points: four tablets at one site, and the picture rests on four tracings. See \*86, \*305, \*306. Rival: cooks.
