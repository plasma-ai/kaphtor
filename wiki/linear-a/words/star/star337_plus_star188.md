### Note

KH Wc 2056 and 2057, two Khania roundels by one hand: \*188 above a pointed figure with a cross-stroke, a long stroke down and another rising to the right. Uchitel counts both among the uses of \*86 'in connection with various commodities' (2016b, 9), and seal KH 5 stamps both and the ship roundel KH Wc 2062 (Hallager 1995, 93 n. 36). Rival: \*337 is another object, a blade on a shaft, set under \*188; Schoep sets 'A 337 (KH Wc 2056-2057)' beside Linear B '\*242 CAPSus or chariot frame', among signs with 'a faint graphical resemblance only' (2002, 91). Two attestations. See \*86+\*188.
