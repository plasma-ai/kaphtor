### Note

A closed angular outline, upright at the right with a pointed projection to the left: a boot in profile, toe to the left. Alone on KH Wc 2067 and 2068, two Khania roundels of one seal with three impressions each (Papapostolou 1977, 163). Schoep lists it among the Linear A signs that 'may tentatively be identified as a kind of textile (AB 164), boots (A 338) and tripods (A 409VAS)' (2002, 91). Rival: a bare foot, or an angular tool. Weak point: the two roundels are of one seal, a single context.

di Mino; Schoep 2002; Papapostolou 1977
