### Note

Alone on eleven Khania roundels (KH Wc 2036–2045, 2111), one commodity to a roundel. Nosch and Weilhartner take it for a logogram "possibly for textiles", since "a similar sign appears as a textile in Linear B": the cloth logogram \*164, known at Knossos only (2026, 126, 130–131). KN L 520 sets it against wool: LANA 18, \*164 3; LANA 12, \*164 2; LANA 24, \*164 4, six units of wool to the piece on each line. KN L 520 writes pe-re-ke before it, and KN L 698 pe-ko-to. Inside a word the sign is a syllable, read SO in RA-\*164-TI rāšōt 'the choicest' (HT 17, HT 19); \*164-RI-DA on the loom weight THE Zg 15 is unread. No witness spells a cloth word. The roundels draw two forms: a frame with two facing curves inside (KH Wc 2040–2044), the form of HT 19 and the one Nosch and Weilhartner set beside Linear B \*164, which adds a fringe (2026, 121, fig. 6.7f); and a frame with two small compartments at the top (KH Wc 2036–2039, 2045, 2111). HT 17 crosses the frame with a saltire, in the same word as HT 19. The meaning rests on the Linear B sign: the picture alone does not show cloth. SigLA's tracing for KH Wc 2036 is the sign of KH Wc 2046; the photograph and drawing show the frame with two compartments (GORILA III 128). Nosch and Weilhartner count, dotted, KH Wc 2095 as well (2026, 122): a fragment with two strokes, an upright and a stroke turning up to meet it, which fit this sign or that of KH Wc 2046–2048. See WA, MA+RU.
