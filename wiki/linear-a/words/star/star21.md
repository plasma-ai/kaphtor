### Note

The sheep sign AB 21 with no mark of sex, the plain form of \*21F and \*21M. HT 38 counts it, \*21 3, after the pig (1) and before a weighed item and two kinds of cloth. It stands alone on two roundels: KH Wc 2102, with four impressions of the seal that receipts tripod cauldrons and O (GORILA III 143; Papapostolou 1977, 164, who dots his reading of the sign), and PH Wc 44 (GORILA II 96). KN Wc 29 bears the he-goat \*22M the same way. Salgarella's table gives AB 21 as the Linear A logogram behind Linear B OVIS, OVISᶠ and OVISᵐ (2020, 219). The sign's Linear B name is QI; in Linear A the plain form never stands inside a word, and qi is written with \*34. Cf. Akk. kirru, said of sheep, ewes and lambs alike: 2 UDU kir-ru-um, U₈ kir-ru-um, SILA₄ kir-ru-um (CAD K 410–411); Ug. kr 'ram' (DULAT 449); Heb. kar 'young ram' (HALOT 496). Weak points: no witness spells the word; the upper element of the sign sits right of the stem at Phaistos and left of it at Khania. The doubtful AB 21 that stands apart before VIN+TE 120 on THE Zb 13 is not counted here.
