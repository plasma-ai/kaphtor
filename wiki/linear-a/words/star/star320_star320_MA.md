### Note

PH Wc 40, a whole roundel: face a \*320-\*320-MA, face b RO (GORILA II 155). With \*320 the Phaistos form of TI (CANDIDATE), TI-TI-MA = ṯiṯṯīma: Ug. ṯṯm 'sixty', nom. /ṯiṯṯūma/ (Huehnergard 2012, 50), consonant for consonant, the geminate ṯṯ spelled with the doubled sign. The second TI writes the ī of the oblique, ṯṯm ksp 'sixty (shekels of) silver' (CAT 2.26:20–21; Huehnergard 2012, 49), the form Heb. šiššîm keeps. RO on face b is the grade mark, as on PH Wc 39. Salgarella reads one sign-group across the faces, 320-320-MA-RO (2020, 324). Weak points: GORILA and Salgarella keep A 320 apart from TI, and the Phaistos roundels write counts as numerals (PH Wc 41 VIN 1½), no other spelling one out. Rival: a personal name, in the slot JA-DI fills on PH Wc 39. One attestation. Cf. TI-DA-TA, ṯidṯīt- 'sixth', from the same root.
