## Unread sign

A sign. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone before a number in the commodity slot: HT 132 A-SE · \*319 5; HT Zd 156 \*319 2¼ and \*319 3⅜, each figure half again the one before. It closes MA-TI-TI on HT Zd 155 and HT Zd 157. A plain I-beam, a stem with a bar at top and bottom; 'a probable transaction sign' on HT 132 (Steele 2024, 103). No reading.

On HT Zd 156 the first two counts follow a sign GORILA reads as 24, NE, each dotted (IV 133); Schrijver (2014, 14) and Corazza et al. (2021, 3) print \*319 before all four. The series 1, 1½, 2¼, 3⅜, 5¹⁄₁₆ is a sum growing by half each term with the interest joined to the capital (CAD Ṣ 159–160, s.v. ṣibtu A), so \*319 is the thing reckoned at interest, given on the two graffiti and counted 5 to the physician.

Rival: a punctuation sign. GORILA's corrigenda have 'I p.-ê. ponctuation' for the graffiti (V 80), and Salgarella keeps it open for the two word-final tokens (2020, 332). GORILA indexes the sign of HT 132.1 as a logogram (V 297), where a divider precedes it and 5 follows; its list of punctuation signs also holds the fraction signs 707 and 708 (V 325); and the writer of HT Zd 155 parts A-JU from the next word with a short stroke.

Rival: a string held taut between two pegs, a monochord, or a measured 'unit length', each from the sign's form and the graffito alone (Stieglitz 1978, 129–130); HT 132 counts 5 of it to the physician beside 27 ewes.

Proposed: silver. It is lent at interest, ṯlṯm ksp b nṯk 'thirty shekels of silver of interest' (KTU 4.682:12; DULAT 644), and five shekels of it are the physician's fee for setting a bone (Laws of Hammurabi § 221; CAD A/2 346, s.v. asû A), beside A-SE · \*319 5. The proposal rests on that one number; nothing in the sign's form says silver. Copper in the ingot is excluded: the Linear B ingot signs are four-horned (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 51, fig. 10), and none of the marked ingots of Haghia Triada (HT Zf 163–169) carries the sign.
