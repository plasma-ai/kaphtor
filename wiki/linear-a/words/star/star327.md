### Note

HT 97a .1–2 \*327 33 after VIR+KA 82 under KA-RU · \*307+\*307 (foot broken, no KU-RO). HT 119 .1 \*327 34 beside VIR 68; the entries sum to 159 with \*327 against KU-RO 160, so it counts persons (Montecchi 2008, 317: summed with VIR/MUL). Cf. \*327-JU 8 (HT 119 .4–5), 'forse un gruppo sillabico' (Montecchi 2008, 318). A closed rectangle, left upright projecting, crossing interior strokes (GORILA I 159, 203); what it depicts is not determinable. No Linear B counterpart (Montecchi 2024, 172). No sound value.
