### Note

KH 42 is a fragment bearing the ligature alone, broken after it: two signs of one size set side by side (Salgarella 2020, A 637). \*307 dubur 'sanctuary' joins the man sign at Khania, VIR+\*307, and HT 127b totals the two apart, KU-RO \*307 156 and \*301 11 within KU-RO 292: \*301 is a body distinct from the sanctuary, here qualified by it. Cf. nəwēh qodeš 'holy abode' (Exod 15:13; HALOT 678).

Rival: two signs in sequence, \*307 and a word opening with \*301. One attestation. See \*307, \*301.
