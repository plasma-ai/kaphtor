### Note

A bowl on three legs with no handles: alone on four Khania roundels (KH Wc 2010, 2020, 2021, 2025), each a sealed receipt, and counted on the Zominthos tablet ZO 1, \*409-VS 217. The side handles of \*411-VS are the one difference between the two signs; KH Wc 2025 is broken to two legs under a rim. A vase logogram can stand for vessels, their contents or a unit of measurement (Schoep 2002, 127), and 217 on ZO 1 is one of the three. Cf. LB \*201 TRIPOD. See \*411-VS.
