### Note

KH 88 QA-NU-MA · \*21F-\*118 · NI 10, before figs; ZA 5a heads a ration list, \*21F-\*118 · VIN-SU over KI-NI-MA 7 and O-TA-NI-ZA-SE 6; ZA 14 an entry of 1. Ug. ḥṭt 'wheat' (DULAT 372), Heb. ḥiṭṭâ (HALOT 306), the final -t unwritten; \*21F writes ḥ. Rivals: ḫṭ 'sceptre, rod' (DULAT 410; Akk. ḫaṭṭu), and ḥṯ 'unleavened bread' (DULAT 373) with \*118 = ṯa.
