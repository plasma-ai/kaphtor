### Note

HT26a.1: unquantified after the vessel heading \*406VAS+KE, before TA-TI 21 and MI-KI-SE-NA 15. \*312 writes KI, the first syllable of the linen word (TELA+\*312, HT38, KI-TA-NA, PEZb3), as \*307 writes DU in \*307-PU₂-RE, and TE-TE writes the rest, E for I as in U-TA-I-SE ~ U-TA-I-SI. Akk. kitītu at Mari: 1 TÚG ki-ti-tum (ARM 7 90:2), a kitītu garment for a woman (ARM 9 129:1f.), and Malku VI 85 ki-ti-tum = lubuštu "garment" (CAD K 466). TE-TE does not write a geminate, since the doubling in kuttōnet is secondary (HALOT 505).
