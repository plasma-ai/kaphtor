### Note

HT 115b \*306+MI 1, a recipient after \*306-TU-JA 1 and A-I 1. Ligature ʿA+MI: ʿAmmī, the geminate unwritten, the -ī of 'my'; a hypocoristic of the ʿAmmī-DN names, cf. Amorite ʿAMM-IJA (Gelb AS 21 no. 735). One attestation.
