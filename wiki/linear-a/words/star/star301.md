### Note

238 standalone occurrences (82% of all \*301 uses), concentrated at Haghia Triada on nodules and tablets. Appears in the same commodity-label position as GRA (dagān), OLE (šamnu), VIN (wayn), CYP (copper). \*301 is the pastoral commodity sign—the missing category in the Aegean Bronze Age commodity system. Two registers: (1) ADMINISTRATIVE: nawum (with archaic mimation), the Akkadian-parallel land-use category marking goods from uncultivated pastureland—livestock, wool, dairy, as opposed to cultivated cereals (GRA) and orchard products (OLE, OLIV, VIN). The 232 standalone nodules at HT use this sense. (2) RITUAL: nawā 'dwelling, abode'—the sacred-dwelling sense, cognate with Greek ναός 'temple.' Attested in the compound \*301-WA = nawā at Iouktas peak sanctuary. The dual economic/sacred semantics is intrinsic to the root: pastureland and divine dwelling are the same word because pastoral economy and temple economy are the same institution. Syllabic value /na/ is acrophonic (first syllable of nawā(n)), paralleling cuneiform acrophony (É = bītu 'house' → syllabic /bi/). This dual function explains why the scribe uses \*301 rather than plain NA in the formula verb A-TA-I-\*301-WA-JA: the sign carries both phonetic /na/ and semantic resonance with the √NWY root of the verb itself.
