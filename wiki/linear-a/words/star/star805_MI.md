## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

KH Wc 2054 and Wc 2055, roundels: GORILA III 134 prints both as facsimiles only, with no transcription. Each carries one incised figure, a vertical stroke with a zigzag joined to a hooked sign, which Younger reads as the single ligature logogram \*301̣+MỊ (\*605), both components dotted, in the logogram column, with 5̣ and 6 seal impressions (Younger, Linear A Texts). A logogram on a receipt, not a sign group. Not a word.
