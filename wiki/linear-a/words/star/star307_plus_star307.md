### Note

HT 85a .1 A-DU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+\[?\], over a roster of 66 men; HT 97a .1 KA-RU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+KA 82, over another; HT 36 JE-DI, then \*307+\*307 with GRA+QE 44½. The sign is \*307 written twice under one top bar (A 638; GORILA I 131), the only logogram Linear A doubles: GRA+L4+L4 (HT 15) doubles a measure mark. The single sign stands after a goddess or a named house, TI-NI-TA · \*307 (HT 27a) and A-SA-RA₂ · \*307 (HT 89), and in a total, KU-RO \*307 156 (HT 127b); the doubled sign stands after headings that name no god: A-DU adû 'work assignment', KA-RU karru 'pasture', JE-DI yedī 'the Idaean'.

The sanctuaries: one sanctuary under a god's name, the sanctuaries as a body under an assignment, a pasture or a recipient. Egyptian writing doubles a sign for the same sense: jtr.tj 'the two rows of shrines', Egypt's shrines as a whole (Wb 1, 148.1–5), is written with the house sign twice (O1-O1) on 13th-Dynasty stelae (Oxford QC 1109; Hannover 1976.80a) and in New Kingdom copies of Book of the Dead spell 18. Cuneiform doubles a logogram for its plural: KUR.KUR(.MEŠ) mātāti 'the lands' in Rib-Hadda's letter from Byblos (EA 362), in EA 1 and EA 359 and at Ugarit, after Sumerian kur-kur. A logogram does not show how its plural was spoken, so no reading is given.

Rivals: a dual, 'the two sanctuaries', the grammar of the Egyptian form, though nothing at Haghia Triada names a pair; a distributive, 'sanctuary by sanctuary', as Hebrew doubles a noun in yôm yôm 'day after day' (Gen 39:10) and ʾîš ʾîš 'any man' (Lev 15:2), though the rosters list work units, not sanctuaries; a variant of \*307 as a transaction sign for rations or payments (Montecchi 2008, 315–16, 316 n. 30). Three attestations, all at Haghia Triada. See \*307, A-DU \*307+\*307.
