### Note

PH Wc 38, a Phaistos roundel (MM II), the sole word on it; TA is dotted (GORILA II 94). ḥatam 'seal', √ḥ-t-m 'to seal, stamp', on a sealed receipt: Heb. ḥōtām (חותם) 'seal, signet ring' (HALOT 300); Phoenician, Ammonite and Hebrew ḥtm 'seal', the verb 'to seal (up)' a document, and Phoenician ḥtm, a function or profession (DNWSI 413–414); Arb. ḫatama 'he sealed' (Lane 702). Egyptian ḫtm 'seal' is the source of the Semitic word (HALOT 300); Ugaritic has ḫtm as a personal name (DULAT 408), and Akk. ḫatāmu means only 'to muzzle, block' (CAD Ḫ 152). \*314 is ḤA in its other words too: see DU-\*314-RE, KA-\*314-SI, RA-\*314-RA. One attestation.
