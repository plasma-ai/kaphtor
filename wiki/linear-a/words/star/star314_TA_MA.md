### Note

Contingent on \*314 = ḤA. Phaistos roundel (MMII)—sole word on the roundel. √ḥ-t-m 'to seal, stamp': Hebrew ḥōtām (חותם) 'seal, signet ring' (HALOT 300), Arabic ḫatama 'he sealed' (Lane 702); Akkadian ḫatāmu means only 'to muzzle, block' (CAD Ḫ 152). Either the noun 'seal' (labeling the administrative sealing device itself) or the 3ms perfective verb 'he sealed' (performative inscription). A word meaning 'seal' inscribed on a seal—contextually perfect. Pan-Semitic root attested from Old Akkadian through Modern Hebrew; Egyptian ḫtm 'seal, contract' extends the family Afroasiatic-wide. Weaker Egyptian rival ḥtm 'provide, supply' would suit a roundel-as-receipt, but the Semitic root carries the exact pharyngeal ḥ that \*314 encodes. \|\| TA is dotted (GORILA II 94). Rival with \*314 = bu, a PU₂ variant: bṭm 'pistachio' (DULAT 249), Arb. buṭm 'fruit of the terebinth' (Lane 219), a roundel commodity, cf. \*188 BUṬNU.
