KH Wc 2123, roundel found out of context in a Geometric levelling deposit: the editors read &#91;&#91;A 373, PI-KA&#93;&#93;, all three signs erased before the seal impressions, the first an isolated bird-shaped sign they number A 373 and tentatively take for a new sign and an ideogram, set apart from PI-KA (Andreadaki-Vlasaki & Hallager 2007, 13–14, Fig. 5). Del Freo and Zurbach tentatively take A 373 for a variant of the tripod logogram (Del Freo, Rapport 2016–2021, 100). The listed group joins the isolated sign to PI-KA. Not a word.
