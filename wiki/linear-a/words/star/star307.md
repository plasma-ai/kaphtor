### Note

The sanctuary logogram by itself: HT 127b KU-RO \*307 156, the largest section of the total; HT 89 A-SA-RA₂ · \*307 · heads the list; HT 32 opens with it. dubur 'sanctuary': PK Za 8 writes JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-\*307-PU₂-RE where PK Za 15 has -DU-PU₂-RE. Heb. dəbîr 'back room, holy of holies' (HALOT 208) and Egyptian group writing dabīr 'naos, inner sanctuary' (GlOS 1, 140) give the sense; dubur keeps the u-u of the base noun 'back, hind part' (Arabic dubur, Akkadian dubur), the back room its later sense. The sign is drawn like a pole tent and heads the men who serve it (HT 27a TI-NI-TA · \*307; VIR+\*307 at Khania). Doubled, \*307+\*307, it heads the personnel lists HT 85a (A-DU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+\[?\]) and HT 97a (KA-RU · \*307+\*307 · VIR+KA). Rivals: a transaction sign for distributing rations or payments, the doubled form its variant (Montecchi 2008, 315–16); a general qualification of personnel groups (Uchitel 2006, 75–76). See DU-PU₂-RE, TI-NI-TA \*307, VIR+\*307, OLE-\*307.
