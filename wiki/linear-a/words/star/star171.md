## Unread sign

A sign. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

A logogram alone on ZA 6a.1 (Salgarella 2020, 330), among the Linear A logograms with a possible Linear B homomorph, \*171 (Schoep 2002, 131–35, apud Salgarella 2020, 220 n. 119); Nosch and Weilhartner class it among the agricultural commodities (2026, 119). On the Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5 the corpus reads \*171 7 where Nosch and Weilhartner print TE (2026, 131).
