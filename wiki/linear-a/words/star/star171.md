## Unread sign

A sign. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

A logogram alone on ZA 6a.1 (Salgarella 2020, 330), among the Linear A logograms with a possible Linear B homomorph, \*171 (Schoep 2002, 131–35, apud Salgarella 2020, 220 n. 119); Nosch and Weilhartner class it among the agricultural commodities (2026, 119). The sign before 7 on the Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5 is TE, as Nosch and Weilhartner print it (2026, 131), and the drawings part the two: on ZA 6a the sign has a curved top, a short crossbar under it and an oblique foot; on the ostracon it is a stem with a straight top bar and a crossbar close under it, with no foot. \*319 of HT 132 is another sign again: two straight bars and nothing between them.
