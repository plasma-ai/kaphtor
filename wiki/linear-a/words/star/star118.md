### Note

After a commodity and before a numeral: HT 12 MA+RU · \*118 5, after the wool sign, and KN 2 \*118 3 after RU-NA 2. The talent, as Linear B \*118 L (Bartoněk 2003, 126; Killen 2024, 141). HT 24b totals 4½ against a recto wool total restored as 45, the Linear B ratio of 10 wool units to the talent (Del Freo 2020, 56). Inside a word the sign is ṬA. Cf. Heb. kikkār 'talent' (HALOT 473), Ug. kkr 'talent' (DULAT 430–431).
