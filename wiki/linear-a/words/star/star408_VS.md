### Note

A top bar, a body tapering to a point, a curl at each side and three strokes below, the same on KH Wc 2103 and 2122: a vessel with two handles on three legs, alone on four Khania roundels, each a sealed receipt. KH Wc 2084 keeps one corner and KH Wc 2114 the body alone. KH Wc 2084 and 2103 bear one seal, with three and five impressions (Papapostolou 1977, 163–64). The pointed body sets it apart from the round cauldron \*411-VS. Cf. LB \*201 TRIPOD and the 'tripod-amphora' ku-ru-su-pa₃ of KN K 740 (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 329). Rival: a container on a pedestal or with hanging attachments. See \*411-VS.
