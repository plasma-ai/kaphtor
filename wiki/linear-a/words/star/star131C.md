### Note

A wine-class logogram of the AB131 family, beside VIN and \*131B: KH 18 \*131C 1, twice; KN 54 PU-RA / \*131C ¹⁄₁₆; KH 60 with a fraction; KH 85 among oil and aromatics (OLE+TA ½, \*308, \*401+RU ¹⁄₁₆, \*131C, \*304, CYP ⅕). Cf. Heb. ḥōmeṣ 'vinegar' (HALOT 329), Ug. ḥmṣ 'vinegar' (DULAT 359–360). Weak point: the value rests on the sign's place in the VIN family; no context sets vinegar apart from another wine product.
