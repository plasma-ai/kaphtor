### Note

Commodity status is secure; the exact identification remains PROBABLE. It occurs in 29 contexts on 26 documents. Three forms occur on damaged HT123+124b: \*188-\*308 (11), \*188-DU (10), and \*188 (1). Their relation cannot be recovered by simply summing the preserved quantities. \*308 also modifies OLIV and may denote a processed or oil product. \*188 co-occurs with VIN on HT98b, appears on Khania roundels, and occurs in ritual context on PSIZa1. The proposed Pistacia identification compares Hebrew boṭnā (Gen 43:11), Akkadian buṭnu, and Arabic buṭm. PA₃-\*188 is compatible with a /b/-initial reading only if PA₃ is a phonetic complement; it cannot independently prove that value. The proposed CH 009 vessel/container ancestry (Ferrara et al. 2022, Pasiphae XVI) likewise establishes neither the commodity nor its name.
