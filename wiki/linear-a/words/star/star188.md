### Note

HT 123+124b, damaged, has three forms: \*188-\*308 11, \*188 1 and \*188-DU 10, quantities with no recoverable relation. \*308, pressed oil, also follows OLIV on HT 123+124a. \*188 stands with VIN on HT 98b, on the Khania roundels, and as \*188-DU-SE on PSI Za 1, a ritual text. It is counted alone, 13 on HT 103, and owed, KI-RO 400 on HT 15, as Knossos books its terebinth, ki-ta-no, by a-pu-do-si 'delivery' and o-pe-ro 'deficit' (KN Ga 1530, Ga 1532; DMic I, s.v. ki-ta-no).

buṭnu, a Pistacia product: Heb. boṭnîm 'pistachio nuts' (Gen 43:11; HALOT 121), Akk. buṭnu 'terebinth' (CAD B 358), Arb. buṭm 'terebinth, its fruit' (Lane 219). Ebla writes the word bù-da-ma-tim and GIŠ.EŠ₁₁ = buₓ-du-du 'pistachio' (GlOS 1, 131), and has bu₃-du-du as a name (Pagan 1998, row 1278). The pistachio is Akk. buṭumtu, buṭdumtu, Mari bu-du-um-ti (CAD B 359): \*188-DU writes the dental as a phonetic complement. Mari sends the nuts as a gift, 'pears and pistachio nuts of GN' (ARM 4 42:18; CAD B 359), as Jacob sends boṭnîm to Egypt.

\*86, a ship in Uchitel's reading of the sign (2016b, 9), is joined to \*188 on ten Khania roundels, HT 8b and HT 26b: a consignment of it by sea. Rival: Uchitel takes the sign itself for a ship's load, heading the shipment list on the reverse of HT 123 (Uchitel 2016b, 9, 14); a load would not be counted without the ship, owed, or joined to \*308.

Two complements carry the name. PA₃-\*188 (HT 8b) gives the b, unless PA₃ is there the transaction sign it is on HT 34 (Schrijver 2014, 7). \*188-DU gives the dental on a tablet, a nodule, a roundel and the Pseira stone vessel (HT 123+124b, HT Wa 1021, HT Wc 3020, PSI Za 1), and Salgarella lists 188-DU among the sign's groups (Salgarella 2020, 302). Weak point: b and d are written, the middle of the word is not. CH 009 has been set beside A 28b, AB 61, A 123 and \*188 alike (Soldani 2013, apud Ferrara, Montecchi and Valério 2022, 96), and decides nothing. Twenty-eight attestations.
