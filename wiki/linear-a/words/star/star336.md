## Unread sign

A sign. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

The sign also stands whole beside a break on [[wiki/linear-a/inscriptions/KH/KH/KH82|KH82]].

Drawn as an animal's head facing right, two ovals above it and an eye within. KH 14 .2 \*336 2, whole, between two cyperus entries, on a tablet of KH Scribe 1 that counts VIR+\*307 2 on the next line (GORILA III 45). KH 82 .4 \]\*336\[, dotted and broken at both sides (III 97), is not counted.
