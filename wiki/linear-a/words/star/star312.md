### Note

Logogram. TELA+\*312 (HT38) ties it to cloth, and KI-TA-NA 'linen' is spelled out on PEZb3. Standalone with 1–2 units at four sites (HT49a, HT123+124b, KH8, ARKH3a). Inside a word it writes KI, the first syllable of kitnu, as \*307 writes DU in \*307-PU₂-RE: \*312-TE-TE = kitītu (HT26a). \*312-TA (HT10b.2, 6½) is the garment with feminine -t, as in Heb. kuttōnet. ZA6b \*312-TA₂ GRA+PA 40 is an entry receiving grain, set out like I-KU-JU-TI-\*28B GRA+PA 50¼ above it, not a grain heading. MU-\*312 (HT78) stands alone on a fragment.
