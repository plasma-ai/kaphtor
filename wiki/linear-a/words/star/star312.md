### Note

Alone before a numeral on four tablets at three sites: HT 49a \*312 1, KH 8 \*312 1, ARKH 3a \*312 2, HT 123+124b \*312 1¾. With \*312 = BI it is bitt 'a length of cloth', written by its first sign, as inside the cloth sign on HT 38: Ug. bt '"length, piece" (?)' of cloth, from b-t(-t) 'to cut', npṣ bt 'a set of pieces' of crimson cloth (KTU 3.13:16; DULAT 247–248); Heb. bāttîm, the 'woven dress' the women wove for the Asherah, a conjectured reading of 2 Kgs 23:7 (HALOT 166); Arabic batt 'a garment'. A length takes the fraction of HT 123+124b, and the cloth sign itself takes fractions on HT 16 and HT 20, "which could have been used as symbols for weight" (Schoep 2002, 35). On HT 49a, in a list of names each with 1, it is bitt 'the daughter': Ug. bt, written bi-it-ta at Ras Shamra (DULAT 241–243). Linear A has the word twice, spelled out in PI-TA-JA Bittaya and written here by its first sign. BI is the voiced partner of PI, as PA₃ is of PA, and PI-SA shares HT 123+124b with it. Weak point: no other script supports the value, and \*333 is also proposed as BI.

Rival: ZO. Melena finds it "suggestive" that A 312 is the ancestor of Linear B zo, with HT 38's ligature as TELA+ZO (2014, 46 n. 50). On the Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5, ZO 9 stands among single signs taken for abbreviated textile names (Michailidou 1992–1993; Boulotis 1998; Burke 2010, 49, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131), and at Khania each stands as one unit in a list with cyperus at ¼: KH 57 \]ZO 1\[ over \]CYP+\[ \] ¼\[, KH 8 \*312 1 two entries after CYP ¼. GORILA keeps A 312 apart from AB 20. See \*312-TA.
