### Note

A broad-topped triangle tapering down, a lattice inside, a loop or hook at each end of the rim and a stroke under the point: alone on KH Wc 2006 and 2007, two Khania roundels of one seal, with eight and two impressions (Papapostolou 1977, 160). Vandenabeele's suggestions include 'A 417VAS (basket)' (Schoep 2002, 127 n. 138). Schoep files the hatched sign of PH 8a under it, where it is counted 7 with a measure sign beneath: a thing that holds a measure. Cf. Akk. sellu 'basket' (CAD S 217). Rival: a triangular harp, its lattice the strings and the lower stroke a stand.
