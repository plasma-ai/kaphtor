### Note

The ewe, counted beside the ram: PH(?) 31a \*21M 5, \*21F 3 after KU-RO, and PH(?) 31b \*21F 1. HT 132 QA-RE-TO · \*21F 27; ZA 9 sets it after names (\[?\]-MA-JU \*21F 2, WI-RA-RE-MI-TE \*21F 1); ZA 26a \*21F 13; ZA 22 \*21F 1\[. The logogram stands alone before a numeral; inside a word the sign of this number is HA. Linear B OVISᶠ. Cf. Heb. rāḥēl 'ewe' (HALOT 1216). Weak point: no witness spells the word.
