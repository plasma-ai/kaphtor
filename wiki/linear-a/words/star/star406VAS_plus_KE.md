### Note

Vessel ideogram (\*406VAS) with phonetic complement KE, functioning as tablet header on both sides of HT26 (Haghia Triada, Villa Magazine, LMIB). The VAS sign is pictographic—a vessel drawing—probably a compound or ligature component, not a phonetic sign. The +KE complement narrows the generic vessel ideogram to a specific type, paralleling Linear B vessel ideogram + phonetic complement conventions. PS #297 \*kaʾs- "vessel for beer, bowl" (Akk. kāsu, Heb. kōs, Arab. kaʾs): the medial aleph triggers vowel fronting in NWS, yielding \*kēs → KE as initial syllable complement. KA-SI (skeleton k-s) attested on HTWa1027 (Haghia Triada nodule)—same site, same consonantal skeleton, possibly the same word spelled syllabically on a different document type. HT93a has the bare form \*406-VS (without KE complement) alongside DA-RI-DA with quantity 2, confirming the sign functions as a vessel-class commodity marker across multiple tablets. HT26b opens with \*406VAS+KE followed by VIR+\[?\] 9 and named allocations (RO-NI 3, KA-U-ZU-NI 4, KI+MU 12)—a personnel/commodity distribution ledger organized by vessel type.
