### Note

E behaves logographically: eight of ten occurrences precede numerals across Haghia Triada, Knossos, and Zakros, including E 5 and E 3 on HT91. Cross-Semitic \*ḥemʾat- (Hebrew ḥemʾā, Ugaritic ḥmʾt, Akkadian ḫimētu) makes dairy fat a semantic possibility if initial laryngeal loss licenses the abbreviation, but no phonetic complement or commodity equation proves it. The corpus establishes a commodity/category, not 'ghee'.
