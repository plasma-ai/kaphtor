## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

HT Wa 1026 is a nodule with one sign on each of two faces, a hooked sign on α (read E), with damage at its foot, and \*301 on γ, filed by GORILA under nodules with two signs (GORILA II 9). The faces give no reading order. The same page has \*301 alone on a face of Wa 1025, 1029 and 1030, and Wa 1027 KA / SI against Wa 1028 SI / KA. The listed form joins two separate marks into one group. Not a word.
