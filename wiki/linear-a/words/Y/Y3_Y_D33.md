### Note

Egyptian group-writing: y3yd33 \[element 6b, Det: D54 'to go'\]. PS \*wṣʾ → NWS \*yaṣʾa with diagnostic \*w→y shift. Akk waṣû (CAD W 319, preserves \*w-), Ug yṣʾ (DLU 946), Heb yāṣāʾ (HALOT 427). Egyptian d renders Semitic ṣ; 33 (double aleph) renders final ʾ + short a-vowel (group-writing never marks length). STEINER'S OWN ROOT: Steiner 1992 (JNES 51) identifies √y-ṣ-ʾ yaṣʾā 'go out!' in this spell. Volitive parse: yaqtula modal prefix-form in -a (Moran 1960, Orientalia 29; Amarna Canaanite, cognate w/ Arabic subjunctive, ancestral to Heb cohortative ʾeṣʾā). Energic rejected (no nasal sign); fem. perfect rejected (14th c. still -at, cf. ḥanqatu -tu in next word). Keftiu Spell #33 (samānu disease), LMP BM 10059.
