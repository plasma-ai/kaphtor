### Note

A tall outline with one side scooped in and tips at the corners, a spread hide: 'AB 180 is attested at Malia (MA 4, MA 6) and resembles the Linear B logogram for hides attested in the RCT (\*180)' (Schoep 2002, 133); 'AB 180 as oxhide (B \*180)' (Salgarella 2020, 219 n. 116). MA 6 counts 941, 620, 233 and 200. The sign 'appears in ligatures to the fractional signs B (A 602) and L (A 603)' and takes SA inside it twice (Schoep 2002, 133), as Linear B marks its hide: 'the KO within the HIDE sign differentiates it as a kōwos, 'sheepskin' (171 = Un718) from the HIDE + WI (wrinos, 'oxhide')' (Ventris and Chadwick 1956, 49). Cf. Akk. mašku 'skin, hide' (CAD M/1 376). Rival: an ingot or a garment of the same outline. The Phaistos signs filed with it are left out: that of PH 10 has the outline, that of PH 15b is nearer an animal's head.
