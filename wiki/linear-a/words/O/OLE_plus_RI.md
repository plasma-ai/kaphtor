OLE ideogram + RI complement (4 att., Haghia Triada + Khania). Akk riqqu 'aromatic substance' (CAD R 371). RI = /ri/ = initial syllable of riqqu. Co-occurs with OLE+NE and OLE+TU on HT23a (all in fractional quantities ≈⅙).
