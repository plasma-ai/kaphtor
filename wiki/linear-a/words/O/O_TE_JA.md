### Note

PK 1 .5 O-TE-JA 1, alone between rulings, TE and JA dotted, in a roster of names counted 1 each, with the Hurrian names TA₂-TI-TE and TA₂-TA-RE (GORILA I 281). Ḫuteya, the Hurrian name Ḫutiya (Talon 1997, 128; Kryszat 2008, 176), at Ugarit Ḫutiyana (Kinlaw 1967, 60): O for ḫu as A for ḫa in A-RU and A-RO-TE, TE for ti, -JA the hypocoristic -ya. One attestation.
