### Note

Hapax. SigLA catalogs the glyph as the single logogram A623, while GORILA decomposes it as OLE+QE+DI. OLE makes an oil category secure. DI permits comparison with Akkadian dišpu and Hebrew dǝbaš 'honey/syrup,' as in the recurrent OLE+DI subtype, but the precise product is unknown. QE is not independently established here as Akkadian qû 'capacity measure,' so the expansion 'qû-measured honey-oil' does not hold.
