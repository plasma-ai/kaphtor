### Note

OLE ideogram + TA complement (8 att., Haghia Triada + Khania). PS \*ṭayb- 'good, pleasant': Akk ṭābu, Heb ṭōb, Ug ṭb. TA = /ṭa/ via T-class neutralization = initial syllable of ṭābu. Direct Ugaritic administrative parallel: šmn ṭb 'good oil' (KTU 4.14, 4.91, 4.158). Small fractional quantities (⅕, 𐝓) suggest a premium grade distributed in measured amounts. \|\| Huehnergard: Ug ṭyb (ṭb) /ṭaṭib-/ (D-stem) "to make good, return"; D-stem: tttb, ṭtb /ṭaṭib-/ "make good." PS \*ṭayb- "good". BH cognate: ṭōb "good" (&lt; PS \*ṭayb-) (Huehnergard 2012, paradigm section)
