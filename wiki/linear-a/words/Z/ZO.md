## Unread sign

A syllabogram. Status **unread** (nothing on file).

Alone on KH 57.2, apparently before a numeral in a damaged context, probably a logogram (Salgarella 2020, 157, 170, 314); Melena takes AB 20 for the ancestor of Linear B zo (Melena 2014a, 8, apud Salgarella 2020, 170 n. 96). On the Akrotiri ostracon THE Zg 5 ZO is one of the single signs before quantities taken for abbreviations of Minoan textile names (Michailidou 1992–1993; Boulotis 1998; Burke 2010, 49, apud Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 131). Del Freo's phonographic signary of Linear A leaves AB 20 out (Petrakis and Salgarella 2026, 31, Fig. 0.1).
