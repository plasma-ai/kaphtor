## Unread sign

A compound. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/Z/ZU|ZU]], [[wiki/linear-a/words/star/star22F|\*22F]], [[wiki/linear-a/words/D/DI|DI]].

Heads HT 101, before GRA+QE 40 and three oil entries; SA-RA₂ heads the next section. The goat sign \*22F stands between ZU and DI, so no run of two syllabic signs is left. It stands among the names as a personal name of unknown affiliation, and HT 101 is translated with it as an opaque heading. No reading.
