### Note

ARKH 2 .3–4 ZU-\*301-SE-DE-\*21F-\*118 6, read ṯannān ṣēd ḥiṭṭa 'the chariot soldiers: provision of wheat, 6', under SI-DA-TE ṣēdat 'provisions', between A-SI-DA-TO-I 12 and A-SU-PU-WA 4 (GORILA III 6–7). \*301 and \*21F over erasures, no dividers. SE-DE ṣēd, √ṣ-y-d as SI-DA-TE. Ugarit lists the ṯnnm as a group with named members (KTU 4.68:70; 4.126:4), with a rb ṯnnm (4.382:5). ZU writes ṯ before any vowel, as in A-ZU-RA ʾAṯirat. Weak points: the must list turns to wheat without a logogram, and DULAT records doubt over the archer sense. Rival: one name ZU-\*301-SE-DE + ḥiṭṭa 6. One witness.
