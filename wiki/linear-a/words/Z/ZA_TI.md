### Note

Fills the gap Gordon (1966 §156) marked between A-KA-NU and DU-RA-RE. ZA-TI may write /zāt/ (historically perhaps /zāʾt/), with TI exposing final /t/ and a nonphonemic support vowel. Hebrew zō(ʾ)t is the closest lexical parallel. Phoenician ʾGN is feminine, and FK D 35 hʾgn hz 'this bowl' provides an independent noun + postposed demonstrative parallel. Because Semitic places the demonstrative after its noun here, the natural English rendering of A-KA-NU ZA-TI is simply 'this bowl'; 'this very bowl' would add emphasis not established by the form. This reading is preferable to /ḏāti/ 'of': Ugaritic dāti and Arabic ḏāti have a live genitive /i/ and require an agreement frame absent here. Caution: ZA-TI is a hapax, GORILA and SigLA disagree on ZA/ZE, and final TI = /t/ is a proposed Linear A spelling rather than a demonstrated Linear B rule.
