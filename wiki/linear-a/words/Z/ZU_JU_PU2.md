### Note

HT 25a .3–4 TU-QE-NU / ZU-JU-PU₂ 1, the pattern of .4–5 DU-RU-WI / I-KI-RA 1: a name at the line end, then a qualifier and one unit (GORILA I 44–45). ṯu-lu-bu sign for sign: ZU writes ṯu, JU lu, PU₂ bu. Ugaritic ṯlb 'flute' (DULAT 892), from Akk. šulpu (CAD Š/3 256). GORILA's tabular copy dots ZU and PU₂, and the left margin beside ZU is abraded. Rival: the Hurrian name tlby, tu-ul-bi-ia (DULAT 856), which needs ZU = tu. One witness.
