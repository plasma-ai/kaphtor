### Note

IO Za 6 I-NA-TA-I-ZU-DI-SI-KA: ZU-DI is the first member of the compound epithet ZU-DI SI-KA ṯudī-šiqā 'Breast-Who-Gives-Drink', after I-NA-TA-I ʿInatī 'my Anat'; its -ī is the construct vowel binding it to SI-KA. ṯudī 'breast': Ug. ṯd (DULAT 886), Arab. ṯady-, Mehri ṯódi, Harsusi ṯōdi (SED I, no. 280). Ugarit has the nursing at Athirat's breast, ynqm b ảp zd ảṯrt 'who suck at the nipples of Athirat' (KTU 1.23:24), zd an allomorph of ṯd (DULAT 983). ZU-DI-RA occurs on HT Wc 3011–3012. See ZU-DU.
