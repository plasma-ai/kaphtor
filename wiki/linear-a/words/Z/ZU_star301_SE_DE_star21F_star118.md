## Unread

Status **edge overlap** (a lexicon word sits at one edge; often a chance 2-sign overlap); role personal name.

ARKH 2 .3–4 ZU-\*301-SE-DE-\*21F-\*118 6, \*301 and \*21F written over erasures, after A-SI-DA-TO-I 12 and before A-SU-PU-WA 4 (GORILA III 6–7). Undotted. GORILA prints one group, but its last two signs form \*21F-\*118, a group that stands alone at KH 88, ZA 5a and ZA 14, and \*118 stands before the numeral, the position of the unit logogram. ZU-\*301-SE-DE occurs nowhere else. No reading.
