### Note

HT 1 .2–3 DI-DI-ZA-KE 52, the first DI dotted, between ZU-SU 70 and KU-PA₃-NU 109 under QE-RA₂-U, where the other three parties are names (GORILA I 3). Dīdī-zakī 'My beloved is pure': DI-DI the kin term with -ī 'my', ZA-KE zak- 'pure, innocent', a name element at Mari (Huffmon 1965, 186). Amorite sets a kin term before it: ʿAmmī-zakkum, ʿAmmī-zakkuhu (Gelb 1980, nos. 755, 1904). One attestation.
