### Note

The sole word on five Haghia Triada nodules (HT Wa 1001–1005), a commodity label. dāgā 'fish', the feminine collective of dāg, used of fish as food: ʾet-haddāgā ʾăšer-nōʾkal 'the fish we ate' (Num 11:5; HALOT 213). KA writes ga, the velar series not marking voice. Ug. dg 'fish', šbšlt dg 'fish stews' (KTU 1.106:22; DULAT 264–265).
