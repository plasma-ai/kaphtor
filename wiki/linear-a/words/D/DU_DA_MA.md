### Note

HT 6b .4 DU-DA-MA 66, the largest entry in a list of names, on a face written over an erasure; DU-DA-MA itself shows no undertext (GORILA I 13). The Egyptian writing board BM EA 5647 v. I 3a writes {d-j}-d-ȝ-d-ȝ-m{a} for one of two 'Aegean' beneficiaries of a wine account, which Dautais & Gabolde equate with Linear B di-du-me (KN L 588), di-du-mo (KN X 5751, MY Oe 129), a name Meissner counts as Minoan and 'not (so far) attested in Linear A' (Dautais & Gabolde 2025, 14 and Table 1, 15). Consonants d-d-m in three scripts; the vowels differ (LA du-da-ma, Egyptian ȝ-ȝ, LB di-du-me). Dautais & Gabolde read Greek Didyme; no etymology is adopted here. One attestation.

### Source
