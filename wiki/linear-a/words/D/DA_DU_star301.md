### Note

DRA Zg 1 is a five-sign retrograde graffito on a clay reel from Drama-Kajrjaka, Bulgaria: DA-DU-\*301̣-\[?\]-\[?\]. Only the first three signs have been compared with Linear A; the final two remain unread, and RILA-S1 D 24 classifies the inscription as doubtful. DA-DU recurs initially in KN Zf 31 and HT 95. With \*301 = /na/, Amorite dādum/dawdum 'beloved' plus Ugaritic 1cp /-nā/ after the case vowel (cf. /malku-nā/ 'our king') permits /dādu-nā-/ 'our beloved ….' No boundary follows \*301, so the sequence may instead begin a longer name and cannot be treated as a complete prayer or epithet.
