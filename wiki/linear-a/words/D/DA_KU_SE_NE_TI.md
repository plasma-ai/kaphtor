### Note

HT 104 .1–2 DA-KU-SE-NE-TI 45½, the first share under TA-PA · TE+RO, before I-DU-TI 20½ and PA-RO-SU-TI 29; KU-RO 95 is their sum (GORILA I 171). The name is the DA-KU-SE-NE of HT 103, Takušenni. TI stands after each of the three partners and is a sign apart, which the editors allow as an ideogram (GORILA I 171); what it counts is not named.

Rival: Gordon's feminine ending -TI on the name (1966, §§135, 153). See DA-KU-SE-NE, I-DU-TI, PA-RO-SU-TI and TI.
