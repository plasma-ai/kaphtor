### Note

HT 25a .4, at the end of the line after ZU-JU-PU₂ 1, with no numeral. WI is complete at the preserved right edge, and .5 opens with I-KI-RA 1. The scribe carries DI-NA-U's 1 from .2 to .3, so DU-RU-WI-I-KI-RA may be one name. DU = ḏu 'the one of' + RU-WI /lēwī/; U-RE-WI on the same tablet spells the element RE-WI. Niesiołowski-Spanò (2020) argues Levites = Mycenaean ra-wo (λαοί). Rival: ḏurwī, from √ḏ-r-w 'to scatter, winnow' (Arabic ḏarā, Ugaritic dry, Hebrew zārâ), whose vowels fit no participle.
