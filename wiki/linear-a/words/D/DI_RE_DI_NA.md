### Note

HT 98a DI-RE-DI-NA ½, a recipient between TA-NA-TI ¾ and TE-\*301 ¾. ʾaddīr-dīnā 'mighty is the verdict': ʾaddīr as in DI-DE-RU ʾaddīru (√ʾ-d-r), dīn 'verdict', the name element diyn (GlOS 1, 155), as in DI-NA-U Dīnāyu and DI-NA-RO Dānīlu (√d-y-n). Ugarit has the name: ảdrdn stands in three lists of men (KTU 4.147:9, 4.148:3, 4.264:5; DULAT 22), and Watson takes the first element as adr 'noble', leaving the second, adn or d(y)n, uncertain (Watson 1996b, 94); DI-NA writes the i of dīn. The NWS sentence name of predicate adjective and noun, the Ebla type ʾaddīr-X 'X is powerful': ʾaddīr-ʾIl, ʾaddīr-Malik (Pagan 1998, rows 848, 852), Punic ʾDRBʿL, ʾDRMLK (Benz 1972, 262). DI-RE leaves ʾa- unwritten, as DI-DE-RU and Punic BALDIR, BALIDDIR for bʿl ʾdr (Benz 1972, 262). Rivals: ʿ-d-r 'help' as the first element, Heb. Adriel 'God is my help' (HALOT 794); a simplex recipient name. One attestation.
