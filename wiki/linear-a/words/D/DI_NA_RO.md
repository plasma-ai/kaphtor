### Note

HT 108 (Scribe HT 2, LM IB), a grain list that KI-RE-TA-NA 'Cretan' heads: DI-NA-RO 2, then DU-SU-NI 4; the three signs are whole (GORILA I 175). Dīnalu 'El is judgment', dīn 'judgment, verdict' (GlOS 1, 155) with ʾilu. DI-NA writes the first word as Alalakh IV writes it before a god's name, di-na-a-du, Dīn-Addu, beside di-ni-a-du at Alalakh VII (Wiseman 1953, 132; Buck 2018, 327). The god's name follows as -lo, without its ʾi-, which is lost after the vowel of dīna. Ebla writes El so in bil-za-lum beside bil-za-il (GlOS 2, 129) and in da-ni-lum (ARET 15 19 r. 2,18; ARET 20 15 v. 6,29), and Amorite in A-BI-LA, ʾAbī-ʾIla, and IR-ḪA-MI-LA, Yirḥam-ʾIla (Gelb 1980, nos. 107, 3749). The -a of DI-NA is the ending of the first word before the god, as in Amorite LA-PA-NA-I-LA, Labana-ʾIla (Gelb 1980, no. 4313), and in DI-RE-DI-NA ʾaddīr-dīnā. RO writes the nominative -u as -o, as in KU-RO kull(u), KI-RO kilʾu and SA-RO beside SA-RU. Where El keeps its own vowel the corpus writes E, in SA-RE-JU Šarr-ēlu and PI-RU-E-JU Pirʾu-ʾēlu.

The same two words make Lā-dīn-ʾIl on the Euphrates, la-di-in-ìl (Gelb 1980, 291–92), and Ladīn-ʾiluma at Tuttul (Buck 2018, 341). With the participle they make Dānī-ʾilu, da-ni-AN, at Tuttul (Buck 2018, 327), Ugaritic dnỉl, Danel the father of Aqhat (DULAT 274), and Hebrew Dānīʾēl (Ezek 14:14); Ugarit sets the god first in ỉldn (DULAT 52). DI-NA-U Dīnāyu is the short form of a dīn name, and the tablet's heading and this entry carry the names of Kirta and Danel.

Rival: Dānīlu, the participle name, with DI for dā-. Weak point: -lu is written RO here and JU in SA-RE-JU and QA-TI-JU. One attestation. See DI-NA-U, DI-RE-DI-NA and SA-RE-JU.
