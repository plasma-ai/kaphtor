### Note

KH 18, by KH Scribe 1, is broken at both ends of every line. Line 4 reads \]\*131C 1 · KU 22 · SI\[ and line 5 \]DA+\*301 · \*131C 1 · RO+RO\[: the ligature follows the transaction signs KU and SI and stands before one measure of \*131C, a wine product. SI and DA share a run on GO 2r (SI, KA ⅛, SI 2, DA) and on HT 106 (SI 5½, SI 1, DA-\[·\] 8, SI 3), and DA rides the grain sign on HT 133, GRA+DA 55. The two signs differ in size and are set side by side, \*301 on the right (Salgarella 2020, A 503).

damʿ nawā 'the abode's due': DA is damʿu, the sanctuary's share, and \*301 the abode it is owed to. Hebrew demaʿ is 'juice', the due of the vat, beside melēʾâ, that of the harvest: 'your melēʾâ and your demaʿ you shall not delay' (Exod 22:28; HALOT 227, 585), and later Hebrew uses it for terûmâ and the priest's share of the fruit (HALOT 227). On KH 18 the due is a product of the vat: \*131C is of the wine family, and vinegar is ḥōmeṣ yayin 'vinegar of wine' (Num 6:3; HALOT 329). Ugarit issues it by the jar in its record of the temple's rations, spr ḥpr bt: kd yn l PN w kd ḥmṣ 'a jar of wine for PN and one of vinegar' (KTU 4.269:1, 27–28; DULAT 359–360, 362), as KH 18 counts 1. The abode is the nāweh: nəwēh qodeš 'holy abode' (Exod 15:13), said of Zion too (2 Sam 15:25; HALOT 678). MI+\*301 'portion' and I+\*301 'ration' are the same build, a word for a share with \*301 after it, the order of a construct.

Weak points: DA as damʿu rests on DA-ME, the share sense of demaʿ is attested from the Mishnah on, and line 5 is broken before the ligature. Rival: ḏā nawā 'he of the camp', DA as in DA-QE-RA; no name stands before \*131C on the tablet to show a recipient's place. Second rival: one word across the line break, SI-DA-\*301, beside SI-DI-\*301 (KN Zf 13). One attestation. See DA, DA-ME, \*301.
