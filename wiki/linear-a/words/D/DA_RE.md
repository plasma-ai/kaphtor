### Note

Attested across Haghia Triada and Palaikastro as a recurring administrative label with variable quantities. Compare Arabic dār and Aramaic dārā(ʾ) 'house, dwelling'; this supports a possible dwelling or estate term, not the more specific gloss 'granary.' Its association with grain and personnel may reflect an administrative unit, but the function is not independently established. The unrelated Ugaritic drʾ /ḏirāʾu/ 'arm' does not explain the contexts.
