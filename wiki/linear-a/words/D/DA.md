### Note

HT 133 has A-DU · TE GRA+DA 55, the grain sign carrying DA under the heading that DA-ME follows on HT 86a and HT 95b. Alone and whole, DA stamps one Haghia Triada nodule, HT Wa 1031, and the single signs of those nodules name transactions: KU, KA, SI, TE. On the tablets the corpus record sets a break after it: beside SI 'tribute' on GO 2r (SI 2, then DA\[), where SigLA reads TE, and at the end of HT 3's list (MA-DI 2, then DA\[). On GO 2v DA\[ ends a line after 1⅛ and RE 1 opens the next, so DA-RE dār 'estate' is as likely there. HT 106 has DA-\[·\] 8 between SI 5½, SI 1 and SI 3, with a sign lost after DA, where GORILA finds DA-MU 'difficult but not impossible' (I 173). On KH 18 it carries \*301, DA+\*301, after KU and SI and before a wine product. Linear B keeps DA alone as a logogram (Salgarella 2020, 216, 224).

damʿu 'the sanctuary's share', the short form of DA-ME, as KU is of KU-RO. Weak point: the first sign alone ties DA to DA-ME, whose own reading has damu 'blood' against it, and the nodule is the one whole witness. Rival: DA-KA dāgā 'fish', spelled in full on five nodules of the same deposit as HT Wa 1031 (HT Wa 1001–1005). DA has no reading on the metal objects HT Zf 163, 164 and 168 or the vessel HT Zb 162bis. See DA-ME, SI, TE.
