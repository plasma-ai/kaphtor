### Note

KY Za 2, three signs on the lip of a steatite ladle from the peak sanctuary of Kythera, the whole text (Sakellarakis and Olivier 1994, 345–349); the same group closes I-DA-MA-TE on the Arkhalkhori axes. A peak sanctuary is where the Tower is expected: Ugarit's rituals offer to ỉlt mgdl, 'the goddess of the tower' (KTU 1.39:11; 1.112:25; Pardee 2000, 967).

damat 'the Tower': Ug. dmt 'tower', in 'the tower of the mother of(?) the god\[s(?)' (KTU 2.31:46; DULAT 272), and the place dmt, 'Tower' or 'Stop, station' (Watson 2001, 114); Akk. dimtu 'tower; fortified area; district' (CAD D 144–147).

The editors allow Δαμάτηρ (Sakellarakis and Olivier 1994, 349); see I-DA-MA-TE. Rival: a personal name on the same noun, Ugarit dmty, dmtn (GlOS 1, 147), Ebla ti-ma-ti (Pagan 1998, row 4489). One attestation.
