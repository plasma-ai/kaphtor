### Note

HT98b .2 (Haghia Triada, tablet): recipient of K (1/10), after \]VIN ¾ and \]\*188\[ (GORILA I 161). Hapax. Both elements recur: DA-RU- in DA-RU-\*329 (HT7b, a recipient of 2) and -NE-TE in KA-KU-NE-TE (ZA10b, a name list beside the Egyptian PA-JA-RE). Dāru-ʿAnēt needs an unwritten ʿa-, and I-NA-TA-I ʿInatī (IOZa6) writes Anat NA-TA; rival Egyptian Neith (Nt), the second element of sꜣ-nt 'son of Neith', wsr-nt 'Neith is strong', rdj-nt 'Neith gives', ḥtp-nt 'Neith is content' (PNM v6). First element Dāru: Amorite noun/DN (Gelb 1980), √DWR 'to turn, dwell, endure.' Akkadian dūru 'wall, fortress,' Hebrew dōr 'generation.' Second element -NE-TE = ʿAnēt(u), the goddess Anat (Huffmon 1965 APNM p. 200: Hanat/ʿAnat, well-attested NWS theophoric element). Amorite parallels: Ḥabdu-ᵈḤanat, Zikri-Ḥanat, ʿAbdi-ʿAnati, Šūm-ʿAnati. Phoenician: ʿBDʿNT. Alternative parse: Dārunēt as simple feminine (√DWR + -at feminine suffix with epenthetic -n-), 'she who endures.' Theophoric compound preferred: (1) 4-syllable name in accounting list, (2) -NE-TE may be compared with the CANDIDATE I-NA-TA-I = ʿInatī at IOZa6, (3) element + DN is the productive Amorite pattern.
