### Note

Third-slot recipient on HT19, the same-scribe minimal pair of HT17 whose identical slot holds Siddāru (SI-DA-RE, HT122a roster control)—name for name across the sister tablets. Spelling is the cleanest on offer: first vowel etymological (ū of √dwm), ME a dead vowel, -DI = /tī/ one voicing step in the post-nasal cluster. Cross-Semitic dūmat- name family: Amorite Dūmat-An (DU-MA-TA-AN, Gelb AS 21 no. 1581, Mari XIV 47:19—vocalization cuneiform-explicit), Hebrew Dūmāh the Ishmaelite eponym (Gen 25:14), Harding p. 242 cluster dmt/dwmt/dymt/dwmn under dm 'enduring', Sabaic dwm 'lasting, permanent' productive (Beeston p. 36, Biella p. 81). -ī hypocoristic per QA-\*310-I = qaynī. Rival: Dimtu-oblique 'the Tower-estate' (DE-ME-TE is no support: HT 94b reads RA-\[?\]-DE-ME-TE, one word with a sign lost after RA), on Ug. dmt 'tower' + dmt toponym (DULAT 272), Akk. dimtum 'tower; fortified settlement, district' (Huehnergard GAk), and CAD D s.v. dimtu confirms the voiced by-form dindu alongside dintu (pp. 144-147; dindu BBSt No. 8 i 27 + NB Elam toponyms)—so the rival's -DI spelling is orthographically regular, not a strike against it; it would lead if a second dmt-form turns up in a heading/destination slot. OSA ḍmd 'friend' is an exact skeleton match too.
