### Note

Spindle whorl from Troy (Schliemann facsimile; Godart, CRAI 1994: 721–23), one hand with TROZg1/TROZg2. Godart reads 39 / 51 for the first sign and AB37 = TI as doubtful. The TROZg2 sign lacks AB51's parallel verticals and reads as an open-based PI: one text twice. MI-TA-TI-RA₂ = mattat-ʾilā 'gift of the goddess' (MA-TI-TI + I-RA₂); cf. Mattattu-Ištar (BRM 2, 45), Ug. Mtn-Baʿlu (KTU 4.115). PI = bin 'son of', n unwritten (Heb. bin-nûn, Exod 33:11; bare Ben-Hur, 1 Kgs 4:8). Against: MI for ma-; no other PI = bin. Rivals: Luwian piya- 'give' (Brown 1998: 52–56); Hurrian -tilla. Perna 2014 doubts the Linear A attribution.
