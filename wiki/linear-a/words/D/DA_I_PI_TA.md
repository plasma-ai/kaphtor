### Note

ZA 10a .4–5 DA-I-PI-TA 1, in a name list after A-DU-KU-MI-NA 1 (GORILA III 169); ZA 8 DA-I-PI-TA 2½, before KA-I-RO 4, by another scribe (GORILA III 165). ṭayyibat- 'good (f.)', fem. of qattil ṭayyib-: DA writes ṭ as in Amorite DA-BA-TUM Ṭaybatum (Gelb no. 1409) and Ebla da-bù 'good'; I writes yyi, the gemination unmarked. LB da-i-pi-ta heads a list of men at Knossos (KN Bk 799), non-Greek (Docs² 537): the name carried into Mycenaean Crete. Rivals: DA-I + PI-TA, DA-I dayyu 'enough' (HT 12) with PI-TA otherwise only inside PI-TA-JA; DA + I-PI-TA, I-PI-TA unattested.

### Note

A recipient on two Zakros tablets by different scribes: 1 in the ZA 10a name list, 2½ on ZA 8, both intact. ṭayyibat- 'good (f.)': Amorite DA-BA-TUM Ṭaybatum, a woman (Gelb no. 1409), DA for ṭ. Same spelling as Linear B da-i-pi-ta, opening KN B 799 beside ke-do-si-ja (Killen, Mycenaean Greek, glossary): the name survives into Mycenaean Knossos. Rival: DA-I + PI-TA.
