### Note

A recipient on two Zakros tablets by different scribes: 1 in the ZA 10a name list, 2½ on ZA 8, both intact. Same spelling as Linear B da-i-pi-ta, opening KN B 799 beside ke-do-si-ja (Killen, Mycenaean Greek, glossary): the name survives into Mycenaean Knossos. No reading.

## Unread

Status **edge overlap** (a lexicon word sits at one edge; often a chance 2-sign overlap); role personal name.

Recipient on two Zakros tablets by different scribes: 1 in the ZA10a name list (across lines 4–5, GORILA III 169), 2½ on ZA8 (III 165), both intact. Same spelling as LB da-i-pi-ta, opening KN B 799 beside ke-do-si-ja, a heading to lists of men (Killen, Mycenaean Greek, glossary, s.vv. da-i-pi-ta and ke-do-si-ja): the name survives into Mycenaean Knossos.
