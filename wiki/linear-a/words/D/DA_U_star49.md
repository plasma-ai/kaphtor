Retained candidate Dawmi, 'the enduring one.' The reading depends on syllabic \*49=/mi/ and U≈/w/. Official \*49 semantics remain PALM/TREE, with TAMIR its candidate Semitic lexical name; DA-U-PALM/TREE remains an unresolved alternative.
