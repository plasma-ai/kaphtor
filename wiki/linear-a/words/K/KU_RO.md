### Note

Function confirmed corpus-internally by final or section-final position and arithmetic. HT89 supplies an exact control: 23+22+24+13+5=87. The comparison with Semitic \*kull- 'all, entirety' is an etymological hypothesis rather than the basis of the decipherment. Linear B normally writes TO-SO for 'total'; no Linear B KU-RO cognate should be claimed. Complements KI-RO 'owed/outstanding' in Linear A accounting.

### Source

Corpus-internal arithmetic; Gordon 1966 for the Semitic comparison; Duhoux 2012
