### Note

KH 6: KI-SA-NE CYP+D ½, a recipient of a vessel. Qīš + -āne, cf. Heb. Qîš (1 Chr 23:21). Weak points: KI for /q/, where QI exists; -āne as a gentilic has no second case. Rival: a Hurrian name in -nni, as DA-KU-SE-NE. One attestation.
