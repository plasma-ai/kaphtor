### Note

ZA 15b: KA-DI · VIN 3, then KU-RO VIN 78. ZA 4a: KA-DI 3, the last entry of the list, after E-\*82 50. Ug. kd 'jar; liquid measure' (DULAT 424), Heb. kad (Gen 24:14), Akk. kandu, a wine container, a West Semitic word (CAD K 148). KA-DI writes kadd-, the geminate unmarked. Rival on ZA 4a: a personal name among the list's entries. Two attestations, both Zakros.
