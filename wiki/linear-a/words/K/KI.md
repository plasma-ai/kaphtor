### Note

HT 118, the pig account SUS · \*516 ·: KI 10, KI 4 and KI 1 follow the first three entries, and KI 15, their sum, follows KU-RO 30, where HT 123a sets KI-RO after KU-RO. Entry and KI together give 25, 10, 5 and 10, a levy of five head or a multiple from each party (Peruzzi 1958, 9–10). KI-RO is spelled in full on eleven Haghia Triada tablets and is the one accounting term among the archive's KI- words. Montecchi: KI 'è sicuramente abbreviazione di ki-ro in HT 118' (2008, 314). KI with a small number also stands in the rosters HT 97a (KI 3), HT 49a (KI 1, twice) and HT 100 (KI 2), as KI-RO stands in the rosters HT 88, HT 94b and HT 117a. HT 100's KU-RO 97 balances with KI 2 counted in and 4 restored after \*304+PA, or with KI left out as the deficit and 6 restored (Montecchi 2008, 314). KI 5 stands among the oils of TY 3a, and KI alone on the vessels KE Zb 3 and KKH Zb 1, on THE 10 and on a line of HT 54b, HT 81, HT 154 and ZA 21b. See KI-RO.
