### Note

HT 115a .5 KA-PO-RU 1, whole and undotted, one of several sign groups with small quantities (NA-\*21F-NE-MI-NA 1, SE-KU-TU ½, PA-RA-NE 1) on a palimpsest side (GORILA I 189). kabīru 'great one' (b→p). Rival: √k-p-r, Hebrew kōper 'ransom' (HALOT 495), kippēr 'to atone' (HALOT 493), Akkadian kuppuru 'to purify' (CAD K 178); both roots give the skeleton kpr. One witness.
