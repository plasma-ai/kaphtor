### Note

HT 63 .1 KA-TI 1, both signs whole and undotted, under the personnel logogram \*305, after TE 1 and before SU-PU₂ (GORILA I 118–119). gat 'press' with the nisbe -ī; KA writes ga. The Ugaritic gt is both the press and the estate around it, as DA-RE dār is a unit of men on HT 122b. Rivals: Qəhātî 'Kohathite' (Num 3:27), the medial h unwritten; Kittî 'Kittian' (Gen 10:4 Kittîm), against the a of KA. One witness.
