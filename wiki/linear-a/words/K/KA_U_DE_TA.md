### Note

HT 13 .1 KA-U-DE-TA, heading the wine list VIN · TE · to RE-ZA, TE-TU, TE-KI, KU-ZU-NI, DA-SI-\*118 and I-DU-NE-SI, KU-RO 130½ (GORILA I 27). KA-U = kaw-, as A-RA-U-DA ʾArwada: Hurrian kaw (NPN 224; Kawiya, Monti 2017, 346) + tatt- 'love' (NPN 263). Terqa writes Tatta da-da, da-da-e (Monti 2017, 325): D for t, and e beside a. Cf. Kawinni, written Qa-wi-in-ni, Qa-we-en-ni, Ka-wi-ni and Ga-wi-in (NPN 224), and Šenna-tati, written Še-en-da-ti (NPN 263). Both elements are attested, never together. Rival: the Ugaritic name kwt, which leaves DE (DULAT 469). One witness.
