### Note

CR(?)Zf1 (unprovenanced metal object), word 2 of 5. Masculine construct participle qāniy (Amorite qanijum) + genitive yammi 'of the Sea'; KA writes /q/. √q-n-y is a Bronze Age divine epithet: Ugaritic qnyt ʾilm of Athirat (KTU 1.4 III 26), Hittite Elkunirša (= ʾil qōnē ʾarṣ, husband of Asherdu), Karatepe ʾl qn ʾrṣ (KAI 26 A III 18), Gen 14:19. El's Ugaritic creation epithet is bny bnwt (KTU 1.4 II 11). Rival: -MI as mimation.
