### Note

PH Wc 37 KA-\*314-SI \*320, a Phaistos roundel (MM II), all three signs whole (GORILA II 94). kaḥsi 'chair, throne', goods on a receipt: Ug. kḥṯ, pl. kḥṯm, the seat of a god or king, l kḥṯ drkth 'to the throne of his dominion' (KTU 1.3 IV 3; DULAT 429); the Amarna gloss GU.ZA ka-aḫ-šu, a chair plated with gold in a list of belongings (EA 120:18); Egyptian group writing ka-ḥ-si, sign for sign (GlOS 1, 303, after Hoch, SWET 336f., no. 496). Egyptian kḥs stands already in an 11th Dynasty inscription, older than the roundel (Ward 1996, 29, in Watson 2006, 721). GlOS compares Hurrian kešḫi 'throne, chair'. ḤA carries the ḥ with its echo vowel, and SI the Canaanite š from ṯ, as SU in PA-RO-SU. \*320 on the other face is unread. One attestation. See \*314-TA-MA.
