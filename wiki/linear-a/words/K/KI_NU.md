### Note

Heads commodity allocation section on ARKH3a. JA-KI-SI-KI-NU (ARKHZf9) does not contain KI-NU: it is the verbal form \*yaksīkīnū from √k-s-y "to cover" (see its entry). kīn- is the Eblaite and Amorite form as well as the Akkadian. \|\| DUAL-FUNCTION note: kīnu is simultaneously PN element and quality adjective 'true/standard'. On ARKH3a the heading slot admits both 'Kīnu (PN): grain 13' and 'standard-grade: grain 13'; parallels the KI dual function (kī conjunction / kīn(u) grade) and the KA-NE/qanē duality on the same tablet. Discriminating test: a second attestation in-list with quantity (PN) or heading an OLE section (grade). \|\| Standalone KI is an administrative sign of open value; the conjunction reading kī has no GORILA basis (Gordon's I,3 and I,4:a are misread). See OLE+KI.
