### Note

Heads commodity allocation section on ARKH3a. Note: JA-KI-SI-KI-NU (ARKHZf9) was initially parsed as containing KI-NU but is now analyzed as a verbal form \*yaksīkīnū from √k-s-y "to cover" (see separate entry). East Semitic vocalism (Akkadian kīnu) rather than NWS kūn—consistent with the intra-Semitic fluidity observed in Linear A onomastics (cf. ARKH3b PI-\*314 Luwian name alongside Semitic JA-PI). \|\| DUAL-FUNCTION note: kīnu is simultaneously PN element and quality adjective 'true/standard'. On ARKH3a the heading slot admits both 'Kīnu (PN): grain 13' and 'standard-grade: grain 13'; parallels the KI dual function (kī conjunction / kīn(u) grade) and the KA-NE/qanē duality on the same tablet. Discriminating test: a second attestation in-list with quantity (PN) or heading an OLE section (grade). \|\| Standalone KI is an administrative sign of open value; the conjunction reading kī has no GORILA basis (Gordon's I,3 and I,4:a are misread).
