### Note

HT 26b .2–3 KA-U-ZU-NI 4, a count of men under VIR+\[?\] 9, between \]-RO-NI 3 and KI+MU 12 (GORILA I 47). ka-u-ṯu-ni: Hurrian kaw (NPN 224) + -šuni (Awa-šuni, NPN 208), the Hurrian š in ZU = ṯu as Ugarit writes ảủpš also ảủpṯ (DULAT 4). Same base as KA-U-DE-TA Kaw-tatta. Rival: Kawṯān, the place name kṯn, Heb. Kûšān (DULAT 467; Hab 3:7). One witness.
