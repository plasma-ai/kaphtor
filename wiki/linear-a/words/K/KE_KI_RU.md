### Note

HT 94b under KI-RO: TU-MA 1, PA-TA-NE 1, DE-DI 1, KE-KI-RU 1, SA-RU 1, KU-RO 5, one unit each with no commodity sign: a name. \*kikkar- 'disk, round loaf' (semitic_roots 1097) as a name, with the i of Heb. kikkār, KE writing ki as RE writes ri in KI-RE-TA₂; Akk. kakkaru (CAD K 49), Amorite KAKKAR-I (Gelb AS 21 no. 3957). Weak point: KI for the a of kikkar-. Rival: a title. KI-KI-RA-JA (HT 85b) is on the same root. One attestation. See QA-KU-RE and QE-KU-RE.
