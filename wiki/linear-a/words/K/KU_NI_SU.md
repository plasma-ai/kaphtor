### Note

Akkadian loanword kunāšu. Co-occurs with GRA ideogram. GRA+KU (7 att., HT61, HT128a ×3, HT128b ×3) is the logographic abbreviation: GRA ideogram + KU complement = initial syllable of kunāšu. On HT128a, GRA+KU co-occurs with GRA+PA as distinct grain subtypes in a distribution tablet.
