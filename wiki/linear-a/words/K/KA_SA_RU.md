### Note

HT 10b .3 KA-SA-RU 6, a personnel entry on a face written over an erasure; the three signs are clear (GORILA I 21). Linear B ka-sa-ro is a man's name at Knossos (KN C(2) 912.8, Dv 1450), with no Greek interpretation. Final -RU against Linear B -ro, as I-TA-NU against u-ta-no. Ugarit has DUMU ka-ša₂-ri (ORACC P503760). The root is open (k-š-r, q-ṣ-r, g-z-r); a reading as Kothar would need S for ṯ, against KA-TA-RE. One attestation.
