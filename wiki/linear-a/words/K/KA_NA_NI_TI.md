### Note

KHWc2005 Khania roundel, standalone. -NI-TI = nisbe gentilic suffix -ītī. Exact morphological parallel to DU-RU-WI = ḏu-lēwī 'the Levite.' ʿayin guttural-transparent. Hebrew כְּנַעֲנִי kĕnaʿanī, Akkadian Kinaḫḫi, El Amarna 'Canaan.' Administrative roundel context—ethnic designation for trade/personnel tracking.
