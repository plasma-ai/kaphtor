### Note

One of the strongest cross-script cognates: LB ku-mi-no is a universally recognized Semitic loanword and KU-MI-NA is its Linear A precursor (exact skeleton). Culture-word chain (Wanderwort): Sumerian U.GAMUN → Akkadian kamūnu → Ugaritic kmn → Linear B ku-mi-no → Greek κύμινον. On the HT Wc 3014 roundel, KU-MI-NA (side a) and QE (side b, the standalone qab-measure √q-b-b before the CAP caprid logogram) are separate entries—QE is not bound to cumin (a Semitic measure precedes its noun in construct, qê kamūni, and coordination is the proclitic wa-, never a postposed -qe). HT 54a.2 reads \]KU-MI-NA-QE\[, broken at both ends (GORILA I 105). A-KU-MI-NA and A-DU-KU-MI-NA on ZA10a are names from the homograph √k-m-n 'hide, shelter' (HALOT 481), not cumin.
