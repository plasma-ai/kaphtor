### Note

HT 24a .1 KU-PA₃-RI-JA · KI-MA+RU\[, the heading of a wool inventory; PA-SA-RI-JA heads the second section at .4. Gubla with the gentilic -iya: Amorite GUBL-AJ-I and GUBL-AJIT-UM (Gelb 1980, nos. 1793–1795) and Gubla in Gelb's glossary; Akkadian Gublaya 'Byblian' at Ugarit (ORACC P503838); Hebrew gibli 'Gebalite'; Ugaritic gbl 'Byblos' (DULAT 290). KU writes g, PA₃ b, RI l. Rival: the Ugaritic place kbry, syllabic URU ku-ba-ri-ia-a (DULAT 424). One document.
