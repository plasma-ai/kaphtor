KN Ze 45, four signs on a lost block of the Isopata Royal Tomb, read 77-41-08-04 by Owens (Minos 25/26, 367–69). Wheel, trident, double axe and branch are the standard masons' marks, and Younger, citing Hood, takes them as such (Younger, Linear A Texts). Not a word.
