## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role toponym.

ZA 26a .1 SI-TE · DI-DI-\[, the first group of the tablet, the logogram and 13 following at the head of .2 (GORILA III 202–203). Intact and undotted on both copies. The editors draw TE with two short bars on each side of the stem and allow that the stroke after it is the numeral 1, not a divider. For MIL Zb 3, the second witness, Younger gives no text: he prints MIL Zb 1 alone for Miletos and reports the rest unpublished (Younger, Linear A Texts). SI-TE also stands inside SI-TE-TU (ZA 20), QE-SI-TE (MA Ze 11) and KE-SI-TE (KN Ze 49). As a tablet heading it compares with a place: the Kom el-Hetan list ends with a damaged oval, restored \[sȝ\]-i-tȝ-\[y\] and tentatively identified as Siteia (Cline & Stannish 2011, 10). No reading.
