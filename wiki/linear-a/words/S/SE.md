### Note

SE alone before the grain sign: KH 104 SE · GRA ½ and KH 28 SE · GRA+F, where ARKH 2 spells SE-DE ṣēd 'provision of' before wheat and KH 7a heads a section SE-\*305 ṣēdā. SE is its first sign, as KI is of KI-RO. It stands alone as the commodity on the roundels HT Wc 3004 and 3005, opposite TA-NI-KA, and before ¾ on LACH Za 1. Akk. ṣidītu 'travel provisions' is issued in grain (CAD Ṣ 172–173).

Rival: the seah, Heb. sĕʾâ (HALOT 737–738), Akk. sūtu (CAD S 420), a measure before its fraction. Rival: barley, Heb. śĕʿōrâ (HALOT 1345–1346), named before the grain sign. Weak point: the first sign alone ties SE to ṣēd, and both full spellings stand once. SE has no reading on GO 2r, broken on both sides, or on KH 95. See SE-DE, SE-\*305.
