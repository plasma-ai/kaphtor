### Note

HT 118 opens SUS · \*516 ·, the pig sign and the account sign as its heading, over four counted names, KU-RO 30 and KI 15. The sign is AB85, AU inside a word; here it is the logogram (SigLA), as Linear B SUS. Cf. Heb. ḥăzîr 'swine, boar' (HALOT 302). HT 38 counts the same sign, 1, between a vessel and three sheep; Peruzzi calls it sus without doubt (1958, 10).
