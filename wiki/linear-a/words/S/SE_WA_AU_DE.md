### Note

ZA Zg 35, a bone or ivory rod found outside the south front of the workshop quarter of the Zakros palace, in a fill with nothing later than LM IA, its use unknown (Kopaka 1989, 7–9): \]A-MI-JA-RU · SE-WA-AU-DE. Two words, verb and object: SE-WA šiwwâ 'set, placed' (Heb. šwh II Piel, HALOT 1438) and AU-DE hawd- 'splendour', h unwritten (Heb. hôd, beside tipʾeret, HALOT 241), 'ʿAmmī-yarūm set the ornament'. Hebrew joins the two: hôd wəhādār təšawweh ʿālāyw 'splendour and majesty you set upon him' (Ps 21:6). Kopaka sets -AU-DE beside Linear B au-de-pi and au-de-we-sa, a term of the furniture tablets at Pylos (PY Ta), said of ivory-inlaid tables and stools and once of a brazier, its meaning unknown and without a Greek etymology (Kopaka 1989, 12–13). Weak points: hôd is an abstract, and 'ornament' rests on the Linear B use; AU for /haw/ has no second witness: the value rests on AU-TA-DE (KO(?) Zf 2) beside A-TA-DE (CR Zf 1), where AU answers A, and Kopaka grades AU very probable, DE only possible (pp. 11–12); -DE is not the accusative -a. Rivals: šawaʿ-dē 'who cries out', √š-w-ʿ (Isa 58:9; HALOT 1443) with the relative dē; -AU-DE the Linear B ornament word alone; a second personal name. One attestation. See A-MI-JA-RU.
