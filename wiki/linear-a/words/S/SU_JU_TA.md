### Note

JU writes /lu/: su-lu-ta, the skeleton s-l-t. KN22a lames tablet: SU-JU-TA 20 KA-JE. Qty 20 consistent with bulk commodity allocation. Biblical sōlet is the primary offering flour (Lev 2:1, Ex 29:2).
