GO 2 recto .3 SI-DA-RO with ¼, broken after the fraction, below single-sign entries SI\[, KA ⅛, SI 2 and DA\[ (Younger, Linear A Texts). Rival: a commodity term, as the single-sign entries around it are. See SI-DA-RE.
