## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

GO 2 recto .3 SI-DA-RO with the fraction E (¼), broken after the fraction, below single-sign entries SI\[, KA ⅛, SI 2 and DA\[ on an LM IB page tablet from the Gournia palace (Younger, Linear A Texts). Younger prints the group intact. SI-DA-RO shares SI-DA-R- with SI-DA-RE (HT 17, HT 122a) and with JA-SI-DA-RA (PH 7a). Nothing on the tablet fixes SI-DA-RO as a name: the entries around it are single signs with numbers and fractions, and Younger compares Cape Sidero. No reading.
