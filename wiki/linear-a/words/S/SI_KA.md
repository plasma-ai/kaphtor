### Note

7 attestations across 2 sites (IOZa6 SI-KA is a separate homograph from √š-q-y, not this entry): (1) six identical HT nodules from Portico 11/Room 13—administrative sealings, plausibly institutional stamps from a steward/prefect office; (2) A-DA-KI-SI-KA at KH5. The administrative analysis compares √š-k-n and the title family represented by Ugaritic skn /sākinu/ 'prefect,' Amarna sū-ki-ni /sōkini/, and SI-KI-NE Sikinet; the precise truncation or suffixation remains unresolved. An alternative compares √s-k-k 'cover' and Hebrew sukkā 'booth.' The older divine-dwelling/Shekhinah comparison is only a late semantic parallel and does not establish the word's function.
