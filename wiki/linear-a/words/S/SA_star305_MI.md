### Note

PE 2 .2 SA-\*305-MI 1⅛, after RU-PI ⅓, in a list of entries with fractions. šadmi 'terrace': Ug. šdmt, yšql šdmth km gpn 'they threw it on the terrace like a vine-stock' (KTU 1.23:10; DULAT 799), Heb. šᵊdēmâ, pl. šadmôt, the terraced vineyards of Deut 32:32 and Isa 16:8 (HALOT 1422–1423); \*305 da. Rival: the town Šdmy on the border with Alalakh, krm PN b šdmy 'the vineyard of PN at Šdmy' (KTU 4.244:13; RGTC 12/2, 255). The first sign is SA (AB31), a fork with no crossbar. One attestation.
