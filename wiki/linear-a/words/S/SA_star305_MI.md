### Note

PE 2 .2 SA-\*305-MI 1⅛, after RU-PI ⅓, in a list of entries with fractions. šadmi 'terrace': Ug. šdmt, yšql šdmth km gpn 'they threw it on the terrace like a vine-stock' (KTU 1.23:10; DULAT 799), Heb. šᵊdēmâ, pl. šadmôt, the terraced vineyards of Deut 32:32 and Isa 16:8 (HALOT 1422–1423); \*305 da. Rival: the town Śdmy, krm PN b śdmy 'the vineyard of PN at Śdmy' (KTU 4.244:13), on the border between Ugarit and Alalakh (RGTC 12/2, 255) or, for Astour, across the Orontes at modern Sarmīn, Ebla Sa-du-maᵏⁱ; he vocalizes \*Sudumiya, reading ś as su (Astour 1995, 68 n. 95): Su- for SA and -miya for -MI. A-\*325-ZA, the next entry, has a town of Ugarit as its rival too, ủbs (DULAT 14); 'terrace' and 'stall' stand together as counted holdings of an estate. The first sign is SA (AB31), a fork with no crossbar. One attestation. See A-\*325-ZA.
