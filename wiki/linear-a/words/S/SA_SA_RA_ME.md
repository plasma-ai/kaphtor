### Note

Palaikastro dialect variant. At PKZa11, the preceding token is standalone 'A' followed by a line break then SA-SA-RA-ME—the scribe split A-SA-SA-RA-ME across lines, with A- on one line and SA-SA-RA-ME continuing. The JA- prefix drops in Palaikastro proto-Phoenician dialect (cf. PKZa27 which retains JA-, standard register). Same reading as JA-SA-SA-RA-ME: \*yaššaram\* 'uprightly' (accusative -am). Huehnergard: /yušru/ "uprightness" (yuCCu nominal pattern); confirms adverbial accusative with -m.
