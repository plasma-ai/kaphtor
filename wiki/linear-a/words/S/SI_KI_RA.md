### Note

HT 8a: JE-DI · OLE+KI 10, then PA₃-KA-RA-TI 1½, PA 3½, TE-\*301 2, QA-\*310-I ¾, SI-KI-RA ¼, KI-RE-TA-NA ½; an entry in a list that mixes commodities (heifers, figs) and persons (the smith, the Cretan). Hebrew שֵׁכָר šēkār 'strong drink' (HALOT 1501; Lev 10:9), √š-k-r 'to become intoxicated' (Ug. /š-k-r/ II, DULAT 804). A-SI-KI-RA (KH 20) stands in the entry slot before CYP+E, parallel to \]DU-RE-ZA, and is no witness of the commodity.
