### Note

Logogram \*322 on the Khania roundels KH Wc 2026, 2027 and 2098, alone, each roundel with two seal impressions (Younger, Linear A Texts, after Hallager 1996, Roundel 2: 64, 65, 131): a flat oval with a narrow upright on top, counted by two. ṣurru 'bag, bundle': Heb. ṣərôr (HALOT 1054), Ar. ṣurrat (Lane 1672), Syr. ṣrārā (Payne Smith 485). Schoep compares Linear B \*158 (Schoep 2002, 91), possibly a textile and listed among the textile logograms of Knossos (Nosch and Weilhartner 2026, 126, 130); eleven Khania roundels receipt textiles. At Knossos one \*158 stands with each stored set of pa-we-a, 'often in sets of 25 or 35' (Nosch 2024, 335): 'It may be a package for the bales of pa-we-a fabrics' (Nosch 2012, 346), the sense of ṣurru, and it is among the cloths that 'may be Minoan textile types' (2024, 341). Weak points: a logogram has no spelling, so nothing in the script carries ṣurr-; \*158 has no spelled name. Rivals: a lidded vessel; a commodity not identified (Schoep 2002, 91). HT 11a \*322-RI is another sign (GORILA I 23; III 124, 141). Three attestations.
