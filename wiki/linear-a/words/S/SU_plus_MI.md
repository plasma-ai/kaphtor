## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/S/SU|SU]].

GORILA's A 540, a hapax on KN 28b .3, classed as SU+MI. Salgarella holds that it 'may be better interpreted' as SU+OLE, her SU+OIL: the second element has the shape OLE takes in A 622 (OLE+MI), which would otherwise read MI+MI, and MI binds only to OLE there and to one unidentified sign in A 634 (2020, 145). GORILA's drawing shows a stem with short ticks at its top beside SU, with no bowl or dot (I 263).
