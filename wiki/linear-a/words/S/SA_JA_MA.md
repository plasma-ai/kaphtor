### Note

HT 31 .3, after \*416-VS-KA-RO-PA₃ 10: SA-JA-MA 30\[, two tens visible and the surface lost after them. MA is the last sign. The five vessel names on the tablet stand over their logograms; SA-JA-MA stands without one, as KI-DE-MA-\*323-NA does on .4. A noun /śaymā/ of √ś-y-m 'to set, place' (Heb. śîm, Amarna sīm); JA writes the y, so not the perfect śām. Gordon (1975, 153 n. 13) read sa-ya-ma-n\[a\] 'silver', after Aramaic סים: the tablet has no NA, and Jastrow (1903, 980) derives סִימָא 'silver' from Persian. Rival: a vessel name written without its logogram. Hapax.
