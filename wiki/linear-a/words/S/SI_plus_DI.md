## Unread sign

A ligature. Status **component read** (a combined sign with a component the lexicon reads).

The lexicon reads [[wiki/linear-a/words/S/SI|SI]], [[wiki/linear-a/words/D/DI|DI]].

GORILA A 528, 41+07 (GORILA V xxiv): the one sign on the stone weight KN Zg 21, whole and with no bracket, DI set over SI (GORILA IV 164). The weight is 96.4 g (Petruso 1978, 181, apud Younger).
