### Note

A personal name in the damaged HT 126a list, immediately followed by 1. Ugarit writes the same name ši-DI-ia, SI-DI-JA sign for sign: two men of Qarātu, one living in Lašabe and one in Armelu (PRU 6 78 \[RS 19.41\]:4, 16), on a tablet that lists Šadeyanu apart. DULAT compares šdy (II), syllabic ša-du-ya, ša-de₄-ya, ša-TI-ya, the name of bn šdy (KTU 4.51:2; DULAT 799), alphabetic śdy in šmbʿl bn śdy (BAH 214, 7, o 14); Kiš has ša-di-ia (de Boer 2014, 401). Sanmartín files ša-du-ya, ša-dì-ia under Sadaw/y 'open field, field, steppe' (GlOS 2, 272–273); Watson reads the name of KTU 4.51:2 'country man' (Watson 1995, 228), later 'weaver/spinner' with the šdym of KTU 4.868:4 (Watson 2018b, 379), DULAT's 'land labourers' (DULAT 799). Other etymologies: \*šad(d)- 'mountain'; a Hurrian element šad- (Kinlaw 1967, 201–202); the god šd (Watson 1996b, 104).
