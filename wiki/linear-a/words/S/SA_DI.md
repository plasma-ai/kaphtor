### Note

HT 111b .1 SA-DI 4\[, whole and undotted, the numeral broken after (GORILA I 181), on a fragment broken above and below. SA writes š in the S-class merger. Ugaritic šdy (I) 'land labourer', as nʿrm šdym 'serving lads, labourers in the fields' (KTU 4.868:4; DULAT 799); Watson reads the same line 'weaver/spinner' (Watson 2018b, 379). A count of four fits a term for workers; the fragment gives no heading to choose between the occupation and the name. Rival: the name Šadī, Ugaritic šdy (II), ša-du-ya, ša-de₄-ya (DULAT 799), 'Country man' (Watson 1995, 228), and Amorite ŠADW-IJA, ŠA-DI-IA (Gelb 1980, no. 5781).
