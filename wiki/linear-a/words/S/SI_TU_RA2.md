### Note

HT 3 .5 SI-TU-RA₂-RE 5, RA₂ and RE dotted, SI against the left edge, which the drawing gives as preserved (GORILA I 6); the copie tabulaire and Younger print \] before SI. No divider is written, but RE before a numeral is le- 'for', as HT 32 OLE+NE · RE 104 and HT 66 TA-MI · RE 1. SI-TU-RA₂ spells šitl- with TU dead and RA₂ at its primary la, as Punic σιθιλ 'cutting of a tree' (Krahmalkov 485): shoots, for 5, in a run of goods after DI 'honey' 2 and MU-RU 'young bull' 1. One attestation.
