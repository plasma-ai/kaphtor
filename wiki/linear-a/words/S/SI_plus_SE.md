### Note

HT 116a: SI+SE GRA 16, OLE+KI 1, OLE+MI 6, OLIV 3, a recipient among KU-PA-JA, PU-RA₂, PI-\*34-TE, SI-KI-NE. HT 42+59: TA · SI+SE · TE · OLE+KI 8, OLE+U 7, beside SA-RO. Ug. Šapšu (špš, DULAT 824), taking the ligature's outer consonants š…š. Weak points: no other ligature abbreviates this way, and the medial p is unwritten. Rival: a syllabic še, as in SI+SE-RA+KA (HT Wa 1021), a name or office. SI+SE-NI (HT 99b) is a separate word. Two attestations.
