### Note

ZA 20, an allocation list: SI-TU 6, after SI-TE-TU šetātu 'woven' 1; HT Wa 1019, a nodule sealed with the name PA-TA-NE. šītu 'garment': Heb. šît 'clothing, garment' (Ps 73:6; Prov 7:10), from šyt 'to put', what is put on (HALOT 1486); the verb is Ug. št 'to place, set' (DULAT 835–837) and Phoenician, Punic and Hebrew šyt (DNWSI 1130). SI writes the ī. Weak point: the noun is Hebrew alone.
