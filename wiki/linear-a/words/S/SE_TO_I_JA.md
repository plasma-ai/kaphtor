### Note

Same spelling as Linear B se-to-i-ja, 17 Knossos tablets (As 40+5093, As 1516, Dc 5687+, Lc 525, Le 654, Mc 4464+, Sd 4407+ and others). PR Za 1 formula slot 2, the site-specific slot (cf. TU-RU-SA at Kophinas, O-SU-QA-RE at Tylissos, I-DA-MI at Syme). Locative ("at Setoiya") or epithet ("she of Setoiya") open.
