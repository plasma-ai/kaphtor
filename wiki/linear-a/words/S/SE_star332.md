## Unread

Status **role only** (classed by role only (onomasticon type or translation label), no reading); role personal name.

THE Zb 14 (formerly THE Zb 15), a graffito incised on the lowest ring of a clay high-footed lamp base from Akrotiri (ÉfA V 15), published by Tzachili and Karnava (Minos 39, 2016, 33–34): AB 09-A 332 2, that is SE-\*332 2. Only the first sign is readily recognisable, and A 332 is a doubtful identification; doubts still weigh on the reading (Del Freo, Rapport 2016–2021, 105–106). RILA-S1 lists it among its doubtful inscriptions (D 15). Not readable.
