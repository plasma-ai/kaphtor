### Note

Egyptian group-writing: s3bw \[element 6a\]. √s-b-b 'to turn, go around': Heb sābab (~163 occ., HALOT 738), 2mp imper. sōbbū (Ps 48:13 סֹבּוּ צִיּוֹן). Group-writing s3 = plain /s/, not /ṣ/—the same scribe renders Semitic ṣ as Egyptian ḏ in the adjacent word yaṣʾā (y3yd33), ruling out √ṣ-b-ʾ. Geminate -bb- invisible in group-writing (standard). Disease-expulsion doublet sabbū yaṣʾā 'Turn away! Go out!' parallels Akkadian serial motion imperatives in Udug.hul. D54 determinative is embedded in the phonogram ḏi of word 2 (Kyriakidis 2002, n. 16), not a freestanding classifier per word. Keftiu Spell #33 (samānu disease), LMP BM 10059.
