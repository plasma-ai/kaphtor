# By scribe

[[wiki/linear-a/_index|..]]

***

<!-- BEGIN kaphtor:record -->

Every number on this page is computed from the data files.

113 scribes are assigned 664 records, 28 of them with a question mark in the edition. A scribe is numbered within one site and one class of document. A record that two scribes wrote stands under each, with the part each wrote.

The numbers are GORILA V's, except on the Haghia Triada tablets, which follow Militello 1989's numbering; for those the column GORILA V gives the cells GORILA V prints for the same tablets.
