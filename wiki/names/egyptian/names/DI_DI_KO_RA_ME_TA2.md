## Note

ZA 11a .1 DI-DI-KO-RA-ME⌊ ⌋TA₂ · \*516 ·, the holder of an account of grain, wine and olives; the destroyed space before TA₂ could have held a divider (GORILA III 173), and SigLA reads one word. Egyptian ḏd-kꜣ-rꜥ 'the ka of Re endures' (Ranke I, 412.17) with mtj 'the reliable one' (TLA lemma 853566): DI-DI writes ḏd as in DI-DI-KA-SE, KO kꜣ as in KO-SA-I-TI, ME-TA₂ mtj on TA₂'s value t. A locally made scarab from Sidon names an MB IIB lord Ḏdkꜣrꜥ, 'beloved of Seth, lord of Iꜣy' (Mourad 2014, I, 319; II, 149). Rival: DI-DI-KO · RA-ME-TA₂, Egyptian rmṯ 'people, personnel' (TLA lemma 450402), which leaves DI-DI-KO unread. One attestation. See DU-DA-MA.

## As a name

Egyptian ḏd-kꜣ-rꜥ (Ranke I, 412.17) with mtj 'the reliable one'. Rival: RA-ME-TA₂ rmṯ 'personnel'. See the lexicon entry.
