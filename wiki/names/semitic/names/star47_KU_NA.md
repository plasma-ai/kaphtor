## Note

ZA 15a .1 \*47-KU-NA SA-VIN, all four signs whole and undotted (GORILA III 183), heading a wine issue with libation portions (SI-PI-KI) and groats. With \*47 = so: sōkuna 'prefect, steward', its first vowel between ā and ō, the ā of sākinu on its way to ō. It is the name of the Phoenician god Sakun, which Sanchuniathon bears, 'Sakun grants'. Philo's Sanchuniathon names Dagon, Adodos, Asklepios, Ousoos and Sidon (Eusebius, Praep. evang. 1.10), each a name read in Linear A. One witness. See SI-KI-NE, DA-KU-NA, A-DU, A-SA-MU-NE, U-SU and SI-DI-\*301.

## As a name

ZA 15a: heads a wine issue. sōkuna 'prefect, steward' with \*47 = so, as the Canaanite gloss su₂-ki-na (EA 362) and Phoenician Sakun, the god (KAI 58; Krahmalkov 2000, 343).
