## Note

ZA 10b .5 RA₂-RO-RE 2, after A-MI-TA 3 and before PA-JA-RE 1 (GORILA III 171), all three signs whole. Akkadian lallaru 'mourner, wailer', a name at OB Nippur, ᵐLa-al-la-r\[u\] (CAD L 48); RA₂ writes l as in SA-RA₂ šarra, RO the second l as in KU-RO kull. Rivals: Amorite Li-la-ru, Li-lu-ri, root LLR (Gelb 1980, nos. 4365, 4367); Hurrian lallari-, meaning unknown (Laroche, GLH s.v.). One witness.

## As a name

Akkadian lallaru 'mourner, wailer', a name at OB Nippur, ᵐLa-al-la-r\[u\] (CAD L 48). Rival: Amorite Li-la-ru, Li-lu-ri (Gelb 1980, nos. 4365, 4367). See the lexicon entry.
