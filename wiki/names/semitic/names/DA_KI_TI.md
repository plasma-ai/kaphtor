## Note

KN Zc 6, an inked cup from the sanctuary deposit above the Basement of the Monolithic Pillars: register .2, after QI-TI-RI 'my incense' and A-DI 'unto', written as one unit with PA-KU 'flask' (DA-KI-TI-PA-KU, closed by a divider before NI-JA-NU; GORILA IV 121). Daqqiti, the genitive of Daqqitu, every vowel written: the goddess who receives a ewe after Pidray at Ugarit (KTU 1.39:15–16; Pardee 2000, 976) and stands in the Hittite offering lists as ᵈda-ki-tu₄, A-NA ᵈda-ki-tu₄ 'to Dakitu' (KBo 14.142+ I 25); Pardee takes the name as Semitic (Pardee 2000, 79). Astour vocalises the Ugaritic name daqqatu or diqqatu, 'as its Arabic and Akkadian cognates' (Astour 1966, 283–84): DA-KI-TI writes the first, JA-DI-KI-TU the second. The paired cup KN Zc 7 names its vessel, its offering and A-ZU-RA Athirat; this cup names its offering, the goddess and its flask.

DA- beside the DI- of JA-DI-KI-TU is the dialect alternation of TA-NA-TI beside TI-NI-TA. Younger reads A-DI-DA-KI-TI-PA-KU as a muddled A-DI-KI-TE (Younger, Linear A Texts, Other Texts: Knossos). Cf. SKO Zc 2 \]DE-KI-TI\[ (Skoteino cave, a painted vessel), broken on both sides. Rival: daqqati 'fine (powder)', incense ground fine, Hebrew qəṭōret … daqqâ (Lev 16:12), which needs KI for qa and a feminine beside the masculine QI-TI-RI. One attestation. See JA-DI-KI-TU; cf. TI-NI-TA and TA-NA-TI.

## As a name

KN Zc 6, an inked cup: QI-TI-RI · A-DI DA-KI-TI-PA-KU · NI-JA-NU, 'My incense, unto Daqqitu: a flask'. Daqqiti, the genitive of Daqqitu after A-DI 'unto': the goddess who receives a ewe after Pidray at Ugarit (KTU 1.39:15–16; Pardee 2000, 976), the Diqqitu of JA-DI-KI-TU and A-DI-KI-TE-TE; Astour vocalises the Ugaritic name daqqatu or diqqatu (Astour 1966, 283–84). Rival: daqqati 'fine (powder)', of the incense. One attestation.
