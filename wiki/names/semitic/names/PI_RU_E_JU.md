## Note

PH 2 .3 PI-RU-E-JU 60, between RA-O-DI-KI 60 and SE-SA-PA₃ 60, under the heading A-SE-TU-\*21F 1 (GORILA I 288–289). pirʾ(u) + ʾēlu: the ʾ of pirʾu goes unwritten, and E-JU writes ʾē-lu as in SA-RE-JU. The name type is Akkadian Pirḫi-ilišu 'Offspring-of-His-God', also written Pi-ir-i-ì-lí-šu (CAD P 418). Rival: pir- 'fruit', 'Fruit of El', a type with no attested name. One witness.

## As a name

PH 2 .3, one of three entries of 60 under A-SE-TU-\*21F (GORILA I 288–289). Pirʾu-ʾēlu 'Offspring of El': Pir-ḫi-ì-lí-šu 'Offspring-of-His-God' (CAD P 418). Rival: pir- 'fruit'. One witness.
