## Note

HT 25a .2 RU-NI 1, both signs whole and undotted, heading the line after .1 DI-NA-U\[ (GORILA I 45), in a roster of cult personnel each counted 1. Runni 'jubilation', √r-n-n 'cry out', the geminate unwritten: Ugaritic rny, a personal name (KTU 4.769:19; DULAT 731), Hebrew Rinnāh (1 Chr 4:20) and Sabaic Rannat, a woman's name (HALOT 1247). Rival: 'the crier', the trade of the Dīnāyu of .1, laid out as TU-QE-NU / ZU-JU-PU₂ 1 and DU-RU-WI / I-KI-RA 1 are, a name at the line's end and the trade with its 1 on the next; two men named Dīnāyu stand on the roster, and Hebrew has hā-rōneh 'the herald, crier' (1 Kgs 22:36, conjectured; HALOT 1246). One witness. See RA-NA-TU-SU.

## As a name

HT 25a .2 RU-NI 1, heading the line (GORILA I 45). Runni 'jubilation', √r-n-n: Ugaritic rny PN (DULAT 731), Hebrew Rinnāh (1 Chr 4:20; HALOT 1247). Rival: 'the crier', Dīnāyu's trade. See the lexicon entry.
