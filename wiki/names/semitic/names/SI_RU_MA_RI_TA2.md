## Note

Name on the HT 55a KI-RO list (SI-RU 1) and first element of HT 90 SI-RU-MA-RI-TA₂ (GORILA I 107, 143). Amorite ṣill- 'shade, protection' (Huffmon 1965, 257): Ṣillī-Baʿli (Huffmon 1965, 58), Ṣill-Haddu, Ṣillān (Gelb 1980, nos. 6482–6483); Heb. ṣēl 'shade' (HALOT 1024), the name Ṣillāh (Gen 4:19; HALOT 1025); Ug. ẓl /ẓillu/ 'shade, shadow' (Huehnergard 2012, 161). ẓill-, written with SI and one RU for the geminate. Two attestations.

## As a name

The second recipient on HT 90, written as one word (GORILA I 143). SI-RU ṣillu 'shade, protection' (Amorite Ṣill-Haddu, Ṣillī-Baʿli) + MA-RI-TA₂ marʾ(i)tā 'the Lady' (Sabaic mrʾt 'lady, divine or human', Biella 283; Akkadian marʾatu, Aramaic mārtā): a 'Shade of DN' name with the Goddess's title.
