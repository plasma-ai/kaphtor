## Note

HT 85b .2 QE-KA 1, whole, among the one-man entries under KI-KI-RA-JA, after KI-RE-TA₂ 1 (GORILA I 133). HT 111a .2 \]QE-KA 8 is broken before, on the line above \]QA-QA-RU 8, so the word there can be longer; it stays out of the count. A royal text of Amenhotep II names qq, wr n qbꜥsmn 'chief of Qebaasumin', a chief of the Levant (Cairo JE 86763; PNM). Ugarit writes one name with either consonant, 'kkln (DLU I, 212) and qqln (4.35 ii 12-15; 4.66:2; 4.155:2 (all bn ...); PTU, 268.277)' (Watson 1996b, 100), as QE-KA has q then k.

Weak point: no root is offered, and the Egyptian spelling gives no vowels.

## As a name

One man on HT 85b; HT 111a \]QE-KA 8 is broken before. Beside qq, a chief of the Levant under Amenhotep II (Cairo JE 86763), and Ugaritic kkln, qqln (Watson 1996b, 100).
