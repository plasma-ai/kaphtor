## Note

HT 94b, under KI-RO: TU-MA 1 opens a list of five persons, 1 each, with PA-TA-NE, DE-DI, KE-KI-RU and SA-RU, totalled KU-RO 5. tuʾma 'twin', a birth-circumstance name, the medial ʾ unwritten: Heb. tôʾămîm 'twins' (Gen 25:24; 38:27), Akk. tū(ʾ)mu (HALOT 1694). The Amorite name TU-MA-NU-UM, Gelb's TUʾM-AN-UM, is built on the same word (Gelb 1980, no. 6114). One attestation.

## As a name

Akkadian tuʾāmu, Aramaic tōmā (= Thomas). First in 5-person roster on HT94b (LMIB).
