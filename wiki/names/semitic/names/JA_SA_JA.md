## Note

KN Zg 55, a steatite disk seal (CMS II 2, no. 213): face a a boar's head and knife, face b JA-SA-JA, the whole text, SA underlined in Younger's text (Younger, Linear A Texts, Other Texts: Knossos). Amorite JŠʿ 'to help', jišʿum 'help', and the hypocoristic name Jišʿ-ija (Gelb 1980, 22; no. 3768): ʿ unwritten, -JA the hypocoristic ending. Rival: an abbreviation of JA-SA-SA-RA (Younger). One attestation. See JA-SI and JA-SI-E.

## As a name

KN Zg 55, a steatite disk seal: face b JA-SA-JA, the whole text, in the owner's slot. Yašʿaya '(DN) has helped', hypocoristic, as Amorite Jišʿ-ija (Gelb 1980, no. 3768), ʿayin unwritten. Weak point: the root's interdental would be written in the T series, so the spelling needs a donor form after \*ṯ &gt; š. Rival: an abbreviation of JA-SA-SA-RA (Younger). One attestation.
