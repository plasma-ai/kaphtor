## Note

HT Wc 3007 WA-DI-NI, the only text, and HT Wc 3008 face a, opposite RU-JA-TA-DI; all signs whole and undotted (GORILA II 74). One word: WA is drawn small and square, as inside a word, where the cloth sign of HT Wc 3019, HT 16 and HT 20 stands tall or wide with four or five uprights, Pierini's two shapes (2022, 43). The roundels of Haghia Triada repeat one word on a pair, PA-SE-JA, TA-NI-KA, ZU-DI-RA; this scribe's other pair is \*333-DI-SU-KA and \*333-DI-NA-SU-KA, and WA-O-MI on HT Wc 3006 has the same build.

Waḏnī, as the Ugarit name wḏny, of unknown meaning (KTU 4.763:10; Watson 1990, 118): ḏ written with a d-sign, the vowel of DI dead. Rivals: WA · DI-NI, cloth and Dīnī 'My judgment', after the point GORILA's transcription prints on HT Wc 3008, as Alalakh Dīnī-Haddu (Gelb no. 1513); \*wadān- 'wild sheep', Ar. wadān- 'mouflon' (semitic_roots no. 2399), an animal received; Hurrian, Watson's 'cf. waš NPN 275'. Two witnesses.

## As a name

Waḏnī, as the Ugarit name wḏny, of unknown meaning (KTU 4.763:10; Watson 1990, 118); one word on two roundels, WA drawn square as inside a word (GORILA II 74). Rival: WA · DI-NI, cloth and Dīnī 'My judgment', after the point GORILA's transcription prints on HT Wc 3008.
