## Note

HT 96a .4 PI-TA-RA · 1, whole, in a grain list after SI-MI-TA GRA 5, RU-SA 4, \*323-\*317 1 and before \*323-\*344 1 (GORILA I 157). bi-da-la, the sign sequence of Ugarit bi-da-lu-ma, glossing LÚ.MEŠ DAM.GÀR 'merchants', and of the name DUMU-bi-da-la-ya (RS 15.0009); Ebla ba-da-lum 'merchant' (GlOS 1, 110). A body of agents drawing grain, cf. MA-KA-RI-TE 'merchants' (HT 117a). Rivals: bitāri 'portions', Emar bi-ta-ri (GlOS 1, 130); the name Paṭir 'released' (Gelb 1980, no. 1151; Ebla ba-du-ri₂, Pagan 1998, row 1090), with PI for pa; the name Batūl 'bachelor' (Gelb 1980, no. 1153); Ug. btlt 'virgin' (DULAT 248), for which the slot gives no cue. One witness.

## As a name

HT 96a .4 PI-TA-RA 1, whole, in a grain list (GORILA I 157): bidāla 'trade agents', the sign sequence of Ugarit bi-da-lu-ma, glossing LÚ.MEŠ DAM.GÀR 'merchants' (GlOS 1, 110). Rivals: bitāri 'portions' (GlOS 1, 130); the names Paṭir and Batūl (Gelb 1980, nos. 1151, 1153). See the lexicon entry. One witness.
