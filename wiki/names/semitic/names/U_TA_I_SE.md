## Note

KH 7b .2 U-TA-I-SE CYP+D ½\[, intact at the left edge, then PA-NA-TU CYP+D ½ (GORILA III 31); KH 7a lists \*313b-men and cyperus under E-NA-SI, I-JA-PA-ME · TA-TA and QA-TI-KI. \]U-TA-I-SI CYP+D\[ on KH 16 (Scribe 1) is the same entry in the other hand's spelling, as A-SE beside A-SI and I-SE beside I-SI. ʿUtay, the hypocoristic of 1 Chr 9:4 and Ezra 8:14 (BDB 738–39), and the Safaitic name ʿty (Harding 1971, 406), U for ʿu; built as MA-KA-I-SE Makī-se, a name in -I with -SE. Cf. LB u-ta-jo, the Knossos 'collector' (DMic I; Killen, Mycenaean Greek, glossary, s.v.). Rival: a Cretan name of another language. One intact witness.

## As a name

KH 7b .2 U-TA-I-SE CYP+D ½\[, a cyperus recipient; \]U-TA-I-SI on KH 16 in Scribe 1's spelling. ʿUtay, Hebrew ʿÛtay (1 Chr 9:4; Ezra 8:14; BDB 738–39) and Safaitic ʿty (Harding 1971, 406), with -SE, as MA-KA-I-SE. Rival: a Cretan name of another language, cf. LB u-ta-jo (DMic I). One witness.
