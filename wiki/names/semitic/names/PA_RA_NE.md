## Note

HT 115a: PA-RA-NE 1 in a GRA+BOSm list beside \*301-U-RA naura 'light', SE-KU-TU, KA-PO-RU and RI-SU-MA; HT 115b: ½; both by Scribe 8, who also writes A-PA-RA-NE (HT 96a and b).

Barān, A-PA-RA-NE ʿAbrān without its first syllable, PA writing b and final NE writing n, as in KA-DU-MA-NE Qadmān. Alalakh IV has Ap-ra beside Ap-ra-an (Wiseman 1953, 128), one name with and without -ān. Names shortened at the front keep the vowel, Ḫa-às-qú-da-an beside Às-qú-da-an (Huffmon 1965, 36). Ug. brn, written bu-ra-na, and prn (DULAT 236, 670) are personal names with these consonants and u in the first syllable.

Rival: A-PA-RA-NE is ha-Barān with the article and PA-RA-NE the bare name, as A-SI-KI-RA beside SI-KI-RA. Heb. Pāʾrān is a place (HALOT 909).
