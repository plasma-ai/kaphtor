## Note

HT 122a .1 U-DE-ZA 2, the first entry after \]RA-RI ·, in a list of names totalled KU-RO 31 (GORILA I 207); HT 122b .3 \]A-RA-JU-U-DE-ZẠ 2, Ḥudaṯu of ʾArāyu, the same count in the same account (GORILA I 208–209). u-de-za for ḥudaṯ(u): U writes ḥu with the guttural dropped, ZA ṯa. The Ugarit PN ḥdṯ, syllabic ḫu-da-ši, and ḥdṯn, ḫu-ud!-ša-nu (DULAT 351; Huehnergard 2008, 230). DE writes de for da.

## As a name

HT 122a .1 U-DE-ZA 2 in a list of names (GORILA I 207); HT 122b .3 \]A-RA-JU-U-DE-ZẠ 2, Ḥudaṯu of ʾArāyu, the same count (GORILA I 208–209). The Ugarit PN ḥdṯ, ḫu-da-ši, and ḥdṯn, ḫu-ud!-ša-nu (DULAT 351; Huehnergard 2008, 230).
