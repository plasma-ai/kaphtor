## Note

HT 97a: KA-RU VIR+KA 82 heads \*327 33, KA-NU-TI 25, PA-I-TO 6, DI 4, NA-TI 4, MA-DI 5, TA-TI 2, DE-\[ \] 3, men by place, KA-RU 82 their total (Younger, Linear A Texts). Ebla has the name ga-nu-ti (ARET 20 15 r. 3, 11). One witness.

Reading from the lexicon (di Mino). Ebla ga-nu-ti, a personal name (ARET 20 15 r. 3, 11).
