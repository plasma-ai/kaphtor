## Note

HT Zd 155, a graffito on stucco: A-JU · NA-MA-MA-TI-TI-A319, A-JU whole and undotted before the divider (GORILA IV 131), where HT Zd 157 has a single stroke before its divider; NA-MA holds WI-JA-SU's slot before MA-TI-TI-A319. A writes ḥa, JU yu: Ḥayyu, the masculine of A-JA ḥayyā, as the Ugaritic name ḥy (DULAT 376) and Amorite ḤAJJ-UM (Gelb 1980, no. 2044). One attestation. Cf. A-JA.

## As a name

HT Zd 155 A-JU · NA-MA-MA-TI-TI-A319, before the divider, where HT Zd 157 has a single stroke (GORILA IV 131). Ugaritic PN ḥy (III), bn ḥy (DULAT 376); Amorite ḤAJJ-UM (Gelb 1980, no. 2044). See the lexicon entry.
