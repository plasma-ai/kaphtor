## Note

KH 14 .1 \]RA · A-MA-JA · CYP 6\[, whole and undotted between dividers, a cyperus recipient (GORILA III 45). A-MA ʿamma 'kinsman' + hypocoristic -ya, as A-SU-JA, PA-SE-JA: the Ugaritic name ʿmy ʿAmmaya, syllabic am-ma-ya, seven times in the lists of men (KTU 4.16:4, 4.197:26, 4.399:8, 4.592:4; as a patronym 4.98:14, 4.117:6, 4.357:25; DULAT 164), and an eighth in KTU 3.10 (RIH 84/33), lines 8–12, which DULAT does not register (Burlingame 2020, 197 n. 3). Rival: ʾAmaya, short for ʾamat- 'maidservant' (Ug. amt, DULAT 71), with t lost. Its Ugaritic match ảmy (KTU 2.76:3) has the spelling a-mu-ya beside it, and the spelling am-ma-ia (RS 88.2158:31') 'may belong to ʕmy' (Watson 2016, 337 and n. 23). One attestation.

## As a name

KH 14 .1 \]RA · A-MA-JA · CYP 6\[, whole between dividers (GORILA III 45). A-MA ʿamma 'kinsman' + -ya: the Ugaritic name ʿmy ʿAmmaya (KTU 4.16:4), syllabic am-ma-ya (DULAT 164). Rival: ʾAmaya 'maidservant'.
