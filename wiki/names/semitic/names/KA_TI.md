## Note

HT 63 .1 KA-TI 1, both signs whole and undotted, under the personnel logogram \*305, after TE 1 and before the name SU-PU₂ (GORILA I 118–119). The name Qāti 'Hand', as Ebla ga-ti (Pagan 1998, row 1975); KA writes qa. Rivals: Gattī, gat 'press' with the nisbe -ī, the Ugaritic gt being both the press and the estate around it; Qəhātî 'Kohathite' (Num 3:27), the medial h unwritten; Kittî 'Kittian' (Gen 10:4 Kittîm), against the a of KA. One witness. See QE-TI.

## As a name

HT 63, under the personnel logogram \*305: the name Qāti 'Hand', as Ebla ga-ti (Pagan 1998, row 1975). Rivals: Gattī 'the Gittite' (DULAT 307; 2 Sam 6:10), Qəhātî 'Kohathite' (Num 3:27), Kittî 'Kittian'. See the lexicon entry.
