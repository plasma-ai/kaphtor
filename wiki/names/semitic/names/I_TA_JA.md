## Note

HT 28a: I-TA-JA OLE+DI 10, the last recipient of the A-SI-JA-KA list, after A-RU-DA-RA GRA 5. Gemination unwritten; -aya written JA. Hebrew ʾIttay, the short form of ʾItīʾēl 'With me is God', Akk. Ili-ittia (HALOT 102, 44): Ittai the Gittite, who marches with the Cherethites and Pelethites (2 Sam 15:18–19), and Ittai the Benjaminite (2 Sam 23:29). Rival: Noth's 'El exists' (HALOT 44). Linear B i-ta-ja is a woman's name at Knossos (KN Ap 769.2): the name outlives Linear A on Crete, which fixes the name but not its language. Two consonants, one attestation.

## As a name

Heb. ʾIttay (2 Sam 15:19); NA {m}i-ta-a-a (Schøyen 52).

## Linear B

Exact Linear B continuity: i-ta-ja, KN Ap 769 (women personnel) + Od(1) 537 (wool). Screening caveat: could be Greek-formed ethnicon \*Idaiā 'of Ida', though Ida is itself a pre-Greek oronym—either way the base is substrate.
