## Note

HT 115a .1–2 RỊ-TẠ-MẠ, all three signs underdotted in both copies, then NU-WI 2; the copie tabulaire joins RỊ-TẠ-MẠ-NU-WI, GORILA's note on .2 allows the divider (GORILA I 189). A person, as TI-NU-JA · NU-WI on HT 115b. rit-mā, TA's vowel echoing MA: Ritmâ, where Israel encamped (Num 33:18), beside NU-WI 'steppe-dweller'. Rival: Arabian rtm rathîm 'broken-nosed' (Harding 1971, 270), which needs TA for ṯ. One witness.

## As a name

HT 115a .1–2 RỊ-TẠ-MẠ · NU-WI 2, all three signs underdotted (GORILA I 189). Ritmā, √r-t-m 'broom': Hebrew Ritmâ, a wilderness encampment (Num 33:18); Arabian PN rtm (Harding 1971, 269). See the lexicon entry.
