## Note

ZA 14 .4 PA-NU-QE 2, after TU-MI-TI-ZA-SE 45 and before JA-WI\[, a recipient among names (GORILA III 181). panūq, a qatūl passive of √p-n-q 'pamper, indulge' on primary values (Heb. pānaq, BDB 819; Syr. paneq 'pamper', Payne Smith 451). Rival: the Amorite name Banuq, BA-NU-KU-UM (Gelb no. 1132), which reads PA as ba. One witness.

## As a name

ZA 14 .4 PA-NU-QE 2, a recipient (GORILA III 181). Panūq 'Pampered', √p-n-q (Heb. pānaq, BDB 819). Rival: Amorite Banuq, BA-NU-KU-UM (Gelb no. 1132). One witness.
