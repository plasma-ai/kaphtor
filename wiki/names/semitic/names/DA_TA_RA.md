## Note

HT 6a .1–2 KA-PA · DA-TA-RA · TE · NI 15, heading a fig entry (GORILA I 13). HT 88 .6 DA-TA-RE 1, in the KI-RO roster before KU-RO 6 (GORILA I 139), the same name. daṯar 'old', the masculine of DA-SE-RA-TE Dašurat: Amorite DA-ŠU-RU-UM beside fem. DA-ŠU-RA-TUM (Gelb nos. 1490, 1489). TA writes ṯ as in TA-RA ṯa(ġ)ra, where DA-SE-RA-TE writes it SE. Rival: ḏā-ṯaġri 'He of the gate', ḏā as in DA-MI-NU + ṯaġr- as in TA-RA, a type with no attested name.

## As a name

HT 6a .1–2 KA-PA · DA-TA-RA · TE · NI 15 (GORILA I 13). daṯar 'old', the masculine of DA-SE-RA-TE: Amorite DA-ŠU-RU-UM (Gelb no. 1490). Also DA-TA-RE (HT 88). Rival: ḏā-ṯaġri 'He of the gate' (TA-RA).
