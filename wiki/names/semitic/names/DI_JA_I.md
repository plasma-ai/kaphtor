## Note

HT 29 .3 DI-JA-I 2, all three signs whole, in a list of names counted in ones and twos (GORILA I 55). Diʾyay 'Kite': Ugaritic dỉy, a raptor (DULAT 257), Hebrew dāʾâ 'red kite' and dayyâ 'bird of prey' (HALOT 207, 220), with the ʾ unwritten and -I the hypocoristic -ay. The root is a name in Arabia, Safaitic dʾy and dʾyt (Harding 1971, 233; Dirbas 2017, 260), and Ebla has the ending on a raptor name, Ba-za-a Bāz-ay (Dirbas 2017, 259). One attestation.

## As a name

HT 29 .3 DI-JA-I 2, at the head of the line, on a tablet written over an erasure (GORILA I 55). Diʾyay 'Kite': Ugaritic dỉy, a raptor (DULAT 257), Hebrew dāʾâ and dayyâ (HALOT 207, 220), the Safaitic names dʾy and dʾyt (Harding 1971, 233; Dirbas 2017, 260). See the lexicon entry.
