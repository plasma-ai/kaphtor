## Note

HT 117a, a personnel list: NA-DA-RE 1, beside MI-TU, KU-PA₃-NU and TE-JA-RE. Nadr 'Vow', √n-d-r 'to vow': G /nadara/, prefix /yaddur-/ with the n assimilated (Huehnergard 2012), BH nādar. Amorite NDR 'to vow' (Gelb 1980, §0.7) gives nadir- 'vowed one' as a name, which fits the roster slot, and LB no-da-ro (KN As(1) 609, personnel) keeps the n-d-r frame. Rival: Gordon's Egyptian name 'of-the-hand-of-Re'. One attestation.

## As a name

HT 117a personnel list (1), beside MI-TU, KU-PA₃-NU and TE-JA-RE. √n-d-r 'to vow': a name on the noun 'vow', cf. Amorite NDR (Gelb). Rival: Gordon's Egyptian personal name 'of the hand of Re'.

## Linear B

Linear B comparandum: no-da-ro (two vowel shifts, consonant frame n-d-r intact), KN As(1) 609 (personnel) + Dc 1228 (shepherd). A √n-d-r 'vow' name-type on both sides of the script divide, matching the Amorite NDR / Ugaritic ndr parallels.
