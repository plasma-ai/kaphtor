## Note

HT 28b: PU-RA₂ figs 6, a recipient between SA-RA₂ the queen and JA-\*21F Yāh; HT 116a: grain 40 and oil 5, after the name KU-PA-JA. Gordon's Bōl, with the proposed values PU = bV and RA₂ = l: aʿ shifts to ō, baʿl- &gt; bōl- as naʿl- &gt; nōl- 'sandal', whence the value of the sandal sign NO (Gordon 1966, §§150, 167); Palmyrene has Bôl, as he notes: Lagrange and Caquot take Bôl as a contraction of Baʿal, Starcky and Dussaud do not, and the difficulty is the lost ʿayin (Du Mesnil du Buisson 1960, 150). Ebla has bare ba-lu, ba₄-lum 'lord' as personal names (Pagan 1998, rows 1110, 1153). ZA 6a \]PU₂-RA₂ GRA+PA 20 is a candidate for the same name at Zakros, with PU₂ = bu, in the same name-with-grain slot; the line is broken before PU₂, so the word can be longer. RA₂ = l stands without this word: SA-RA₂ šarra writes the marked liquid, as Linear B ra₂ /rya, lya/ (Judson 2020, 72, 74), and KN 54 writes PU-RA with plain RA. Rival: with RA₂ = lya, the name Bulya, Ugaritic bly, bu-lu-ya (Kinlaw 1967, 46), or ply, pí-la-ya (DULAT 663). Two attestations.

## As a name

PU-RA₂ occurs as an allocation heading on HT28b and HT116a. The Baʿal/Bōl comparison is semantically suitable but requires PU=/bV/, RA₂=/l/, loss of ʿayin, and an acknowledged U/O correspondence. The reading remains CANDIDATE and does not by itself establish a cultic document.
