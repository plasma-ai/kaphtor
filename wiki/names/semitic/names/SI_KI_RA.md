## Note

HT 8a: JE-DI · OLE+KI 10, then PA₃-KA-RA-TI 1½, PA 3½, TE-\*301 2, QA-\*310-I ¾, SI-KI-RA ¼, KI-RE-TA-NA ½; a recipient of KI-oil beside Tēnā 'Fig', the smith and the Cretan. √ś-k-r 'to hire' (Ug. /š-k-r/ I, DULAT 804; Heb. śākār 'wages', HALOT 1330): at Mari Sa-ki-rum and the woman's name Sa-ki-ra, under \*škr 'hire, reward, favor' (Huffmon, APNM 245–46), Sa-ki-rum compared to Hebrew śākîr 'hired; hireling' (APNM 145); Gelb, AS 21, no. 5400 ŠAKIR-U (SA-KI-RU). Rival: Hebrew שֵׁכָר šēkār 'strong drink' (HALOT 1501; Lev 10:9), √š-k-r 'to become intoxicated' (Ug. /š-k-r/ II, DULAT 804). A-SI-KI-RA (KH 20) stands in the entry slot before CYP+E, parallel to \]DU-RE-ZA. One witness.

## As a name

HT 8a, a recipient of KI-oil ¼ between QA-\*310-I and KI-RE-TA-NA, in the list with TE-\*301 Tēnā 'Fig'. Mari Sa-ki-rum and the woman's name Sa-ki-ra, under \*škr 'hire, reward, favor' (Huffmon, APNM 245–46). Rival: šēkār 'strong drink'. A-SI-KI-RA (KH 20) is the same name with the article ha-. One witness.
