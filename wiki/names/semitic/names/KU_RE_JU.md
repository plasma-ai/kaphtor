## Note

The HT 117b roster, headed \*21F-TU-NE, fixes the personal-name slot, KU-RE-JU 1 before DI-KI-SE 1; on HT 39 KU-RE-JU takes 8. The quantity 8 on HT 39 and DI-KI-SE, a name in the same layout, show a name.

kull + ʾēlu, 'El is all': KU writes kul, the geminate unwritten, RE carries the l and the ē once the ʾ drops at the join, and JU writes lu, the nominative of ʾēlu, as in SA-RE-JU Šarr-ēlu and PI-RU-E-JU Pirʾu-ʾēlu. kull is KU-RO 'all, total', Ugaritic kl 'totality' (DULAT 432–433).

Amorite has the same two elements in the same order in KU-LA-IL-E (Gelb 1980, no. 4114), with the vowel open before ʾil where Linear A contracts it. kull stands beside a god in Baʿlu-kullim 'Lord of all', ba-aḫ-lu-ku-li-im, at Tuttul (Buck 2018, 325; Gelb 1980, no. 1031), the element Huffmon lists as kul(im) (1965, 221), and in be-el-ku-ul-la (GlOS 1, 305).

The root makes names in each branch. Hebrew has the bare noun, Kᵉlāl 'perfection', a man of the Return (Ezra 10:30). Akkadian names a child for the wreath, kilīlu 'circlet, headband': Ilum-kilīlī 'God is my wreath' (Ur III), Irra-kilīlī (Old Babylonian), Nanā-kilīlī-uṣri (CAD K 358), and the one-word name {m}ki-li-lu at Middle Assyrian Dunni-Aššur (Tell Sabi Abyad), T 98-038 obv. 4' (ORACC tcma/tsa1, P532066, normalized Kulilu there). The D adjective kullulu 'crowned, veiled' (CAD K 518) is the Neo-Assyrian name Kullulu, {1}ku-lu-lu, SAA 6 22 rev. 9'.

Weak point: KU-LA-IL-E is the one name of this build in the name lists, and Gelb prints it unanalysed. Two documents. See SA-RE-JU, PI-RU-E-JU and KU-RO.

## As a name

The HT 117b roster fixes the personal-name slot. kull + ʾēlu, with -RE-JU writing -l-ēlu as in SA-RE-JU Šarr-ēlu; Amorite KU-LA-IL-E has the same two elements (Gelb 1980, no. 4114): see lexicon KU-RE-JU.
