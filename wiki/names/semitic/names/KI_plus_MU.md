## Note

HT 127b KI+MU 15, after VIR+\*313c 24 and before \*301 11, with an erased KI+MU 14, KU-RO 292, and HT 26b KI+MU 12, after KA-U-ZU-NI 4: a category label on personnel registers. A two-sign onset abbreviation of kumru 'priest' (√k-m-r): Akk. kumru 'a priest' (CAD K 534), Old Syrian kamr, kumr 'a priest' (GlOS 1, 309), Emar ka-ma-ri 'priests' (Pentiuc 2001, 95), Heb. kōmer (HALOT 482). Weak point: the one fuller spelling, HT 96a \]KU-MA-RO, is broken before KU. Two attestations.
