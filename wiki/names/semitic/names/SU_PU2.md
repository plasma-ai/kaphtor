## Note

HT 63 .1 SU-PU₂, whole and undotted, the third entry under \*305 · TE 1, after KA-TI 1, against the tablet's upper right corner; no numeral survives, and GORILA's normalized line brackets the end, SU-PU₂\[ (GORILA I 118–119). ṣu-bu, PU₂ for bu as in DU-PU₂-RE dubur, the final ʾu unwritten as in TA-PA tappāʾu: the Amorite name Ṣubʾu (Gelb 1980, nos. 6581–6582). Linear B su-po (KN Dc 5812) and su-pu-wo (KN C 912) are men's names at Knossos (DMic II 304). Cf. HT 8b .1 SU-PU₂-\*188 1. Rival: ṣābu 'workers, troop' (#1332), a collective under the personnel sign \*305. One witness.

## As a name

Ṣubʾu, the Amorite name ṢUBUʾ-UM (Gelb 1980, AS 21, 652, nos. 6581–6582) and Eblaite zu-bu₃ (Pagan 1998, row 5264), the third entry under the personnel sign \*305 on HT 63. Rival: ṣābu 'workers, troop' (semitic_roots no. 1332).
