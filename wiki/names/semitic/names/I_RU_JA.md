## Note

HT 7a .2 I-RU-JA 3, all three signs whole and undotted, the first entry under .1 QE-TI · VIR+\[?\], before DU-JA 4 (GORILA I 15). ʾIlūya, 'My god', with RU writing lu: Mari has i-lu-ia, ʾilūya 'My gods', for a man of Qaṭna (ARM 18 61) and a Sutean (ARM 22 167; Buck 2018, 338), and Ugarit the name ily, syllabic i-lu-ya (RS 11.856:6), beside DINGIR-ya and i-li-ya (DULAT 66; Kinlaw 1967, 64). Both give I-RU-JA sign for sign, and the tablet's other entries are Semitic.

Rival: Nuzi Iruja, I-ru-ia, var. E-ru-ú-ia, I-ru-ú-ia, the 'Hypocoristic for Irup-tešup' (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, s.v. IRUJA, IRUP-TEŠUP; 220), with RU for ru. One attestation.

## As a name

ʾIlūya 'My god': Mari i-lu-ia (Buck 2018, 338) and Ugarit i-lu-ya (DULAT 66), RU writing lu. Rival: Nuzi Iruja, I-ru-ia, the hypocoristic of Irup-tešup (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, s.v. IRUJA; 220). One witness.
