## Note

HT 117a: U-DI-MI 1, the seventh of the ten men in KU-RO 10, on the list with U-SU ʾUšu (mainland Tyre) and, in its second section, KO-SA-I-TI 'the Kushite woman'. ʾUdmī 'the Udumite': Udum, King Pabil's city, which Kirta besieges, Ugaritic udm rbt 'Udum the great' (KTU 1.14 III 30), Amarna ᵘʳᵘú-du-mu (EA 256:24) (DULAT 18–19), set east of Lake Tiberias or at Admum (RGTC 12/2, 304). Udumites are listed at Ugarit, where ủdmym receive garments (KTU 4.337:15; DULAT 19). Watson glosses udm 'Red (?)' (Watson 2001, 112).

udm is \*ʾudum-, the form of Hebrew ʾēdōm (UVST 275 n. 39), Edom, '? orig. the area distinguished by red soil' (HALOT 12); HALOT 16 reads Adami-nekeb as 'red (place) at the pass', with a queried Ugaritic udm. The gentilic is ʾudumī, syncopated ʾudmī; DI takes the vowel of MI, as KI writes the k of -kr- in SU-KI-RI-TA Sukrita. The unsyncopated form would be written U-DU-MI. One attestation. See U-SU.
