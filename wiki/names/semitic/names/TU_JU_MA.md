## Note

HT 117a: TU-JU-MA 1 in the roster under MA-KA-RI-TE, 'merchants'. √ʿ-l-m 'to be hidden' (HALOT 834), ʿ unwritten, JU writing lu. Rival: √t-l-m, Heb. telem 'furrow' (HALOT 1740). One attestation.
