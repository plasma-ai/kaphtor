## Note

HT 122b .2 \*306-KI-TA₂ 7, all three signs whole, the first entry under JE-DI 'the Idaean' · \*346 · VIR (GORILA I 208–209). ʿAkkīta, the feminine gentilic of Akko, with \*306 at the proposed value ʿa and the geminate unwritten: Ugaritic ʿky, town and personal name, syllabic URU a-ki-ya and ak-ku-ya (DULAT 150–151); Phoenician-Punic ʿKY 'Akkoite', 'gentilic as a name' (Benz 1972, 378). The feminine in -t follows Phoenician LBT and MṢRT (Benz 1972, 238) and Ugaritic alṯt 'from alṯy' (Segert 1995, §4.1). One attestation.

## As a name

HT 122b .2 \*306-KI-TA₂ 7, at the head of the line, the left edge whole beside \*306, the first entry under JE-DI 'the Idaean' (GORILA I 208–209). ʿAkkīta, the feminine gentilic of Akko: Ugaritic ʿky, town and personal name (DULAT 150–151); Phoenician-Punic ʿKY 'Akkoite', 'gentilic as a name' (Benz 1972, 378).
