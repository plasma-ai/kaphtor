## Note

HT 26a .3–4 U-NU-\*21F 5\[, whole and undotted, the numeral broken after (GORILA I 47): the last surviving entry under the cup heading \*406VAS+KE · \*312-TE-TE, after TA-TI 21 and MI-KI-SE-NA 15; side b counts men (VIR) under the same heading. \*21F writes the guttural: U-NU-\*21F spells ᵘʳᵘÚ-nu-ḫi, the town of a witness, 'man of Unuḫu', in a legal text from Ugarit (RS 17.251 \[PRU 4 236f.\]:19f.; RGTC 12/2, 324, location unknown).
