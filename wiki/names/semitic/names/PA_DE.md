## Note

HT 9a SA-RO · TE · VIN · PA-DE · 5¾, the first entry of the wine roster; HT 9b PẠ-DẸ 3, both signs dotted (GORILA I 18–19); HT 122a PA-DE 1. HT 122a recopies the HT 9 roster, so the three are one context. Ugaritic pdy (II) is a personal name, spelled syllabically pa-di/dì-ya, etym. unc. (DULAT 653); Padi is also the king of Ekron in Sennacherib's annals (RINAP 3, Sennacherib 4). DE writes the final ī. Rival: pa-de, a divine recipient at Knossos (KN Fp 1, Fp 48; DMic II 65–66), which Landau compares with PA-DE (Landau, Namen 94, apud DMic II 66).

## As a name

Gordon direct match. Part of consistent HT work gang alongside DI-NA-U (Dīnāyu), Kubanu, Yamidare (Egyptian theophoric). Receives 5.75 on HT9a and 3 on HT9b, and appears in a personnel headcount (1 on HT122a). NWS name with Philistine parallels. Ugaritic PN pdy (II), syll. pa-di, pa-di-ya (DULAT 653); Padi, king of Ekron (ORACC). Rival: Knossian divine recipient pa-de (DMic II).

## Linear B

PA-DE is also attested as a theonym at Knossos in the Linear B Fp offering series, receiving regular oil allocations (Killen 2024, Ch. XIX). Dual Linear A personal-name / Linear B theonym attestation strengthens the NWS reading Padi (√p-d-y). Cf. Padi king of Ekron—NWS theophoric from a Caphtorian-origin population.
