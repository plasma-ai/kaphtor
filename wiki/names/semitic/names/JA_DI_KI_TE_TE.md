## Note

PK Za 8 JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-\*307-PU₂-RE, and PK Za 15 \] · JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-DU-PU₂-RE \[, a divider before JA and the stone broken after RE (GORILA IV 41): one sign group with dubur each time, yā Diqqitet dubur 'O Diktaean of the sanctuary'. JA- is the vocative yā of JA-DI-KI-TU, written in full where PK Za 11 and PK Za 12 write A-. TE-TE is the feminine nisbe, read -et as written: JA-DI-KI-TU ~ JA-DI-KI-TE-TE as \*312-TA ~ \*312-TE-TE (Davis 2026, 46). Two attestations, both at Palaikastro. Cognates, rival and weak point under A-DI-KI-TE-TE.

## As a name

PK Za 8 JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-\*307-PU₂-RE and PK Za 15 JA-DI-KI-TE-TE-DU-PU₂-RE, one group with dubur each time (GORILA IV 41). JA- and A- spell the one vocative yā: Palaikastro writes JA-DI-KI-TE-TE on PK Za 8 and PK Za 15 and A-DI-KI-TE-TE on PK Za 11 and PK Za 12, the same name before the same dubur, and PK Za 11 writes A-SA-SA-RA-ME for JA-SA-SA-RA-ME as well. See the lexicon entry. Two witnesses.
