## Note

HT 88 .3 NI · KI-KI-NA 7: after the fig logogram NI and before the quantity, a recipient, group or other named entity, 'figs: Kikkina, 7'. Amorite has the close name KIKKIN, written KI-IK-KI-NI, KI-IK-KI-NU and KI-IK-KI-NUM (Gelb 1980, nos. 4036–4038). Ugaritic kkn is a personal name of unknown etymology (DULAT 429). One attestation.

## As a name

HT88 places the name after the NI fig logogram and before 7. Gelb's Amorite onomasticon repeatedly writes KIKKIN-I/U/UM as KI-IK-KI-NI, KI-IK-KI-NU and KI-(IK)-KI-NU-UM. The Ugaritic 'pot' comparison fails because the vessel word is kknt, with final -t; Ugaritic kkn is itself a personal name of uncertain etymology.
