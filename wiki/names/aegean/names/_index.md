# Aegean names

[[wiki/names/aegean/_index|..]]

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/MA_RI|MA_RI]]: Mari: Mari (place name; Linear B ma-ri); rival Mari on the Euphrates. Aegean, SPECULATIVE.

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/RI_KA_TA|RI_KA_TA]]: Likata: Lyktos (place name); compare Egyptian Ri-ka-ta. Aegean, SPECULATIVE.

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/RU_SA|RU_SA]]: Rusa: personal name; compare Egyptian rw-sȝ, one of the 'names of Crete' on BM EA 5647; rival Egyptian Rs 'he who is awake'. Aegean, SPECULATIVE.

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/WI_NA_DU|WI_NA_DU]]: Winadu: Inatos (place name on the south coast; Linear B wi-na-to). Aegean, SPECULATIVE.

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/WI_SA|WI_SA]]: Wisa: Wisa (personal name). Aegean, SPECULATIVE.

[[wiki/names/aegean/names/star305_WA_NA|star305_WA_NA]]: Dawana: Dawana (personal name). Aegean, SPECULATIVE.
