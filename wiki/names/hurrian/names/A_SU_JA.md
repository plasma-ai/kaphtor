## Note

HT 11a .3–4 A-SU-JA 1, A at the end of .3 and SU-JA on .4, whole and undotted, the first entry after KU-RO 10 (GORILA I 23). Spelled as Nuzi A-zu-ia: SU writes zu, JA the hypocoristic -ya. Rival: Ugaritic PN ùsy (DULAT 111).

## As a name

HT 11a .3–4 A-SU-JA 1, the first entry after KU-RO 10 (GORILA I 23). Spelled as Nuzi A-zu-ia. See the lexicon entry.
