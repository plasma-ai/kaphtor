## Note

HT 13 .1 KA-U-DE-TA, heading the wine list VIN · TE · to RE-ZA, TE-TU, TE-KI, KU-ZU-NI, DA-SI-\*118 and I-DU-NE-SI, KU-RO 130½ (GORILA I 27). KA-U = kaw-, as A-RA-U-DA ʾArwada: Hurrian kaw (NPN 224; Kawiya, Monti 2017, 346) + tatt- 'love' (NPN 263). Terqa writes Tatta da-da, da-da-e (Monti 2017, 325): D for t, and e beside a. Cf. Kawinni, written Qa-wi-in-ni, Qa-we-en-ni, Ka-wi-ni and Ga-wi-in (NPN 224), and Šenna-tati, written Še-en-da-ti (NPN 263). Both elements are attested, never together. Rivals: the Ugaritic name kwt, which leaves DE (DULAT 469); the island Kaudos (Gavdos), Linear B ka-u-da (KN Fs 21), barley and flour from the island, HT 13 then wine sent to it (Peruzzi 1957, 35–36). One witness.

## As a name

Kaw-tatta: Hurrian kaw (NPN 224) + tatt- 'love' (NPN 263), Tatta written da-da, da-da-e at Terqa (Monti 2017, 325), heading the HT 13 wine list. Cf. Kawinni, written Qa-wi-in-ni, Qa-we-en-ni, Ka-wi-ni and Ga-wi-in (NPN 224). Rival: the Ugaritic name kwt (DULAT 469).
