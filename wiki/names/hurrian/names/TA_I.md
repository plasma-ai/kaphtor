## Note

HT 9a TA-I 2½ and HT 9b TA-I 2, a recipient of wine beside A-RU Ḫaru; HT 39 TA-I 10 at the head; each time joined to \*123, the aromatics sign; I dotted on HT 9b (GORILA I 19). ta-ḫi sign for sign, ḫ unwritten as in A-RU: the Hurrian name Taḫe 'Man', ta-ḫe at Mari, ta-ḫi at Ašnakkum and Karana, ta-ḫe and ta-ḫi-e at Šubat-Enlil, ta-aḫ-e at Alalaḫ VII, with the short form Tae, ta-e, at Mari (Monti 2017, 297–98, 350, 374, 393, 483); Mari TA-ḪI (Gelb 1980, no. 6007). The element stands in A-RE-DA-I Ar-tae. Weak point: \*123 on every witness, perhaps the man's trade, as Linear B sets AROM by an unguent-boiler (PY Un 249.2). Three attestations.

## As a name

Taḫe 'Man', the Hurrian name ta-ḫe at Mari and ta-ḫi at Ašnakkum and Karana (Monti 2017, 298, 374, 393), ḫ unwritten as in A-RU; a recipient of wine on HT 9a and 9b, always with \*123.
