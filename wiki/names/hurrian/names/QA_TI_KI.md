## Note

KH 7a .4 QA-TI-KI VIR+\*313b 4, CYP+D ⅓, the entry under I-JA-PA-ME · TA-TA · (GORILA III 29). kat(t)- + the formative -ki (NPN 224), on kad- 'to say' (Richter 2012, 195). Nuzi writes the base with QA (Qa-ti-ri), as QA-TI-DA-TE Kati-tati. Rival: katki 'utterance' (Richter 2012, 196), a noun that cannot draw men. One witness.

## As a name

Kattike: Hurrian kat(t)- + the formative -ki (NPN 224), on kad- 'to say' (Richter 2012, 195), drawing four \*313b-men on KH 7a. Rival: katki 'utterance' (Richter 2012, 196).
