## Note

HT 37 .1 KA-KỊ, after the heading MI+JA+I, and .5 ḲA-KI 11, after KI-RO 'owed' (GORILA I 70–71). Each line dots a different sign: KA keeps its inner cross on .1, KI is whole on .5, and the sign before it there has the slanted oval of .1's KA. The Hurrian name Kakki, kakk=i: ka-ak-ki at Mari, ga-ki-i at Kaneš, Kakki-še at Karana (Monti 2017, 252, 388, 453), Qa-ak-ki at Nuzi (NPN 78); the element stands in QA-KI-SE-NU-TI. Rival: Amorite Kakku, as KA-KU. Weak point: on .5 one tick survives inside KA, no cross. Two attestations, one tablet.

## As a name

Kakki, the Hurrian name ka-ak-ki at Mari and ga-ki-i at Kaneš (Monti 2017, 252, 453); twice on HT 37, the second time owing 11 after KI-RO. Each line dots a different sign.
