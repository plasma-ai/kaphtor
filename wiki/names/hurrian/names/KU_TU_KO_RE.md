## Note

ZA 8 .2 KU-TU-KO-RE with a fraction, whole and undotted, an entry in the fig issue headed KI-RA · (GORILA III 165). ku-tu-k(k)o-re: the Hurrian base kutukk- of Nuzi Kutukka (NPN 93), with the suffix -re of Antare and Humere (NPN 248), the geminate unwritten. Base and suffix are each attested, not together. One witness.

## As a name

Kutukkore, the Hurrian base kutukk- of Nuzi Kutukka (NPN 93) + -re (NPN 248); an entry in the ZA 8 fig issue.
