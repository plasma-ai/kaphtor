## Note

PK 1 .1 KA-QA 2, after SI-\[·\]-NE-TI 1, the only entry with 2 in a roster of names (GORILA I 281). The Hurrian woman's name Kakka, kakk=a, at Mari and Alalaḫ VII (Monti 2017, 252, 475): the base of KA-KI Kakki, with QA for k as in QA-KI-SE-NU-TI and QA-TI-KI; TA₂-TI-TE Tati-te, Hurrian, stands on the same tablet. Weak point: Younger marks QA doubtful, and the copie tabulaire draws the second sign as a wide oval with knobs on a stem. One witness.

## As a name

PK 1 .1 KA-QA 2, after SI-\[·\]-NE-TI 1 (GORILA I 281). Kakka, the Hurrian woman's name ka-ak-ka at Mari and Alalaḫ VII (Monti 2017, 252, 475), the base of KA-KI Kakki. Younger marks QA as doubtful. See the lexicon entry.
