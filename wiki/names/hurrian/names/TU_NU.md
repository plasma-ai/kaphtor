## Note

KA Zf 1, a bronze double axe from Kardamoutsa (BM 1954,10-20.1), TU and NU · on either side of the shaft hole, whole and undotted (GORILA IV 149). tu-nu on primary values: the Hurrian element tun- (NPN 269), as in the Nuzi name Tun-tešup (NPN 158). One witness.

## As a name

Tunu, the Hurrian element tun- (NPN 269), as in Nuzi Tun-tešup (NPN 158); on a bronze double axe.
