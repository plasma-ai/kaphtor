## Note

PH 2 .4 SE-SA-PA₃ 60, the last entry before the break, intact and undotted (GORILA I 288–289). Mari Zizap(-)a, zi-za-ba (VHN 277), and Zizapān, ziz=a=b=ān (ARM XVI/1, 244), on the stem ziz- of Zizi, ziz=i 'Chest' (Monti 2017, 319). Sign for sign, PA₃ for b, SE for zi. Same tablet as A-SE-TU-\*21F Aštuḫḫa. Rival: Amorite ʿIziz-ab (Gelb 1980, no. 2677), ʿzz + ʾab, with the initial unwritten. One witness.

## As a name

Zizapa: Mari Zizap(-)a, zi-za-ba (VHN 277), Zizapān, ziz=a=b=ān (ARM XVI/1, 244), stem ziz- 'chest' (Monti 2017, 319). On PH 2 with Aštuḫḫa. Rival: Amorite ʿIziz-ab (Gelb 1980, no. 2677).
