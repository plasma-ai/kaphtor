## Note

HT 18 .1 PA-SE GRA+QE 20, OLE+KI 2, \*304 3, a section before SA-RA₂ GRA 10, NI 10; HT 27b PA-SE · VIN+WA · SA 4, RE 70\[, parallel to \]MI-DA · VIN+WA · SA 7. A section holder that never carries its own quantity. The bare name of PA-SE-JA Paššiya, as Paššiya is the hypocoristic of Pašši-tilla at Nuzi (Gelb, Purves, and MacRae 1943, 112, 243). Cf. PA-SE-JA.

## As a name

Heads HT 18 before SA-RA₂ and the second section of HT 27b, never with its own quantity. Hurrian pašš 'send', pa-aš-še, Mari 6:20 (NPN 243); Nuzi Paššija, Pašši-tilla (NPN 112). The bare name of PA-SE-JA Paššiya. See the lexicon entry.
