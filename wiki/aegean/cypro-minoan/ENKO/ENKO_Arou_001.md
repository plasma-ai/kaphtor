Enkomi baked clay cylinder (##097, CM 1, 27 lines, 182 tokens, 47 legible groups including single-sign entries; excavated 1967, Schaeffer et al. 1968, 266–269; Valério 2016, 404–407, 420–424). Tripartite structure: Part I heading (.01), Part II entry-marked list (.02–.13) repeatedly using an X \| Y-o-ti formula, and Part III continuous script (.13–.27) organized around recurring thematic stems (ta-la-ka, sa-ra-ni, sa-ri-jo-ti) with productive 069- (ja-) prefixing (.17, .27).

Thirty-six of 47 legible groups (31 of 42 multi-sign groups) read on the ruled grid. Twelve distinct multi-sign groups end in -023 (-ti), splitting into -po-ti (×3), -ro-ti (×3) and -jo-ti (×3), matching 1st-millennium Eteocypriot genitive/possessive -o-ti (Valério 2016, 398–400, 421–424). Shares exact parallels with CM 3 RASH Atab 001 (019-023 u-ti), KOUR Psce 001 (027-013-110-097-023 si-to-ku-ro-ti) and IDAL Avas 001 / TIRY Abou 001 (041-041-097 zi-zi-ro).
