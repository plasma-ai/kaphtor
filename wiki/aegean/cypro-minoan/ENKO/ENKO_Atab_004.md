Enkomi baked clay tablet (##209, 'tablette n° 3', CM 2, 85 legible groups; Valério 2016, 527–538). Entered on Valério's critical re-edition (text: 'emended'), restoring 064 → 065 (ni, ×19), 013 → 008 (na, ×4) and B.06 38-24 → 104-24 (i-le); 49 of 85 emended legible groups read on the ruled grid.

Face A divides into 11 ruled paragraphs closing with a recurring three-group refrain centered on e-ni (preceded 4× by nu₂-sa-nu-wa); even Faucounau (1980, 394–396) concedes the refrain resists Hurrian interpretation. Face B preserves verbatim clauses opening with pa-pa-sa and closing with i-le (B.02–03, parallel B.06), ka-pa-ta \| se-su-wa-ra \| ta-ra-we₂ (B.18, B.20; matching Amathus Eteocypriot ICS 193 ta-ra-wi beside 003.B.17 ta-ra-wo₂ ↔ ICS 194 ta-ra-wo), the five-form nu₂-ka-rV paradigm, and the B.14–16 e-no suffixal triad.
