Enkomi baked clay tablet (##208, excavated 1953 by P. Dikaios; CM 2, 118 legible groups; Valério 2016, 447–448 Table II.2, 520–526). Entered on Valério's critical re-edition (text: 'emended'), which systematically restores 013 → 008 (na, ×12) and 064 → 065 (ni, ×5) across the tablet—separating CM 1 064 (o, 83% initial) from CM 2 065 (ni, 0% initial).

Exhibits three-stage agglutinative suffix chains (e-re → e-re-pi → e-re-pi-na; ta-ka → ta-ka-pi-na; and an unruled stem taking -ko and -na), the ka-pa-ni member of CM 2's four-case ka-pa-na/-ni/-sa/-ta paradigm, and on B.17 the adjacent pair ta-ra-wo₂ \| a-sa-na matching Amathus Eteocypriot ICS 194 (ta-ra-wo, a-so-na) beside ENKO Atab 004 ta-ra-we₂ (ICS 193 ta-ra-wi). Faucounau's (1980, 384–386) Hurrian reading as a letter to the king of the Lukki fails against ruled 024=le, 075=ra, 051=pi and Valério's B.15 epigraphy.
