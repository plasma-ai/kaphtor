Large Enkomi baked clay tablet (##207, 'tablette n° 4', CM 2, 121 corpus / 120 emended legible groups; Valério 2016, Appendix A). Entered on Valério's critical re-edition (text: 'emended'), restoring 013 → 008 (na, ×9) and 064 → 065 (ni, ×8); 74 of 120 emended legible groups read on the ruled grid.

Contains undamaged, unemended 102-075-027-069 = a-ra-si-ja (B.I.02, immediately after a-ka-na; compare Alashiya / Egyptian ỉrs3 / Linear B a-ra-si-jo, weighed against CM 2's five-form a-ra- stem family: a-ra, a-ra-ta, a-ra-ma, a-ra-to, a-ra-si-ja), cross-subcorpus 006-025 pa-ka (RASH Atab 001.A.05), e-la-la-si \| e-la-na (B.I.23), e-po-ro-no (B.I.13, extending ENKO Atab 004.B.14 e-po-ro), and the ka-pa-na / ka-pa-sa members of CM 2's four-case ka-pa- paradigm.
