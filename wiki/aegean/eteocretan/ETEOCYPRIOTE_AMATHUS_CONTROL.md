## Text

```text
a-na . ma-to-ri . u-mi-e-s[a]-i mu-ku-la-i la-sa-na ...
```

Greek: The city of the Amathusians honored noble Ariston son of Aristonax.

Gordon's transliteration:

```text
a-na ma-to-ri ... la-sa-na ... a-lo ... ka-i-li-po-ti
```

## Gordon's readings

| Form | Meaning | Basis | Constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| a-na | the/this | Greek city phrase | bilingual |
| la-sa-na | for this one | Semitic l + zana | bilingual/contextual |
| a-lo | over him | monument setting | contextual |
