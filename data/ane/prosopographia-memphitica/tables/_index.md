# Prosopographia Memphitica tables

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/_index|..]]

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_collections.jsonl|pm_collections.jsonl]]: 109 collectors and collections, with life dates and nationality.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_dating.jsonl|pm_dating.jsonl]]: 60 dating concepts (period, ruler), linked to THOT, TLA and PNM.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_location.jsonl|pm_location.jsonl]]: 134 places, with Trismegistos, TopBib, GeoNames and Wikidata ids.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_material.jsonl|pm_material.jsonl]]: 42 materials in three levels, linked to THOT, TLA and PNM.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_names.jsonl|pm_names.jsonl]]: 1,574 names, with Ranke references and the persons who bear them.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_objects.jsonl|pm_objects.jsonl]]: 1,687 objects: type, technique, dimensions, condition, inventory number and bibliography.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_persons.jsonl|pm_persons.jsonl]]: 2,409 persons: name, other name, gender, provenance and English rendering.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_provenance.jsonl|pm_provenance.jsonl]]: 238 holding collections and catalogue links.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_provenience.jsonl|pm_provenience.jsonl]]: 128 find contexts: place, site and context type.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_relations.jsonl|pm_relations.jsonl]]: 30,743 relations between rows of the other tables, each naming both ends.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/tables/pm_titles.jsonl|pm_titles.jsonl]]: 992 titles, transliterated and translated by component.

***

Every cell is ProM's string as released, and an empty cell is `''`. The transliterations are in the Umschrift_TTn font
encoding; `persons.jsonl` and `names.jsonl` one folder up carry their Unicode conversion.
