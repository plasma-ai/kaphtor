# Ancient Near East data

[[data/_index|..]]

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary|akkadian_literary]]: 6,233 Akkadian words and proper nouns from ORACC's CAMS/GKAB glossary, with their meanings, parts of speech and match keys; CC0 1.0.

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary.index.json|akkadian_literary.index.json]]: A lookup from normalized match key to entry id, derived from the glossary.

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary.jsonl|akkadian_literary.jsonl]]: The Akkadian literary glossary (vocabulary and proper nouns), from ORACC CAMS/GKAB, CC0 1.0.

[[data/ane/cypro-minoan/_index|cypro-minoan/]]: The 263 Cypro-Minoan texts (Valério's Appendix A and 9 outside it), keyed by siglum with site, object type, edition, date, script, subcorpus, and cited metadata, plus di Mino's readings of 98 texts (sign groups of read words only) and read-word grammar, lexicon, onomasticon, and 72-sign grid; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/diana-safaitic/_index|diana-safaitic/]]: The 1,546 entries of Al-Jallad's Dictionary of the Inscriptions of Ancient North Arabia I (Safaitic), the dictionary and the toponym glossary, each with its gloss, notes, compared forms, attestations and printed page, and the 340 group and divine names of its two lists, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

[[data/ane/diana2-taymanitic/_index|diana2-taymanitic/]]: The 556 headwords of Al-Jallad's Dictionary of the Inscriptions of Ancient North Arabia II (Taymanitic), the dictionary, toponyms, group names and onomasticon, each with its gloss, compared forms, attestations and printed page, CC BY-NC 4.0.

[[data/ane/elisir-eblaite/_index|elisir-eblaite/]]: The 37 published entries of Erica Scarpa's ELiSiR, a linked-data lexicon of Eblaite, each with its forms, senses and editorial note, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/gauthier-dng/_index|gauthier-dng/]]: 7,443 entries, cross-references and addenda of Gauthier's Dictionnaire des noms géographiques, t. 1–6 (1925–1929), read from the scans' OCR text layer as a page finder, public domain.

[[data/ane/hatra-marcato/_index|hatra-marcato/]]: The 407 entries of Enrico Marcato's catalogue of personal names in the Aramaic inscriptions of Hatra (376 names, 31 incomplete), each with his translation, other readings, analysis, inscription numbers and printed page, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/ig-xv-1-cypriot/_index|ig-xv-1-cypriot/]]: 311 inscriptions of Inscriptiones Graecae XV 1 (Inscriptiones Cypri syllabicae) in the Cypriot syllabary, Greek and Eteocypriot, with their editions, dates, findspots and German translations, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/isicily-semitic/_index|isicily-semitic/]]: The 73 inscriptions of I.Sicily whose language is Punic, Phoenician or Hebrew, with findspot, object, date and the edition where the source gives one, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/kassite-concordances/_index|kassite-concordances/]]: 9,621 entries of the name indexes in seven volumes of Kassite-period and kudurru texts (Clay, Hinke, Radau, King, 1906–1912), as OCR leads to the printed page, public domain.

[[data/ane/palmyra-public-inscriptions/_index|palmyra-public-inscriptions/]]: The 1,135 public inscriptions of Palmyra in Raja, Steding, Yon and Romanowska's dataset, with their PAT, IGLS, CIS and RES numbers, dates, buildings, gods and the persons named, and a concordance of those numbers, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/palmyrene-theonym-elements/_index|palmyrene-theonym-elements/]]: Thirteen divine names of Palmyra with the Aramaic, Greek and vocalised forms in which the sources write them, each form with the work and page that prints it, for finding the names built on them, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/place-authorities/_index|place-authorities/]]: Pleiades place records (CC BY 3.0), used as identity controls.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/_index|pnm-v6/]]: Persons and Names of the Middle Kingdom, version 6 (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15806808), CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/_index|prosopographia-memphitica/]]: New Kingdom Memphite persons and names from Prosopographia Memphitica (ProM, release 2024-11-15), with titles, dating, objects and Unicode transliterations, CC BY 4.0 and ODC-By 1.0.

[[data/ane/ugarit-texts-database/_index|ugarit-texts-database/]]: Catalogue metadata for 4,582 inscribed objects from Ras Shamra and Ras Ibn Hani (find number, KTU number, language, script, genre, findspot), from František Válek's Ugarit Texts Database, CC BY 4.0.

***

Each dataset names its licence: the glossary in `akkadian_literary.md`, the rest in their `_index.md` and
`MANIFEST.json`.
