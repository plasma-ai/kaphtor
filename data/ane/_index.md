# Ancient Near East data

[[data/_index|..]]

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary|akkadian_literary]]: 6,233 Akkadian words and proper nouns from ORACC's CAMS/GKAB glossary, with their meanings, parts of speech and match keys; CC0 1.0.

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary.index.json|akkadian_literary.index.json]]: A lookup from normalized match key to entry id, derived from the glossary.

[[data/ane/akkadian_literary.jsonl|akkadian_literary.jsonl]]: The Akkadian literary glossary (vocabulary and proper nouns), from ORACC CAMS/GKAB, CC0 1.0.

[[data/ane/gauthier-dng/_index|gauthier-dng/]]: 7,497 entries, cross-references and addenda of Gauthier's Dictionnaire des noms géographiques, t. 1–6 (1925–1929), read from the scans' OCR text layer as a page finder, public domain.

[[data/ane/kassite-concordances/_index|kassite-concordances/]]: 9,621 entries of the name indexes in seven volumes of Kassite-period and kudurru texts (Clay, Hinke, Radau, King, 1906–1912), as OCR leads to the printed page, public domain.

[[data/ane/place-authorities/_index|place-authorities/]]: Pleiades place records (CC BY 3.0), used as identity controls.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/_index|pnm-v6/]]: Persons and Names of the Middle Kingdom, version 6 (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15806808), CC BY 4.0.

[[data/ane/prosopographia-memphitica/_index|prosopographia-memphitica/]]: New Kingdom Memphite persons and names from Prosopographia Memphitica (ProM, release 2024-11-15), with titles, dating, objects and Unicode transliterations, CC BY 4.0 and ODC-By 1.0.

***

Each dataset names its licence: the glossary in `akkadian_literary.md`, the rest in their `_index.md` and
`MANIFEST.json`.
