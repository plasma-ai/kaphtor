# Place authorities

[[data/ane/_index|..]]

[[data/ane/place-authorities/MANIFEST.json|MANIFEST.json]]: Sources, licences, checksums and counts for the place layer.

[[data/ane/place-authorities/pleiades_places.jsonl|pleiades_places.jsonl]]: Pleiades place records, CC BY 3.0.

***

These records identify places. They are identity controls, never linguistic attestations. Trismegistos places
(CC BY-SA 4.0) sit in `data/share-alike/trismegistos/`, with their own manifest. iDAI.gazetteer publishes no licence
for its place data, so its records are not here; `tools/ane/query_idai_gazetteer.py` queries it one page at a time.
