# PNM v6 tables

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/_index|..]]

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/alternative_readings.jsonl|alternative_readings.jsonl]]: Alternative readings, each linking a personal name to a spelling.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/attestations.jsonl|attestations.jsonl]]: Name attestations, one per occurrence of a personal name in an inscription.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/biblio_refs.jsonl|biblio_refs.jsonl]]: Bibliographic references attached to records, with pages and reference type.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/bonds.jsonl|bonds.jsonl]]: Kinship bonds between attestations, with the wording that states each one.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/children_temp.jsonl|children_temp.jsonl]]: Child-to-parent pairs of attestations, a working table kept as published.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/collections.jsonl|collections.jsonl]]: The museums and collections that hold the objects.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/criteria.jsonl|criteria.jsonl]]: Dating criteria, cited by id in the inscriptions' dating notes.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/find_groups.jsonl|find_groups.jsonl]]: Find groups, with site, location, dating and disturbance.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/info.jsonl|info.jsonl]]: The database's explanatory notes, among them its list of abbreviations.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/inscriptions.jsonl|inscriptions.jsonl]]: The inscriptions, with dating, origin, script and text content.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/inscriptions_workshops_xref.jsonl|inscriptions_workshops_xref.jsonl]]: Links from objects to the workshops proposed for them, with status.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/inv_nos.jsonl|inv_nos.jsonl]]: Museum inventory numbers of the objects, by collection.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/name_types.jsonl|name_types.jsonl]]: Name types, the patterns personal names follow, in a hierarchy.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/name_types_temp.jsonl|name_types_temp.jsonl]]: Child-to-parent pairs of name types, a working table kept as published.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/names_types_xref.jsonl|names_types_xref.jsonl]]: Links from personal names to name types.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/objects.jsonl|objects.jsonl]]: The inscribed objects, with type, material, dimensions, provenance and find group.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/objects_inscriptions_xref.jsonl|objects_inscriptions_xref.jsonl]]: Links from objects to the inscriptions they carry.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/personal_names.jsonl|personal_names.jsonl]]: The personal names, with gender and search and sort forms.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/persons.jsonl|persons.jsonl]]: Person dossiers, with name, titles, gender, dating and region.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/persons_attestations_xref.jsonl|persons_attestations_xref.jsonl]]: Links from persons to their attestations, with the reasoning and status of each.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/persons_bonds.jsonl|persons_bonds.jsonl]]: Kinship bonds between persons.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/places.jsonl|places.jsonl]]: Places, with coordinates and Pleiades, Trismegistos and Topographical Bibliography ids.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/publications.jsonl|publications.jsonl]]: The publications cited, with author-year and CSL JSON.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/siblings_temp.jsonl|siblings_temp.jsonl]]: Sibling pairs of attestations, a working table kept as published.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/spellings.jsonl|spellings.jsonl]]: The spellings of each personal name.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/spellings_attestations_xref.jsonl|spellings_attestations_xref.jsonl]]: Links from attestations to spellings, with the classifier.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/spouses_temp.jsonl|spouses_temp.jsonl]]: Spouse pairs of attestations, a working table kept as published.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/thesauri.jsonl|thesauri.jsonl]]: The controlled vocabularies the other tables draw on.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/title_relations.jsonl|title_relations.jsonl]]: Relations between titles.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/titles.jsonl|titles.jsonl]]: Titles, with English and German translations and their Ward-Fischer, Taylor, Hannig and Ayedi numbers.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/titles_att.jsonl|titles_att.jsonl]]: Title attestations, linking an attestation to its titles in sequence.

[[data/ane/pnm-v6/tables/workshops.jsonl|workshops.jsonl]]: Workshops, with dating and production place.

***

Table counts are in `../MANIFEST.json`.
