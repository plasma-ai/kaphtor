# Linear A data

[[data/_index|..]]

[[data/linear-a/census_classes.json|census_classes.json]]: The hand-curated record sets the corpus census counts by; datasheet `corpus.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/corpus|corpus]]: One record per Linear A inscription or face, with its object, findspot, edition, Unicode text and transliteration.

[[data/linear-a/corpus.json|corpus.json]]: The inscriptions, one record per inscription or face; datasheet `corpus.md`; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/egyptian_roots.json|egyptian_roots.json]]: The Egyptian root keys of the competitor null, with forms and citations; datasheet `rival-roots.md`; CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/eteocretan|eteocretan]]: The Eteocretan inscriptions (Dreros, Praisos), one proven fake and one Eteocypriote control, with Gordon's word readings and word analyses.

[[data/linear-a/eteocretan_inscriptions.json|eteocretan_inscriptions.json]]: The Eteocretan inscriptions; datasheet `eteocretan.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/eteocretan_word_analysis.json|eteocretan_word_analysis.json]]: Gordon's analyses of the Eteocretan words; datasheet `eteocretan.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/hurrian_roots.json|hurrian_roots.json]]: The Hurrian root keys of the competitor null, with forms and citations; datasheet `rival-roots.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/inscription_image_files|inscription_image_files]]: Each corpus record's photograph and facsimile files, as paths inside a checkout of the image archive; names only, no images.

[[data/linear-a/inscription_image_files.json|inscription_image_files.json]]: The image file names by record; datasheet `inscription_image_files.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/lexicon|lexicon]]: One entry per Linear A word, logogram or sign group, with its reading, meaning, root, cognates, tier and attestations.

[[data/linear-a/lexicon.json|lexicon.json]]: The lexicon; datasheet `lexicon.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/linear-b-sign-map.json|linear-b-sign-map.json]]: The Linear B and Aegean Numbers characters with Unicode fields; datasheet `sign-maps.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/luwian_roots.json|luwian_roots.json]]: The Luwian root keys of the competitor null, with forms and their analogues' labels; datasheet `rival-roots.md`; CC BY-SA 4.0 for its Wiktionary entries, and the ACLT and TIED terms set out there.

[[data/linear-a/missing_sound_map|missing_sound_map]]: The phonemic gaps of the Linear A syllabary against the Proto-Semitic consonants, the signs whose values fill them, and the positional behaviour of the frequent unknown signs.

[[data/linear-a/missing_sound_map.json|missing_sound_map.json]]: The gaps, their fillers and the positional index; datasheet `missing_sound_map.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/morphemes|morphemes]]: The prefixes, suffixes, case vowels and particles the morphological analysis strips or tests, each with its value, function, tier and evidence.

[[data/linear-a/morphemes.json|morphemes.json]]: The affixes and particles; datasheet `morphemes.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/onomasticon|onomasticon]]: One entry per Linear A name, toponym, theonym, gentilic or title, with its type, its linguistic affiliation and the evidence for it, and every document where it appears.

[[data/linear-a/onomasticon.json|onomasticon.json]]: The names, toponyms, theonyms, gentilics and titles, with their linguistic affiliation; datasheet `onomasticon.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/paradigm-b/_index|paradigm-b/]]: The frozen inputs and the five production runs of Paradigm B, the pre-registered held-out paradigm-prediction test, with the freeze's SHA256SUMS and the port ledger.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/_index|protocol-c/]]: The phase C0 and C1 record of Protocol C, the pre-registered blind-inventory test, with the signature and the port ledger; nothing rendered or scored.

[[data/linear-a/rival-roots|rival-roots]]: The Egyptian, Luwian and Hurrian root keys the competitor null rescores every Semitic match against, each with its form and its citations or source labels.

[[data/linear-a/sigla-sign-map.json|sigla-sign-map.json]]: The SigLA sign list with Unicode fields; datasheet `sign-maps.md`; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (SigLA's dataset).

[[data/linear-a/sign-maps|sign-maps]]: The SigLA sign list of Linear A (362 signs) and the 268 characters of Unicode's Linear B and Aegean Numbers blocks, each with its code point, name and status.

[[data/linear-a/sign-registry|sign-registry]]: One record per Linear A sign, with its readings and their status, its Unicode rendering, a SigLA image, its Cretan Hieroglyphic and Linear B lineage, and the Gordon 1966 words that use it.

[[data/linear-a/sign-registry.json|sign-registry.json]]: The signs, with their readings, rendering and lineage; datasheet `sign-registry.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/sign_values|sign_values]]: The phonetic values of the Linear A syllabograms, the logograms and the fraction signs, with the resolved and proposed values listed apart.

[[data/linear-a/sign_values.json|sign_values.json]]: The syllabogram, logogram and fraction values, resolved and proposed; datasheet `sign_values.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/translations|translations]]: Translations of Linear A inscriptions, keyed by inscription id, each with a word-by-word analysis.

[[data/linear-a/translations.json|translations.json]]: Inscription translations with word-by-word analyses; datasheet `translations.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/linear-a/unread_words|unread_words]]: Every Linear A word of two or more signs with no reading in the lexicon, translations or onomasticon, each with its attestations and a note on the edition's text.

[[data/linear-a/unread_words.json|unread_words.json]]: The unread words; datasheet `unread_words.md`; CC BY 4.0.
