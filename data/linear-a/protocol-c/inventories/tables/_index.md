# Protocol C unit lists

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/_index|..]]

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/cs|cs]]: The units the cs builder transcribes (Central Semitic), with section and page, as signed at C0.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/egyptian|egyptian]]: The units the egyptian builder transcribes (Middle Egyptian), with section and page, as signed at C0.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/greek|greek]]: The units the greek builder transcribes (Mycenaean Greek), with section and page, as signed at C0.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/hurrian|hurrian]]: The units the hurrian builder transcribes (Hurrian), with section and page, as signed at C0.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/luwian|luwian]]: The units the luwian builder transcribes (Luwian), with section and page, as signed at C0.

[[data/linear-a/protocol-c/inventories/tables/yd|yd]]: The units the yd builder transcribes (Ya Diktu), with section and page, as signed at C0.

***

Each list names its grammar (Huehnergard 2019 with Huehnergard 2005 and Huehnergard and Rubin 2011 for `cs`; *Ya
Diktu* for `yd`; Yakubovich 2015 for `luwian`; Wegner 2000 for `hurrian`; Allen 2014 for `egyptian`; Bartoněk 2003
for `greek`) and the units its builder reads, one row each: category, unit type, section or table number, title as
printed, printed page and PDF page or anchor. `greek.md` also gives Bartoněk's Linear B spelling rules, which §5.5
uses. Each file is, below its frontmatter, the `units.md` in its build's kit.
