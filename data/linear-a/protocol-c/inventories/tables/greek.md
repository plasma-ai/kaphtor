## Not tabulated

- Pronominal: Bartoněk gives no table or list of bound possessive or object pronouns; the free "Pronomina" list (printed p.343) is dropped.
- Verbal: no table of person endings; they are prose only (row VII.E.4.b). The attestation lists of whole verb forms under VII.E (printed pp.306–336) are dropped as not affix units, since they give no ending paradigm.
- Nominal: the definite article is absent from Mycenaean (VII.B, printed p.154). No state markers. Duals appear as columns inside the declension tables.
- Clitics: -qe (Konjunktionen) and -de I/II (Partikeln) are the only enclitics (printed p.349). The free Präpositionen list is dropped.
- Dropped under the unit rule: numerals and comparatives example tables (p.301), word-class and declension count tables, per-stem attestation word-lists (from p.169), the Bildung einzelner Tempora prose (VII.E.4.e, p.339), whose heading names no category.
