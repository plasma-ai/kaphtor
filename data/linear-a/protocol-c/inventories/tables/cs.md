## Not tabulated

- Huehnergard 2019 has no table of nominal gender/number markers apart from Table 3.7, and none of the article (PS has none), numerals, non-finite forms, suffix conjugation, or weak-root paradigms; those are covered by the headed-section rows only.
- Huehnergard and Rubin 2011 has no paradigm tables, only trees (Figs. 9.1–9.3) and prose; its rows are stated innovations. Its §7.1 (loss of feminine -t) and §7.4 (definite article) are areal features, not innovations, and are excluded.
- Huehnergard 2005 names five confident innovations (summary, p. 191). Items (1) and (11) carry bound morphemes and enter above; they restate H&R §§8.1–8.2 and add no form. Items (10) the tens, (14) halləz ~ allaδī and (15) ma-zzot / *mah- are numerals, a demonstrative-relative pronoun and interrogative constructions: free words, out by the unit rule. Items (2)–(9), (12), (13) and (16) are listed as possible retentions, areal or parallel developments (pp. 191–193), and stay out under A1; this keeps out §II.4 feminine *-at > -ā, §II.5 suffix-conjugation -tu and §II.12 G passive *yuqtal.
- Free pronoun Tables 3.3 and 3.4 (pp. 53–54) are excluded as non-bound. Tables 3.1–3.2 are consonant inventories, outside all categories.
