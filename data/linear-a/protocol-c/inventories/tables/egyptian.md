## Not tabulated

- Nominal: no state markers (Egyptian has no mimation or nunation) and no table of case endings (no case system). The definite article does not appear as a bound affix; the pʒ/tʒ/nʒ article series (§5.11) is a free word.
- Pronominal: no table of bound object pronouns apart from the dependent-pronoun and suffix-pronoun rows above.
- Verbal: no table of a separate passive -tw paradigm; passive -tw is described in prose (§14.5 impersonal subject pronoun tw; §17.6 sdm.n.f with suffix tw; §18.3 sdm.f with suffix tw) and §26.8–26.9 summarize verb forms and endings as numbered lists, not tables. No table for the derived-stem causative s- prefix beyond the "caus." rows inside the by-root-class tables.
- Dropped under the unit rule: numerals (§9.2, §9.3), and ky (§6.7), ntj (§22.2) and jwtj (§22.8), which Allen presents as free words, not bound or affixal forms.
- Clitics: §8.2 and §15.7 are numbered lists, not tables, and are included on a literal reading of "prepositions that take suffixes" and "enclitic particles"; strike them if the protocol requires tables only.

## Excluded by category (judgement calls, listed so the signer can reinstate)

- Free-form pronoun tables: independent pronouns (§5.5, md lines 1815 and 1836, pp.62–63), demonstratives (§5.8–5.10, lines 1922, 1947, 1987, pp.65–67), "new pronouns" (§5.11, line 2004, p.67), interrogatives (§5.12, line 2022, p.68), independent pronoun + pw sentence patterns (§7.12, lines 2720 and 2735, pp.94–95). The independent column does appear in the §5.6 summary row.
- Genitival adjective n/nw/nt (§4.13, line 1502, p.50): free word.
- Other apparent adjectives (§6.7, lines 2263 and 2276, p.79).
- Root-class stem tables (§12.5, lines 4610–4774, pp.167–170, and §12.6, lines 4799 and 4815, p.171): list base and geminated stems, not affixes. §21.6 (line 10188, p.335) likewise.
- Proclitic particles (§15.6, p.213): written as separate words.
- Syntax and clause-distribution tables (§§7, 10–11, 20–22, 25–26: negations, predicate types, emphatic sentences), numerals' signs and measures (§9.1, 9.7–9.10), honorific transposition (§4.15), and essay and exercise tables.
