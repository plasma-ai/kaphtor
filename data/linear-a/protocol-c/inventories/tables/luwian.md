Headed sections (unit c): none. The section headings are 6.2 Noun and adjective, 6.3 Numeral, 6.4 Pronoun, 6.5 Verb, 6.6 Other forms, 7.1 Clause structure, and none names a category. The non-finite forms paragraph (participle -mm(a/i)-, infinitive -una, gerundives -min(a)) sits under 6.5 Verb (p. 17), no such heading, so it is left out.

Not tabulated:

- Participial and infinitival affixes: no table (none listed).
- Duals, energics: none.
- Tables 1, 4 and 6 fall outside the four categories as defined and are left out: Table 1 (p. 9) is the consonant system; Table 4 (p. 15, "Luwian personal tonic pronouns") and Table 6 (p. 15, "Luwian demonstrative and relative pronouns") tabulate free (tonic) pronouns, not bound possessive or object pronouns. Tell the signer if free-pronoun tables are wanted in scope.
- The possessive adjective ami(ya)- is given as a running-text paradigm on p. 16, not a table, and is a free form.
- Verb example forms by person on pp. 16-17 (1sg.pres. kars-ui, etc.) are a list of attested examples, not a table.
