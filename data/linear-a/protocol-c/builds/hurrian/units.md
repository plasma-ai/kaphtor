Not tabulated or left out:

- Nominal gender: none (Hurrian has no gender, p. 52). Definite article and state markers: the "Artikel" -ni/-na appears only as a column in Tabelle 1 and Tabelle 2.
Predicative participles in -i=b/-a=b appear in Tabelle 12 only.
- Headings that name no listed category and are left out: "2. Die Nominalmorphologie", "4. Die Verbalmorphologie", "Die Suffixkette beim Nomen", "1. Silbenstruktur und Wortbildungselemente" (nominal derivation, pp. 46-51, lists of derivational suffixes), "2. Übungsbeispiele zur „Suffixaufnahme“", "4. Übungsbeispiele zu den Verben".
- Free forms, out by ruling: independent personal pronouns (p. 71), demonstratives and indefinites (pp. 72-73), numerals (pp. 70-71).
- Exercise sets F.1 to F.4 (pp. 119-126) are practice material and are not entered.
- Out of category: phoneme table (p. 40), Morphemverzeichnis (pp. 257-262, an index).
- Person-by-person example segmentations of the jussive (pp. 88-89) and the intransitive examples (p. 84) are examples under their own headings, not entered.
