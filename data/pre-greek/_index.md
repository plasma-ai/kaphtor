# Pre-Greek

[[data/_index|..]]

[[data/pre-greek/pregreek_features.json|pregreek_features.json]]: The Pre-Greek features of Beekes 2014, chapters 2–3, with their kind, chapter, page and Greek notation; datasheet `pregreek_lexicon.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/pre-greek/pregreek_lexicon|pregreek_lexicon]]: Greek words that scholars class as Pre-Greek or as early Semitic loans, with the works that treat each word, the Semitic comparisons proposed for it, Beekes's Pre-Greek features and a sidecar of Wiktionary glosses; what each work gives and what is withheld.

[[data/pre-greek/pregreek_lexicon.index.json|pregreek_lexicon.index.json]]: A lookup from match key to word id, derived from the lexicon.

[[data/pre-greek/pregreek_lexicon.json|pregreek_lexicon.json]]: Greek words classed as Pre-Greek or as early Semitic loans, one record per headword, with their sources and Semitic comparisons; datasheet `pregreek_lexicon.md`; CC BY 4.0.

[[data/pre-greek/pregreek_wiktionary_glosses.json|pregreek_wiktionary_glosses.json]]: Short English glosses from Wiktionary for the lexicon's words, keyed by word id; datasheet `pregreek_lexicon.md`; CC BY-SA 4.0.
