# Egyptian texts

[[data/share-alike/_index|..]]

[[data/share-alike/egyptian/egyptian_documents.index.json|egyptian_documents.index.json]]: The document lookup index, derived (23,707 keys).

[[data/share-alike/egyptian/egyptian_documents.jsonl|egyptian_documents.jsonl]]: ORAEC document records, one per ORAEC text (13,026).

***

Every record is ORAEC's, as imported (`review_status: machine_imported`): title, date, genre, object type,
provenance, bibliography and Trismegistos id. Titles
and translations are German. The sentence file (`egyptian_sentences.jsonl`, 103 MB) and its index wait for LFS or a
release asset.
