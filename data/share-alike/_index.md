# Share-alike data

[[data/_index|..]]

[[data/share-alike/anatolian/_index|anatolian/]]: The 19,725 preserved-word document records of TLHdig 0.3 covering Hittite, Luwian, Hurrian, Hattic and Boğazköy Akkadian, CC BY 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/cuneiform/_index|cuneiform/]]: The 1,420 CC0 ORACC Old/Standard Babylonian Akkadian documents, 1,855 CC0 ORACC Sumerian documents and 3,589 clean vocalized ePSD2 Sumerian glossed lemmas, CC0 1.0.

[[data/share-alike/egyptian/_index|egyptian/]]: The 13,026 ORAEC/TLA Egyptian document records (open TLA republication), CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/greek/_index|greek/]]: The 117,130 headwords and 101,410 glossed Linear B keys of Liddell, Scott and Jones's Greek lexicon, and the 2,465 sections of Diodorus Siculus, Books 1–5, in the Perseus text, CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/hebrew/_index|hebrew/]]: The 306,785 pointed words of the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (Westminster Leningrad Codex), CC BY 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/latin/_index|latin/]]: The 816,985 inflected Latin word forms of the LiLa LatInfLexi lexicon, CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/linear-b/_index|linear-b/]]: The syllabic tokens of the Pylos and Knossos Linear B tablets, each with its tablet, line, place and its status against DAMOS, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/oracc-levant/_index|oracc-levant/]]: The 2,583 ORACC editions of Ugarit's Akkadian texts, the Amarna letters and the Statue of Idrimi, with 7,122 translation units, 1,153 find numbers and 11,155 proper-name occurrences, CC BY-SA 3.0.

[[data/share-alike/tla-v18-names/_index|tla-v18-names/]]: The 9,186 proper-name tokens of the Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae's Original Earlier Egyptian sentences (corpus v18), with their hieroglyphs and dates, under 1,644 lemmas, CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/trismegistos/_index|trismegistos/]]: Trismegistos Geo place records, CC BY-SA 4.0.

[[data/share-alike/ugaritic/_index|ugaritic/]]: The 2,510 vocalized Ugaritic tokens of the Electronic Ugaritic Poetic Texts (EUPT), CC BY-SA 4.0.

***

Anything derived from these files carries the same share-alike terms; the Linear B tokens are also for
non-commercial use. The Syriac lexicon in `data/contact/` and the
Egyptian and Luwian rival-root keys in `data/linear-a/` also carry CC BY-SA 4.0, named in their datasheets.
