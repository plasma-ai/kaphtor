# Docs

[[_index|..]]

[[docs/anatomy|anatomy]]: How the repository is laid out, how its pages are named, and which pages are generated.

[[docs/bibliography|bibliography]]: The full references for the works the Linear A data, the Semitic roots and the Pre-Greek lexicon cite, under the author–year keys and sigla the data uses.

[[docs/confidence|confidence]]: The confidence tiers, the tests a reading passes to rise a tier, and what counts as a resolved sign value.

[[docs/corpora/_index|corpora/]]: What each local ancient Near East corpus holds, its rights and gaps, and how to query it.

[[docs/corpus-census|corpus-census]]: How many Linear A documents GORILA and RILA-S1 publish, which of them corpus.json holds and under which keys, and why the corpus holds more.

[[docs/human-review/_index|human-review/]]: The kinds of artifact that serve a person making the final call on a Linear A reading, what question each settles, and the two review pages with their templates, worked examples and screenshots.

[[docs/pre-greek-lexicon|pre-greek-lexicon]]: How the Pre-Greek lexicon is compiled: its sources, the folding of headwords, the identity rule, each work's verdict codes, and what ships and what is withheld.

[[docs/reproduce|reproduce]]: What `kaphtor reproduce` reruns, what each headline number rests on, and the tolerance a rerun must meet.

[[docs/semitic-roots-scan|semitic-roots-scan]]: How the Semitic roots grow from the dictionaries they cite, entry by entry: the units and the coverage measure, the seven verdicts, the rules for joining a row or adding one, the cell form, and the gold sets that test a reader before a full scan.

[[docs/shoresh-preregistration|shoresh-preregistration]]: The full pre-registration of the Shoresh root-match engine, with its Phase 0 baseline and the dated amendments A1–A21 and A23.

[[docs/shoresh-usage|shoresh-usage]]: How to build Shoresh's index, run the root-match command and read its output, from the statistic and verdicts to the gold guard and exit codes.

The tiers and their tests are in `confidence.md`; the layout and naming of the record are in `anatomy.md`.
