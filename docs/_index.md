[[docs/semitic-roots-scan|semitic-roots-scan]]: How the Semitic roots grow from the dictionaries they cite, entry by entry: the units and the coverage measure, the seven verdicts, the rules for joining a row or adding one, the cell form, and the gold sets that test a reader before a full scan.
