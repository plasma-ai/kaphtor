# Corpus guides

[[docs/_index|..]]

[[docs/corpora/aegean-comparative-corpora|aegean-comparative-corpora]]: The Hurrian, Cypro-Minoan, Cypriot, Old Assyrian and Eblaite layers indexed as controls for Linear A, with their coverage, boundaries and acquisition gaps.

[[docs/corpora/aegean-scripts-databases|aegean-scripts-databases]]: The open online databases for Linear A, Linear B and the scripts beside them (SigLA, DAMOS, MNAMON and INSCRIBE), with what each holds, how to reach it and its licence.

[[docs/corpora/arabic-corpora|arabic-corpora]]: Acquisition routes, file formats and APIs for the Quran text and morphology, the classical Arabic lexica, pre-Islamic Arabian epigraphy and the sources on pagan Arabia.

[[docs/corpora/aramaic-corpora|aramaic-corpora]]: The passage-level Aramaic index (Biblical Aramaic, the Targums, Qumran, Syriac, inscriptions and the Babylonian Talmud), its fields, validation, sources and gaps.

[[docs/corpora/bronze-iron-semitic-corpora|bronze-iron-semitic-corpora]]: The Bronze- and Iron-Age Semitic corpora the project has indexed, from CDLI and ORACC to the Ebla, Ugarit and Hebrew sets, with their rights and the acquisitions still outstanding.

[[docs/corpora/classical-egyptian-databases|classical-egyptian-databases]]: The open Greek, Latin and Egyptian text databases (TLA, Perseus, TLG and Digital Egypt for Universities), what each holds and how to reach it.

[[docs/corpora/cuneiform-corpora|cuneiform-corpora]]: What the local CDLI, ORACC, SEAL and ARCHIBAB snapshot covers, how each layer was acquired, and how the layers are kept apart.

[[docs/corpora/cuneiform-databases|cuneiform-databases]]: How to reach one record at a time on the live CDLI and ORACC sites, and how ATF transliteration is written.

[[docs/corpora/cuneiform-indices/_index|cuneiform-indices/]]: Tables of contents and coverage statistics for the CDLI and ORACC snapshots.

[[docs/corpora/egyptian-corpora|egyptian-corpora]]: Acquisition routes, file formats and gaps for the Egyptian texts layer, built on the open ORAEC/TLA corpus, with the texts that name Keftiu or the Levant.

[[docs/corpora/proper-name-and-place-authorities|proper-name-and-place-authorities]]: ORACC QPN, PNM v6, Prosopographia Memphitica, the TLA v18 names, Gauthier's DNG, the Kassite name concordances, the Egyptian foreign-name witnesses and the place authorities, and why a place match identifies a place but never counts as linguistic evidence.

[[docs/corpora/query-notation|query-notation]]: How a typed root or form is read and spelled in each view's own transliteration, and how a matched row reports the basis of its match.

[[docs/corpora/sefaria_api|sefaria_api]]: How to query Sefaria's public API for Hebrew Bible passages, Targums, commentaries, links and search, one passage at a time.

[[docs/corpora/single-query-portals|single-query-portals]]: The one-query clients for DiCoNab, DEAPS, Elephantine and RSTI, what each request may fetch, and what the parsers keep.

[[docs/corpora/south-semitic-corpora|south-semitic-corpora]]: The Geʿez, Ancient South Arabian and Modern South Arabian layers, kept as separate comparative evidence, with their scope and how to search them.

***

Read `query-notation.md` before querying any view: each corpus keeps its own transliteration, and a hand-spelled
query misses rows stored in another scheme.

Each guide describes a corpus, its licence and its gaps. A corpus whose terms do not allow redistribution is reached
by its fetcher, never shipped.
