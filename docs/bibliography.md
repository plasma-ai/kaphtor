# Bibliography

[[docs/_index|..]]

The Linear A data (`lexicon.json`, `translations.json`, `onomasticon.json`, `corpus.json` and `unread_words.json` in `data/linear-a/`) the Semitic roots (`data/semitic-roots/semitic_roots.json`) and the Pre-Greek lexicon (`data/pre-greek/pregreek_lexicon.json`) cite works in two ways:

- **By author and year with a page:** "(Rendsburg 2026, 204)". Each such key is listed below in full, in alphabetical order. A key the data cites only through another work is listed under "Cited at second hand", with the work it is cited through.
- **By dictionary or corpus siglum:** "(DULAT 307)", "(HALOT 1099)", "(CAD K 466)". Each siglum is listed under "Sigla", with the work it names.

A key or siglum not listed here names its work in the record that cites it.
